Every MahekOne app has a bell in its header. Until October 2026 every bell showed the same list: everything addressed to the person, from every app. So somebody who works in both the ERP and the CRM opened the ERP's bell at the godown and read about a declined CRM order, and the red count on a tablet counted leads. Each bell now belongs to its app.

## What a bell shows

| You are in | The red count and the first list | "All apps" |
|---|---|---|
| An app — the CRM, Accounts, the ERP, HRMS… | That app's notifications, plus the **general** ones | Everything, each marked with its app |
| A screen in no app (the launcher, Feedback) | Everything | — |

At the foot of an app's list, **Unread in other apps** names what is waiting elsewhere ("Accounts 1 · HRMS 2") and opens the All apps view. When nothing is unread in this app but something is elsewhere, the bell shows a small grey dot instead of a red count.

<Callout kind="why">
Nothing is hidden: every notification is one tab away, and the line at the foot of the list says how many are waiting and where. What changed is what is *counted* and what *pops up* — a decision about another app's work no longer rings over the screen somebody is working in.
</Callout>

## Which app a notification belongs to

A notification belongs to the app **its link opens**. "Order declined" links to the customer in the CRM, so it is the CRM's; "3 orders waiting for approval" links to Accounts' approval queue, so it is Accounts'.

- **General** — a company-wide announcement, something about your own account, or a link into no app (a reply to your feedback, for example). These are the only notifications shown in every app's bell.
- **Salesman App** — a notification with a phone screen and no web screen goes to the phone, and is in no web bell, not even All apps.

A notification with a web link and no phone screen stays at the desk: a stock alert on the ERP does not buzz a phone. One with both reaches both, for people who work in both places.

Nothing is stored to decide this; it is read from the link every time. So it was right for every notification already sent the day it shipped, and it is right for any new kind somebody adds later.

## The pop-up and "Mark all read"

In every app, a new notification is a pop-up over the screen. It waits until you finish typing or close any dialog that is already open.

- **Several at once** show as **Notification 1 of 4**. The arrows (or ← and → on the keyboard) move between them without answering any.
- **Each one is answered on its own**:
  - **Take action now** marks it done and opens the page it names. The rest stay waiting, and come back when you leave that page (or after ten minutes on it).
  - **Mark as done** marks it done and leaves you where you are.
  - **Remind me**: in 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, or tomorrow morning (10:00). It stays unread in the bell while it waits, then comes back.
- **Until it is answered, it stays.** A notification that was shown and not answered comes back after a reload, in a new tab, or on another computer. Escape or a click outside counts as "remind me in 30 minutes", never as done.
- **Remind me about all** and **Mark all as done** answer the whole stack at once.
- A pop-up comes only for this app's notifications and the general ones. One that arrived for another app while you were elsewhere pops when you open that app, as long as it is still recent (ten minutes).
- The factory phone screens and the print pages never get a pop-up, so it cannot cover a scan or stop a print.
- **Mark all read** in an app's bell marks that app's notifications and the general ones. **Mark everything read** in the All apps view marks every one.

## Which apps have a bell

The CRM, Accounts, the Sales Dashboard, the ERP, HRMS, Hire, Website Enquiries, Documentation and the Admin Console. The Founder Command Centre draws its own header and has no bell; its notifications are in any other app's All apps view.
