Five small screens over shared machinery: field settings are the `mbos.*` slice of the configuration registry, saved through `updateSettings`; bindings and sync health read `mbos_devices`, `mbos_sync_receipts` and `mbos_conflicts`; notifications go through the shared `announce` → `notifyUsers` → `pushToUsers`; the audit trail is a filter over `audit_log`.

The handset receives `mbos.*` keys on its pull; keys that must not reach a phone are excluded from the wire (pinned in `mbos-wire.test.ts`).

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="A new field setting needs a group and a type">
Add it to `registry.ts` with a `Config` type entry; give it a `category` that `SETTING_GROUPS` claims, or it lands under *Everything else*. If the handset reads it, add it to the handset's config defaults and check the wire test.
</Step>
<Step title="Never let the notify path skip scope">
Any new audience option must pass through `scopeCovers(managerScope)`.
</Step>
<Step title="Write the record before the push">
A push is best-effort; nothing may fail because a push failed.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Known gaps

| Gap | Where |
|---|---|
| `fieldAudit` and `fieldAuditCount` are not scoped to the manager's team | `sales-service.ts` `fieldAudit` |
| `pushReadiness` counts every active device in the company, not the sender's team | `mbos/push.ts` `pushReadiness` |
| The page is titled "Handsets and sign-in" while the module and nav say "Login history" | `logins/page.tsx`; `lib/modules.ts` |
| The preferences page is titled "Field settings" while the module says "App preferences" | `prefs/settings-screen.tsx`; `lib/modules.ts` |
