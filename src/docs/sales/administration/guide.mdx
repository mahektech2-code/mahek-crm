Five screens under **Administration** run the handsets from the office: **App preferences** (the thresholds every phone reads), **Login history** (which phone each salesman is bound to), **Sync health** (whose phone has gone quiet, and what the office has refused from it), **Send a notification** (a message to one salesman, a few, or the whole team) and the **Audit trail** (every decision made on the Sales Dashboard, with a name against it).

Opening this screen needs the App preferences module; **saving** also needs permission to change settings, which a manager on the Sales Dashboard holds and an associate does not. Only field settings can be saved here. Saved changes reach each handset on its next sync. Settings for the rest of MahekOne are in the Admin Console.

## Login history

One row per salesman: the handset he is bound to, its app version, when it was bound and when it last spoke, and its state.

| State | It means |
|---|---|
| <Pill tone="success">Active</Pill> | Bound to a phone. |
| <Pill tone="warn">No handset</Pill> | He has never signed in on a phone. |
| <Pill>Released</Pill> | The binding was released — hover for the reason. |

With one phone per person switched on, a second phone is **refused** until the first is released. That is not a fault; it is what stops one salesman's visits being logged from somebody else's phone. **Release** asks for a reason.

<Callout kind="note">
Despite its name this is not a log of sign-in attempts — MahekOne keeps no failed-attempt records or sign-in locations. What it shows is each device binding and the last time each handset spoke.
</Callout>

## Send a notification

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick who">
The whole field team, or named people from your team.
</Step>
<Step title="Write a title and the message">
The title is what shows first ("New price list from Monday"); the message says what you mean. Both are required.
</Step>
<Step title="Read it back, then send">
There is no undo. It lands in the app straight away and pushes to any handset with notifications turned on.
</Step>
</Steps>

The screen warns you when **push cannot reach anybody** (push switched off, no project id set, or no phone holding a push token), when **recent pushes did not arrive**, and when **some of your team have no handset registered for push** — they still see the message the next time they open the app, they just do not feel a buzz.

## Audit trail

Every decision made on the Sales Dashboard — approving or refusing a request, publishing or withdrawing a document, releasing a handset, sending a notification, publishing a route — with **when**, **who**, **what**, and the detail. It is the same audit log the rest of MahekOne writes, filtered to the field. The newest rows are shown, with a sentence saying how many there are in all.

## Common questions

<Faq q="I changed a setting and the salesman's phone still shows the old value.">
Settings reach a phone on its next sync. Ask him to open the app with a signal.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A salesman has a new phone and cannot sign in.">
His old phone is still bound. Release it on Login history (or on his row in Salesmen), with a reason.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I sent a notification and nobody felt it.">
Read the banners on the Send a notification screen — push may be switched off, or their phones may hold no push token yet. The message is still in their app.
</Faq>
