Two screens put material on your team's phones. **Documents** is the library a salesman can open in front of a customer — price lists, catalogues, policies, agreements, certificates. **Knowledge** is training: the courses a salesman is expected to do, and how many of them he has finished.

Whatever you publish reaches the handsets on their **next sync**, and whatever you withdraw leaves them the same way.

### Publishing

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the file first">
A document **must** have a file. A row the handset lists and cannot open is worse than no row, so publishing is refused without one.
</Step>
<Step title="Give it the title a salesman would look for">
The handset lists documents by title — "Dealer price list — October", not the file's name.
</Step>
<Step title="Decide who it is for">
Leave *Who sees it* empty for the whole field, or tag named salesmen. Tagged people are notified: "A document was shared with you".
</Step>
<Step title="Wait for the sync">
It reaches each phone on its next sync and downloads there.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Withdrawing

**Withdraw** takes a document off every phone on its next sync. It is not deleted — a policy a salesman quoted to a customer in March is a fact about March — and **Publish again** puts it back. Untagging somebody takes it off his phone the same way.

### What the salesman sees

On his phone the document opens **inside the app**: a PDF is drawn page by page with zoom buttons and double-tap, a picture as a picture, with no signal needed once it has downloaded. **Open in another app** is always there for sharing or printing.

A course has a title, a kind you type freely, a length, an optional file, an optional **pass mark**, and whether it is **compulsory**. A deadline can only be set on a compulsory course. Unlike a document, a course **does not need a file** — a briefing given in a meeting is still training worth recording and ticking off. Withdrawing a course takes it off the phones; anybody half way through is not made unfinished.

## Common questions

<Faq q="I published a document but a salesman cannot see it.">
His phone has not synced yet, or the document is tagged to other people. If he holds no Salesman App grant, he cannot be tagged at all.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I edit a published document?">
Yes — open it from the row menu to change its title, its kind, the file behind it, or who it is for. Phones pick up the change on their next sync.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can I not change who sees an HRMS document?">
HRMS decides its audience. Change it on HRMS → Documents.
</Faq>
