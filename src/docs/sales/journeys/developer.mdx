Journeys & visits is one route, `/sales/journeys`, with four tabs rendered server-side from `src/app/sales/journeys/page.tsx`. Plans live in `mbos_journey_plans` (one row per person per day, carrying `day_state`) and `mbos_journey_stops`. The office writes through `proposeJourneyDays`, `answerRefusal` and `saveJourneyPeriod`; the handset writes through `handlePlanDay` (agree, refuse, or start a day) and `handlePlanStops` (the whole shop list) in `src/lib/actions/mbos.ts`. Visit verdicts are `acceptVisit`, `decidePinCorrection` and `askAboutVisit`. `/sales/visits` survives only as a redirect to the Visit log tab.

## Access

- **Module:** <ModuleRef k="sales.journeys" /> on the page and on every office action, at manager level.
- **Scope:** `salesmanOutsideScope` on the salesman (or the plan's user) in every action; every read narrows through `managerScope`.
- **Self-dealing:** `acceptVisit` and `decidePinCorrection` refuse the salesman's own record.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Keep plan_stops whole-list">
Never change the handset contract to a delta. A merge makes unpicking impossible.
</Step>
<Step title="Never refuse a visit on the server">
Mark it unverified and say why. A refused visit loses the work attached to it.
</Step>
<Step title="Tombstone removed stops">
Anything that deletes a stop must write an `mbos_deletions` row, or the phone keeps it.
</Step>
</Steps>
