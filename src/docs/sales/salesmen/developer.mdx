The team is `users` joined to `app_access` where `app = 'field'`, narrowed by `managerScope`. Each row adds the active `mbos_devices` binding, the territories in `mbos_user_territories`, and the hometown in `expense_hometowns`. The record at `/sales/people/[id]` is `salesmanRecord` plus `performance` for this month and last, plus `dayEvidence` for the chosen day. Handset health is a pure function, `handsetNotes`, drawn on the Live map.

## Access

<ModuleRef k="sales.people" /> <ModuleRef k="sales.territory" />

`setSalesmanTerritories` and `setManagerTerritories` accept `sales.people` or `sales.territory` at a manager's level; `setSalesmanHometown` needs `sales.people`. A salesman outside `managerScope` is simply absent from `fieldTeam`, so the record 404s.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Read scope inside the service">
Never take a scope argument from a page — call `managerScope()` in the read.
</Step>
<Step title="Anything that removes rows from a phone tombstones them">
Diff over his own book, not over `customers`.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a handset fact as nullable and optional on the wire">
An APK cannot be recalled; omit rather than null so an old build never wipes a newer one's answer.
</Step>
<Step title="Put a new health sentence in handsetNotes">
Not in a screen. Give it a threshold in configuration if it decides what a screen asserts.
</Step>
</Steps>
