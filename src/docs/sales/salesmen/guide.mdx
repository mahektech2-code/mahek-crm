Open a person to see **everything MBOS has recorded for them** — visits, orders, money, attendance, leave, expenses, samples, leads and tasks — and to check any single day photograph by photograph.

## Where a salesman works

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Where they work on his row">
A tree of the places in your book — states, then cities, then beats — with a shop count on each, biggest first.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the narrowest place you mean">
Picking Maharashtra and then Pune inside it means **Pune**, not all of Maharashtra. The narrowest pick in a branch is the allocation; the ones above it are the path to it. Different branches add up: Pune and Nashik is both.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
His phone shows only his own shops inside those places from the next sync. Shops that leave his area are removed from his phone.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warn" title="No area means no shops">
A salesman with no area allocated sees **no customers** on his handset — his phone says "no area set for you yet". Managers and administrators are not affected. Allocating an area is what gives a new salesman his book.
</Callout>

An area **narrows** his book, it never widens it: he sees only shops that are already his, never another salesman's.

## Releasing a handset

Each account is bound to one phone. If a salesman changes phones, the new one cannot sign in until the old binding is released. **Release their handset** asks for a reason — whoever holds the old phone could otherwise bind it again. See [Login history](/docs/sales/administration) for every binding and release.

## A salesman's record

Open a name to see his record. The header shows his email, work number, how many shops are in his book, and when his handset last synced. Below are this month's figures — orders taken, order value, collected, visits, new shops, days worked — beside last month's, and ten tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Visits** | Every visit, with check-in and check-out times; a visit that never closed is marked. |
| **Orders** | Orders taken on his handset and their state. |
| **Money** | Collections he reported, cash held and deposited. |
| **Attendance** | His days: in, out, hours, off-site flags. |
| **Day check** | One day, photograph by photograph — check-in selfies and meter readings — to accept or decline. The tab counts what nobody has answered. |
| **Leave** | What he asked for and what was decided. |
| **Expenses** | His claims and their state. |
| **Samples** | Samples he asked for. |
| **Leads** | The leads in his name. |
| **Tasks** | What is open on him. |

**Export** downloads the month as a spreadsheet.

## Handset health

Why a salesman's phone is not reporting is explained on the [Live map](/docs/sales/live-map)'s team list, in plain words — not "no fix today", but which of these it is:

| You see | It means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Location switched off on the phone** | Location is off in the phone's own settings. | Ask him to switch it on. |
| **Location only while the app is open** | The trail will have gaps whenever the app is closed. | Set MahekOne's location permission to *Allow all the time*. |
| **Location refused for MahekOne** | Permission was refused outright. | A different conversation — he has to allow it. |
| **His phone stopped the tracker** | The phone's battery manager killed the recorder. | Allow MahekOne to autostart and set its battery use to unrestricted. |
| **No position all day — checked in … ago** | Checked in, and not one position since. | As above; then check out and back in. |
| **Battery 8% — read 12 min ago** | The battery at the phone's last contact. | There is no live battery reading — the age is part of the figure. |
| **Not heard from for 2 hours** | The phone has not synced. A phone with no signal cannot say so — silence is the only evidence. | Wait, or ring him. |
| **Running MBOS 1.4 — 1.5 is out** | An older app build than the one the office has published. | Ask him to update. |

A healthy phone says nothing at all.

## Common questions

<Faq q="A new salesman's phone shows no customers.">
Two possible reasons, both on this screen: he has no area allocated (<i>Works</i> is empty), or no customer names him as their salesperson (<i>Empty book</i>).
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can I not send a password reset?">
Handing out credentials is an account decision, kept for platform administrators. Releasing a handset is yours.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Somebody is missing from the team.">
The list is narrowed to your team, and to people who hold the Salesman App. Grant the app on the Access screen in the Admin Console.
</Faq>
