Expense money lives in `mbos_expenses`: a logged expense is a row the handset (or the office) wrote, an **allowance** is a row `refreshDayMoney` writes with `source_type` `expense_day` or `travel_leg`. Each logged expense has an `mbos_approvals` row of type `expense_claim`. The pure engine `engines/expense-policy.ts` prices a day; named policies in `expense_policy_sets` feed it. Flags are `mbos_expense_exceptions`.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="No number in code">
A rate, cap or time belongs in a policy rule. A new kind of rule is a new `POLICY_RULE_KINDS` entry with a form, a validation in `checkRules`, a branch in the engine and words in `policyInWords`.
</Step>
<Step title="Never refuse a record">
Raise an exception and let a person decide.
</Step>
<Step title="Re-price through refreshDayMoney">
It is the one writer of allowance rows and eligible figures; a second writer drifts.
</Step>
<Step title="Gate a new decision through decideApproval">
It holds the own-request, scope and reason rules.
</Step>
</Steps>
