## The four figures

| Figure | What it counts | What it does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Out now** (today) / **Punched in** (another day) | Salesmen who started their day, out of everyone with an active account. The bar is the share. | A salesman on leave still counts in the total. |
| **Visits** | Visits checked in on this day, and how many of the planned route stops were walked. | Unverified visits count — the row says how many. |
| **Orders** | Orders taken on the phone this day, with their value. | Not sales yet. Each sits at *pending approval* until accounts decide it. |
| **Collected** | Money salesmen reported collecting this day. | Not money in the bank. It counts only once accounts confirm it against the statement. Rejected payments are left out. |

## What is waiting on you

| Card | Opens | Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Orders over the credit limit** — *Nothing dispatches until you decide* | Orders | Field orders still waiting for a decision. |
| **Expense claims** — *Money the field is owed back* | Expenses | Claims waiting for an answer. |
| **Samples awaiting feedback** — *All past their follow-up date* | Samples | Samples whose follow-up date has passed with no verdict recorded. |
| **Leave requests** — *Somebody is waiting to book time off* | Leave | Leave waiting for a decision. Withdrawn requests are not counted. |
| **Days he will not walk** — *Refused with a reason, waiting on your answer* | Journeys & visits | Days a salesman refused, from today on. |
| **Salesmen with nothing tomorrow** — *They will decide for themselves where to go* | Journeys & visits | Active salesmen with no day at all for tomorrow. |

<Callout kind="note">
The **Review** button adds every card together and opens [Approvals](/docs/sales/approvals). Some of what it counts — refused days, empty tomorrows, overdue samples, orders — is decided on its own screen rather than on Approvals. Use the cards to go straight to the right place.
</Callout>

## Reading the team list

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| <Pill tone="success">Out since 09:12</Pill> | Checked in and not yet checked out. |
| <Pill>Finished 17:05</Pill> | Checked out. |
| <Pill tone="danger">Not started</Pill> | No check-in on this day. |
| <Pill>Account closed</Pill> | The salesman's account is disabled. Listed last. |

Under the status or the visits you may see:

- **Punched in off site** — he checked in outside the permitted distance from where he is expected to start. It is a flag, not a refusal.
- **N unverified** — visits saved without the checks passing. Open [Journeys & visits](/docs/sales/journeys) → *Visit log* to read why and accept them.

The **Planned route** bar shows stops walked out of stops planned. *No route* means no shops were picked for that day.

<Steps>
<Step title="Find who has not started">
Press **Not started**. Anyone here at mid-morning is worth a call — or check the [Live map](/docs/sales/live-map), which says whether the phone itself is reporting.
</Step>
<Step title="Open a salesman's day">
Click his row. His day opens in a new tab: the trail, each visit, orders and payments, so you can come back to the team list without losing your place.
</Step>
<Step title="Look at a past day">
Use ‹ to step back. The four figures and the team list are for that day. Of the waiting cards, refused days, tomorrow's empty days and overdue samples follow the day you picked; orders, leave and expenses always show what is waiting now.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Orders shows a value but Performance shows less revenue.">
Today counts orders as they were taken. Performance counts only what accounts approved. An order declined by accounts stays in Today's figure for that day.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Collected says one figure, accounts say another.">
Today shows what salesmen reported. Accounts confirm money against the bank, and only confirmed money moves outstanding.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A salesman is missing from the list.">
Only people holding the Salesman App, inside your patch, are listed. A salesman whose shops are all in another manager's regions is theirs.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why does Review say 9 when Approvals shows 4?">
Review adds every card, including refused days, tomorrow's unplanned salesmen, orders and overdue samples. Approvals lists only requests raised for a yes or no.
</Faq>
