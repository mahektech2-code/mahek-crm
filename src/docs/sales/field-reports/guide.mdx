Two screens hold what the field team has **reported** rather than sold. **Field reports** is what salesmen file from shops — complaints, notes on the competition, internal notes, tours and order changes. **Activity history** is every visit the field team has ever logged: MBOS visits, and before the switch-over date, the old field app's log (EMP 2.0) beside them — including the shop names that still need matching to a real account.

### What the match means

| Match | Means |
|---|---|
| <Pill tone="success">Matched</Pill> | Exactly one account carries that name, or a person linked it. The visit is on that customer's history. |
| <Pill tone="warn">Needs review</Pill> | Several accounts carry the name, or only close names exist. Nothing is linked until somebody decides. |
| <Pill>No match</Pill> | Nothing close on the book, or a person decided the shop is not on MahekOne. |

MBOS visits always carry their account, so the match filter only applies to the old app's rows.

<Steps>
<Step title="Find the names to review">
Pick **Old app** and **Needs review**.
</Step>
<Step title="Open a name and decide">
The panel lists accounts with exactly that name, then close names with their towns. Press **This is the shop** on the right one, or search the book. If it is not a MahekOne account at all, say so.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the customer's history">
A linked visit appears on that customer's timeline in the CRM and on the salesman's phone as history. A decision can be undone from the same panel.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="The old app's salesman has no MahekOne account.">
Most of them never will. Their rows show the name as typed and belong to nobody, so every manager can see them.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A visit from the old app is on the wrong customer's timeline.">
Open the row, decide the right shop. The old link is taken back and the right one written.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why is a complaint here and in the CRM?">
It is the same complaint. The CRM's complaint desk works it; here a manager sees which of their salesmen raised it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="An internal note shows no text.">
It is restricted to roles you do not hold. You can see that it exists and who wrote it.
</Faq>
