Attendance is one row per salesman per day in `mbos_attendance_days`, written by the handset through `handleAttendance`, closed and judged by the MBOS jobs, and read by `attendanceForDay` for the roll-call. Photographs are `attachments` rows with `parent_type = 'mbos_attendance'`, gated by `canReadAttendanceSelfie` and swept by `sweepAttendanceSelfies`.

## Access

<ModuleRef k="sales.attendance" />

- Roll-call: `onlyMine(managerScope(), "u.id")` over active holders of the `field` app.
- Photographs: `canOpenModule("sales.attendance")` **first**, then `scopeCovers(managerScope(), owner)`. `managerScope` alone is national for anybody without a region row — every plain salesman — so it cannot be the whole check.
- `reviewDayEvidence` refuses your own day and anybody outside `managerScope`.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Never null a selfie id">
The sweep removes the image only. "Taken and deleted" and "never taken" must stay distinguishable on the record a payslip is read against.
</Step>
<Step title="Never invent a closing time">
`markMissedCheckouts` closes at evidence or not at all. Do not add a fixed hour.
</Step>
<Step title="Gate any new photograph read on the module, then the scope">
Anything reachable from `/api/attachments` must ask `canOpenModule` before `managerScope`.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep the handset's selfie parameter non-nullable">
The type is the rule; a screen that forgets to ask cannot omit it.
</Step>
</Steps>
