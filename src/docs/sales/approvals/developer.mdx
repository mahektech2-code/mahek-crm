Approvals is one table, `mbos_approvals`, written by the handset when a salesman asks for something and decided from the Sales Dashboard by `decideApproval` in `src/lib/actions/sales.ts`. The queue is `pendingApprovals()` in `src/lib/services/sales-service.ts`, which reads a sentence for each row out of the subject table in SQL. Three kinds are deliberately refused by the generic action and decided on their own desks; orders are accounts'.

## The queue

`pendingApprovals` builds the *What* sentence per type with a `CASE` over correlated lookups into the subject table, and falls back to "… the office cannot find" when the subject is gone. Withdrawn leave is removed by `NOT_WITHDRAWN`. It is narrowed by `onlyMine(scope, "ap.requested_by_user_id")` and capped at 200, oldest first.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="A new kind needs five things">
An enum value (in its own migration — a value added to an enum may not be used in the same transaction), a `summary` arm in `pendingApprovals`, a label in `APPROVAL_LABEL`, an `owning` module in `decideApproval`, and an `approvalHref` for the handset.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep the state derived">
Never add a status column to the subject table. Read the approval.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep the pending guard in the WHERE">
Two managers on one queue is ordinary; the update must only land on a pending row.
</Step>
</Steps>
