Two screens answer "how is the month going" for the field team. **Performance** scores each person out of 100 against the target you published for them. **Cost & return** sets what the field brought in against what it cost to send them out. Both work a calendar month at a time.

The score itself — the six things measured, the weights, the mix bands and the forecast — is the same engine the CRM uses, explained on [Performance in the CRM](/docs/crm/performance). This page covers the two Sales Dashboard screens.

### Who is on the list

Everybody with a **published** target for the month, and everybody who **sold something** without one. A person with sales and no target shows their figures with *No target set* and no score.

### Reading a row

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| **Score** | Out of 100, against what was actually asked. With the rating word under it. |
| **Revenue** | Approved orders credited to the person, excluding GST, and how much of the target that is. |
| **Volume** | Litres, from each product's pack size. Money whose product name matched nothing counts as revenue but adds no litres. |
| **Mix** | How close the formulation mix is to the bands you set. |
| **New** | Customers whose first order landed this month. |
| **Collected** | Confirmed money received against what was **already overdue at the start of the month**. |
| **Activity** | Visits and calls against the activity target. |

Under the name, **what wants attention** — for example *behind pace*, *mix below minimum*, *no new customers* — so the reason is read with the person it is about.

<Callout kind="note">
A component nobody set a target for is **dropped**, and its weight is shared among the rest. It is never scored 0% or 100%. The detail panel names what was dropped.
</Callout>

## Common tasks

<Steps>
<Step title="Find who needs a conversation">
Open Performance. Read the banner first, then the lines under each name. Click the name for the breakdown.
</Step>
<Step title="Fix money that counts towards nobody">
The blue banner names how much and how many customers. Give those accounts a salesperson (accounts do this — it moves revenue), then the figure moves on the next rebuild.
</Step>
<Step title="Compare who travels well">
Open Cost & return and sort by *Sales per km* or *Revenue to spend*.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Somebody's revenue here is lower than their orders on Today.">
Today counts orders as they are taken. Performance counts only orders accounts approved.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why is a back-office person on the Performance list?">
A customer with no salesperson is credited to the back-office person who works it. If they sold something, they are listed.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Where is salary?">
Not on the Sales Dashboard. Pay is kept in HRMS; Cost & return counts approved expenses only.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Does a reported payment count towards collection?">
No. Only money accounts have confirmed against the bank counts.
</Faq>
