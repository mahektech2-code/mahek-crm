A leg is written by the handset through the MBOS sync dispatcher (`handleTravelLeg`), measured by `rescoreLeg`, priced by the pure expense-policy engine through `refreshDayMoney`, and read back by `travelLedger` for the screen. Nothing on `/sales/travel` writes; corrections happen on the day check (`reviewDayEvidence`).

Points worth knowing before changing it:

- **Absent is "unchanged", never "false".** `claimExcluded`, its reason and `manualReason` are read from the prior row when an update omits them. The handset opens a visit leg excluded and then sends arrival/abandon updates without those columns; upserting blindly re-claimed bus-day legs.
- **Unevidenced is an exclusion, not a rejection.** Only an unknown mode or an out-of-scope customer rejects.
- **`closedAtOwnReading`** (end equals start) needs no second photograph — it is how the handset closes an abandoned trip or an un-punched session.
- Media is bound after the upsert, because the camera may have closed before the leg existed.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Never reject for missing evidence">
A rejected leg blocks the visit that depends on it in the handset's outbox. Exclude it from the claim with a reason instead.
</Step>
<Step title="Treat an omitted field as unchanged">
Handsets in the field are several builds behind; read the prior row for anything an update may not carry.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep money in the policy">
No rate or cap belongs in this code. A new way of paying a mode is a new policy rule kind in `expense-policy.ts`, with a form and validation.
</Step>
<Step title="Re-price through refreshDayMoney">
Anything that changes a leg's evidence calls `repriceDay`; nothing writes `mbos_expenses` figures directly.
</Step>
<Step title="Bind new media parents in MBOS_PARENTS">
A photograph with no parent is swept by the nightly orphan job.
</Step>
</Steps>
