Tasks is **what your salesmen have been asked to do**, by you or by MahekOne itself, and whether it got done. You can give one salesman one job, or send the same job — with a short form to fill in — to many salesmen at many shops at once, and read the answers as they come back.

## Where tasks come from

| Raised by | When |
|---|---|
| **You**, or another manager | You assign it here. Even a single task is sent as an assignment of one. |
| **MahekOne — refused order** | Accounts refused an order the salesman took. He stood in the shop and said it was placed, so he is the one to go back. |
| **MahekOne — reorder due** | A customer is past their own measured reorder date. Raised once, until somebody deals with it. |
| **MahekOne — lead work** | Steps on the lead ladder: a validation call, a sample in transit, a sample review, a negotiation visit, nurture follow-ups. |
| **The salesman** | He can add a task for himself on his phone. |

*Raised by* shows a person's name for a task somebody assigned, and is blank for one MahekOne raised.

## Assigning a task

<Steps>
<Step title="Press Assign a task">
Give it a title, a due date and a priority. Instructions are optional.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who and where">
**No shop — just the salesmen**: pick the people. **Shops I pick**: search and tick shops. **Shops in an area / matching a filter**: pick places and kinds of account.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who does each shop">
*Carried by* — each shop goes to the salesman who carries it. Or pick salesmen yourself: *Each of them gets one task for every shop.*
</Step>
<Step title="See who this reaches, then assign">
The preview counts exactly how many tasks will be created. If the book changes before you press assign, you are asked to check again rather than sending a different number.
</Step>
</Steps>

He is told *"A task was assigned to you"* with the title and due date.

## Tasks with a form

Add questions to a task and each salesman fills them in at the shop. A task with no questions is *done with a note*.

| Question type | Asks for |
|---|---|
| Short text / Long text | A line, or a paragraph in his own words |
| Number | A count, litres, an amount — with a unit and range |
| Yes / No | One tap |
| Pick one / Pick several | From your list |
| Photos | From the camera or gallery |
| Date / Birthday | A day; a birthday is day and month, never a year |
| Phone number | A ten-digit mobile |
| Rating | One to five |
| Location | Where he is standing |
| Instruction | Text he reads; asks nothing |

A question can be shown **only if** an earlier answer was something. Up to 40 questions.

<Callout kind="note" title="Answers that belong on the customer record">
When a question looks like customer data — an owner's birthday, a phone number — the dialog offers to **link** it to the record. *Collect where missing* skips shops that already have it and completes itself if somebody fills it in elsewhere. *Check and correct* shows the phone what the record holds and writes back whatever he confirms or corrects.
</Callout>

If an AI provider is configured, you can describe what you need in plain words and the dialog drafts the form; you still review every question.

## Reading an assignment

Open an assignment from the **Assignments** list. It shows how far through it the field is, every answer by shop and salesman, totals for numbers and counts for choices, and photos. From there you can **remind** everybody still open, **export answers**, or **withdraw the open tasks** — finished ones are kept.

## Overdue and escalation

A task becomes **overdue** the day after its due date. Once <Setting k="mbos.tasks.escalationHours" /> hours have passed from the start of its due date and it is still open, the salesman is told *"… is past its date and your manager has been told."* It escalates once.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="A salesman marked a task done with no note.">
Whether a note is required to close a task is a setting (currently <Setting k="mbos.tasks.requireCompletionNote" />).
</Faq>
<Faq q="I assigned to a whole area and fewer tasks were created than shops.">
Shops nobody carries cannot go to "the shop's salesman", and shops whose record already holds every linked answer are skipped. The preview says how many of each.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I take back an assignment?">
Withdraw it from the assignment page. Open tasks are cancelled; done ones stay.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why is a task 'On nobody'?">
It is not against any shop — a job for the salesman himself, like a training to finish.
</Faq>
