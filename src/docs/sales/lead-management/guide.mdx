The **Lead Management** group in the Sales Dashboard is the same set of lead screens the CRM has — the same list, the same lead record, the same ladder and gates, the same buttons. There is one lead book, worked by the field team on foot and by the telecallers on the phone. What changes on the Sales Dashboard is **whose leads you see**: your team's, rather than your own.

Everything about how to use each screen is documented once, on the CRM pages linked below. This page tells you where to find each screen here, and what is different.

## What is different here

<Cards cols={2}>
<Card title="You see your team's leads" tone="brand">A manager with regions allocated sees the leads held by salesmen whose books lie in those regions. A manager with no regions sees everybody's. A lead **nobody owns** is visible to every manager, so it can be assigned.</Card>
<Card title="Telecaller-only screens are not here">The Telecaller calling desk and the Lost leads record live only in the CRM — callers work in the CRM.</Card>
<Card title="Every screen is its own permission">Each of the eleven entries above is ticked separately on the Access screen. Being given All Leads does not give you Samples, and the reverse.</Card>
<Card title="What you can do is the same">Moving a lead, logging a visit, requesting a sample — the same rules and the same gates apply wherever you press the button.</Card>
</Cards>

## The Sales Manager page

**Sales Manager** opens a full-width page of its own at `/sales-lead-pipeline`: a dashboard, the pipeline, the list of leads and the lead record, each linking to the next. It works the same lead book as All Leads, through the same team narrowing.

It is granted **one person at a time**. Holding the whole Sales Dashboard — even as an administrator — does not include it; somebody has to tick the *Sales Manager* box for you on the Access screen.

## Common questions

<Faq q="A lead my telecaller can see is missing for me.">
Your view is narrowed to your team's patch. If the lead is owned by somebody outside your regions, it is theirs to see, not yours.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why are some desks not in my sidebar?">
Seven of the lead screens left the main navigation. If you hold All Leads you reach them from its <i>Other desks</i> list; if you do not, they appear in the sidebar on their own.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I am an admin and Sales Manager is missing.">
It is the one Sales Dashboard screen that must be ticked by name. Ask for it on the Access screen.
</Faq>
