Three screens show the money side of your team's field work. **Orders** is every order a salesman took on his handset and what became of it. **Payments** is every collection he reported, and — most importantly — who is still holding company cash. **Invoices** is every bill raised against the accounts your team covers, and what is still owed on each.

You read these screens; you do not decide on them. Orders are approved by accounts, and money counts only once accounts find it in the bank. What these screens give you is the knowledge that one of your people is stuck, and why.

<Callout kind="live">
Limits and windows quoted here are the settings your deployment is using now.
</Callout>

### What an order's state means

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| <Pill tone="warn">Waiting</Pill> | Taken, waiting for accounts. |
| <Pill tone="danger">Above limit</Pill> | Waiting, and it would take the customer past their credit limit. These are the hard ones. |
| <Pill tone="danger">Credit blocked</Pill> | Waiting on a customer accounts have blocked. |
| <Pill tone="danger">declined</Pill> | Accounts turned it down. The reason is under the pill; the salesman has been told. |
| <Pill tone="success">approved / dispatched / in transit / delivered</Pill> | Counting as a sale. |
| **Shop agreed the order** | The shop confirmed what was written down. |
| **Shop confirmed delivery** | The shop said the goods arrived. |
| **Shop reports: …** (red) | The shop said what arrived did not match. Worth a call. |

<Callout kind="note">
Most orders over a limit never reach this screen. When <Setting k="mbos.credit.blockOnLimitExceeded" /> is on, an order that would take a customer past their limit is turned away when it syncs, and the salesman reads why on his handset's *Not accepted* list.
</Callout>

The deposit window is <Setting k="mbos.payments.cashDepositSlaHours" /> hours, shown on the screen in days. A salesman marks cash as deposited on his handset, with a photograph of the slip; that is what clears it from **Cash in hand**.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Reported is not received">
A receipt is what the salesman says he collected. It counts against a bill only when accounts confirm it against the bank. Until then the bill still stands at its full amount on Invoices.
</Callout>

## Invoices

Every bill raised against accounts your team looks after, with what is left on each **after confirmed money only**. It opens on **Open** bills, biggest balance first, with chips for **Overdue**, **Unspoken for** and **Everything**, a search box for a bill number, customer or salesman, and an ageing strip (*Not due, 1–30 days, 31–60, 61–90, Over 90*). Click a bill to see what was ordered on it.

**Unspoken for** bills are bills nobody has said were paid or unpaid — usually imported from the order sheet. They count as neither paid nor owed and are kept out of the ageing strip. See [Bills](/docs/crm/bills/how-it-works) for the whole rule.

## Common questions

<Faq q="Can I approve an order here?">
No. Approving is the accounts desk's job, in Accounts → Order approvals. The person chasing a target must not sign off the orders that hit it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="An order taken this morning is not here.">
An order reaches the office on the salesman's next sync. One taken with no signal appears later, not never. If it was turned away — credit limit, blocked customer, a price that has changed — it is on his handset's <i>Not accepted</i> list with the reason, and not here.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A cheque bounced. What happens?">
The salesman reports it on his handset. His manager is notified (or every manager, if nobody is recorded as his manager), and a note is added to the receipt. If the receipt was already confirmed, accounts have to reverse it — the notification says so.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why does a bill still show its full amount after he collected the money?">
Because the money is only <i>reported</i>. It is subtracted once accounts confirm it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I was notified about a collection.">
A collection of <Setting k="mbos.payments.managerNotifyThresholdPaise" /> paise or more notifies the salesman's manager when it syncs. It is still reported, not confirmed.
</Faq>
