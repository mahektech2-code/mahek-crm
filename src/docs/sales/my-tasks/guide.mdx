**My tasks** is everything you have to do in the Sales Dashboard: tasks your manager gave you, tasks the app raised for you, and tasks you added yourself. The same tasks are in the **Tasks** app, beside the ones you have in other apps. It is in the sidebar under the Field work group.

## The screen at a glance

- **The counts** across the top — Due today, Late, Upcoming, From your manager and Done today. Click one to open its tab.
- **The tabs** — Needs action, Late, Upcoming, Done and All.
- **New task** adds one. It starts on the Sales Dashboard; you can pick another app, or none.
- **Tasks in other apps** are counted under the figures, so nothing waits out of sight. Open them in the **Tasks** app, or in the other app's own My tasks.

## Closing a task

**Mark done** asks you to confirm it is done, then for the remark and the screenshot — each required or optional, as whoever gave you the task chose.

## Handing a task to somebody else

Open the task and press **Transfer**. A colleague accepts or rejects it; your manager, or anybody above you, can move it straight away.

## Questions

**Can somebody take My tasks away from me?** No. It is always open to anybody who holds the Sales Dashboard, whatever screens they were given, because it only ever shows your own work.

**Why is a task here and not in the Tasks app?** It is in both. This screen shows only the tasks that belong to the Sales Dashboard and your personal ones.
