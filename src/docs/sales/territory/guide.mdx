Territory answers **who covers which part of the book**. Pick a role — field sales, sales account manager, back-office account manager or sales manager — and the screen shows everybody holding that role, the cities their shops are in, and a map of every shop. It is also where the gaps show: shops with nobody in a role, and shops with no pin on the map.

Deciding **where a salesman works** — which states, cities or beats reach his phone — is done from the [Salesmen](/docs/sales/salesmen) screen, with **Where they work**. This page explains both, because the two are read together.

## The four roles

| Role | Who it is on an account |
|---|---|
| **Field sales** | The salesman who visits the shop, on MBOS. Lists every active salesman, including those with no shops. |
| **Sales account manager** | Whose book the account is in for revenue and targets. |
| **Back-office account manager** | Who raises its paperwork, dispatch and billing. |
| **Sales manager** | Who the salesperson answers to. Moves no revenue. |

- **Hollow pins are prospects** — shops a salesman pinned in the field that do not match any account yet. They show a plain label, because there is no record behind them.

## Setting where a salesman works

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Salesmen and find him">
Go to [Salesmen](/docs/sales/salesmen) and press **Where they work** on his row.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the states first">
*Pick the states they cover.* Each state shows how many shops it holds, biggest first.
</Step>
<Step title="Narrow to cities, and beats, if you want to">
*Narrow to cities, if you want to.* Picking Pune inside Maharashtra means **Pune only** — the narrowest pick in a branch is what he works.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
His phone drops the shops outside the new area on its next sync and he is told *"Where you work has changed"*. Nothing is taken out of his book — only off his phone.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warn" title="No area means an empty phone">
A salesman with no area set sees **no customers at all** on his phone, and the row says *No area · empty handset*. This does not apply to managers or admins carrying a phone — their phone shows their whole scope.
</Callout>

The row also says *Not yet accepted on the handset* until his phone has picked up the change, and *The areas have changed since he accepted them* if you change them again before it does.

### Setting a manager's regions

On the same screen, a **national** manager sets each regional manager's patch with **Set the patch**. *Give them everything* clears it, which means the whole country. A regional manager cannot change regions, and nobody can set their own.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Why are there so many shops under 'Nobody'?">
Most of an imported book has never been allocated to anybody. The Territory screen shows them on purpose — they are the shops a coverage map most needs to show.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I allocated a salesman a city and he still sees a shop in another city.">
A shop he raised himself as a lead on his phone stays on his phone wherever it is. Everything else follows the area.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Does giving a salesman a city let him see another salesman's shops there?">
No. An area only narrows his own book. Whose shops are whose is decided separately.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A city appears as a whole postal address.">
Cities come from what was typed into the customer records, and some of them are addresses. The tree puts the real cities with the most shops at the top.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A salesman asked for different cities on his phone.">
His request lands on [Approvals](/docs/sales/approvals). Approving it says yes; you still change his area here on Salesmen.
</Faq>
