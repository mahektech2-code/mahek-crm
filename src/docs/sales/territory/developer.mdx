Territory is two features that share one table. The **Territory screen** (`/sales/territory`) is a read-only coverage view over the four account seats, narrowed geographically by `placeScope`. **Allocation** is written from the Salesmen screen by `setSalesmanTerritories` (working areas) and `setManagerTerritories` (regions), both in `src/lib/actions/sales.ts`, into `mbos_user_territories`. The handset's book is then `customerIdsInScope` in `src/lib/services/mbos-service.ts`, which ANDs `territoryClause` onto the seat-based visibility clause.

<Callout kind="rule" title="Diff over HIS book, and an empty before is everything">
`bookIdsIgnoringTerritory` is his seats-only book. Diffing over `customers` would tombstone thousands of shops that were never on the phone. A salesman with no previous working area is read as having had his whole book — which is the deploy case, where a phone synced under the old rule still holds shops `territoryClause([])` says it never had.
</Callout>

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Never make territory a permission">
New visibility rules belong in the seat clause (`principalBookClause`). The territory clause is ANDed on and may only remove rows.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep the parent">
Any new kind needs an entry in `PARENT_KIND` and a matching branch in `territoryClause`; the dialog, the action and the clause all read that map.
</Step>
<Step title="Tombstone what leaves">
Any new path that changes a salesman's area must diff his own book and write `mbos_deletions` rows, or shops stay on phones for ever.
</Step>
</Steps>
