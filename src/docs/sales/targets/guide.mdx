Sales Targets is where you decide **what each person is asked for in a month** and show it to them. A target is five figures — revenue, volume, new customers, collection and activity — plus a product mix by formulation. It stays a **draft** while you work it out and reaches the person only when you **publish** it. Once published, changing it needs a reason.

How the target is turned into a score is on [Performance](/docs/sales/performance) and, in full, on [Performance in the CRM](/docs/crm/performance).

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick the month and the person">
Use ‹ › for the month. Press **Set target** on a row, or **Add someone** for somebody not listed.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in what you are asking for">
Only fill the figures you want to measure. An empty figure is not asked; a zero is a target nobody can fail.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the mix">
Press **Add formulation** for each liquid you want a share of. Each needs minimum ≤ target ≤ stretch.
</Step>
<Step title="Save as draft, or save and publish">
Publishing needs at least one of the five figures. They are told *"Your target for … is set"*.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Changing a published target

Once a target is published, the drawer's button reads **Save the change** and asks for a reason.

<Steps>
<Step title="Change the figures">
Edit what needs to move.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a reason">
Choose from the list (*Pick a reason…*) and add a note if it helps.
</Step>
<Step title="Save the change">
Each figure that moved is recorded with its old value, new value and the reason, and the person is told *"Your target for this month has changed"*.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note">
A draft is simply edited — nothing has been promised, so nothing is logged figure by figure.
</Callout>

## What each status means

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| <Pill tone="warn">Draft</Pill> | Your working-out. Not on their phone, not scored. |
| <Pill tone="success">Published</Pill> | They can see it; Performance scores against it. |
| <Pill tone="brand">Carried forward</Pill> | Copied automatically from last month's published target on the 1st. Saving any change clears it. |
| No target | Not scored. |

## The same screen in Accounts

The accounts desk sets targets too. **Accounts → Sales targets** is the same feature — the same people, figures and drafts — with one addition: a **Revision history** drawer listing every published change, its reason and who made it. Per-customer monthly targets are a different screen: [Monthly targets in the CRM](/docs/crm/targets).

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I cannot open Sales Targets.">
You need permission to set targets as well as the screen. Managers and accounts hold it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A salesman says his target changed and nobody told him.">
Every change to a published target notifies him and is recorded with a reason. Check Accounts → Sales targets → Revision history.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why did last month's target appear on its own?">
On the first of the month, anybody with no target yet gets last month's published one, marked *Carried forward*. Drafts are never carried.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why is 'Other' always in the mix?">
It catches every formulation nobody classified and every order line whose product name did not match the catalogue, so the shares always add up to 100%.
</Faq>
