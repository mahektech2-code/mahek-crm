Two screens decide when a salesman is not expected to work. **Leave** is where you answer the leave your team asks for on their handsets. **Holidays** is the calendar of days off — company-wide, for a state or a city, or for named people — and it decides whether a day reads as absent, what leave costs, and what each salesman's phone shows.

### Answering a request

<Steps>
<Step title="Check the overlap">
If *Also off* names somebody, decide whether the patch can be covered before approving.
</Step>
<Step title="Approve or refuse">
Approving is one press. Refusing asks you to **say why** — he has to be able to do something differently, and cannot work it out from the word alone.
</Step>
<Step title="He sees it on his phone">
The decision reaches the handset on its next sync, with your note.
</Step>
</Steps>

| State | It means |
|---|---|
| <Pill tone="warn">Waiting</Pill> | Nobody has answered yet. |
| <Pill tone="success">Approved</Pill> | Approved, with who decided and when. |
| <Pill tone="danger">Refused</Pill> | Refused, with the reason. |
| <Pill>Withdrawn</Pill> | He took it back. There is nothing to decide. |

**Days** counts only his working days: a Friday-to-Monday request across a Sunday off is three days, not four, and a holiday that applies to him is not counted. A half day applies only to a single-day request.

### Who gets a holiday

| Level | Reaches |
|---|---|
| **Company-wide** | Everybody on the field team, except anybody it was taken from. |
| **State, district, city, area** | The salesmen who work in the places you pick. |
| **Named people** | Exactly the people you name — a community's festival, a family occasion. |

On any holiday you can **give** it to somebody it would not otherwise reach, or **take** it from somebody it would — say why; he will ask. The *By employee* view shows each salesman's holidays for the year and is where this is done.

A change reaches the handsets on their next sync. Holidays are also copied to HRMS for the same people.

## Common questions

<Faq q="Can I change how many days of leave somebody is allowed?">
No. Allowances are kept in HRMS. This screen shows days taken, never days left.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A request I approved has gone from Waiting but he says he never asked.">
If he withdrew it, it shows <i>Withdrawn</i>. Approving a withdrawn request is refused.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I answer my own leave?">
No. Somebody else has to — that is what makes it an approval.
</Faq>
<Faq q="It says the holiday is already on the calendar.">
A holiday with the same name on the same date already exists. Edit that one instead — two entries would be two answers to who is off.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A state holiday does not show for one of my salesmen.">
He is not allocated a place in that state. Allocate his territory on <a href="/docs/sales/salesmen">Salesmen</a>, or give him the day by name.
</Faq>
