Leave is `mbos_leave_requests` (what was asked) plus an `mbos_approvals` row of type `leave` (whether it was granted), written by the handset and decided through `decideApproval`. Holidays are `mbos_holidays` with a level, per-person allocations in `mbos_holiday_assignments`, and a resolved audience in `mbos_holiday_members`. Every per-person reader asks one SQL predicate, `holidayAppliesSql`.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Ask holidayAppliesSql">
Any new per-person reader of holidays goes through it. A bare `from mbos_holidays` gives everybody every state's holidays.
</Step>
<Step title="Rebuild after anything that changes an audience">
A new territory, place or allocation path must call `rebuildHolidayMembers`.
</Step>
<Step title="Never store a used-leave counter">
Derive it from approved requests in `leaveBalances`.
</Step>
</Steps>
