The ERP is where the factory and the back office keep their work: what is bought, what is tested, what is mixed, filled and packed, where every lot and box is sitting, which orders are being made ready, what has left on a lorry, and the petty cash that keeps production moving. Every screen reads from one database, so a lot received at the gate this morning is the same lot a batch consumes this afternoon and the same lot a box can be traced back to next month.

<Callout kind="note">
You only see the parts of the ERP your job needs. If a screen mentioned on this page is not in your sidebar, it has not been given to you — ask whoever manages access. Nothing is hidden by mistake.
</Callout>

## The journey of the material

Everything in the ERP follows the paint from the supplier to the customer. Each step happens on its own screen, and each step can only use what the step before it produced.

<Flow caption="From a department's need to money in the bank" chart={`
flowchart TD
  A["A department needs something<br/>Purchase requirements"] --> B["How is it bought?<br/>direct · quotation · buyer decides"]
  B --> C["Purchase order<br/>raised, approved, sent to the vendor"]
  C --> D["Goods arrive at the gate<br/>Goods receipt (GRN) against the PO"]
  D --> E{"Does the item<br/>need testing?"}
  E -- "Yes, a chemical" --> F["Purchase testing<br/>evidence, then the verifier decides"]
  E -- "No" --> G["Purchase register<br/>a lot with a rate"]
  F --> G
  G --> H["Raw-material stock<br/>lot by lot, godown by godown"]
  H --> I["SFG batch<br/>raw-material lots mixed into liquid"]
  I --> J{"SFG QC"}
  J -- "Approved" --> K["FG filling<br/>liquid into cans or drums"]
  K --> L["Packing batch<br/>loose cans into boxes, each box gets an id"]
  K --> M["Loose cans sold as cans"]
  L --> N["Orders<br/>lots allocated, billed"]
  M --> N
  N --> O["Dispatch desk<br/>every box scanned against its order"]
  O --> P["Transport<br/>LR, tracking, delivered"]
  P --> Q["The bill in MahekOne's ledger<br/>outstanding, collections"]
`} />

Read it top to bottom and you have the whole ERP:

1. **Buy.** A department raises a [purchase requirement](/docs/erp/purchase-requirements). The item's purchase rule decides whether the store buys it directly, collects quotations first, or lets the buyer decide.
2. **Order.** Nothing is bought without a [purchase order](/docs/erp/purchase-orders). Somebody other than the person who raised it approves it, and then it is sent to the vendor.
3. **Receive and test.** Goods are received at the gate [against the PO](/docs/erp/goods-receipt-and-testing). A chemical goes to the tester; everything else goes straight into the register and into stock.
4. **Make.** [Production](/docs/erp/production) mixes raw-material lots into semi-finished liquid (SFG), fills approved SFG into cans or drums (FG), and packs loose cans into boxes.
5. **Stock.** [Stock](/docs/erp/stock) shows what is in every godown at every stage, every box by its own id, and every movement between godowns — including write-offs.
6. **Sell and ship.** [Orders](/docs/erp/orders) are made ready and billed, the [dispatch desk](/docs/erp/dispatch) scans every box onto its order, and [transport](/docs/erp/transport) follows the consignment until the customer has it.
7. **Money.** Once the ERP takes the orders, every bill lands in MahekOne's own ledger — see [the order book](/docs/erp/order-book). [Petty cash](/docs/erp/petty-cash) keeps production's day-to-day spending.

## The screen layout

Every ERP screen has the same parts, so once you know one you know them all.

- **The header** — the search box ("Search customers, orders, lots, PRs, bills, LRs…"), the **Working at** godown chip with a button that finds your godown from where you are standing, the notification bell, and the app switcher.
- **The sidebar** — the screens you hold, grouped by area. A number beside a screen is work waiting there: tests the verifier has not decided, pending LRs, boxes with no label yet.
- **The list** — search, a godown filter, grouping with totals, chips for the commonest filter, and a download button on most lists.
- **The record drawer** — click a row to open it. It shows every field, the record's history line ("Created by … on …") and the actions you may take. An action you may not take is drawn greyed out with the reason beside it.
- **The form drawer** — new records and edits open in a drawer from the side. Fields marked as derived are calculated as you type, exactly as the server will calculate them when you save.

## Common questions

<Faq q="Why does a number on the dashboard not match the list I open from it?">
It always matches the rows it opens on — the tile and the list are the same function. If you then clear the filter pill, or your godown filter is set to a different godown, the list shows more than the tile counted.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The dashboard says 'Nothing on your account has figures to show yet'.">
None of the screens you hold has recorded any work yet, or you hold only screens without dashboard figures. Sections appear by themselves as soon as there is something to count.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why can I see a quantity but not its rate or value?">
Money columns are given separately from screens. Purchase money, cost and margin, the sales rate and sales amounts are each a power somebody is given on purpose. Without it the column is not sent to your screen at all, and the list says which columns are withheld.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why is the summary at the top of the dashboard missing?">
It is written by the ERP's nightly pass, for the day before. Until the first one has been written, or if the job did not run, there is nothing to show and the box is simply not drawn.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Where do I set my godown?">
In the header's **Working at** chip, or on [Settings](/docs/erp/access-and-designations). You can only pick godowns you are assigned to.
</Faq>
