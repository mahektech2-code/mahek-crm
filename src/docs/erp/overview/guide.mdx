The ERP is where the factory and the back office keep their work: what is bought, what is tested, what is mixed, filled and packed, where every lot and box is sitting, which orders are being made ready, what has left on a lorry, and the petty cash that keeps production moving. Every screen reads from one database, so a lot received at the gate this morning is the same lot a batch consumes this afternoon and the same lot a box can be traced back to next month.

<Callout kind="note">
You only see the parts of the ERP your job needs. If a screen mentioned on this page is not in your sidebar, it has not been given to you — ask whoever manages access. Nothing is hidden by mistake.
</Callout>

## The journey of the material

Everything in the ERP follows the paint from the supplier to the customer. Each step happens on its own screen, and each step can only use what the step before it produced.

<Flow caption="From a department's need to money in the bank" chart={`
flowchart TD
  A["A department needs something<br/>Purchase requirements"] --> A2["Blind count of the shelf<br/>Stock accuracy"]
  A2 --> B["How is it bought?<br/>direct · quotation · buyer decides"]
  B --> C["Purchase order<br/>linked from the Quotation manager, or created"]
  C --> D["Goods arrive at the gate<br/>Goods receipt (GRN) against the PO"]
  D --> E{"Does the item<br/>need testing?"}
  E -- "Yes, a chemical" --> F["Lot testing<br/>evidence, then the verifier decides"]
  E -- "No" --> G["Purchases register<br/>a lot with a rate"]
  F --> G
  G --> H["Raw-material stock<br/>lot by lot, godown by godown"]
  H --> I["SFG batch<br/>raw-material lots mixed into liquid"]
  I --> J{"SFG QC"}
  J -- "Approved" --> K["FG filling<br/>liquid into cans or drums"]
  K --> L["Packing batch<br/>loose cans into boxes, each box gets an id"]
  K --> M["Loose cans sold as cans"]
  L --> N["Orders<br/>lots allocated, billed"]
  M --> N
  N --> O["Dispatch desk<br/>every box scanned against its order"]
  O --> P["Transport<br/>LR, tracking, delivered"]
  P --> Q["The bill in MahekOne's ledger<br/>outstanding, collections"]
`} />

Read it top to bottom and you have the whole ERP:

1. **Buy.** A department raises a [purchase requirement](/docs/erp/purchase-requirements). What is on the shelf is [counted blind](/docs/erp/stock-accuracy) first, and the item's purchase rule then decides whether the store buys it directly, buys it at a rate from the [Quotation manager](/docs/erp/quotation-manager), or lets the buyer decide.
2. **Order.** Nothing is bought without a [purchase order](/docs/erp/purchase-orders). A quotation item links the PO the Quotation manager made; a direct-purchase item has one created on the requirement. No PO waits for an approval on either route.
3. **Receive and test.** Goods are received at the gate [against the PO](/docs/erp/goods-receipt-and-testing). A chemical goes to the tester; everything else goes straight into the register and into stock.
4. **Make.** [Production](/docs/erp/production) mixes raw-material lots into semi-finished liquid (SFG), fills approved SFG into cans or drums (FG), and packs loose cans into boxes.
5. **Stock.** [Stock](/docs/erp/stock) shows what is in every godown at every stage, every box by its own id, and every movement between godowns — including write-offs. [Stock accuracy](/docs/erp/stock-accuracy) counts it against the book.
6. **Sell and ship.** [Orders](/docs/erp/orders) are made ready and billed, the [dispatch desk](/docs/erp/dispatch) scans every box onto its order, and [transport](/docs/erp/transport) follows the consignment until the customer has it.
7. **Money.** Once the ERP takes the orders, every bill lands in MahekOne's own ledger — see [the order book](/docs/erp/order-book). [Petty Cash](/docs/erp/petty-cash) records what was paid and received, in cash and through the bank, each transaction once.

Beside the main line sit the returns: material going back to a supplier or coming back from a customer is on [Material returns](/docs/erp/material-returns), and every emptied drum and what is sold as scrap is on [Empty drums and scrap](/docs/erp/empty-drums-scrap).

| **Purchase** | Requirements, Purchase orders, Purchases, Testing | Requirements waiting for a blind count, a PO number or a PO to be created; urgent and late requirements; POs to send, awaiting delivery or overdue; goods awaiting routing; tests waiting for the verifier; purchases with no rate or no bill |
| **Stock** | Any stock tab | Lots and quantity at each stage; stock value only with the cost power |
| **Production and re-order** | Production screens, Transfers, Levels | Incomplete packing batches, production today and this month, transfers today, write-offs this month, items below their minimum |

## The screen layout

Every ERP screen has the same parts, so once you know one you know them all.

- **The header** — the app switcher, one box to search or ask ("Search customers, lots, PRs, POs, boxes, orders…"; when Ask is switched on, a question typed into it goes to the ERP's answer drawer), the **Working at** godown chip with a button that finds your godown from where you are standing, Feedback, the <Kbd>?</Kbd> shortcuts button, the notification bell and your account.
- **The sidebar** — the screens you hold, grouped by area. A number beside a screen is work waiting there: tests the verifier has not decided, pending LRs, boxes with no label yet.
- **The list** — search, a godown filter, grouping with totals, chips for the commonest filter, and an **Export** button that downloads the rows as filtered, as CSV.
- **The record dialog** — click a row to open it. It shows every field, the record's history line ("Created by … on …") and the actions you may take. An action you may not take is drawn greyed out with the reason beside it.
- **The form dialog** — new records and edits open in a dialog in the middle of the screen. A long form is split into pages — the steps across the top — and ends on a Review page that says back what will be saved; a document's lines are a page of their own. Next checks the page you are on, and Save goes back to any page that still needs fixing. Fields marked as derived are calculated as you type, exactly as the server will calculate them when you save.

## Common questions

<Faq q="Why does a number on the dashboard not match the list I open from it?">
It always matches the rows it opens on — the tile and the list are the same function. If you then clear the filter pill, or your godown filter is set to a different godown, the list shows more than the tile counted.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The dashboard says 'Nothing on your account has figures yet'.">
None of the screens you hold has recorded any work yet, or you hold only screens without dashboard figures. Sections appear by themselves as soon as there is something to count.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why can I see a quantity but not its rate or value?">
Money columns are given separately from screens. Purchase money, cost and margin, the sales rate and sales amounts are each a power somebody is given on purpose. Without it the column is not sent to your screen at all, and the list says which columns are withheld.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why is the summary at the top of the dashboard missing?">
It is written by the ERP's nightly pass, for the day before. Until the first one has been written there is nothing to show and the box is simply not drawn. If a night's job did not run, the box still shows the newest paragraph there is — read its date.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Where do I set my godown?">
In the header's **Working at** chip, or on [Settings](/docs/erp/access-and-designations). You can only pick godowns you are assigned to.
</Faq>
