One pure engine, one service that both the ERP screens and the factory phone call, one screen module with fourteen lists, and a `scrap` record panel. Stock and receivables are counts and sums over records — there is no balance column to keep right.

## Registering a drum

<Code file="src/lib/erp/drums.ts" symbol="recordEmptyDrum" collapsed />

The lot is locked with an advisory lock while its next drum number is read, and a partial unique index on `(purchase_id, seq)` holds it at the database. The phone sends a `DRM-…` key minted when the drum is scanned; a resend is answered from `client_key`.
