The **Alerts** screen lists the unusual things the ERP noticed — a purchase rate that jumped, an LR that never arrived, a credit note accepted and never issued, stock below its level — each with a sentence saying what is unusual, in figures you can check, and a button that opens the records behind it. The checks run every hour; an alert closes itself once whatever it is about is put right.

Around the ERP there are also a handful of **AI helpers** that read things for you — a supplier's bill, a customer's WhatsApp message, a lorry receipt, a test photograph, a complaint — and **never save anything until you have checked it**. This page shows where each one is.

### What you see

You see an alert only if you can open the screen its records are on — and, for an alert that shows money, cost or write-offs, only if your account can see those too. A storekeeper never sees a purchase-rate alert; somebody without the Transport screen never sees an LR alert.

### Working an alert

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the records">
**Open the records** takes you to the list the alert is about, filtered to exactly the rows it counted.
</Step>
<Step title="Acknowledge it">
Say what is being done about it — the note is required. The alert stays on the list as **Acknowledged** so others know somebody has it.
</Step>
<Step title="It resolves itself">
When the condition clears — the rate is entered, the LR recorded, the credit note issued — the next hourly run marks the alert **Resolved** with *Condition cleared*. You can also **Resolve** it by hand with a reason.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warn" title="Resolving by hand does not make the problem go away">
If you resolve an alert whose condition is still true, the next hourly run raises it again as a new alert. Resolve by hand only when the thing is genuinely dealt with.
</Callout>

### The kinds of alert

| Alert | Raised when | Screen it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Purchase rate jump** | A purchase's litre rate is far from the median of the same supplier's recent rates for that item | Purchases register |
| **Rate missing too long** | A lot has had no rate for days, so it is not in stock | Purchases register |
| **High SFG loss** | An SFG batch line lost more than the allowed share of what it used | SFG batches |
| **High filling loss** | A filling lost more than the allowed share of its cans | FG filling |
| **Repeated write-offs** | Several write-offs to Item Lost Record by one person, or from one godown, in a short time | Item transfers |
| **Packing batch incomplete** | A packing batch has been short of (or over) its cans for days | Packing batches |
| **Test unverified** | A purchase test has waited too long for the verifier | Purchase testing |
| **Negative or thin margin** | A dispatched order line lost money, or made less than the minimum margin | Orders → Billing & dispatch |
| **Slow fulfilment** | An order line took more than the allowed days from order to dispatch | Orders → Billing & dispatch |
| **Ready but not billed** | A line has been Ready and fully allocated for hours with no Tally bill number | Orders |
| **LR missing** | A bill left days ago with no LR number | Transport → Pending LR |
| **Credit note not issued** | A credit-note request was accepted days ago and not issued | Complaints & credit notes |
| **Duplicate-looking expense** | One person recorded the same amount for the same particular twice within a few days | Petty cash → Expenses |
| **Petty cash exception** | Anything on the petty-cash exception queue (waiting too long, cash not closed, a fund below zero…) | The petty-cash tab concerned |
| **Stock below re-order level** | A followed level is short | Raw-material or Finished-goods levels |

The thresholds behind each are on the [How it works](/docs/erp/alerts-and-ai/how-it-works) tab, read live.

## The morning summary

The ERP **dashboard** opens with a short paragraph about yesterday: how many orders were taken, lines dispatched, inward lines received, SFG batches made, and which alerts are open. It carries **counts only** — never a rate, amount, cost or margin — so everybody who can open the dashboard may read it. It is written overnight; where an AI writer is available it puts the figures into sentences (the line says *summarised by AI from the figures below*), and otherwise a plain sentence built from the same figures stands in.

## The AI helpers, and where they are

Every helper follows the same rules: it **reads**, you **check**, and nothing is saved until you press the save button on the review. Each says plainly when it is unavailable — switched off, no provider key, or this month's allowance used — instead of failing.

| Helper | Where | What it reads | What you check | More |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Read a supplier bill** | Purchases → Register (for people who can see purchase money) | A photo or PDF of the supplier's bill | Supplier and GSTIN, which register row each line is, quantities and rates, a bill number already used | [Goods receipt, testing and the register](/docs/erp/goods-receipt-and-testing) |
| **Draft an order from a message** | Orders → WhatsApp inbox | A pasted or spoken message, or WhatsApp replies | The customer, each SKU and its quantity in cans | [Orders](/docs/erp/orders) |
| **Read LR photo** | Transport | A lorry receipt | The LR number; a transporter mismatch or a repeated LR is flagged | [Transport](/docs/erp/transport) |
| **Read test photos** | Purchase testing | The density meter, pH and test photos | Density and pH; a density far from the master is flagged. It never sets the verification. | [Goods receipt, testing and the register](/docs/erp/goods-receipt-and-testing) |
| **Suggest type and summary** | Complaints & credit notes | The complaint's description | The type (only from the existing list) and a one-line summary | [Complaints and credit notes](/docs/erp/complaints-and-credit-notes) |
| **Suggested levels** | Raw-material and Finished-goods levels | — (arithmetic on recent use, no AI model) | The suggested minimum and maximum | [Minimum levels and reordering](/docs/erp/stock-levels) |
| **Ask the ERP** | The search box at the top of every ERP screen | Your question | The answer and the links under it | Below |
| **Dictation** | The microphone on note and remark boxes | What you say | The text before it goes into the box | — |

### Ask the ERP

Type a question in the ERP's search box **ending with a question mark** and press Enter — *how many lines are Ready at Godown A?* or *which bills have no LR?* The answer comes back in one to three sentences, in the language you asked in, with the figure first, and with links to the lists it read.

It only ever answers from **the screens you can open**, and only with **the columns you can see**: if you cannot see sales amounts, it cannot tell you them. If the question needs a screen you do not have, it says so. It never changes anything.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I fixed the problem but the alert is still Open.">
It resolves at the next hourly run. A manager can press **Run checks now** to run them at once.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can't I see an alert my colleague can see?">
You don't hold the screen it is about, or it shows money or cost your account cannot see.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A helper says 'This month's … readings are used'.">
Each helper has a monthly allowance. It comes back on the 1st, or when the allowance is raised in settings.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Did the AI change my record?">
No. Every helper saves only when you press save on its review, and the review shows exactly what was read and how sure it is (marked *check* where it is not).
</Faq>
<Faq q="I got a notification 'Below re-order level'.">
You are assigned to that godown and can raise purchase requirements, and an item there just fell short. Open it and raise a requisition from the levels list.
</Faq>
