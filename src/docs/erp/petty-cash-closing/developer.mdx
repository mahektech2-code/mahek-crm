The control tabs are the second half of the `expenses` screen: `pettyClosing`, `pettyLedger` and `pettySettings` in `screens/petty-funds.ts`, and `pettyBank`, `pettyBudgets`, `pettyReports`, `pettyTally` in `screens/petty-books.ts`. Their writes are in `petty/funds.ts` (closings, adjustments, fund accounts, openings), `petty/bank.ts`, `petty/insight.ts` (budgets) and `petty/tally.ts`; the arithmetic in `engines/petty-cash.ts`. The machinery they share — `post`, `lock`, `queueTally`, roles — is on [the petty cash developer page](/docs/erp/petty-cash/developer).

## Access

- **Module:** <ModuleRef k="erp.expenses" /> (every petty-cash tab).
- `pettyAccounts`: bank import, matching and conversions, the Tally queue (`requireAccounts` in `bank.ts` and `tally.ts`).
- `pettyApprove` / `pettyOwner`: review closings, decide adjustments, verify openings, set budgets.
- `pettyOwner` alone: approve budgets. Owner or ERP administrator: fund accounts, categories, the `limits` tool.
- Maker-checker is enforced in each function: counter ≠ reviewer, requester ≠ decider, proposer ≠ verifier, setter ≠ budget approver.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Never let a statement line create money">
A new conversion must create its record through that record's own function (`initiateTransfer`, `requestFundAdjustment`) and then match to it.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep matching in matchLine">
Only an exact reference may settle a line alone. A rule that auto-settles on amount will reconcile the wrong ₹5,000.
</Step>
<Step title="Respect the closing reopen">
Anything that posts into the past goes through `post`, which reopens approved closings. Set a closing approved only after your own posting.
</Step>
<Step title="Queue Tally through queueTally">
One row per record, keyed by its code. A second queue path would give one record two remote ids.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Known gaps, with file references

| Gap | Where |
|---|---|
| `bookVsStatement` takes the last-imported line of the latest date; a newest-first statement gives that day's earliest balance (the import's `closing_balance_paise` has the same assumption) | `petty/bank.ts` `bookVsStatement`, `importStatement` |
| A variance adjustment posted on its closing's date reopens every later approved closing of the fund | `petty/funds.ts` `reviewClosing`; `petty/core.ts` `post` |
| Transfers still `initiated` are bank-match candidates, so a line can reconcile against a movement not yet posted | `petty/bank.ts` `candidates` |
