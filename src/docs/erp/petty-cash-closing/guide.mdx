These are the **control** tabs of the Petty cash screen: the cash count at the end of each day, the bank statement read against what was recorded, the fund ledger every balance comes from, the month's budgets, the reports, the queue of vouchers for Tally, and the settings — the funds and the expense categories. Recording expenses, payments, transfers and customer cash is on [Petty cash: expenses, payments and funds](/docs/erp/petty-cash).

## Daily closing

At the end of each day the custodian of each cash fund counts what is actually in the box. The ERP has already worked out what **should** be there from the ledger; the difference is the **variance**.

  <span key="1"><b>Close the day.</b> Pick the cash fund and the day; the expected balance fills in from the ledger.</span>,
  <span key="2"><b>Variance.</b> Counted less expected. Negative is a shortage.</span>,

<Steps>
<Step title="Count the cash and press Close the day">
Pick the **Cash fund** and the **Day**. **Expected (from the ledger)** fills in — it cannot be typed. Enter **Counted (₹)**.
</Step>
<Step title="Explain any difference">
If the count is short or over, **Explain any difference** is required: *The count is ₹500 short. Explain the difference.* Add a **Photo of the count** if you can.
</Step>
<Step title="Submit">
An approver is told. A count that matches says *Closing submitted · the count matches*.
</Step>
</Steps>

An approver then:

- **Approves** a matching count with an optional note.
- **Approves** a count with a variance by choosing a **Resolution** — *Explained — accept as it stands*, or *Post an adjustment for the variance* — and writing it in words. The adjustment, if chosen, is a separate entry that brings the book into line with the box; the variance stays on the closing.
- **Returns** it for a recount, saying what to recount or explain. The custodian then presses **Count again**.

<Callout kind="money" title="A shortage is never an expense">
Cash that is missing is a variance for review. It is never quietly turned into an expense, and it never disappears into a "miscellaneous" line.
</Callout>

| Closing status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Counted, waiting for an approver |
| **Approved** | Reviewed — with its resolution, if it had a variance |
| **Returned** | Sent back to be counted again |
| **Reopened** | Something was posted into that day after it was approved. Count and submit it again. |

A bank account is not counted — it is reconciled against its statement instead.

## Bank reconciliation

<Hotspots items={[
  <span key="1"><b>Import and match.</b> Accounts import the bank's CSV statement; the ERP matches what it can.</span>,
  <span key="2"><b>Unmatched.</b> A line nothing recorded explains — somebody has to say what it is.</span>,
  <span key="3"><b>On a line.</b> Confirm a match, match it by hand, or record what it was.</span>,
]} />

<Steps>
<Step title="Import statement">
Pick the bank **Account** and choose the **Statement (CSV)** exported from the bank. The ERP finds the header row itself — statements start with the account's name and address — and reads dates and amounts the way Indian banks print them. Rows it cannot read are listed and skipped. Importing the same statement again, or one that overlaps, adds nothing twice: *This statement was imported before — all 54 lines are already here; nothing was added.*
</Step>
<Step title="Read what was matched">
**Matched** — the reference or UTR on the line is exactly a recorded payment's or transfer's. **Suggested** — one recorded movement has the same amount within a few days; a person has to confirm it. **Exception** — two or more fit, or somebody marked it; pick the right one. **Unmatched** — nothing recorded explains it.
</Step>
<Step title="Reconcile">
Press **Reconcile** (or **Confirm the suggestion**) on a line, or **Reconcile matched** on a selection. The line and the payment or transfer it matched are both marked reconciled.
</Step>
<Step title="Deal with what is left">
**Match by hand** to pick the recorded movements a line is (a split may not add up to more than the line). **It is a cash withdrawal** raises the withdrawal transfer from the line — the cash counts once the custodian confirms it. **It is a bank charge** (or *bank interest / other* on a credit) requests a fund adjustment, matched to the line, posted once approved. **Mark as exception** with what is wrong. **Unmatch** with a reason undoes a match.
</Step>
</Steps>

The card at the top compares the **statement balance** on the last line with the **book balance** of the fund on the same date, and shows the difference.

## Fund ledger

Every movement of every fund, one row each, with the **balance after** it: date, number, fund, the kind of movement, in or out, amount, status and narration. Nothing on it can be edited — a mistake is corrected by a new entry, and the drawer says which entry a reversal reverses.

**Request an adjustment** on this tab is for a correction that is not an expense or a transfer: a **Bank charge**, a **Correction**, an **Advance recovered** (pick the advance), or **Other**, with whether it adds to or takes from the fund, and why. Somebody other than whoever asked — an approver or the owner — **Approves and posts** it, or **Rejects** it with a reason.

|---|---|
| **Incurred** | Received expenses in the month, submitted or approved |
| **Paid / Unpaid** | How much of that has been paid |
| **Committed** | Ordered but not yet received |

**Set a budget** for a month, a category, a godown (or *All godowns*) and a department. An approver or the owner sets it; it starts as a **Draft** and only the owner can **Approve** it — not one they set themselves. Changing an approved budget's amount makes it a draft again. Only approved budgets count: in the Overview's *Budget used* figure, the over-budget exception, and the approval route of a new expense.

## Reports

Read-only and downloadable, for the current month:

- **By category**, **By godown**, **By employee**, **By vendor**, **By transaction type** — incurred, paid, outstanding and count, largest first.
- **Monthly trend** — all expenses for the last six months.
- **Paid out by mode** — confirmed payments this month, by UPI, transfer, cheque or cash.

## Tally sync

Tally stays the authority for the books. Every confirmed payment, approved expense, confirmed transfer, customer receipt and approved adjustment is queued as a voucher for Tally, under a fixed id of its own.

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting to go to Tally | **Post to Tally** (when live posting is on), or **Export voucher** and post it by hand |
| **Syncing** | Being sent right now | Wait |
| **Posted** | Tally has it | Nothing |
| **Failed** | Tally refused it or could not be reached | Read *Last error*, then **Retry** |
| **Correction** | It was posted, then reversed in MahekOne | Fix or cancel it in Tally, then **Reversed in Tally** |
| **Not required** | It no longer needs a voucher | Nothing |

After posting by hand, press **Record voucher number** with the voucher type and number Tally gave it. **Not required** takes an entry off the queue with a reason. Only the accounts team works this tab.

<Callout kind="note" title="A retry never doubles a voucher">
Each voucher carries its record's fixed id. If Tally already has it — a post that timed out but got through — a retry updates that voucher instead of creating a second one: *Tally already had it — the voucher was updated, not duplicated.*
</Callout>

## Settings

**Fund accounts** — one row per fund: code, name, type (bank, bank cash, customer cash), bank account, the **custodian** (the person who signs in to confirm and count, and their HRMS record), godown, Tally ledger name, active or inactive. Only the owner (or an ERP administrator) adds or edits a fund.

**Opening balance** — a fund's starting figure, so its ledger begins at the truth. The accounts team or the owner **Proposes** it, with the date and what it is based on; somebody else — an approver or the owner — **Verifies and posts** it. After that it cannot be changed except by an adjustment.

**Approval limits** — the owner sets, in rupees, how much is approved on submission (0 sends everything to an approver), how much an approver may decide, what happens when an expense passes its budget (*Warn* or *Needs the owner*), how many days an expense may wait for a decision, and how many hours customer cash may be held. Today they are <Setting k="erp.pettyCash.autoApproveUpToPaise" /> and <Setting k="erp.pettyCash.approverLimitPaise" /> paise, <Setting k="erp.pettyCash.budgetRule" />, <Setting k="erp.pettyCash.reviewDays" /> days and <Setting k="erp.pettyCash.handoverHours" /> hours.

**Expense categories** — code, name, the **approval** rule (standard, always an approver, always the owner), whether a **bill or receipt is required**, whether it is **budgeted**, its Tally ledger and cost centre, and whether it is active. A retired category stays on old expenses.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Expected is different from what I worked out.">
Expected is the fund's ledger up to the end of that day: everything posted before it, plus what came in that day, less what went out. A transfer still in transit, a payment request and an unpaid bill are not in it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A closing I approved last week says Reopened.">
Something was posted into that day afterwards — a back-dated payment or a reversal on its own date. Count that day again.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The bank line has the right amount but stays Suggested.">
Amount and date alone never settle a line — somebody has to confirm it. Only an exact reference settles a line on its own.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I can't approve a budget I set.">
The owner approves budgets, and never one they set themselves.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Post to Tally is greyed out.">
Live posting is off. Export the voucher, post it in Tally by hand, and record its number.
</Faq>
