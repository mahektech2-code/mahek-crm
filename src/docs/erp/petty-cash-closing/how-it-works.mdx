The control tabs exist so that every rupee the production funds hold can be shown three ways that must agree: by **counting the cash box**, by **reading the bank statement**, and by **adding up the ledger**. Where they disagree, the difference is recorded as what it is — a variance, an unmatched line — and resolved by a person, separately, with the original left in place.

The everyday tabs — expenses, payments, transfers, customer cash — are on [Petty cash: expenses, payments and funds](/docs/erp/petty-cash/how-it-works).

## The daily cash count

<Rule title="Expected is worked out, never typed">
For one cash fund and one day: **opening** (everything posted before the day) **+ in** (credits posted that day) **− out** (debits posted that day) = **expected**. Only posted ledger entries count, so an unpaid bill, a payment request and a handover still in transit cannot reach it.
</Rule>

<Rule title="A variance is reviewed, never absorbed">
Counted less expected is the variance. A non-zero variance needs an explanation to submit, and a **resolution** to approve: *explained and accepted as it stands*, or *adjusted* — which posts a separate adjustment for the variance on that day. Either way the original count, the expected figure and the variance stay on the closing. A shortage is never turned into an expense.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Counter and reviewer are two people">
Whoever submitted the count cannot approve or return it — whatever their powers. Approving needs *Approve petty-cash expenses* or the owner's power.
</Rule>

<Rule title="A closed day reopens if it changes">
Any posting dated on or before an approved closing's day reopens it, with the reason (*… was posted after the day was closed*). Approving a variance adjustment is the one posting that does not reopen its own closing — it is set approved after posting.
</Rule>

<Callout kind="why">
A count that could be quietly edited, or a shortage that became "miscellaneous expense", is how a cash box drifts away from its book a few hundred rupees at a time with nobody's name on any of it.
</Callout>

A cash fund that had movements on a day in the last week with no closing for that day raises *Cash closing not submitted*; a variance waiting for review raises *Cash shortage* or *Cash overage*; a reopened day raises *Backdated entry reopened a closed day*.

## The bank statement

<Rule title="A statement line confirms; it never creates">
An imported line can be matched to a recorded payment, transfer or fund adjustment through the same bank fund — never to nothing. A line nothing explains is an exception for a person. The two conversions offered (*it is a cash withdrawal*, *it is a bank charge*) create the transfer or the adjustment request through their own rules first, and then match to it.
</Rule>

### How lines are matched

In this order, per line, against movements no line has claimed yet:

| Rule | Result |
|---|---|
| The line's reference (or a long reference inside its narration) is exactly one recorded movement's reference, with the same amount | **Matched** — settled on its own |
| Several movements share that reference | **Exception** — ambiguous |
| One movement in the same direction has the same amount within <Setting k="erp.pettyCash.matchWindowDays" /> days | **Suggested** — a person confirms |
| Several do, and exactly one shares a word with the narration (the payee) | **Suggested** — by narration |
| Several do, and the narration does not settle it | **Exception** — ambiguous |
| Nothing fits | **Unmatched** |

<Callout kind="why" title="Why only a reference settles a line on its own">
Two vendors paid ₹5,000 in the same week is an ordinary week. An amount and a date are evidence, not proof; a UTR names one transfer.
</Callout>

<Rule title="Re-importing adds nothing">
Each line is fingerprinted by the fund, date, amounts, reference, running balance, narration and the bank's own id — the balance is in it so two genuine ₹500 debits on one day are still two lines. A second import of the same file, or an overlapping one, adds only lines it has not seen, and says so.
</Rule>

The **statement less book** figure compares the balance on the last statement line with the fund's ledger balance up to that line's date.

## The ledger

<Rule title="Append-only, by the database">
`Fund ledger` is every posting, ever. The database refuses an edit or a delete, whatever the application does. A correction is a new posting: a reversal names the entry it reverses; an adjustment names its kind and reason.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Adjustments take two people">
A fund adjustment — a bank charge, a correction, an advance recovered in cash, a variance — is requested with a reason and posted only once somebody else with the approver's or owner's power approves it.
</Rule>

## Budgets

<Rule title="Budgets are controlled on incurred spending">
A month's budget for a category (at a godown, or all godowns) is compared with what was **incurred** — received expenses, submitted or approved — not with what was paid. **Remaining** also takes off what is **committed** (ordered, not received), because money promised is not money free. Paid is shown beside incurred, never instead of it.
</Rule>

| Figure | Rule |
|---|---|
| Forecast | This month: incurred ÷ days gone × days in the month. A past month: incurred. A future month: none. |
| Variance | (incurred − budget) ÷ budget |

Only an **approved** budget counts. The owner approves (never one they set); an approver or the owner sets; changing an approved amount returns it to draft. What happens when an expense would pass its approved budget is <Setting k="erp.pettyCash.budgetRule" />: *warn* flags it, *approval* routes it to the owner.

## Tally

<Rule title="A completed payment is not a posted voucher">
MahekOne keeps the operational record and a queue of what should reach Tally, with a status of its own. Tally stays the authority for the books.
</Rule>

| What is queued | Voucher type |
|---|---|
| An approved, received expense | Journal |
| A confirmed payment | Payment |
| A confirmed transfer | Contra |
| A customer cash receipt | Receipt |
| An approved fund adjustment | Journal |

How vouchers reach Tally is <Setting k="erp.tally.mode" />:

| Mode | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| off | Nothing is queued |
| export | Queued; accounts export each voucher's XML, post it by hand, and record the voucher number |
| live | Queued; accounts post from the screen to the Tally server, retrying on failure |

<Rule title="A retry alters, never duplicates">
Every voucher carries the record's fixed MahekOne id as Tally's remote id. A post that timed out but reached Tally is altered by the retry rather than created twice. Tally being unreachable fails the attempt safely and leaves the record untouched. A record reversed after posting moves its entry to **correction**; one reversed before posting becomes **not required**.
</Rule>

## Settings

<Rule title="A fund starts from a verified opening">
An opening balance is proposed (accounts or the owner) and verified and posted by somebody else (an approver or the owner). After that it is changed only by an adjustment. A fund whose opening is not verified is flagged.
</Rule>

Adding and editing fund accounts and expense categories, and the **Approval limits** button (which writes the five `erp.pettyCash.*` limits and timings through the same audited settings store the Admin Console uses), are the owner's or an ERP administrator's. Expense categories carry the **approval rule** that routes an expense, whether a **bill is required** (which stops approval by policy and raises *Bill or receipt missing*), whether it is **budgeted**, and the Tally ledger the voucher posts to.

## Where the code and the intent disagree today

- **The statement balance assumes oldest-first files.** The last balance is taken from the latest date, and among lines of that date the one imported last. A statement exported newest-first gives the balance of that day's earliest transaction.
- **An approved variance adjustment reopens later approved days.** It is posted on the closing's own date, so any later day of the same fund already approved reopens and must be counted again.
- **Bank matching treats an in-transit transfer as a candidate.** A withdrawal still waiting for the custodian's confirmation can be matched and reconciled before it has posted to the ledger.
