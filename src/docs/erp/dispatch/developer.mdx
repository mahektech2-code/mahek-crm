The desk is a bespoke route, `src/app/erp/dispatch`, not a generic list. Its rules are one pure function, `scanVerdict` in `engines/trace.ts`; the reading and the transaction that acts on a verdict are `lib/erp/dispatch.ts`; the writes are four server actions in `actions/erp-trace.ts`. Do Verified is not reimplemented here — the desk calls the Billing & dispatch tab's own bulk handler.

## Access

- **Module:** <ModuleRef k="erp.dispatch" />.
- **Power:** `dispatchOverride` to decide an override (and, on Boxes & labels, to reject or return a box).
- **Cross-screen:** Do Verified needs `orderDetails` (a tab of Orders); hand allocation needs `orders`. The desk hides both buttons without them and the actions refuse them.
- A preview refuses every write (`requireErpWrite`).

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Put a new check in scanVerdict">
It is pure and runs before any lock — a rule added in `scanOntoOrder` instead cannot be tested without a database and is invisible to `requestOverride`, which calls the same verdict.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep refused scans logged">
`logScan` runs whatever the verdict. The dashboard's mismatch and duplicate counts read `erp_dispatch_scans`.
</Step>
<Step title="Never let the desk verify by its own rules">
`erpDispatchVerify` delegates to `orderDetails.bulk.verify`. A second verify path would drift from the transport record and the order-book sync.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Known gaps, with file references

| Gap | Where |
|---|---|
| A different SKU of the same brand line is always a "size" mismatch, even when both pack labels are equal (boxed vs loose of one finished good) | `engines/trace.ts` `scanVerdict`; `dispatch.ts` `lineRows`, `unitFor` (`b.id as productId`) |
| `unscanUnit` leaves the allocation where the scan put it, so a swap is not reversed | `dispatch.ts` `unscanUnit` |
| A boxed SKU whose `cans_per_box` is 1 needs 0 boxes (`boxQuantity` returns 0 for `cpb` of 1 or less), so its line reads allocated in full with no lot, has nothing to scan, and the queue counts it as 0 | `engines/sales.ts` `boxQuantity`; `dispatch.ts` `scanLines`, `dispatchQueue` |
| `decideOverride` names the decider by `ctx.user` rather than `ctx.actor` (harmless today because previews refuse writes) | `dispatch.ts` `decideOverride` |
