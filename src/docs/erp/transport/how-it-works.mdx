Logistics is the part of a sale after the goods leave the factory and before the party says they arrived. One record per bill is opened by dispatch; the stage and the LR say where it is; every step writes an event; and the customer is told on WhatsApp at the steps that matter to them.

## One record per bill, opened by dispatch

<Rule title="Dispatch opens the record, once">
When the **first** line of an order number is dispatch-verified, a record is written for that bill (a unique index on the order number makes "once" hold). Straight after, its box count and litres are read off its lines, it gets a random tracking token, and its timeline opens with *Left the factory*.
</Rule>

## Where a bill is: the phase is derived

| Phase | Stage and LR |
|---|---|
| Going to the transporter | *Dispatched*, no LR |
| Waiting for the LR | *At transport* or later, no LR |
| On the road | Has an LR, not closed |
| Delivered | *Close - Received to Party* |

<Rule title="The phase is never stored">
It is read from the stage and the LR every time, so the desk, the old list and the order book cannot disagree about one bill. A bill moved to *In Transit* with no LR is still *waiting for its LR*: the customer cannot be told anything about a lorry nobody can name.
</Rule>

## The LR is uploaded by one team and attached by another

<Rule title="Collecting and attaching are two jobs">
The dispatch team collects the LRs from the transporters and uploads them (Logistics screen or Dispatch desk). Logistics decides which bills each carries. An uploaded LR is *unassigned* until attached; a wrong upload is **voided** with a reason, never deleted. An LR number is unique per transporter.
</Rule>

<Rule title="One LR is one party">
An LR is one consignment to one consignee, so the bills under it must go to one delivery party. A bill already under another LR, or delivered, cannot be attached.
</Rule>

The desk *suggests* the bills an LR carries — same transporter, destination naming the bill's area or party, box count matching, already at the transporter — and attaches nothing by itself.

## Telling the customer

<Rule title="Only what is on the record">
Each message is built from the record: the bills, the LR, the transporter, the boxes, the expected day and the tracking link. A box count nobody entered or a date nobody gave is left out, not guessed. One message per party per step; the billing party is told, and the delivery party too where it is somebody else.
</Rule>

| Step | Messaged? |
|---|---|
| LR attached | Yes — LR, transporter, boxes, expected delivery, tracking link |
| Stage or expected day moved | Yes |
| A call that moved nothing | No |
| A problem reported | Yes, unless unticked |
| Delivered | Yes — the day and who received it |

It goes through Wati only when <Setting k="erp.logistics.notifyCustomers" /> is on, the founder has WhatsApp switched on, and <Setting k="erp.logistics.watiTemplate" /> names an APPROVED template asking only for values logistics can fill. Otherwise it waits under **Customer messages** with the reason, to send by hand. A customer marked do not contact, on WhatsApp DND, with no mobile, or carrying a staff member's number is recorded as *not sent* and says why.

<Callout kind="why" title="Why not the CRM's message log">
A confirmed WhatsApp in the CRM's log is contact, and quiets the customer on the Call Log. A note that the goods are on a lorry is not a sales conversation and must not cost one, so logistics keeps its own messages.
</Callout>

## Calls, lateness and the tracking link

- After an update the next call falls due in <Setting k="erp.logistics.callEveryDays" /> days unless somebody picks another day.
- A consignment is *running late* the day after its expected delivery, or, with none, after <Setting k="erp.logistics.lateAfterDays" /> days from dispatch.
- The customer's link `/track/<token>` needs no sign-in. It shows the steps shared with the customer — never call notes, problem notes, prices or staff names.

The hourly ERP checks still raise an **LR missing** alert for a bill that left more than <Setting k="erp.ai.alerts.stuckLrDays" /> days ago with no LR.
