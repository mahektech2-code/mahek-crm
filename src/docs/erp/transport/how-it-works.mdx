Logistics is the part of a sale after the goods leave the factory and before the party says they arrived. One record per dispatch is opened by dispatch; the stage and the LR say where it is; every step writes an event; and the customer is told on WhatsApp at the steps that matter to them.

## One record per dispatch, opened by dispatch

<Rule title="Every dispatch opens a record">
Lines of one order and one bill that are dispatch-verified together become one record. A line of the same bill verified later joins it only while the record is still at the factory gate — no LR and not yet at the transporter. Anything else opens a record of its own: an order sent in two goes, or an order number that already has an old record from the Taken Order history. Each line remembers the record it left on. Straight after, the record's box count and litres are read off **its own** lines, it gets a random tracking token, and its timeline opens with *Left the factory*.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Nothing dispatched is left behind">
When Logistics opens, and every hour, any line dispatch-verified in the last <Setting k="erp.logistics.syncDays" /> days that is on no record is given one, dated its own dispatch date. That is the net under the rule above. Before October 2026 the record was one per order number, and lines on an order number that already had one opened nothing, so they never reached Logistics.
</Rule>

## Where a bill is: the phase is derived

| Phase | Stage and LR |
|---|---|
| Going to the transporter | *Dispatched*, no LR |
| Waiting for the LR | *At transport* or later, no LR |
| On the road | Has an LR, not closed — or delivered by a Mahek Vehicle, which needs none |
| Delivered | *Close - Received to Party* |

<Rule title="The phase is never stored">
It is read from the stage and the LR every time, so the desk, the old list and the order book cannot disagree about one bill. A bill moved to *In Transit* with no LR is still *waiting for its LR*: the customer cannot be told anything about a lorry nobody can name.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Every bill carries a delivery status in the PRD's words">
Beside the phase, each bill says where it stands as the Order-to-Delivery PRD names it — derived, never typed:

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| **Logistics Pending** | Going to the transporter, or waiting for its LR |
| **In Transit** | Has an LR and is on the road |
| **Delivery Pending Confirmation** | Stage *Reached Destination Area*, or its expected day has passed, and nobody has confirmed delivery |
| **Delivered** | *Close - Received to Party* — only ever the explicit Delivered act |
| **Exception** | An open problem, whatever the stage |
</Rule>

## Two ways to deliver, two kinds of proof

**Delivered by** on the order is a **Transporter** or a **Mahek Vehicle**. A Mahek Vehicle is a reserved name on the Transporters list, so every place that carries a transporter — the order, the bill, loading, trips — carries it unchanged; what differs is the proof. A Transporter bill goes to the transporter's godown and is put on the road by its **LR**. A Mahek Vehicle bill is on the road the moment it is dispatch-verified — which then needs the vehicle number and the driver — the team rings the driver for updates, and it is closed only with the **customer-signed copy** and who signed it. Who delivers a bill can change until it is delivered, except that a bill already under an LR keeps its transporter until the LR comes off.

<Rule title="The phone only uploads">
The floor sends photos — up to 10 at a time, LRs or signed copies — and types nothing. Every copy is checked by the laptop team on the **Documents desk**: what the AI read, what is missing, whether it is a second copy of something, and which bill it belongs to.
</Rule>

<Rule title="A duplicate is flagged, never thrown away by itself">
The exact same photo sent twice is stored once. Anything else that looks like a copy already on the desk — the same transporter and LR number, a number one character from another with the same date and boxes, the same number with a transporter not read, a signed copy of the same bill — is shown beside the first one for a person to decide: discard it, or keep both. The same LR number of two different transporters, and different numbers of one transporter, are different papers.
</Rule>

## The LR is uploaded by one team and attached by another

<Rule title="Collecting and attaching are two jobs">
The dispatch team collects the LRs from the transporters and uploads them (Logistics screen or Dispatch desk). Logistics decides which bills each carries. An uploaded LR is *unassigned* until attached; a wrong upload is **voided** with a reason, never deleted. An LR number is unique per transporter. The transporter on an LR, an LR edit and an order line must already be on the Transporters list — the same name in other capitals, spacing or "Pvt Ltd" is taken as the listed one, anything else is refused. Nothing adds to the list but the Transporters tab, which is what keeps one company from becoming three spellings.
</Rule>

<Rule title="A photo is read against the transporter's own style">
Each LR photo or PDF is read by OpenAI (<Setting k="erp.ai.visionModel" />) as soon as it is uploaded, while <Setting k="erp.ai.photos.enabled" /> is on, an OpenAI key is set and this month's <Setting k="erp.ai.photos.monthlyCap" /> readings are not used up. A receipt carries a phone, a GSTIN, an invoice number and a pin code beside the consignment number, so the model is told every transporter on the desk, what it calls its number (its *consignment label* — GR Number, G.C Number…) and what its last few numbers looked like. The answer is then checked by rules: the transporter is matched to the desk's own spelling (a name not on the list is left blank and said, never added), a number not shaped like that transporter's earlier ones is marked to check, and a date in the future or months old is not filled in. Only fields nobody has typed are filled, and Upload is still the only save. Whether each reading was accepted or edited is recorded against the LR it became.
</Rule>

<Rule title="One LR is one party">
An LR is one consignment to one consignee, so the bills under it must go to one delivery party. A bill already under another LR, or delivered, cannot be attached.
</Rule>

The desk *suggests* the bills an LR carries — our invoice number printed on it, its consignee being the bill's party (or, where the LR names the buyer, the billed party), same transporter, destination naming the bill's area or party, box count matching, already at the transporter — and attaches nothing by itself. A consignee that is plainly another shop counts against a bill. Party names are compared on their own words: "Hardware", "Traders", "Paints" and the like are set aside, so "Om Hardware" is never taken for "Sai Hardware".

An LR's date is read from the date as printed, day first (31/10/26 is 31 October), not from the AI's own conversion. A date that cannot be this LR's — in the future, or more than four months old — is not used; where it reads as January and exactly one of October, November or December fits (a handwritten 10 read as 1), that month is taken and the desk is told to check it.

## Transport expenses

<Rule title="Only Paid freight is a company expense">
An order line's freight is the consignment's payment type, else the billing party's freight term. **Paid** (we pay the transporter) is included. **To Pay** (the customer pays the transporter) and **TBB** are shown on the bill so nobody wonders where they went, and are never counted.
</Rule>

<Rule title="One bill is one vehicle on one day, split by litres">
A bill names the vehicle and the day it left. Every order line dispatch-verified onto that vehicle that day is offered — matched with spaces and case ignored, so *MH04 AB 1234* and *mh04ab1234* are one vehicle — and an order that left on another vehicle can be added by its number. The bill is shared over the ticked lines by dispatched litres only, never by boxes, customers or orders:

- **Cost per litre** = the bill ÷ the ticked lines' litres;
- **A line's share** = its litres × the cost per litre.

₹1,000 over 500 litres is ₹2 a litre: 100 L carries ₹200, 150 L ₹300 and 250 L ₹500. Shares are worked in paise and always add up to the bill exactly: each is rounded down and the paise left over go to the lines that lost most to the rounding. The add-bill dialog previews the split with the same function the save writes with.
</Rule>

<Rule title="The same freight cannot reach an order line twice">
An order line carries at most one live share, enforced by the database. A line already on another bill is greyed out with that bill's number. The same LR number from the same transporter cannot be entered twice.
</Rule>

<Rule title="The split goes on the order line by itself">
Nobody types a line's transport expense. It appears on Orders → Billing & dispatch as **Transport expense**, is added to the line's costing and margin where the cost comes from allocated lots, and reaches the Founder's Margins desk with the line's typed extra expenses. A line costed from the old order sheet already carries the sheet's whole cost, so its share is shown and not added again.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Pending until paid; a manager can undo">
A new bill is **Pending**. **Mark as paid** asks the payment date and Bank, UPI or Cash (a reference and remarks are optional) and moves it to **Paid history**; the split stays on every order. A paid bill cannot be edited. A **manager** can move a paid bill back to Pending with a reason — the split stays and the undone payment is kept in the bill's history. A pending bill can be edited, or deleted with a reason, which takes its shares off every order; the bill itself is kept, marked deleted.
</Rule>

<Rule title="A corrected dispatch makes the split stale">
If an order line on a pending bill changes after the split — its litres are corrected, it stops being Paid freight, or its dispatch is undone — the bill says so and cannot be paid until somebody presses **Split again**, which shares the bill over its lines as they are now (lines no longer Paid freight drop out). A paid bill is a record of what was paid and is not re-checked.
</Rule>

<Callout kind="money" title="Two different freight figures">
This is our cost to the transporter. The freight charged to the customer on the bill (Transportation cost on the order line) is a different figure and is never mixed with it. Marking a bill paid records the payment; it does not move money in Petty Cash/Bank.
</Callout>

Every write is a row in the bill's history and an audit row (`erp.freightBill.*`). Bills, reports and the history are read on **Transport expenses**; Trips & freight and Paid freight were retired into it, and the old addresses open it.

## Delivery is confirmed by somebody

<Rule title="Only the Delivered act closes a bill, and it records who">
Delivered needs the day; who received it and a proof or reference (a POD number, a signed copy) are asked, not demanded. The person who presses it is recorded as having confirmed the delivery. A delivery nobody can confirm stays open: report it as a problem — **Unconfirmed** — alongside *Delayed*, *Damaged*, *Short received*, *Held at transport*, *Wrong address* and **Refused**. Reopening a delivered bill clears who confirmed it and the proof.
</Rule>

## Telling the customer

<Rule title="Only what is on the record">
Each message is built from the record: the bills, the LR, the transporter, the boxes, the expected day and the tracking link. A box count nobody entered or a date nobody gave is left out, not guessed. One message per party per step; the billing party is told, and the delivery party too where it is somebody else.
</Rule>

| Step | Messaged? |
|---|---|
| LR attached | Yes — LR, transporter, boxes, expected delivery, tracking link |
| Stage or expected day moved | Yes |
| A call that moved nothing | No |
| A problem reported | Yes, unless unticked |
| Delivered | Yes — the day and who received it |

It goes through Wati only when <Setting k="erp.logistics.notifyCustomers" /> is on, the founder has WhatsApp switched on, and <Setting k="erp.logistics.watiTemplate" /> names an APPROVED template asking only for values logistics can fill. Otherwise it waits under **Customer messages** with the reason, to send by hand. A customer marked do not contact, on WhatsApp DND, with no mobile, or carrying a staff member's number is recorded as *not sent* and says why.

<Callout kind="why" title="Why not the CRM's message log">
A confirmed WhatsApp in the CRM's log is contact, and quiets the customer on the Call Log. A note that the goods are on a lorry is not a sales conversation and must not cost one, so logistics keeps its own messages.
</Callout>

## Calls, lateness and the tracking link

- After an update the next call falls due in <Setting k="erp.logistics.callEveryDays" /> days unless somebody picks another day.
- A consignment is *running late* the day after its expected delivery, or, with none, after <Setting k="erp.logistics.lateAfterDays" /> days from dispatch.
- The customer's link `/track/<token>` needs no sign-in. It shows the steps shared with the customer — never call notes, problem notes, prices or staff names.

The **Today** tab lists bills from the last three days with no vehicle recorded, so they can be put on a trip.

The hourly ERP checks still raise an **LR missing** alert for a bill that left more than <Setting k="erp.ai.alerts.stuckLrDays" /> days ago with no LR — and a **Signed copy missing** alert for a Mahek Vehicle bill that old with no signed copy.

## Where the code and the intent disagree today

- **A trip is matched on the vehicle and the dispatch date only.** A tempo that does two runs in a day puts both runs' bills on one trip; split them by creating a second trip and moving the bills.
- **Litres are the bill record's own figure**, read off its lines when it opened. A line edited after dispatch does not move it, and the split uses what the record says.
- **Bills dispatched before trips existed have no vehicle.** They are not flagged on Today (only the last three days are) but they are listed under Bills on no trip for 30 days.
