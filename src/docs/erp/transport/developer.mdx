The Logistics screen is a bespoke route, `src/app/erp/transport`: its first eight tabs are the desk, drawn from `lib/erp/logistics.ts` and written through `actions/erp-logistics.ts`; **All bills** is an ordinary list module from `screens/logistics.ts` drawn by the same route. `screens/logistics.ts` also keeps `transportList` modules for `pendingLr` and `trackLr` so a dashboard tile or an alert naming them still resolves, but the desk draws those tabs itself. The record, `erp_transports`, is opened in one place — `openTransports` in `screens/sales.ts`, called by dispatch verification and by `syncDispatchedTransports`, its net. (`calcs/logistics.ts`, despite its name, holds the complaint form's calculators; see [Complaints and credit notes](/docs/erp/complaints-and-credit-notes/developer).)

- **Lines on a vehicle:** `erp_order_details.transport_id` → `erp_transports.vehicle_no` and `erp_order_details.dispatch_date`; litres are `qty_cans × millilitres_per_can`, the reading `billFigures` makes; freight is `coalesce(erp_transports.payment_type, customers.freight_term)`.
- **The split:** `splitBill` is `allocateByLitres` keyed on the order line; `writeSplit` re-reads the lines under `for update`, marks the bill's old shares not live and writes new ones.
- **Writes:** `erpFreightBillSave`, `erpFreightBillResplit`, `erpFreightBillPay`, `erpFreightBillUnpay` (manager — `isErpManager`), `erpFreightBillDelete` in `actions/erp-logistics.ts`, each through `requireErpWrite("transport")`; `erpFreightVehicleLines` and `erpFreightOrderLines` are reads for the dialog.
- **One live share per order line:** the partial unique index `erp_freight_bill_lines_live_key` on `order_id where live`; a race on it is answered as a conflict.
- **Costing:** `detailRows` reads `freightSharesFor` and adds the share to `lotCosting`'s extras; `margin-service.ts` counts it in `extraPaise` for lot-costed lines only.
- **Events:** `erp_freight_bill_events` (created, edited, split, paid, unpaid, deleted) and `audit_log` (`erp.freightBill.*`).
- **Delivery:** `markDelivered` writes `delivered_by_id` (the person confirming) and `delivery_proof`; `reopen` clears both.

- **Page:** `src/app/erp/transport/page.tsx` (a static route ahead of `[slug]`), tabs in `src/app/erp/transport/parts/`, one consignment at `/erp/transport/[id]`, the Upload modal in `parts/upload-lrs-button.tsx` (pick-only, LR or signed copy, opened from the Logistics header and the Dispatch desk; `/erp/transport/upload` only redirects to it), the Documents desk in `parts/documents-desk.tsx`, crop and rotate in `parts/photo-editor.tsx`, the public link at `/track/[token]`.
- **Delivery proof (0301):** a signed copy is an `erp_lrs` row of `kind = signed_copy`; `erp_transports.signed_copy_id` points at it. Mahek Vehicle is the reserved transporter `MAHEK_VEHICLE` (`isMahekVehicle`), seeded by 0301. The desk's rules are pure in `src/lib/erp/engines/delivery-proof.ts` (`duplicateFlags`, `rankForSignedCopy`, `confidentPick`, `wrongKind`, `missingFor`); the writes are `setLrKind`, `clearDuplicate`, `discardDuplicate`, `replaceLrFile`, `attachReady` and `setDeliveredBy` in `src/lib/erp/logistics.ts`. Photos are read after upload by `readStoredLrs` (`read_lr_no`, `read_kind`, `read_at`; a signed copy's `bill_hint`, `party_hint`, `received_by`, `delivered_on`).
- **Writes:** `src/lib/actions/erp-logistics.ts`, each through `requireErpWrite("transport")`; the upload also accepts the Dispatch desk.
- **Reading an LR photo:** `erpLogisticsReadLr(fileId)` → `readUploadedLr` in `src/lib/erp/lr-upload-read.ts` (only the uploader's own unfiled attachment; `featureState("photos", true)`; `readImages` with every transporter's `consignment_label` and last five LR numbers in the prompt), then `cleanLrReading` in `src/lib/erp/engines/lr-read.ts`. Logged as an `erp_ai_suggestions` row (`feature = photos`, `record_type = erp_lr`); `uploadLrs` settles it accepted/edited against what was saved via `settleLrReadings`.
- **Wati ticks:** `applyWatiEvent` hands an `etmsg_` id to `applyLogisticsWatiEvent`.
