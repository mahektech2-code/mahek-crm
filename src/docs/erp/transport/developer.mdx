The Logistics screen is a bespoke route, `src/app/erp/transport`: its first eight tabs are the desk, drawn from `lib/erp/logistics.ts` and written through `actions/erp-logistics.ts`; **All bills** and **Paid freight** are ordinary list modules from `screens/logistics.ts` drawn by the same route. `screens/logistics.ts` also keeps `transportList` modules for `pendingLr` and `trackLr` so a dashboard tile or an alert naming them still resolves, but the desk draws those tabs itself. The record, `erp_transports`, is opened in one place — `openTransports` in `screens/sales.ts`, called by dispatch verification and by `syncDispatchedTransports`, its net. (`calcs/logistics.ts`, despite its name, holds the complaint form's calculators; see [Complaints and credit notes](/docs/erp/complaints-and-credit-notes/developer).)

- **Writes:** `erpTripSave`, `erpTripAddBills`, `erpTripRemoveBill`, `erpTransportPaymentRecord`, `erpTransportPaymentReverse` in `actions/erp-logistics.ts`, each through `requireErpWrite("transport")`.
- **Events:** each trip change, payment and reversal writes an `erp_transport_events` row of kind `trip` (`shared = false`) on every bill it touches, and an `audit_log` row (`erp.trip.*`, `erp.transportPayment.*`).
- **One live share per bill:** the partial unique index `erp_transport_allocations_live_key` on `transport_id where not reversed` — the service checks first so the refusal can name the order.
- **Delivery:** `markDelivered` writes `delivered_by_id` (the person confirming) and `delivery_proof`; `reopen` clears both. `ISSUES` in `engines/logistics.ts` gained `Refused` and `Unconfirmed`.

## Paid freight

<Code file="src/lib/erp/screens/sales.ts" symbol="setExtraExpenses" />

`paidFreight.load` reads `detailRows()` from `screens/sales.ts` and keeps lines whose billing party's `freightTerm` is `Paid` and whose `extra_expenses_paise` is null. Its columns go through `visibleCols`, so Payment type is hidden without the cost power exactly as it is on Orders → Billing & dispatch.

Extra expenses are written by ONE function, `setExtraExpenses`, whichever screen they are entered on: it writes the order-details line and the bill's transport record together, and both screens ask with the same `EXTRA_EXPENSE_PROMPT`. Orders used to have its own copy that skipped the transport record.
