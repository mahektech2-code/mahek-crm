Transport is four list modules in `screens/logistics.ts`: `transportList` built for three scopes (`transport`, `pendingLr`, `trackLr`) and `paidFreight`. The record they read, `erp_transports`, is written in exactly one place — the dispatch verification in `screens/sales.ts` — and updated by the `update` action and the AI LR review. (`calcs/logistics.ts`, despite its name, holds the complaint form's calculators; see [Complaints and credit notes](/docs/erp/complaints-and-credit-notes/developer).)

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the record only from verify">
A second writer of `erp_transports` would race the unique index and lose the copied figures.
</Step>
<Step title="Derive, do not store, where the goods are">
Add a stage to `MATERIAL_STAGES`; keep `onTheRoad` the only reading of "on the road".
</Step>
<Step title="Sync the order book on anything the CRM shows">
`syncBookOrders` is called on a stage change. Anything new that the book projects needs the same call.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Known gaps, with file references

| Gap | Where |
|---|---|
| The reminder flag is `today`, whose label is "Transferred today" | `screens/logistics.ts` `transportList` flags; `ui.ts` `FLAG.today` |
| The `update` action does not refuse an LR number already on another bill; only `applyLr` does | `screens/logistics.ts` `update`; `ai-photos.ts` `applyLr` |
| An LR recorded without a stage change, and every `applyLr`, skip `syncBookOrders` | `screens/logistics.ts` `update`; `ai-photos.ts` `applyLr` |
| `track_status` is written and never read | `screens/logistics.ts` `update` |
