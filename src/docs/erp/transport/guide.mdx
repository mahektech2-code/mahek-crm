The **Logistics** screen follows every bill from the factory gate to the party's door: on its way to the transporter's godown at Bhiwandi, waiting for its lorry receipt (LR), on the road, and delivered. The customer gets a WhatsApp at every step. A bill appears here by itself the moment it is dispatch-verified — nobody has to add it. Every bill also says where it stands in plain words: **Logistics Pending**, **In Transit**, **Delivery Pending Confirmation**, **Delivered** or **Exception**. When a transporter sends its bill for a vehicle, it is entered once on **Transport expenses** and split over every Paid-freight order on that vehicle by litres.

A bill goes one of two ways, chosen on the order as **Delivered by**: a **Transporter**, whose proof is the **LR**, or a **Mahek Vehicle** — our own vehicle — whose proof is the **customer-signed copy** of the bill. A Mahek Vehicle skips the transporter's godown and the LR: it is on the road the moment it leaves, the team rings the driver for updates, and it closes only with the signed copy.

## The flow, step by step

<Steps>
<Step title="1. To transporter — follow the goods to Bhiwandi">
Every bill that has left the factory, grouped by transporter. Under each bill is the vehicle dispatch recorded and its trip — or *No vehicle recorded* in amber. Tick bills and say when they reach the transporter: **Reaches today**, **Reaches tomorrow**, or a day. Correct the **Boxes** count if it is wrong (it is read off the bill's lines). When the transporter has them, press **Reached transporter** — or **All reached** for a whole transporter.
</Step>
<Step title="2. The phone uploads the LRs and signed copies — nothing else">
The dispatch team photographs each LR as the transporter hands it over, and each customer-signed copy the Mahek Vehicle driver brings back. On the **Mahek Factory** phone app it is **More → Upload LR or signed copy**: pick **LR** or **Signed copy**, take or pick up to 10 photos, and **Send**. Tap a photo to crop, rotate or remove it first. Nothing is typed and nothing is checked on the phone — the office does that on the Documents desk. On a laptop, **Upload** on the Logistics screen or the Dispatch desk does the same: choose LR or Signed copy, drop up to 10 photos or PDFs, **Send**. The same photo sent twice is stored once. Each photo is read by the AI after it arrives: an LR's number, date, transporter, consignee (the party it is for), our invoice number where the transporter wrote it, destination, boxes and freight; a signed copy's bill number, party, who signed and the date.
</Step>
<Step title="3. Documents desk — check each copy and attach it">
Two tabs, **LRs** and **Signed copies**. On the left, every copy waiting, with who sent it and when — a search box above them finds one by transporter, LR or bill number, party, city or who uploaded it — and a mark: a green tick is ready to attach, an amber warning has something to add or fix, a red copy mark may be a duplicate, a clock is still being read. Pick one: the photo is on the left of the panel (open it full size, step to the previous or next, or **crop and rotate** it), what was read is beside it, and anything missing — an LR number the AI could not read, who signed a copy — is typed there and **Save**d. An LR shows its **Consignee** and **Our invoice no.** — correct them if the AI misread them, because they are what picks the right bill out of one transporter's pile. Below, every bill waiting for its LR, the likeliest first, with why (same bill no., same party, same transporter, destination, box count; for a signed copy, the bill number and party) — ten at a time with **Show more**, and a search box above them that finds a bill by party, bill or order number, transporter or city. A search that only matches bills not yet at the transporter says so, with a button to show them. Ticking a bill for a different shop than the consignee says so beside **Attach**. Tick them and press **Attach** — the bills go on the road and the customer gets the LR number and a tracking link — or, for a signed copy, **Attach & mark delivered**, which closes the delivery. A signed copy is offered every bill not yet delivered, Mahek Vehicle ones first — a bill whose transporter was typed as the vehicle number, "Tempo" or "Portal" is closed by its signed copy just the same. **Attach N ready** at the top does every copy the desk is sure of in one press. The third tab, **Pending**, turns it round: the bills still waiting for their LR or signed copy, longest waiting first, filtered by why (no LR for days, at the transporter for days, signed copy missing), with a search box to find one by party, bill or order number, transporter or city. A bill whose transporter is not on the transporter list — usually a vehicle number like *MH04NB7322* typed where the company belongs — is flagged, and **Not a listed transporter** lists them all. **Delivered by…** settles each one: **Mahek Vehicle** if it went in our own vehicle (the plate becomes its vehicle number, the old name stays on its timeline, and the signed copy closes it), or the right transporter from the list. Pick one to ring the transporter or driver, and to see the copies on the desk that may be it — LRs and, for a transporter's bill, signed copies too — **Check and attach** opens that copy with the bill already ticked. A copy that looks like another is shown **side by side**: **Same — discard this one** or **Different — keep both**. A copy uploaded under the wrong tab says so (*Looks like an LR*) with a button to move it. A wrong upload is discarded with the bin, with a reason.
</Step>
<Step title="4. On the road — ring and update">
One card per LR, with the transporter's number — or, for a Mahek Vehicle, one card per bill with the vehicle number and the driver's number. **Call today** lists the ones owed a call. After the call press **Update status** (In transit, Near destination, At destination — the customer is told), **Called — no change** (the next call is booked, nobody is messaged) or **Report a problem** (delayed, damaged, short, held, wrong address, **refused**, **unconfirmed**…). A bill with a problem shows **Exception** until the problem is solved or the bill is delivered.
</Step>
<Step title="5. Delivered">
When the party has confirmed they have the goods, press **Delivered ✓**, give the day, who received it and — if you have one — a **proof or reference** (a POD number, a photo's name). A Mahek Vehicle card says **Signed copy & delivered** instead: it needs the customer-signed copy — upload it there, or pick one already on the Documents desk — and who signed for it. **Delivered by…** on a card, or **Send in a Mahek Vehicle** on To transporter, changes who delivers a bill until it is delivered (a bill under an LR keeps its transporter until the LR comes off). Your name is recorded as the person who confirmed it. The customer gets a thank-you message, and the bill moves to **Delivered**. If the party has not confirmed yet, do not close it: **Report a problem → Unconfirmed** keeps it open and visible.
</Step>
<Step title="6. Transport expenses — the transporter's bill">
Only **Paid** freight is our expense; **To Pay** (the customer pays the transporter) is never counted. When a transporter's bill arrives, press **+ Add transport bill** and fill in three pages. **Bill**: the bill or LR number, its date, the transporter, the vehicle (picked from the vehicles that left in the last 45 days) and the total amount. **What was on the vehicle**: every order that left on that vehicle that day is listed and the Paid-freight ones are ticked; To Pay, TBB and orders already on another bill are greyed out with the reason, and **+ Add a dispatch that left on another vehicle** takes an order number. **Check the split**: the cost per litre (bill ÷ litres) and each order line's share, by order line, customer or product — always adding up to the bill exactly. Save it as pending, or save and mark it paid. The share appears on the order line by itself (Orders → Billing & dispatch, **Transport expense**) and counts in its margin.
</Step>
<Step title="7. Mark as paid, and undo">
On **Pending expense**, press **Mark as paid**, pick Bank, UPI or Cash and the payment date; a reference and remarks are optional. The bill moves to **Paid history**; its split stays on each order. A manager can **Undo payment** on a paid bill, with a reason: it goes back to Pending expense and the payment stays in its **History**. If an order on a pending bill is corrected after the split — its litres change, it stops being Paid freight, or its dispatch is undone — the bill says **Dispatch changed · split again** and cannot be paid until you press **Split again**. A pending bill entered by mistake is **Delete**d with a reason.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | The four steps as counts, the calls owed today, and what needs attention: problems, lorries running late, bills at the transporter two days with no LR, bills from the last three days with no vehicle recorded, messages not sent. |
| **To transporter** | Bills on their way to the transporter's godown. |
| **Documents desk** | Every LR and signed copy uploaded from the phone or a laptop: checked, fixed, de-duplicated and attached. |
| **On the road** | One card per LR — or per Mahek Vehicle bill — until delivery. |
| **Delivered** | Closed in the last 45 days, with how long each took, who received it, the proof and who confirmed it. **Reopen** if one was closed by mistake. |
| **Customer messages** | Every WhatsApp the flow wrote. Those that could not go through Wati wait here: **Open in WhatsApp** (the text is already typed), send, then **Mark sent**. |
| **Transport expenses** | What we owe transporters, for **Paid** freight only — To Pay never appears. **Pending expense** has two lists. **Bill not received yet** is every Paid-freight sales bill of the last 45 days that no transport bill covers, one row per sales bill and newest first, with its bill date, billing party, bill number, vehicle, transporter, order lines, litres and the day it left; clicking a row (or **Add bill** in its ⋯ menu) opens the bill form on that vehicle. **Bill received, to pay** is the bills entered and not paid, oldest first, with what is owed, the litres, the cost per litre and how many days it has waited. **Paid history** lists what was paid in a month, with the payment date and mode. All three are ordinary ERP lists: search, a filter on every column (tick values, or a from–to on a figure), sorting, choosing columns, ticking rows to add them up, and export. **Reports** shows pending and paid by customer, product, order and transporter, and the cost per litre month by month. Click a bill for its split by order line, customer or product, the bill and payment, and its history. |
| **Transporters** | Each transport company: its head office GST number (optional), what it calls its consignment number (LR, GR, CN…), its online tracking page, and its godowns. The godowns are listed on the transporter's form; **+ Add a godown** or **Edit** opens the godown in its own window. Each godown needs all four of State → District → City → Area — each list shows only what is under the one above it, and **+ Add a … not listed** at the foot of the district, city and area lists types a new one, added under the one above when you save (a state is always picked from the list). Each godown can carry its own GST number — it is optional — and because GST is registered per state, a number that is given must start with the code for the godown's state. It also takes its pin on the map — use **Find in map** and click the spot, type the latitude and longitude, or use the device's location when standing there — and the people there, a name and a phone number each. A godown saved before this asks for its city and area the next time the transporter is edited. Click a row to see it all; a card on the road rings the first person with a number. |
| **All bills** | The register as a list, with each bill's vehicle. |

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="A bill I dispatched is not here.">
A bill appears once it is **dispatch-verified** (Mark dispatch-verified on Orders or at the Dispatch desk). Billing alone is not enough. If a verified bill is still missing, open Logistics again: it hands over anything dispatch-verified in the last <Setting k="erp.logistics.syncDays" /> days that is not on the board yet.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The customer did not get the WhatsApp.">
Open **Customer messages**. It says why: no Wati template is set for logistics, WhatsApp is switched off by the founder, the number is not a mobile, or the customer is on DND. Send it by hand from there.
</Faq>
<Faq q="One LR carries three bills of the same party.">
Tick all three on the Documents desk. The customer gets one message naming the three bills.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The same LR was photographed twice.">
The second one is marked as a possible duplicate on the Documents desk and shown beside the first. Press **Same — discard this one**. The exact same photo sent twice never reaches the desk at all — it is stored once.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The AI could not read the LR number.">
The copy has an amber mark on the Documents desk and the LR number box is empty. Read it off the photo beside the box, type it and **Save**, then attach. Where the AI read a number it was not sure of, it is offered under the box to check.
</Faq>
<Faq q="How does a Mahek Vehicle delivery close?">
With the customer-signed copy. The driver brings it back, the dispatch team photographs it as a **Signed copy**, and on the Documents desk's **Signed copies** tab the office checks the bill, the date and who signed, and presses **Attach & mark delivered**. It can also be done from the card on **On the road**. Without the signed copy the delivery stays open, and after <Setting k="erp.ai.alerts.stuckLrDays" /> days it raises a **Signed copy missing** alert.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I attached the wrong bill to an LR.">
Open the bill (click its number) — or use the consignment's page — and take it off the LR with a reason. It goes back to waiting for its LR.
</Faq>
<Faq q="One tempo carried goods for three customers. How is the ₹1,000 bill split?">
By litres only. On **Transport expenses**, press **+ Add transport bill**, type the LR number, pick the transporter and the vehicle, and type ₹1,000. The orders on that vehicle are ticked for you. If they carry 100, 150 and 250 litres, that is ₹2 a litre: ₹200, ₹300 and ₹500. Boxes, customers and the number of orders play no part. A line with no litres cannot be ticked.
</Faq>
<Faq q="One of the orders on the vehicle was To Pay.">
It is listed, greyed out, and not counted: the customer pays that transporter, so it is not our expense. The bill is split over the Paid-freight orders only.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I marked a bill paid by mistake.">
Ask a manager to open it on **Paid history** and press **Undo payment** with the reason. It goes back to Pending expense with its split unchanged.
</Faq>
<Faq q="What does the customer's tracking link show?">
The bill, the LR, the transporter and the steps so far — for a Mahek Vehicle, the vehicle and, once delivered, the signed copy. Never prices, call notes or staff names.
</Faq>
