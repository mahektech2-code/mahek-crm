The **Logistics** screen follows every bill from the factory gate to the party's door: on its way to the transporter's godown at Bhiwandi, waiting for its lorry receipt (LR), on the road, and delivered. The customer gets a WhatsApp at every step. A bill appears here by itself the moment its first line is dispatch-verified — nobody has to add it.

## The flow, step by step

<Steps>
<Step title="1. To transporter — follow the goods to Bhiwandi">
Every bill that has left the factory, grouped by transporter. Tick bills and say when they reach the transporter: **Reaches today**, **Reaches tomorrow**, or a day. Correct the **Boxes** count if it is wrong (it is read off the bill's lines). When the transporter has them, press **Reached transporter** — or **All reached** for a whole transporter.
</Step>
<Step title="2. The dispatch team uploads the LRs">
Once the goods are with the transporter, the dispatch team collects the LRs and uploads them on **Upload LRs** (also on the Dispatch desk): a photo of each and its number, transporter, destination and box count. **Add several photos** makes one row per photo. Nothing is attached to a bill yet.
</Step>
<Step title="3. LR desk — attach each LR to its bills">
On the left, the LRs waiting to be attached. Pick one: the bills it most likely carries rise to the top, with why (same transporter, destination matches, box count). Tick the bills, set the expected delivery and the next call to the transporter, and press **Attach LR & tell customer**. The customer gets a WhatsApp with the LR number, the transporter, the boxes and a tracking link.
</Step>
<Step title="4. On the road — ring and update">
One card per LR, with the transporter's number. **Call today** lists the ones owed a call. After the call press **Update status** (In transit, Near destination, At destination — the customer is told), **Called — no change** (the next call is booked, nobody is messaged) or **Report a problem** (delayed, damaged, short…).
</Step>
<Step title="5. Delivered">
When the party has the goods, press **Delivered ✓**, give the day and who received it. The customer gets a thank-you message, and the bill moves to **Delivered**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | The four steps as counts, the calls owed today, and what needs attention: problems, lorries running late, bills at the transporter two days with no LR, messages not sent. |
| **To transporter** | Bills on their way to the transporter's godown. |
| **LR desk** | Uploaded LRs, attached to their bills. |
| **On the road** | One card per LR until delivery. |
| **Delivered** | Closed in the last 45 days, with how long each took. **Reopen** if one was closed by mistake. |
| **Customer messages** | Every WhatsApp the flow wrote. Those that could not go through Wati wait here: **Open in WhatsApp** (the text is already typed), send, then **Mark sent**. |
| **Transporters** | Who to ring, their GST number, what they call their consignment number (LR, GR, CN…), the places they keep godowns, and their online tracking page. A card on the road links to it. |
| **All bills / Paid freight** | The registers as lists. Paid freight is where the freight amount for *Paid* bills is entered. |

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="A bill I dispatched is not here.">
A bill appears once its first line is **dispatch-verified** (Do Verified on Orders or at the Dispatch desk). Billing alone is not enough.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The customer did not get the WhatsApp.">
Open **Customer messages**. It says why: no Wati template is set for logistics, WhatsApp is switched off by the founder, the number is not a mobile, or the customer is on DND. Send it by hand from there.
</Faq>
<Faq q="One LR carries three bills of the same party.">
Tick all three on the LR desk. The customer gets one message naming the three bills.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I attached the wrong bill to an LR.">
Open the bill (click its number) — or use the consignment's page — and take it off the LR with a reason. It goes back to waiting for its LR.
</Faq>
<Faq q="What does the customer's tracking link show?">
The bill, the LR, the transporter and the steps so far. Never prices, call notes or staff names.
</Faq>
