The **Logistics** screen follows every bill from the factory gate to the party's door: on its way to the transporter's godown at Bhiwandi, waiting for its lorry receipt (LR), on the road, and delivered. The customer gets a WhatsApp at every step. A bill appears here by itself the moment it is dispatch-verified — nobody has to add it. Every bill also says where it stands in plain words: **Logistics Pending**, **In Transit**, **Delivery Pending Confirmation**, **Delivered** or **Exception**. One tempo or truck carrying several bills is a **trip**, and the transport payment for it is recorded once and shared out by litres on **Trips & freight**.

A bill goes one of two ways, chosen on the order as **Delivered by**: a **Transporter**, whose proof is the **LR**, or a **Mahek Vehicle** — our own vehicle — whose proof is the **customer-signed copy** of the bill. A Mahek Vehicle skips the transporter's godown and the LR: it is on the road the moment it leaves, the team rings the driver for updates, and it closes only with the signed copy.

## The flow, step by step

<Steps>
<Step title="1. To transporter — follow the goods to Bhiwandi">
Every bill that has left the factory, grouped by transporter. Under each bill is the vehicle dispatch recorded and its trip — or *No vehicle recorded* in amber. Tick bills and say when they reach the transporter: **Reaches today**, **Reaches tomorrow**, or a day. Correct the **Boxes** count if it is wrong (it is read off the bill's lines). When the transporter has them, press **Reached transporter** — or **All reached** for a whole transporter.
</Step>
<Step title="2. The phone uploads the LRs and signed copies — nothing else">
The dispatch team photographs each LR as the transporter hands it over, and each customer-signed copy the Mahek Vehicle driver brings back. On the **Mahek Factory** phone app it is **More → Upload LR or signed copy**: pick **LR** or **Signed copy**, take or pick up to 10 photos, and **Send**. Tap a photo to crop, rotate or remove it first. Nothing is typed and nothing is checked on the phone — the office does that on the Documents desk. On a laptop, **Upload** on the Logistics screen or the Dispatch desk does the same: choose LR or Signed copy, drop up to 10 photos or PDFs, **Send**. The same photo sent twice is stored once. Each photo is read by the AI after it arrives: an LR's number, date, transporter, destination, boxes and freight; a signed copy's bill number, party, who signed and the date.
</Step>
<Step title="3. Documents desk — check each copy and attach it">
Two tabs, **LRs** and **Signed copies**. On the left, every copy waiting, with who sent it and when, and a mark: a green tick is ready to attach, an amber warning has something to add or fix, a red copy mark may be a duplicate, a clock is still being read. Pick one: the photo is on the left of the panel (open it full size, step to the previous or next, or **crop and rotate** it), what was read is beside it, and anything missing — an LR number the AI could not read, who signed a copy — is typed there and **Save**d. Below, the bills it most likely belongs to, with why (same transporter, destination, box count; for a signed copy, the bill number and party). Tick them and press **Attach** — the bills go on the road and the customer gets the LR number and a tracking link — or, for a signed copy, **Attach & mark delivered**, which closes the Mahek Vehicle delivery. **Attach N ready** at the top does every copy the desk is sure of in one press. A copy that looks like another is shown **side by side**: **Same — discard this one** or **Different — keep both**. A copy uploaded under the wrong tab says so (*Looks like an LR*) with a button to move it. A wrong upload is discarded with the bin, with a reason.
</Step>
<Step title="4. On the road — ring and update">
One card per LR, with the transporter's number — or, for a Mahek Vehicle, one card per bill with the vehicle number and the driver's number. **Call today** lists the ones owed a call. After the call press **Update status** (In transit, Near destination, At destination — the customer is told), **Called — no change** (the next call is booked, nobody is messaged) or **Report a problem** (delayed, damaged, short, held, wrong address, **refused**, **unconfirmed**…). A bill with a problem shows **Exception** until the problem is solved or the bill is delivered.
</Step>
<Step title="5. Delivered">
When the party has confirmed they have the goods, press **Delivered ✓**, give the day, who received it and — if you have one — a **proof or reference** (a POD number, a photo's name). A Mahek Vehicle card says **Signed copy & delivered** instead: it needs the customer-signed copy — upload it there, or pick one already on the Documents desk — and who signed for it. **Delivered by…** on a card, or **Send in a Mahek Vehicle** on To transporter, changes who delivers a bill until it is delivered (a bill under an LR keeps its transporter until the LR comes off). Your name is recorded as the person who confirmed it. The customer gets a thank-you message, and the bill moves to **Delivered**. If the party has not confirmed yet, do not close it: **Report a problem → Unconfirmed** keeps it open and visible.
</Step>
<Step title="6. Trips & freight — the vehicle and its transport payment">
When dispatch records a vehicle number, every bill leaving in that vehicle on that day goes on the same **trip**, whatever customers they are for. Bills with no vehicle wait under **Bills on no trip**: tick them and press **New trip with … bills**, or open a trip and **Add bills**. When the transport payment is confirmed, open the trip and press **Record transport payment**: type the total once, tick the bills it covers, and the screen shows the eligible litres, the cost per litre and each bill's share before you save. The shares always add up to the amount exactly. Logistics records the confirmed payment — it does not make it. A wrong one is **Reverse**d with a reason, and its bills can be shared again.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | The four steps as counts, the calls owed today, and what needs attention: problems, lorries running late, bills at the transporter two days with no LR, bills from the last three days with no vehicle recorded, messages not sent. |
| **To transporter** | Bills on their way to the transporter's godown. |
| **Documents desk** | Every LR and signed copy uploaded from the phone or a laptop: checked, fixed, de-duplicated and attached. |
| **On the road** | One card per LR — or per Mahek Vehicle bill — until delivery. |
| **Delivered** | Closed in the last 45 days, with how long each took, who received it, the proof and who confirmed it. **Reopen** if one was closed by mistake. |
| **Customer messages** | Every WhatsApp the flow wrote. Those that could not go through Wati wait here: **Open in WhatsApp** (the text is already typed), send, then **Mark sent**. |
| **Trips & freight** | One card per vehicle trip: its vehicle, driver, transporter and day, the bills it carried for every customer, their litres, and the transport payment shared out over them. Filters: **Vehicle missing**, **Cost not shared out**, **Reconciliation required** (a bill's litres changed after the split, so the payment should be reversed and recorded again). |
| **Transporters** | Each transport company: its head office GST number (optional), what it calls its consignment number (LR, GR, CN…), its online tracking page, and its godowns. The godowns are listed on the transporter's form; **+ Add a godown** or **Edit** opens the godown in its own window. Each godown needs all four of State → District → City → Area — each list shows only what is under the one above it, and **+ Add a … not listed** at the foot of the district, city and area lists types a new one, added under the one above when you save (a state is always picked from the list). Each godown can carry its own GST number — it is optional — and because GST is registered per state, a number that is given must start with the code for the godown's state. It also takes its pin on the map — use **Find in map** and click the spot, type the latitude and longitude, or use the device's location when standing there — and the people there, a name and a phone number each. A godown saved before this asks for its city and area the next time the transporter is edited. Click a row to see it all; a card on the road rings the first person with a number. |
| **All bills / Paid freight** | The registers as lists. All bills also shows each bill's trip, vehicle and share of the transport cost. Paid freight is where the extra expense for *Paid*-freight bills is entered — the amount billed to the customer, not the transport payment, which is on Trips & freight. |

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="A bill I dispatched is not here.">
A bill appears once it is **dispatch-verified** (Mark dispatch-verified on Orders or at the Dispatch desk). Billing alone is not enough. If a verified bill is still missing, open Logistics again: it hands over anything dispatch-verified in the last <Setting k="erp.logistics.syncDays" /> days that is not on the board yet.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The customer did not get the WhatsApp.">
Open **Customer messages**. It says why: no Wati template is set for logistics, WhatsApp is switched off by the founder, the number is not a mobile, or the customer is on DND. Send it by hand from there.
</Faq>
<Faq q="One LR carries three bills of the same party.">
Tick all three on the Documents desk. The customer gets one message naming the three bills.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The same LR was photographed twice.">
The second one is marked as a possible duplicate on the Documents desk and shown beside the first. Press **Same — discard this one**. The exact same photo sent twice never reaches the desk at all — it is stored once.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The AI could not read the LR number.">
The copy has an amber mark on the Documents desk and the LR number box is empty. Read it off the photo beside the box, type it and **Save**, then attach. Where the AI read a number it was not sure of, it is offered under the box to check.
</Faq>
<Faq q="How does a Mahek Vehicle delivery close?">
With the customer-signed copy. The driver brings it back, the dispatch team photographs it as a **Signed copy**, and on the Documents desk's **Signed copies** tab the office checks the bill, the date and who signed, and presses **Attach & mark delivered**. It can also be done from the card on **On the road**. Without the signed copy the delivery stays open, and after <Setting k="erp.ai.alerts.stuckLrDays" /> days it raises a **Signed copy missing** alert.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I attached the wrong bill to an LR.">
Open the bill (click its number) — or use the consignment's page — and take it off the LR with a reason. It goes back to waiting for its LR.
</Faq>
<Faq q="One tempo carried bills for three customers. How is the ₹1,000 we paid split?">
By litres only. On **Trips & freight**, open the trip, press **Record transport payment**, type ₹1,000 and tick the three bills. If they carry 250, 500 and 250 litres, that is ₹1 a litre: ₹250, ₹500 and ₹250. Boxes, weight and the number of bills play no part. A bill with no litres stops the split and is named, so its litres can be corrected first.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The payment covers only some of the bills on the trip.">
Tick only those bills, and say in **Which part of the trip this covers** what the payment is for. The rest stay unshared, so nothing is paid for twice — and a bill already carrying a share cannot be ticked again until that payment is reversed.
</Faq>
<Faq q="What does the customer's tracking link show?">
The bill, the LR, the transporter and the steps so far — for a Mahek Vehicle, the vehicle and, once delivered, the signed copy. Never prices, call notes or staff names.
</Faq>
