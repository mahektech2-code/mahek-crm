The **Complaints & credit notes** screen is where the factory side sees, raises and decides customer complaints — and the credit notes customers ask for. These are **the same records the CRM keeps**: a complaint raised here lands on the customer's record in the CRM, and one a telecaller raised on a call is here too. How complaints work from the CRM side — categories, the call panel, SLAs — is on [the CRM's Complaints page](/docs/crm/complaints); this page covers what the ERP adds.

## The tabs and statuses

| Tab | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every complaint, grouped by status. |
| **Credit notes** | Complaints where the customer asked for a credit note. |
| **Without a credit note** | The rest, grouped by complaint type. |

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Requested** | Raised, nobody has decided it yet. |
| **Accepted** | The office accepted it. Now it is resolved — or, if a credit note was asked for, the credit note is issued. |
| **Rejected** | The office turned it down. |
| **Resolved** | Closed: resolved with a remark, or its credit note issued. |

## Raising a complaint

<Steps>
<Step title="Press Raise complaint">
**Name of salesman** is filled with your name if you are on the sales staff list; pick another if you are raising it for somebody.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the customer and the type">
The **Mobile number** fills from the customer. The **Complaint type** list is the same one the CRM uses.
</Step>
<Step title="Describe it">
Type or speak the **Complaint description** in the customer's words. Add a **Complaint picture** if you have one.
</Step>
<Step title="Say whether a credit note is wanted">
If **Credit note required** is **Yes**, pick the **Bill number** (the customer's bills of the last year), check the **Bill date** that fills in, and pick or type the **Description of goods** — the goods on that bill are suggested.
</Step>
<Step title="Raise">
*Complaint raised for Customer A · on their CRM record too, waiting for the office.* The photograph becomes the complaint's own attachment, opened by whoever can see the complaint.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Deciding a request

If your account can decide customer requests:

- **Accepted** — the request goes ahead. A credit-note request moves to *under review* in Accounts.
- **Rejected** — asks you to confirm, then closes it. A credit-note request is rejected too.

Both can be done to several requests at once from the list. Each decision is written to the complaint's history with your name, so the CRM shows who decided it in the ERP.

## Closing an accepted request

| Accepted request | What closes it | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Without a credit note** | **Resolve Complaint** — a **Remark** saying what was done, and the **Responsible employee**. | Somebody who can decide requests |
| **With a credit note** | **Issue Credit Note** — the **amount as it comes off the bill**, the **date**, the **credit note number**, a remark and the credit note file. | Accounts — the button says *Accounts issue credit notes — it comes off the bill in the ledger* to anybody else |

Issuing the credit note takes the amount off the customer's bill in Accounts, exactly as a credit note issued there would, marks the complaint resolved, and the credit note's amount comes off that order line's margin on [Orders](/docs/erp/orders). See [the CRM's Bills page](/docs/crm/bills) and [Outstanding](/docs/crm/outstanding) for what that does to what the customer owes.

**Add More Credit** on any complaint opens a new one for the same customer, type and bill — for a second credit note against the same bill.

## The trace beside a complaint

When the complaint helper is on (currently <Setting k="erp.ai.complaints.enabled" />), each complaint that names a bill shows:

- **The trace** — from the bill and the goods back through the lots dispatched, packing, filling, the SFG batch, the raw-material lots, the suppliers and their tests. A link it cannot find is named.
- **A batch notice** — when <Setting k="erp.ai.complaints.clusterThreshold" /> or more complaints within <Setting k="erp.ai.complaints.clusterDays" /> days trace to the same lot, which customers they came from and which lots they share. It is a reason to look, not a verdict.
- **Who made it** — the people who made or dispatched what was traced, to help name the responsible employee.
- **This customer's earlier complaints** — up to five, with their status and any credit note.

## Suggest type and summary

**Suggest type and summary** reads the description and suggests one of the existing complaint types (never a new one) and a one-line English summary. Nothing changes until you open **Review the suggestion**, check it against the description shown beside it, and press **Confirm** — or **Reject the suggestion**. A confirmed type is the complaint's type in the CRM too; the summary is shown on the complaint here. The helper never suggests a credit-note amount.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I raised a complaint in the CRM. Is it here?">
Yes. It is one record, whichever app raised it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Issue Credit Note is greyed out.">
Credit notes are issued by Accounts, because the credit note comes off the bill in the ledger. Ask somebody in Accounts to issue it here or on their own screen.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The bill I want is not in the list.">
Only the customer's bills of the last year are offered.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The bill date says 'Not dispatched yet'.">
The bill has no date in MahekOne yet.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why does the trace say 'This complaint names no bill'?">
It was raised without a credit note, so no bill was asked for, and there is nothing to trace from.
</Faq>
<Faq q="There is an alert saying a credit note has not been issued.">
A credit-note request was accepted some days ago and Accounts have not issued it yet. It clears itself once it is issued.
</Faq>
