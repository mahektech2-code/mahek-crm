The screen is three list modules from one factory, `complaintList("requests" | "issueCn" | "complaints")`, in `screens/complaints.ts`. It has no table of its own: it reads `complaints` joined to `customers` and `bills`, raises through `createComplaint` (the CRM's writer), decides by updating `complaints` and appending `complaint_status_history`, and issues credit notes through `issueCreditNote` in the Accounts service. The trace panel is `trace.ts`; the AI helper is `ai-complaints.ts`; the form's derived fields are in `calcs/logistics.ts`.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Write complaints only through the CRM's paths">
`createComplaint` to raise, the status plus `complaint_status_history` to decide, `issueCreditNote` for money. A shortcut that updates `cn_amount` directly would put a credit note on a complaint that never came off a bill.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep the power and the capability apart">
Deciding is the ERP's `decideRequests`; issuing is Accounts' `creditnote.issue`. Folding one into the other is how the person chasing a customer ends up crediting them.
</Step>
<Step title="Watch the cost of assist">
`assist` traces every row with a bill on every load. A long complaint list is one trace query chain per row.
</Step>
</Steps>
