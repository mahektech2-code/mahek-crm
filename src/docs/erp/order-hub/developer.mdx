## Where it lives

- **Rules, pure:** `src/lib/erp/engines/order-hub.ts` (who makes which check, when the order number is made, the gates, hold departments and reasons) and `src/lib/erp/engines/mrp.ts` (the MRP arithmetic). Tests beside each.
- **Writes:** `src/lib/erp/order-hub.ts` — `createIntake`, `editIntake`, `checkIntake` (the second check calls `makeOrder` in the same transaction), `sendBackIntake`, `holdIntake`, `releaseIntake`, `cancelIntake`.
- **Screens:** `src/lib/erp/screens/order-hub.ts` — the Order Hub (`orderHub`, `hubCorrection`, `hubDone`), `mrpCheck`, `toHold`, and `holdOrder` / `releaseOrderHold` for a desk's hold.
- **Accounts:** `holdOrderForAccounts`, `releaseOrderForAccounts` and `heldOrders` in `src/lib/services/order-approval-service.ts`; the queue leaves out TOs and held orders.
- **The three doors:** the ERP's New order form (`saveOrder` in `screens/sales.ts`), the TeleCRM save (`interaction-service.ts`) and the Salesman App (`handleOrder` in `actions/mbos.ts`) all call `createIntake`.

## Counted once

A TeleCRM or Salesman App order already has its `orders` row. The TO points at it (`origin_order_id`); `makeOrder` sets its `external_ref` to `ERP-<order no>`, so `syncBookOrders` adopts that row rather than writing a second. Until then the row stays `pending_approval` (never `captured`, which counts as a sale) and the Accounts queue skips it.
