Every order starts as a **TO number** (a temporary order) wherever it was taken: on a TeleCRM call, on the Salesman App, or typed into the ERP from WhatsApp or a phone call. **Accounts** approve it, and that makes its **order number**. The Production Head then clears it against materials and capacity before anything is made.

## The flow

1. **An order is punched.** Before it is saved, MahekOne checks whether the same order was already punched today: the same billing party, the same delivery party and the same items. If it was, a popup shows both orders side by side and asks **Same order - don't create** or **Different order - create it** (with a reason).
2. **TO number.** The order lands as a TO, with where it came from and who took it. Accounts are told.
3. **Accounts approve it** in **Accounts › Order approvals**. They check what was entered, the customer and their credit, and can change the lines (product, cans, rate, discount) for this order only. They **Approve** (this makes the order number and sends the customer "Confirmed"), **Send back** (to whoever took it, with the reason), **Hold**, or **Decline** with a reason.
4. **MRP check.** The Production Head sees what the order needs against what is on the shelf, ticks that the floor can make it, and clears it - or holds it.
5. **Production.** A cleared order joins Under Process and the day's plan, and goes on as before: Ready, Bill it, invoice check, dispatch, Logistics.

## The Order Hub

- **Waiting for Accounts** - every TO Accounts have not decided yet. **Sent back** - TOs Accounts returned for correction. **Decided recently** - approved, declined and cancelled TOs.
- **⋯** has **Edit TO** and **Cancel TO**. Editing a TO sends it back to Accounts.
- A TO marked **Possible duplicate** came from a handset with no signal and matched an order already punched; Accounts decide it.

## Editing an order

Until it is billed, the **Production Head** can change an order from **Orders › Edit order**: products, cans, rates, discounts, the parties, the transporter and the dispatch day, and add or take off a line. Nothing is approved again. A line whose product changes, is taken off or whose cans drop gives its allocated lots and scanned boxes back to the shelf; a Ready line the shelf no longer covers goes back to Under Process. Once billed it is locked - **Undo billing** unlocks it.

Whoever took the order can change it from TeleCRM › My orders or the Salesman App only until Accounts approve it.

## Holds

| Held by | Release |
|---|---|
| Accounts (a TO) | back to Accounts › Order approvals |
| Accounts (an order) | back to Accounts › Order approvals |
| Production (MRP check › On hold) | clears it: straight to Under Process |
| Office | back to where its lines were |

A production hold is internal unless **Tell the customer** is ticked; an internal hold never shows on the customer's tracking page.
