Production turns raw material into boxes on a shelf in three steps, each on its own screen: **SFG batches** mix raw-material lots into semi-finished liquid, **FG filling** fills approved liquid into cans or drums, and **Packing batches** put loose cans into boxes. **Recipes** say what one batch should take. Each step can only use what the step before it put in stock at the same godown.

An SFG batch blends raw-material lots into one lot of semi-finished liquid. One line per raw-material lot consumed.

<Steps>
<Step title="New SFG batch">
**Date**, **Godown** (your working location), **SFG product** and **Number of batches**. If the product has a recipe, its raw materials appear as lines with their quantity per batch, in litres.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the lot for each line">
**Raw item** offers only items with stock at this godown — cans are never consumed here. **Raw-material lot** offers that item's lots at the godown, with what is **Available (litres)**.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the quantities">
**Quantity per batch** × batches = **Total use incl. batches**. Enter any **Litres adjusted (loss)**; **Available SFG** is what reaches stock.
</Step>
<Step title="Save batch">
"SFG 12 saved · 406 Ltr of Nano Thinner in stock · lot 12SDSOA1230 waits for QC before it can be filled."
</Step>
</Steps>

If a lot has less than a line needs, the save is refused with "Low Stock!" — including when two lines of the same save draw on the same lot. **Add another** adds another lot to the same batch, **Show the whole SFG** lists only that batch's lines, **Print SFG label** prints the lot's 80 × 80 mm label, and **Trace this lot** opens [Traceability](/docs/erp/traceability).

### SFG QC

Somebody with the QC power opens a line and presses **QC approve** or **QC reject**. Both ask, for each test on the Testing list, the same evidence as Lot testing — a photo per test, the smell, the pH and density readings, a video — and a note. The readings sit on one row and every photograph is a tile: tap a tile to take or upload its picture, and × takes it off. None of it is required; a reject still needs the reason it failed. What was recorded shows in the line's record under QC evidence. On an approved lot the reject button reads **Withdraw QC approval (reject)**: it stops any further filling from it; what was already filled stays traceable to it. A rejected lot is flagged "Failed SFG QC".

The same decision can also be made on the Mahek Factory phone, by Mixing & Blending. There each test on the Testing list is answered **Pass** or **Fail**: approving needs every test answered and every one a Pass, and a reject needs the reason.

To decide several at once, tick the lines — a ticked line stands for its whole batch, **Select all** on a batch's header takes the batch, the box at the top takes the page, and **Select all** on the bar at the foot takes everything the search and filters leave — then press **QC approve** or **QC reject** on that bar. A batch already at that verdict is skipped and counted. Only somebody with the QC power sees the boxes.

## FG filling

<Steps>
<Step title="New FG filling">
**Date** and **Godown**, then **SFG product** and **SFG lot code** — only lots QC has approved, with stock at this godown.
</Step>
<Step title="What it is filled into">
**FG product**, **Can size (litres)** and **Can use** — the can or drum. Leave Can use blank for a **Naket** fill, which takes no packing item.
</Step>
<Step title="How many">
**Cans or drums filled**, and **Cans adjusted (lost)**. You see the litres it uses, the **FG cans available** that reach stock, and the **Finished lot code**.
</Step>
<Step title="Save filling">
"Filled 120 × 5 L · lot FG14NA · 118 cans in stock".
</Step>
</Steps>

| Refusal | Means |
|---|---|
| Low SFG Stock | The lot does not hold the litres the fill uses |
| Low Packing Quantity | No cans (or drums) of that packing item are available |
| Minus Quantity Not Allowed | Cans must be above zero |
| SFG lot … is waiting for QC approval / was rejected at QC | Only an approved lot can be filled |

On a filling: **Add to this run** adds another fill under the same FG number, **Edit cans adjusted** corrects the cans lost (refused if those cans were already packed or moved), **Trace this lot** opens [Traceability](/docs/erp/traceability).

## Packing batches

A packing batch puts loose cans into boxes of one boxed SKU. It may draw its cans from more than one FG lot, one line per lot.

<Steps>
<Step title="New packing batch">
**Date**, **Godown**, **FG product**, the boxed SKU (**Description of goods**) and **Boxes in the whole batch**. **Total cans** = boxes × cans per box.
</Step>
<Step title="The first lot">
Pick the **Finished lot** and how many **Cans from this lot** — blank takes as many as the batch still needs.
</Step>
<Step title="Add another until the cans match">
"FP40NA saved · 24 cans still to draw from another lot". Add another takes the next lot.
</Step>
<Step title="Complete">
When the cans used match the boxes exactly: "FP40NA complete · 50 boxes of … in packing stock, each with its own box id · print their labels from the batch". **Print box labels** is now the batch's main button; **Trace this batch** and **Show the whole batch** sit beside it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="rule" title="A batch posts only when the cans match">
Until the lines draw exactly boxes × cans per box, the batch's boxes are **not** in packing stock, and the dashboard counts it under "Incomplete packing batches". The empty boxes must be at the godown on the first line: "Low Box Quantity · … needed, … at …".
</Callout>

## Recipes

A recipe is what **one batch** of an SFG product takes, in litres, of each raw material.

- **Set a recipe** adds an SFG product's raw materials and litres per batch; **Change quantity** and **Remove from recipe** are on each line.
- **Start a batch** on a recipe line opens a new SFG batch with the recipe's lines already listed — you still pick each lot.
- A batch line that used more than the recipe allows (plus a tolerance) is flagged **Above the recipe** on the SFG batches list. Nothing is refused.
- Setting, changing and removing a recipe line is for ERP managers. Everybody holding the screen can read it.

## What the codes mean

| Code | Example | Made of |
|---|---|---|
| SFG lot | `12SDSOA1230` | The SFG number and the batch's first raw-material lot |
| FG lot | `FG14NA` | "FG", the FG number and the godown's first two letters |
| Packing batch | `FP40NA` | "FP", the batch serial and the godown's first two letters |
| Box id | `BX-261006-000125` | One per box, printed in the box's QR |

## Common questions

<Faq q="My SFG lot is not offered on FG filling.">
It is waiting for QC, it was rejected, or it has no stock at the godown you picked.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The packing batch is saved but its boxes are not in stock.">
The cans drawn do not yet equal boxes × cans per box. Add another from another lot until they match.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why can't I see rates or costing on these screens?">
Purchase rate, costing, SFG rate and FG costing need the cost-and-margin power.
</Faq>

<Faq q="I need to delete a batch line.">
Only an ERP administrator can. For an SFG line or a filling, the stock entry it made stays in the log, flagged, so the change is visible; deleting a packing line takes its batch's boxes back out of stock.
</Faq>
