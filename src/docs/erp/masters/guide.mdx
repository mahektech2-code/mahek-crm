The Masters are the lists every other ERP screen picks from: what the business buys, who it buys from, what it makes, who it sells to, where it keeps stock, and the small value lists behind every drop-down. Get these right and every form further on fills itself in; get one wrong and every record that names it carries the mistake.

<Callout kind="note">
Several of these are not the ERP's own lists. **Sales parties** are MahekOne's customers and **Products** are the catalogue's SKUs. The ERP adds what it needs beside them — so a change you make here is seen in the CRM and Accounts too.
</Callout>

### The fields

| Field | What to put | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Raw item** | The name everybody uses | Must be unique, whatever its capitals |
| **Item code** | A short code | It becomes part of every lot number bought of this item |
| **Item unit** | Kg, Litre or Unit | Unit is for things counted in pieces |
| **Material type** | Chemical, Can, Drum, Box, Stationary… | From the Material type reference list |
| **Density (kg to litre)** | Chemicals only, between 0.1 and 3 | Turns kilograms bought into litres in stock |
| **Testing list** | Chemicals only, at least one test | Every lot of it must pass these before it reaches stock |
| **Price (₹)** | Only if you can see purchase money | Used as the packing rate for cans and drums, and as the rate when **Create PO** orders this item on a requirement — an item with no price is ordered at ₹0 |
| **Purchase rule** | Direct purchase, Quotation, or Buyer decision | Chemicals start on Quotation, everything else on Direct |
| **Default vendor** | For Direct or Buyer decision | Given to a direct-purchase requirement when it is raised, and offered first on Create PO |
| **Remark** | Anything worth knowing | Has a microphone if voice notes are on |

### Can photos and can groups

A **Can** row has a **Photos** action: up to three pictures of that can — front, back, the lid, whatever tells it apart. A photo may be up to 20 MB, so a full-size phone picture goes straight up. The record shows them, with its group's photos beside them.

The **Can groups** tab gathers look-alike cans, one print in several sizes, and shows each group as a card with its photo.

- **Group cans by name** puts every can that is not in a group into one, read from its name. "Packing Material 1 liter Mahek Yellow" and "Mahek Yellow 5 Ltr" land together. It never moves a can somebody already placed, and it lists the cans it could not place.
- **Edit group** changes the name, the cans (ticking a can from another group moves it here) and up to three group photos, each up to 20 MB.
- **Rename** changes only the name. **Delete group** keeps its cans, just not grouped.
- A single can can also be moved from its own **Edit** form, under **Can group**.

## Purchase parties

Suppliers, grouped by state. **Party name** and **Own party code** are required; the code is part of every lot number bought from them, so choose it once and keep it. The record shows a call and WhatsApp button and how many purchases they have. **Retire** a party instead of deleting it: existing purchases keep the name, and new receipts stop offering it.

## Sales parties

MahekOne's customers, as the ERP sees them, grouped by status.

| Status | What it means | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | Created but not yet verified by an admin or the office | Orders can be taken but not billed until approved |
| **Active** | Verified | Orders move normally |
| **Deactive** | Closed | Closed in the CRM too — it is the same customer |

### Adding a customer

The ERP does not add customers. Open the account in **Accounts → Customers → Add customer**: it is created as a customer straight away, with its salesperson, credit days and price list, and is on this list as **Active** the moment it is saved. Anybody without Accounts asks the Accounts desk.

**Activate**, **Deactivate** and **Mark pending** stay here for somebody with the status power. **Activate** asks "Are You Sure! This Party Is Verified By Admin".

**Edit details** changes the party name, grade, area, location, state, transporter, payment type (Paid / To Pay), delivery type (Door / Godown), weight type, contacts, counter types, credit days, segment, standing instructions, GST number, company name and who the party is allocated to. Emptying the city or the mobile keeps what was there, because the CRM needs both.

**Renaming a party** changes the name on the bill and on every list, in the CRM and Accounts too. The Sales Party and order sheets still find the party under the name they use, which the record shows as "Name on the sheets"; new orders under that name land on the same party, and the sheet never writes the old name back. A name another party already has is refused.

**The dropdowns** offer what is set on Settings → Reference lists and every value already in use on the party book, so Segment, Counter type and Transport detail are never empty while customers carry them. A party's saved value is always offered, so saving never blanks it.

## Godowns

Every godown by region, with the address, map pin and the people assigned to it. Only an ERP administrator registers, edits, switches off a godown or assigns its employees.

- **Assign employees** decides who may choose it as their working location. Only people who hold the ERP are listed.
- **Latitude and longitude** are the map pin. Without one, the ERP cannot find the godown by where somebody is standing.
- **Item Lost Record**, where written-off stock goes, is not listed here. It still appears on a transfer form for whoever holds the write-off power — see [Stock](/docs/erp/stock).

## Reference lists

The editable value lists every form picks from: material type, testing list, area, state, transporter, segment, counter type, complaint type, expense category and particular, credit note fund reference, zone, godown city, video search tag, short label names (Item=Label), payment terms, scrap category, and the consignment number name (LR, GR, CN…).

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a value">
Open the list and press **Add a value**. It is offered on every form at once.
</Step>
<Step title="Retire a value">
"Records that already carry it keep it; new forms stop offering it." Nothing is ever deleted.
</Step>
<Step title="Restore a value">
Brings a retired value back onto the forms.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warn">
Some words are not reference lists, because a rule depends on their exact spelling: the item units (Kg, Litre, Unit), box types, payment types, delivery types, weight types, grades (A+ to C) and regions. They cannot be changed from here.
</Callout>

## Common questions

<Faq q="It says 'Duplicate Entry!' but I cannot see the item.">
The name exists already, perhaps with different capitals, or retired. Switch the chip to Inactive and restore it rather than creating a second one.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why can't I see the Price column?">
It needs the purchase-money power. The list says which columns it withheld.
</Faq>

<Faq q="A new customer's order is stuck.">
The customer is Pending. Somebody with the status power must activate it, or — once the ERP takes the orders — Accounts approves the order from their queue.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I delete a godown?">
No. Mark it Inactive. Its stock history stays where it is.
</Faq>
