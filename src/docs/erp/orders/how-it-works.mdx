The Orders screen is the factory's half of a sale. The CRM and the field team decide **that** a customer wants goods; this screen decides **which goods, from which lots, on which bill, leaving on which day**. Every rule below exists so that an order is checked before it is made, a line cannot claim stock that is not there, a bill cannot leave before somebody independent has checked it, and goods cannot leave without each box being checked.

The flow follows Mahek's Order-to-Delivery process (PRD v1.2): the order is entered, verified by the back office, made, billed in Tally, checked by an invoice checker, stickered, scanned and dispatched, then handed to [Logistics](/docs/erp/transport). How these lines become MahekOne's order book — what the CRM, targets and outstanding read — is explained on [The ERP and MahekOne's order book](/docs/erp/order-book).

## The tabs, one step each

| Tab | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **To verify** | Orders not cancelled, not billed, whose back-office check is Pending or sent back for correction. |
| **Production** | Lines **Under Process** or **Ready** and not yet billed. |
| **Ready to bill** | Ready lines of verified orders, not yet billed — the material waiting for its bill. |
| **Invoice check** | Billed orders whose invoice is waiting for the checker, or was rejected. |
| **Dispatch stickers** | Lines under process or ready, or billed, whose goods have not left: what the stickers are printed for. |
| **Billing & dispatch** | Every billed line: the invoice check, the dispatch, the vehicle, the bill's figures. |
| **On hold** | What the office is holding — Hold From Office, or a status nobody has worked yet. |
| **Batch codes** | Every lot allocated to a line. |
| **All lines** | The register: every line, by order date. |

## Separate status words

<Rule title="Order, production, billing, dispatch and logistics are each their own word">
A line carries several teams' answers at once, and none may overwrite another (PRD rule 4). Every list and record shows them side by side:

| Domain | Values |
|---|---|
| **Order** | Verification Pending, Correction Required, Verified, On Hold, Cancelled, Closed (delivered) |
| **Production** | Under Process, Ready — and nothing else. There is no *Partially Ready*. |
| **Billing** | Invoice Pending, Verification Pending, Approved, Correction Required |
| **Dispatch** | Pending, Scanning / Verification Pending, Ready to Dispatch, Dispatched |
| **Logistics** | Logistics Pending, In Transit, Delivery Pending Confirmation, Delivered / Closed, Exception — once the goods have left |

Production completing marks nothing billed, and dispatch closes nothing in Logistics.
</Rule>

<Callout kind="note" title="How a status is stored">
**Under Process**, **Ready**, **Hold From Office** and **Cancel** still live in one column, because the godown, the order book and the Factory app read it. Under Process and Ready are shown as Production; Hold From Office and Cancel are shown as the Order's *On Hold* and *Cancelled*.
</Callout>

## Taking an order

<Rule title="Every order says how it came">
**Order received by** is required: WhatsApp, Phone, Sales Order or Other. With it the order keeps who entered it and when, the dispatch date the customer asked for (**Dispatch on**) and their instructions (**Remark / customer instructions**).
</Rule>

<Rule title="A likely duplicate is asked about, not refused">
An order for the same billing party, on the same order date, with exactly the same goods in the same quantities as one already taken is stopped with *Order 1234 for this party on this day has exactly these goods*. Answering **Not a duplicate** saves it. A different day is a reorder and different goods are a second order, so neither is asked about.
</Rule>

## The back office verifies the order

<Rule title="Production starts on a verified order">
Somebody holding *Verify orders (back office)* checks the entered order against what the customer asked for, then presses **Verify order** or **Send back for correction** with what needs correcting. The person who took the order is told. **Ready** is refused until the order is verified: *order 1256 is not verified by the back office yet*.
</Rule>

<Rule title="A material change goes back to the back office">
Before billing, changing a verified order's quantity, delivery party, rate or discount, or adding or deleting a line, puts the order back to Verification Pending, and the check history says what changed. Correcting an order that was sent back sends it to verify again. A remark or a transporter is not a material change.
</Rule>

## Ready means the goods are there

<Rule title="Ready is refused without stock">
A line is marked Ready only where its godown holds what it still needs — **boxes** from packing stock for a boxed SKU, **cans** from FG stock for a loose one — after subtracting what every line **already Ready and not yet allocated** will take. Lines marked Ready together are judged in order, each spending what it needs, so two lines can never both claim the last box.
</Rule>

When every live line of an order is Ready, the back office is told the order is **Ready to bill**.

<Rule title="A status moves either way">
Until a line is dispatched it can be put at any status, back as well as forward. Ready needs the order verified and the stock on the shelf; **Cancel** needs the line unbilled and its batch codes released. When several lines are moved together, the ones that pass move and the rest are named.
</Rule>

<Callout kind="why" title="Dispatch on is a date, not a status">
The source system had *Today*, *Tomorrow* and *Delay* as statuses. Each line now carries **Dispatch on**, and the lists say *Dispatch today*, *Dispatch tomorrow* or *Dispatch late* from it.
</Callout>

## Prices

<Rule title="The rate comes from the price list the Accounts app applies to the billing party">
Which list applies is decided exactly as the Accounts app decides it: the narrowest list for the customer, their salesman, beat, area, city, district, state, customer type, or everybody. The rate is the SKU's flat row on that list, or a row for another SKU of the same finished good; a dash is no rate. It is the **ex-GST** rate, read as of today.
</Rule>

<Rule title="A list's discounts are shown, never applied">
Every discount a list carries depends on something, so the form prints them under **List discounts** and the discount on a line is only what somebody typed.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Somebody who cannot see the rate cannot set it">
Rate is offered only to holders of *See sales rate*, Discount only to *See sales amounts*. For anybody else the list decides the rate and no discount is given.
</Rule>

<Callout kind="money" title="Freight on a line is what the customer is charged">
A line's **Freight charged to the customer** is added to the bill. It is not the transport payment, which Logistics records against the vehicle trip and shares out by litres — see [Logistics](/docs/erp/transport).
</Callout>

## Allocation: setting the stock aside

<Rule title="An allocation takes stock the moment it is written">
Each **batch code** row says "this many boxes (or cans) of this lot are this line's", written under the lot's lock. A line is **Done**, **Add More Quantity** or **Remove Some Quantity** against what it needs.
</Rule>

Allocation happens at the [Dispatch desk](/docs/erp/dispatch), by scanning boxes. Releasing an allocation gives the stock back; it is refused once the line is billed and while boxes are scanned against it.

## One order, one complete bill

<Rule title="Bill it bills the whole order">
One order number is one Tally invoice (PRD rule 1). **Bill it** needs:

1. the order **verified** by the back office;
2. **every line not cancelled Ready**;
3. a **rate** on every line;
4. approval, if the billing party was Pending.

It asks for the **Tally invoice no.** and **invoice date**, writes them on every live line and bills them in one transaction. A billed order takes no more lines.
</Rule>

<Rule title="One invoice number is one order">
An invoice number already on another order is refused, naming that order. Numbers are compared without case or spaces, so *mmi/26-27/ 1119* is *MMI/26-27/1119*.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Goods that go later go on a new order">
A line that will not leave with this bill is **moved to a new order** — the whole line, or part of it in whole boxes, as long as it has no batch codes. The new order number is linked both ways: the new line says *Moved out of order 1256*, the old order says *Continued on order 1260*. There is no partly-ready order.
</Rule>

**Undo billing** takes the whole order back out of billing until it is dispatched; its invoice check goes with it. **Change invoice** changes the number or date on a billed order.

## The independent invoice check

<Rule title="Somebody else checks the invoice">
Somebody holding *Check invoices before dispatch* compares the invoice with the order — customer, order number, goods, quantities, invoice number, rate and discount — then presses **Approve invoice** or **Reject invoice** with a reason. Nobody checks a bill they entered themselves, unless they are an ERP administrator: *You entered this bill, so somebody else checks it.* The checker is told of every new bill; whoever billed is told of a rejection.
</Rule>

<Rule title="A change after the check sends it back">
After approval or rejection, a change to the invoice number or date, a rate, a discount or the GST puts the invoice back to Verification Pending, and the checker is told what changed. A remark or a transporter is not looked at by the checker and does not.
</Rule>

Every step — sent to check, verified, sent back, approved, rejected, back to check — is appended to the order's **Check history**, shown on its record with who and when.

## Dispatch stickers

<Rule title="The dispatch sticker carries four things, and only after approval">
One sticker for every box or can: the **customer** (the delivery party), the **product**, the **invoice number** and the **order number** — nothing else, and no lot or box code. Those are on the box's own label, made by the box-id system, and the two never share a sticker. Stickers are printed from the moment a line is under process or ready — before billing, with the invoice number left blank — until the goods leave. **Stickers are on** records that they were applied; it is not required to dispatch.
</Rule>

## Confirm dispatch

<Rule title="An order leaves whole, once it is billed">
**Confirm dispatch** asks for the **dispatch date**, the **vehicle / tempo / truck number**, the **driver or transporter** and any **remark or exception**. It dispatches every billed line of the order together, and refuses while the invoice was rejected by the checker (a pending check holds nothing), while any line of the order is not on the bill, or while any line still has boxes to scan (setting: <Setting k="erp.dispatch.requireScan" />). The date may not be before the order date.
</Rule>

Confirming sets the lines **Verified** and **Dispatched**, turns every scanned box **dispatched**, freezes the lots, and puts the order on one record on [Logistics](/docs/erp/transport). Bills recorded with the same vehicle on the same day join one **trip** there.

## The customer's tracking link

<Rule title="Verified is confirmed, and the customer is told">
When the back office verifies an order (and Accounts have approved a new customer), the order gets **one tracking link** and the customer gets the confirmation: order number, date, each product with its quantity, and the link. Their page then follows the order — preparing, ready, invoice available, dispatched, with the transporter, in transit, delivered — showing only steps somebody actually recorded, each with its time. A step saved twice is told once.
</Rule>

<Rule title="A hold says why, in words the customer may read">
**Put order on hold** asks for a reason, the sentence the customer sees (filled from the reason) and an internal note they never see. Every line not yet dispatched goes to Hold From Office; **Resolve hold** puts each back. After dispatch, a delay is reported on Logistics instead.
</Rule>

<Rule title="The invoice PDF reaches the customer only once it is approved">
Bill it takes the Tally invoice as a PDF (or **Invoice PDF** adds it later). The customer can open it from their link once the invoice check approves it — never a delivery party's link, which shows no invoice.
</Rule>

The order's **Customer tracking** tab shows the link, what the customer sees now, the hold, and every message with Copy and Open WhatsApp for those waiting to be sent by hand.

## The bill's figures

| Figure | How it is worked out |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | cans × rate — or litres × rate on a drum line |
| **Discounted amount** | amount × discount % |
| **Final amount** | (amount − discount + freight charged) plus GST on that |
| **Company** | 18% GST bills from Mahek Marketing India; 0% from Mylac |
| **Lot-code costing** | average cost per can (per litre on a drum) of the allocated lots × cans, plus extra expenses |
| **Margin** | amount − discount − lot costing − credit notes (taken back to before GST) |
| **Monthly sale** | the customer's amount across every line dispatched in the same month |
| **Monthly target** | the customer's target from the CRM's [Monthly targets](/docs/crm/targets) |
| **Due date** | dispatch date + the customer's credit days |

## The WhatsApp inbox

The inbox turns a customer's message into a **draft**, never an order: the customer, each SKU and the quantity in cans are decided by rules after a model reads the words. A draft becomes an order only through the normal order form, with every rule above. It needs <Setting k="erp.ai.orders.enabled" />, a provider key, and to be under <Setting k="erp.ai.orders.monthlyCap" /> readings a month. More is on [Alerts and the AI helpers](/docs/erp/alerts-and-ai).

## Where the code and the intent disagree today

- **Hold and Cancel are stored as statuses.** They are shown as the order domain, but live in the same column as Under Process and Ready, so the Factory app and the order book read them as before.
- **Orders already in the ERP when the checks arrived read as passed.** Every line and bill that existed before migration 0285 reads *Verified* and *Approved*, with nobody named, so orders in flight were not held.
- **On hold is the old Pending tab.** It still lists what is neither Under Process nor Ready nor cancelled, and still hides a whole order once any line of it is billed.
- **Lines copied in from the Taken Order tab look like any other line.** They carry `source = sheet`, and the order book never writes them, but the tabs draw them unmarked.
- **A line marked Not Verify can never be dispatched again.** Confirm dispatch accepts only a blank or Pending verification; the way back is to undo billing and bill again.
- **The "Ready but not billed" alert dates Ready from the line's last change**, not from when it became Ready.
- **Ready to bill lists lines, not whole orders.** A Ready line of a verified order is listed even when another line of the same order is still Under Process; Bill it then names what holds the bill.
