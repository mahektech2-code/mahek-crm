The **Orders** screen follows a customer's order from the moment it is taken to the moment its bill leaves the godown. Each tab is one step: what is still pending, what is being prepared and is ready, what has been billed and is waiting to be dispatched, which lots went on which order, the label list, every line ever taken, and the WhatsApp messages the ERP read as possible orders.

How these orders then reach MahekOne's order book — the CRM, targets and outstanding — is on its own page: [The ERP and MahekOne's order book](/docs/erp/order-book).

## The tabs

| Tab | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Every order that has not reached billing yet — not cancelled, not an empty-drum line. Grouped by date and order number. Once any line of an order is billed, the whole order leaves this tab. |
| **Under process & ready** | Lines that are **Under Process** or **Ready** and not yet billed. This is the working list for the office. |
| **Billing & dispatch** | Lines that have been billed. Here you set the dispatch date and mark them **Verified** once the goods leave. |
| **Batch codes** | Every lot set aside for an order line, and how many. |
| **Labels** | The same lines as *Under process & ready*, laid out for the label maker and downloadable. Read only. |
| **All lines** | Every order line ever taken, whatever its state. |
| **WhatsApp inbox** | Messages the ERP read as possible orders, each with a drafted order you can accept. |

## Taking an order

<Steps>
<Step title="Press New order">
Pick the **Date**, the **Godown** it leaves from and the **Billing party**. A deactivated party is not offered.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the delivery party, if it is different">
Leave **Delivery party** blank to deliver to the billing party. **Area** and **Standing instructions** fill in from the delivery party. Leave **Transporter** blank to use the delivery party's usual transporter.
</Step>
<Step title="Set Dispatch on">
The day it should leave the godown. The lists then say **Dispatch today**, **Dispatch tomorrow** or **Dispatch late** from this date — you never have to update it by hand.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a line per SKU">
Pick the **Description of goods** and type the **Order qty** in **cans**. For a boxed SKU the **Box quantity** is worked out for you, and it must be a whole number of boxes — otherwise it reads *INVALID — 12 cans a box* and the order will not save.
</Step>
<Step title="Rate and discount, if you can see them">
If your account can see the sales rate, a **Rate** box and the **Price-list rate** appear. Leave the rate blank to take the price list's. The same goes for **Discount %**. If you cannot see them, the price list decides both.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
You are told the order number and how many lines were saved. If a line has no rate, the message says so: *1 without a rate: none on price list "…"* — a line with no rate cannot be billed until somebody adds one.
</Step>
</Steps>

To add another SKU to an order already saved, open any of its lines and press **ADD More**. The order number, date, godown and parties stay as they were.

<Callout kind="note" title="Orders from a Pending customer">
An order whose billing party is still **Pending** is marked *Pending customer* and cannot be billed until it is approved. Who approves it depends on whether the ERP is where orders are taken — see [The ERP and MahekOne's order book](/docs/erp/order-book).
</Callout>

## From Under Process to Ready

A new line starts **Under Process**. Press **Ready** when the goods are on the shelf.

<Callout kind="rule" title="Ready is refused without stock">
The ERP only marks a line Ready if the godown holds the boxes (or cans) it needs, **after** what the lines already Ready and not yet allocated will take. If it does not, you see: *Stock is not there: order 1256 · Nano Thinner – 1 Liter at Godown A needs 3 boxes, 1 in stock. Pack or move the stock first, then mark it Ready.* When you mark several lines Ready together, those that have stock are marked and the rest are named.
</Callout>

**Under Process** takes a Ready line back. The other statuses — **Hold From Office** and **Cancel** — are set from **Edit**.

## Allocating lots

Before a line can be billed, the exact lots it will leave from must be set aside — this is the **allocation**. A lot allocated to an order takes that stock the moment it is written, so nobody else can promise it.

Lots are allocated **at the Dispatch desk, by scanning the boxes**. Each box scanned onto a line sets aside one unit of its lot. For stock packed before box labels existed, the desk offers **Allocate a lot by hand**. See [The dispatch desk](/docs/erp/dispatch).

The **Batch codes** tab lists every allocation. From a row you can:

- **Change quantity** — not below the boxes already scanned against that lot, not above what the line still needs, and not above the lot's stock.
- **Release** — gives the stock back to the lot. Refused once the line is billed (*Undo its billing first*) or while boxes are scanned against it (take them off at the Dispatch desk first).

## Billing a line

Once the office has entered the bill in Tally, press **Bill it**. It needs, in this order:

1. approval, if the customer is Pending;
2. status **Ready**;
3. lots allocated in full (*Can quantity verification* reads **Done**);
4. a **Tally bill no.** (set it from **Edit**);
5. a **rate**.

If anything is missing, hovering the button says exactly what: *Needs a Tally bill no., a rate.* A billed line moves to **Billing & dispatch** and is marked *In order details*.

**Undo billing** takes a billed line back, as long as it has not been dispatch-verified.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Do Verified** | Asks for the **dispatch date** and marks the line **Verified** and **Dispatched**. Needs the lots allocated in full and every box scanned. The date cannot be before the order date. The first verified line of a bill opens its record on [Transport](/docs/erp/transport). |
| **Extra Expenses** | Records extra freight on the line, once. It is added to the line's cost. |
| **Change GST** | 18% or 0%, until the line is verified. 18% bills from *Mahek Marketing India*, 0% from *Mylac*. |
| **Not Verify** | ERP administrators only. Marks a line *Not Verify*. |
| **Dispatch stickers** | Prints a sticker for every box scanned onto the order. |
| **Scan its boxes / Dispatch desk** | Opens the order at the Dispatch desk. |
| **Trace this order** | Opens [Traceability](/docs/erp/traceability) on the order. |

Do Verified and Extra Expenses can also be done to a selection of lines at once.

### What the drawer shows

Besides the columns, a billed line shows the delivery party, litres dispatched, which company bills it, the **final amount** (with GST), the **lot-code costing** and **cost per can** (if you can see cost), the credit notes issued against it, **order fulfil days**, the customer's **credit days** and the **due date** that gives, the delivery party's segment and salesman, and the customer's **monthly target** from the CRM. The row is flagged *Sales target reached* when the customer's sales in the dispatch month meet that target.

## Labels

The **Labels** tab is a list for the label maker: every line being prepared, with its order id, parties, area, transporter, weight and **No of label** — one a box on a boxed line, one a can otherwise. Press download to take it as a file.

Printing the labels themselves — the QR labels on each box and the dispatch stickers — is done from the label sheet, reached from a packing batch, the Boxes & labels screen, or **Dispatch stickers** on a billed line.

<Steps>
<Step title="Read a message">
Choose **Paste a message** and paste (or speak) what the customer sent. If you know who it is, pick the **Customer**; if it came from a number, type the **Sender's number**. Or choose **Screen new WhatsApp replies** to read the replies that came in over the last few days. The replies are also screened every hour on their own.
</Step>
<Step title="Read what was drafted">
The message is read into a draft: the customer, and a line for each product mentioned with its quantity in cans. Anything the ERP could not decide is left for you: *Several SKUs fit "NC 1L" — choose one*, *No quantity was said*, or *no customer matched — pick one*. A message that is not an order is filed as **Not an order**.
</Step>
<Step title="Accept the draft">
**Accept the draft** opens the ordinary new-order form with the draft in it, the original message beside it and a note on how each line was read. Check every line against the message. Nothing is an order until you press **Create the order**. Saving closes the message as **Converted** with the order number.
</Step>
</Steps>

If drafting is switched off or has used up this month's allowance, the tab says so instead of offering the button.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I pressed Ready and it said the stock is not there, but I can see it on the Stock screen.">
Other lines already marked Ready at the same godown are counted first. If they will take what is on the shelf, your line has to wait until more is packed or moved in.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Bill it is greyed out.">
Hover it: it names everything still missing — approval, Ready, the allocation, the Tally bill number or the rate.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Where do I allocate lots?">
At the Dispatch desk, by scanning each box onto the order. For old stock with no box labels, the desk has **Allocate a lot by hand**.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Do Verified says 'Scan the boxes first'.">
At least one lot on the line has box ids, so every box has to be scanned at the Dispatch desk before the line can be verified.
</Faq>
<Faq q="An order disappeared from Pending but one of its lines is not billed.">
Pending hides a whole order once any of its lines is billed. The unbilled lines are still on **Under process & ready** and **All lines**.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I cannot change the quantity on a line.">
It has been billed. Undo billing first (only possible before it is dispatch-verified).
</Faq>
<Faq q="I cannot delete a line.">
A billed line has to have its billing undone first, and a line with lots allocated has to have them released first — they hold stock.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I don't see Rate or Discount columns.">
They need the *See sales rate* and *See sales amounts* powers. Without them the price list sets the rate and discount for you, and the list's toolbar says *2 columns hidden* — hover it to see which.
</Faq>
