The **Orders** screen follows a customer's order from the moment it is taken to the moment its bill leaves the godown. Each tab is one step of the flow: the back office verifies the order, production makes it Ready, the office bills it in Tally, an invoice checker approves the bill, the dispatch stickers go on, and the order is dispatched. Beside those are what the office is holding, which lots went on which order, every line ever taken, and the WhatsApp messages the ERP read as possible orders.

How these orders then reach MahekOne's order book — the CRM, targets and outstanding — is on its own page: [The ERP and MahekOne's order book](/docs/erp/order-book).

## The tabs

| Tab | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **To verify** | Orders the back office has not verified yet, or sent back for correction. Holders of *Verify orders* verify them here. |
| **Production** | Lines that are **Under Process** or **Ready** and not yet billed — the godown's working list. |
| **Ready to bill** | Ready lines of verified orders, not yet billed. The back office makes the bill in Tally from here. |
| **Invoice check** | Billed orders for the invoice checker — optional: a pending check holds nothing, only a rejected invoice holds dispatch. |
| **Dispatch stickers** | Every line under process or ready, billed or not, until its goods leave: print and apply their stickers. No invoice check needed. |
| **Billing & dispatch** | Every billed line, with its invoice check, dispatch, vehicle and the bill's figures. Dispatch is confirmed here. |
| **On hold** | Lines the office is holding — **Hold From Office** — that have not reached billing. |
| **Batch codes** | Every lot set aside for an order line, and how many. |
| **All lines** | Every order line ever taken, with its Order, Production, Billing, Dispatch and Logistics words. |
| **WhatsApp inbox** | Messages the ERP read as possible orders, each with a drafted order you can accept. |

## Taking an order

<Steps>
<Step title="Press New order">
Pick the **Date**, **Order received by** — *WhatsApp*, *Phone*, *Sales Order* or *Other* — the **Godown** it leaves from and the **Billing party**. A deactivated party is not offered.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the delivery party, if it is different">
Leave **Delivery party** blank to deliver to the billing party. **Area** and **Standing instructions** fill in from the delivery party. Leave **Transporter** blank to use the delivery party's usual transporter.
</Step>
<Step title="Set Dispatch on">
The day it should leave the godown — the date the customer asked for, if they gave one. The lists then say **Dispatch today**, **Dispatch tomorrow** or **Dispatch late** from this date — you never have to update it by hand.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a line per SKU">
Pick the **Description of goods** and type the **Order qty** in **cans**. For a boxed SKU the **Box quantity** is worked out for you, and it must be a whole number of boxes — otherwise it reads *INVALID — 12 cans a box* and the order will not save.
</Step>
<Step title="Rate and discount, if you can see them">
Picking the billing party fills in **Price list** — the list the Accounts app applies to that party, the same one its price-list screens show — or *None applies to this party*. Picking an SKU then fills its **Rate** from that list. Where the list has no rate for the SKU, or no list applies, the box stays empty: type the rate. You can type over a filled rate too. Changing the billing party fills the rates in again from the new party's list.

**List discounts** shows the list's discount terms, such as *3% off for advance payment*. None is applied for you, because each depends on how the party pays or how much it takes: type the **Discount %** the party has earned. If you cannot see the rate, the price list sets it and no discount is given.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
You are told the order number and that it is waiting for the back office to verify. If the same party already has an order today with exactly these goods, you are asked first: pick **Yes — it is a separate order** under **Not a duplicate** and save again. If a line has no rate, the message says so: *1 without a rate: none on Western line Paid — 1 Aug 2026*, or *no price list applies to the party* — a line with no rate cannot be billed until somebody adds one.
</Step>
</Steps>

To add another SKU to an order already saved, open any of its lines and press **Add another**. The order number, date, godown and parties stay as they were. A verified order goes back to the back office to verify again, and a billed order takes no more lines — take a new order.

## The back office verifies the order

Before production can mark anything Ready, somebody with the *Verify orders (back office)* power checks the order against what the customer asked for, on **To verify**:

- **Verify order** — the whole order is verified. You can tick several orders and verify them together.
- **Send back for correction** — say what is wrong. The person who took the order is told, and it shows *Order sent back for correction* until it is corrected. Correcting it sends it back to verify.

Changing a verified order's quantity, delivery party, rate or discount, or adding or deleting a line, sends it back to **To verify** with what changed. A remark or transporter does not. The record's **Check history** lists every verification, with who and when.

<Callout kind="note" title="Orders from a Pending customer">
An order whose billing party is still **Pending** is marked *Pending customer* and cannot be billed until it is approved. Who approves it depends on whether the ERP is where orders are taken — see [The ERP and MahekOne's order book](/docs/erp/order-book).
</Callout>

## Moving a line between statuses

A new line starts **Under Process**. Press **Ready** when the goods are on the shelf — once the back office has verified the order. Until then Ready says *The back office has not verified the order yet*. Production has only these two values; there is no *Partially Ready*. When every line of an order is Ready, the back office is told it is ready to bill.

Every line offers each status it is not at — **Ready**, **Under Process**, **Hold From Office** and **Cancel** — on its record and in the bulk bar, so a status can move back as well as forward. Two checks stay, because they protect stock: Ready needs the order verified and the stock (below), and **Cancel** needs the line unbilled and its batch codes released. A dispatch-verified line stays where it is. The same statuses can still be set from **Edit**.

<Callout kind="rule" title="Ready is refused without stock">
The ERP only marks a line Ready if the godown holds the boxes (or cans) it needs, **after** what the lines already Ready and not yet allocated will take. If it does not, you see: *Stock is not there: order 1256 · Nano Thinner – 1 Liter at Godown A needs 3 boxes, 1 in stock. Pack or move the stock first, then mark it Ready.* When you mark several lines Ready together, those that have stock are marked and the rest are named.
</Callout>

## Allocating lots

Before a line can be billed, the exact lots it will leave from must be set aside — this is the **allocation**. A lot allocated to an order takes that stock the moment it is written, so nobody else can promise it.

Lots are allocated **at the Dispatch desk, by scanning the boxes**. Each box scanned onto a line sets aside one unit of its lot. For stock packed before box labels existed, the desk offers **Allocate a lot by hand**. See [The dispatch desk](/docs/erp/dispatch).

The **Batch codes** tab lists every allocation. From a row you can:

- **Change quantity** — not below the boxes already scanned against that lot, not above what the line still needs, and not above the lot's stock.
- **Release** — gives the stock back to the lot. Refused once the line is billed (*Undo its billing first*) or while boxes are scanned against it (take them off at the Dispatch desk first).

## Billing an order

One order is one bill. Once every line of the order is **Ready** and the bill is made in Tally, press **Bill it** on any line of it, on **Ready to bill**. It asks for the **Tally invoice no.** and the **invoice date**, and bills the whole order. It needs:

1. the order **verified** by the back office;
2. **every line not cancelled Ready**;
3. a **rate** on every line;
4. approval, if the customer is Pending.

If anything is missing, hovering the button names it: *Needs Astar 20 L to be Ready — or moved to a new order to go later*. One invoice can cover several orders for the same customer: tick them and press **Bill together** to enter the invoice once, or bill an order with a number already used on another order for the same customer and date. A number already on an order for a different customer, or with a different date, is refused: *Invoice MMI/26-27/1119 is already on order 1240, billed to Shree Paints.* The billed order moves to **Invoice check**, and the checker is told.

**Goods that will go later.** Press **Move to a new order** on the line and say how many cans move — the whole line or part of it, in whole boxes. The cans get a new order number linked to this one, and this order can be billed without them. Release a line's batch codes before moving it.

**Undo billing** takes the whole order back out of billing until it is dispatched. **Change invoice** changes the invoice number or date on a billed order; an approved invoice then goes back to the checker.

## The invoice check

Somebody with the *Check invoices before dispatch* power opens **Invoice check** and compares each invoice with its order — customer, order number, goods, quantities, invoice number, rate and discount.

- **Approve invoice** — the order can be stickered and dispatched.
- **Reject invoice** — say why. Whoever billed it is told, and it shows *Invoice rejected* until somebody corrects it (Edit for the rate or discount, Change invoice for the number or date). The correction sends it back to the checker.

You cannot check a bill you entered yourself: *You entered this bill, so somebody else checks it* (an ERP administrator may). If a rate, discount, GST, the invoice number or date changes after approval, the invoice goes back to **Invoice check** with what changed.

## Dispatch stickers

On **Dispatch stickers**, press **Print dispatch stickers** for any order under process or ready — before billing too. Each sticker carries who it is **delivered to** (with the area) and who is **billed**, the **order no.** and date, the **product**, the **order qty**, **type** (box or can), **weight**, **lot size** (the pieces on the whole order), the order's **remark** and the **transporter**. It carries no lot or box code; the box's own label carries those. Once they are on the boxes, press **Stickers are on** to record it. Stickers cannot be printed after the goods have left.

### What the record shows

Besides the columns, a billed line shows the Tally invoice and its date, the invoice check (who approved it and when, or why it was rejected), the stickers, the vehicle, driver and remark it left with, the delivery party, litres dispatched, which company bills it, the **final amount** (with GST), the **lot-code costing** and **cost per can** (if you can see cost), the credit notes issued against it, **order fulfil days**, the customer's **credit days** and the **due date** that gives, the delivery party's segment and salesman, and the customer's **monthly target** from the CRM. The row is flagged *Sales target reached* when the customer's sales in the dispatch month meet that target.

## Dispatch stickers and labels

The **Dispatch stickers** tab lists every line under process or ready, or billed, whose goods have not left, with its parties, invoice, area, transporter and **Stickers** — one a box on a boxed line, one a can otherwise. Open a line and press **Print dispatch stickers** for the whole order, or press **Export** to take the list as a file.

### The four labels

Every label is drawn from the record. Nothing on a label is typed.

| Label | Size | Printed from | What it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Box label** | 50 × 50 mm | A packing batch, Boxes & labels, FG stock (loose units) | The box's QR and id, the pack size, cans per box, the product, the batch and box number, the packing date |
| **SFG label** | 80 × 80 mm | SFG batches, Stock → Semi-finished | The product, the lot code (also the QR), batches, batch size, date, location, inventory id |
| **Purchase label** | 80 × 80 mm | Purchase barcode, the purchase register | The item, the lot (also the QR), purchase date, quantity, litres, density, drums, litres per drum, PR number, PR bill no, testing id, remark. One per drum to begin with; change **Copies** for more or fewer. |
| **Dispatch sticker** | 80 × 80 mm | Orders, the Dispatch stickers tab, the Dispatch desk — once the line is under process or ready | The customer (delivery party), the product, the invoice no. and the order no. Never a lot or box code. |

A long name is set smaller so it fits; only a name too long for the smallest size is cut with "…".

### Printing

The label sheet offers three ways to paper, and all three print exactly what the sheet shows:

- **Send to printer** — straight to a USB label printer, with no print dialog and no paper size to choose. It appears in Chrome and Edge. The first time, the browser asks which printer; after that it remembers one printer for 50 mm labels and one for 80 mm on that computer. **Change printer** asks again.
- **Save print file** — the same printer commands as a `.prn` file, for a printer the browser cannot reach.
- **Print…** — the browser's own print dialog, one label a page at the label's size.

If **Send to printer** answers that the computer is keeping the printer for its own driver, that computer's Windows printer driver holds the USB port: use **Print…** there.

Four settings under Admin Console → Settings → ERP → *Label printer* describe the printers: **dots per millimetre** (8 for a 203 dpi printer, 12 for 300 dpi), the **gap between labels** on the roll, **which way up** the label leaves the printer, and **move the print up or down**. The printer must be loaded with stock the size of the label: 50 × 50 mm for box labels, 80 × 80 mm for the other three.

If the print runs onto the next label:

- **It lands at a different height on each label** — the printer has not learnt the roll. Press **Calibrate to this roll** beside Send to printer (or hold the printer's feed button until it feeds a few labels and stops). The printer feeds a few blank labels and prints nothing. Do it again whenever a different roll goes in.
- **It is the same distance off on every label, after calibrating** — set **move the print up or down** to that distance in millimetres; if it moves the wrong way, use the same figure with a minus sign.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I pressed Ready and it said the stock is not there, but I can see it on the Stock screen.">
Other lines already marked Ready at the same godown are counted first. If they will take what is on the shelf, your line has to wait until more is packed or moved in.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Ready is greyed out.">
The back office has not verified the order yet. It is on **To verify**.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Bill it is greyed out.">
Hover it: it names everything still missing — the back office's verification, a line that is not Ready yet, a rate, or approval for a Pending customer. The whole order is billed together, so every line has to be Ready; move a line that will go later to a new order.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I cannot approve this invoice.">
You entered the bill yourself, so somebody else checks it. You also need the *Check invoices before dispatch* power.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Print dispatch stickers is greyed out.">
The invoice has not been approved yet, or the goods have already left.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Where do I allocate lots?">
At the Dispatch desk, by scanning each box onto the order. For old stock with no box labels, the desk has **Allocate a lot by hand**.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Confirm dispatch says something is still to scan.">
At least one lot on the order has box ids, so every box has to be scanned at the Dispatch desk before the order can leave — and the order leaves whole, so one line still to scan holds it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Confirm dispatch says the invoice is waiting for the invoice check.">
The checker has not approved it yet, or rejected it. It is on **Invoice check**.
</Faq>
<Faq q="An order disappeared from On hold but one of its lines is not billed.">
On hold hides a whole order once any of its lines is billed, and hides any line that is Under Process or Ready. Those lines are on **Production** and **All lines**.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I cannot change the quantity on a line.">
It has been billed. Undo billing first (only possible before it is dispatch-verified).
</Faq>
<Faq q="I cannot delete a line.">
A billed line has to have its billing undone first, and a line with lots allocated has to have them released first — they hold stock.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I don't see Rate or Discount columns.">
They need the *See sales rate* and *See sales amounts* powers. Without them the price list sets the rate for you and no discount is given, and the list's toolbar says *2 columns hidden* — hover it to see which.
</Faq>
