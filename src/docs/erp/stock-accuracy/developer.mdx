Inventory Accuracy Control is one pure engine, one service that hands it figures from the ledgers, and one screen module with six tabs. It is not part of the purchase flow: a requirement opens no count and waits on none. A difference is a flag and a notification, never a hold. Nothing in it moves stock except an approved adjustment, which posts through the same ledgers every other movement uses.

## Not a purchase gate

Until November 2026 a requirement opened a blind count (`kind = 'reorder'`) and sat at a **Blind count** stage until it was entered. The gate, the stage, the requirement's stock facts and flags, and the "more than the shelf needs" check are gone; `0312_requirements_without_blind_count` cancelled the reorder counts still open. `erp_stock_counts.requisition_id` is still written where an older Factory APK sends a count with a requirement — that count is opened as an ordinary check of the shelf and holds nothing.

## The notification

<Code file="src/lib/erp/stock-check.ts" symbol="notifyStockFlag" collapsed />

It runs after the count's transaction has committed and swallows its own failure, so a bell that cannot be written never costs the count.

## Posting an adjustment

<Code file="src/lib/erp/screens/stock-check.ts" symbol="postAdjustment" collapsed />

Every lot of the item at the godown is locked under both lock spellings the ERP uses for raw material (`rm` from production, `Purchase` from transfers), the lots are read again under the locks, and only then is anything written.

`erp_requisitions.stock_check_required` is no longer set. The urgent-purchase columns `stock_exception_*` and the quantity confirmation `qty_confirm_*` are no longer written; they are kept, and shown in the record, as the record of decisions made under the old rule. `erp_raw_materials.count_tolerance_percent` overrides the tolerance per item. `erp_rm_entries.source_type` may now be `adjustment`.

## Tests

`src/lib/erp/engines/stock-check.test.ts` pins the verdict, the recount, ABC, the cycle picks and the causes; `purchase-flow.test.ts` pins that there is no count in the requirement flow. `erp-stock-check.test.ts` drives it end to end: a requirement opens no count and goes straight to its purchase method, a count is blind, and a red flag's notification reaches stock control and the administrator and nobody else.
