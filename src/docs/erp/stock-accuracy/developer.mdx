Inventory Accuracy Control is one pure engine, one service that hands it figures from the ledgers, one screen module with five tabs, and a gate threaded through the purchase flow. Nothing in it moves stock except an approved adjustment, which posts through the same ledgers every other movement uses.

## The gate

<Code file="src/lib/erp/engines/stock-check.ts" symbol="stockGate" />

`gatesFor` decides which counts a requirement reads: counts raised for it, any later count of its shelf, and any count of its shelf still unresolved. Only ROOT counts carry the state — a recount is a child (`recount_of_id`) and moves its root.

<Code file="src/lib/erp/stock-check.ts" symbol="gatesFor" collapsed />

`requirementViews` computes `ReqView.stock` for every row and `stageOf` hands `gate` to `requirementStage`, so every reader of the stage — the list, the PO form's "ready for PO", the dashboard tiles and the badge — sees the hold with no change of its own. Each buying handler asks `heldByStock` again.

## Posting an adjustment

<Code file="src/lib/erp/screens/stock-check.ts" symbol="postAdjustment" collapsed />

Every lot of the item at the godown is locked under both lock spellings the ERP uses for raw material (`rm` from production, `Purchase` from transfers), the lots are read again under the locks, and only then is anything written.

`planCycleCounts` counts the cycle counts already due today against `erp.stockCheck.dailyCount`, so the hourly job opens a day's allowance once.
