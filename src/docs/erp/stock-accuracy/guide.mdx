**Stock accuracy** is where the ERP checks that what is on the shelf is what the book says. It is the screen behind **Inventory Accuracy Control**: before anything is bought, somebody counts the item on the shelf without seeing the book; if the count and the book disagree, the purchase waits until the difference is recounted, explained and resolved. Stock is never changed quietly — only through an adjustment somebody else approves.

  AP --> J

| Tab | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **To count** | Counts waiting for somebody to count them, oldest due first. A recount is marked **Independent recount**. |
| **Red flags** | Checks whose count differs from the book beyond the item's tolerance, until they are resolved. |
| **Adjustments** | Every adjustment ever requested — waiting, approved or rejected — with its reason. |
| **Missing records** | Batches, fills, receipts and transfers across the ERP that should have a stock entry and do not. |
| **Every count** | The full history, with who counted, the book at the time and how each was resolved. |

## Counting

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the count and press Count it">
Go to the shelf. Count every drum, can or box of the item, in litres or pieces as the prompt says.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter what you counted">
Write the figure — zero if the shelf is empty — where on the floor you counted, and any lot numbers you saw. A recount is counted in the same place.
</Step>
<Step title="Submit">
Only now is the book shown: "Counted 480 Ltr · book 500. Red flag: −20 Ltr, beyond the 10 tolerance — a recount by somebody else comes next." A count within tolerance says "Matches the book" and a requirement waiting on it continues.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note">
A recount is always counted by **somebody other than whoever counted first**. Its **Count it** button is drawn disabled for the first counter, and the server refuses it too.
</Callout>

## A red flag

Open the row on **Red flags**. Its drawer shows the count against the book, every count of it in order, and the **possible causes** the records suggest — goods at the gate not yet in stock, a lot under test, a batch issue, a write-off — each with a link to the record.

<Steps>
<Step title="Ask for a recount">
Opens an independent recount on **To count**. Its result decides what happens next: matching the book resolves the check ("the first count was wrong"); agreeing with the first count confirms the difference and moves the check to **Investigating**; agreeing with neither sends it back for another recount.
</Step>
<Step title="Suggest causes (AI)">
The discrepancy assistant ranks the records the rules found and explains, in a sentence each, whether a **record** looks missing (the goods are there, the paperwork is not) or the **goods** themselves look missing. It is a suggestion; nothing is entered for you.
</Step>
<Step title="Resolve it">
Either enter the missing record on its own screen and press **The missing record is entered** — the book is read again and must now agree with the count — or **Request an adjustment** with a reason. Resolving needs the stock-control power.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Adjustments

An adjustment changes the book to what was counted. It is requested with a reason (damaged, spillage, evaporation, theft or loss, issued or received and never recorded, a measuring difference, other) and a sentence saying what happened, and it posts **only when somebody else approves it**:

| Adjustment | Posts as |
|---|---|
| A shortage | Transfers to **Item Lost Record**, oldest lot first — refused if the lots do not hold it, because stock never goes below zero |
| A surplus | A stock entry on the newest lot, shown in the raw-material entry log as **Stock adjustment** |

Rejecting sends the check back to **Investigating**.

## On a purchase requirement

A requirement raised while the stock check is switched on waits at **Stock check** in its flow. Its drawer says where the check stands, what is on the shelf and how much the count justifies buying.

| Stage | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Count stock** | Press **Count the stock** — it opens the count. |
| **Stock discrepancy** | Open the count and resolve it; vendor, quotations and the PO wait. |
| **Exception requested** | Somebody with the stock-control power decides the urgent purchase (not the person who asked). |
| **Confirm quantity** | The count matched, but more is asked for than the shelf justifies. Confirm the quantity with why, or **Recount the shelf**. |

**Request urgent purchase** lets a requirement go ahead before its count is finished, with a reason and an authorised person's approval. The count still has to be finished.

## On the dashboard

The **Inventory Accuracy Control** strip at the top of the dashboard shows what is waiting at each step — held requirements, counts to do, red flags, recounts, investigations, approvals, and requirements cleared to buy — and every number opens the records behind it. The **Inventory accuracy** section adds missing records, how often counts are right first time, the most frequent cause of adjustments, and when the next full count is due.
