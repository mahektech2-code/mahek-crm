The two levels screens are four ordinary ERP list modules built by two factories in `screens/movement.ts` — `rmLevelList("rmLevels" | "reorderRm")` and `fgLevelList("fgLevels" | "reorderFg")`. The figures are pure functions in `engines/production.ts`, availability comes from the stock ledger in `stock.ts`, and suggestions from `suggest.ts`. Every block below is cut from the source this deployment was built from.

<Callout kind="warn" title="rmReorderRows filters on required, not on min">

## Access

- **Modules:** <ModuleRef k="erp.rmLevels" /> and <ModuleRef k="erp.fgLevels" />. Each screen's tabs come with it (`erpKeysOf`); a tab is never granted on its own.
- **Writes** go through `requireErpWrite(screen)` in `actions/erp.ts`: the screen must be held, and a preview ("view ERP as") refuses every write.
- **No ERP power** is needed to set or change a level.
- The **raise** form opens under the levels screen (`requireErpForm`); its submit goes to the Purchase requirements screen and is checked there.

## Changing levels safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Keep the arithmetic in engines/production.ts">
The tab, the alert and Ask the ERP all read `rmReorderRows` / `fgReorderRows`, which read the engine. A second copy of "what is below" in a screen will disagree with the alert inside a release.
</Step>
<Step title="Read availability from stock.ts">
Never subtract fills or packing again in a level — lot stock is already net of them, and counting twice is the bug the comment in `rmLevelAvailable` exists to prevent.
</Step>
<Step title="Make thresholds settings">
Cover days and look-back are `erp.ai.reorder.*`. A new knob is a registry key with a `Config` type entry, read through `getConfig()`.
</Step>
<Step title="Never apply a suggestion without a press">
`applyRm` / `applyFg` are the only writers of a suggested value, and both are actions a person runs.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Known gaps, with file references

| Gap | Where |
|---|---|
| Raw-material "Below minimum" lists anything below the **maximum**; the sub-title, empty sentence and alert title say minimum | `screens/movement.ts` `rmReorderRows`; `registry.ts` `rmLevels` sub |
| The `belowLevel` alert sentence quotes the minimum even when stock is above it | `engines/alerts.ts` `belowLevel` |
| `Drum` and `Stationary` levels never get a suggestion (they look up the chemical map) | `suggest.ts` `levelSuggestions` `rm()` |
| Drum availability is company-wide, so a drum level ignores its godown | `stock.ts` `rmLevelAvailable` |
| Box use is matched to raw materials by name (`lower(m.name) = lower(pk.box_type)`), so a renamed box material silently loses its suggestion | `suggest.ts` |
| Finished-goods rows offer no **Raise** action and nothing routes a short SKU to production | `screens/movement.ts` `fgLevelList` |
