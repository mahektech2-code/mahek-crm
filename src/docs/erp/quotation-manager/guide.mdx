The **Quotation manager** is the purchase desk's rate sheet. Each **row** is one quotation — the date and the party (supplier) who quoted — and each **column** is something quoted for: the pack on top, the material in the middle and the brand underneath, exactly as the sheet heads them. A column can also hold text, such as the broker's name.

Everything on it can be changed in place: the cells, the date and party of a row, and the columns themselves.

## Working the grid

- **Click a cell and type** to replace it; **double-click** or press **Enter** to change what is there.
- **Enter** saves and moves down, **Tab** saves and moves right, **Esc** cancels.
- **Arrow keys** walk the grid; hold **Shift** (or drag) to select a block.
- **Delete** clears the selected cells.
- **Ctrl+C** copies the selection and **Ctrl+V** pastes a block copied from Excel or Google Sheets, starting at the selected cell. Rows past the bottom are added. A value that is not a number in a rate column is left out and the sheet says which.
- **Ctrl+Z** (or **Undo**) takes back the last change.

Every change is saved as you make it. The words beside Undo say whether everything is saved.

## Choosing a party or a broker

The **Party** column, and any text column such as **Broker name**, open a menu rather than a typing box. It shows ten at a time with the current one ticked. With more than ten to choose from it has a search box, and **Show more** brings the next ten. A name that isn't on the list is added from the same box (**Add “…”**), and **Clear** empties the cell.

## Buying a rate

1. **Ready to buy (grey).** A manager or administrator selects the rate to buy (one cell or a block) and presses **Ready to buy**. The cell turns grey: the team may now buy it.
2. **Purchase it.** Anybody on the team selects a grey cell and presses **Purchase it**, or right-clicks it. They enter the **number of drums** and the **PO number**, then **Save and mark bought**.
3. **Bought (yellow).** The cell turns yellow with a small mark in its corner, like a note in Excel. **Hover** over it to read the purchase: the PO number, drums, rate, material, party, quote date, the sheet's row number, and who bought it and when. **Click** it to pin the note, then **Edit** or **Undo purchase**, which puts it back to grey.

Only a manager or an administrator marks a rate Ready to buy or takes the mark off. A rate that isn't grey can't be bought.

**Right-click** any cell for everything it offers: edit, Ready to buy, Purchase it, the PO note, cancelled, picked up, clear and copy.

## Rows

Use the **⋯** beside a row number to add a row below it with the same date, duplicate the row, or delete it. A date can be typed as `14-Oct-2026`, `14/10/2026` or `2026-10-14`. The party offers the purchase parties as you type, and a name that matches one exactly is linked to it.

## Columns

Double-click a heading, or use its **✎**, to change its three lines, whether it holds rates or text, and (optionally) which raw material it is for. **Remove column** hides it; its rates are kept, and **Removed columns** brings it back.

## The two rows at the foot

**Latest rate** is the newest rate in each column for the rows shown, and **Lowest rate** the lowest. Cancelled rates count for neither. The lowest rate in a column is also drawn in green where it sits.
