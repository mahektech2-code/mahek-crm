**Under Process** is the day's production plan. Every morning the Production Head writes down the mixing, loose-can and packing work for the day, prints it on A5, and hands the sheet to the floor. When more work comes in during the day, they add it to the same plan and print again. The floor works only from the printed sheet.

## Making the day's plan

1. Open **Sales → Under Process** and press **Create daily plan**. The date starts at today. There is one plan per day: if the day already has one, it opens. Once today has a plan the button reads **Plan another day** and starts at tomorrow. A plan cannot be made for a day that has passed.
2. The plan is one page: one table with a tick box on every row. Every product the orders ask for and every base they need is already a row.
3. Tick what the floor makes today, change **Make today** where needed, and press **Print plan**. Only ticked rows are printed.

| Column | What it says |
|---|---|
| Product | the product, its size and how it is packed |
| Confirmed | cans (or litres of base) on orders Accounts approved and the MRP check cleared |
| Waiting | what still waits: **MRP check N**, **Accounts N**, or **Expected N L** for a base |
| In stock | stock at the plan's godown, read from the stock screens |
| If all approved | **Enough**, or **Short N** if everything waiting is approved |
| Make today | the quantity to print; editing it ticks the row |
| Unit | Cans, Boxes or Cans for packing, Litre or Kg for a base |

Rows are split into **Loose cans and packing** and **Bases (SFG)**. Rows with confirmed work start ticked; rows that only wait for the MRP check or Accounts start unticked. Tick one and it prints as an ordinary line.

- **Search** narrows the table by product.
- The **funnel** on a column heading filters by its values (for example Waiting → MRP check). Filters on show as chips above the table; **Clear** removes them all.
- **Select confirmed** ticks every row with confirmed work.
- **Add a line** types work no order asks for (a base, loose cans or packing). It appears as its own ticked row.

The plan does not move any stock. The work itself is still recorded on SFG batches, FG filling and Packing batches.

## Printing

Press **Print plan**. The ticked rows become the plan and the A5 sheet opens; use **Print or save as PDF**, with **Margins** left on **Default** in the print dialog ("None" removes the sheet's margins and its page numbers).

The sheet carries the Mahek logo and the date and weekday at the top. Each section heading says how many lines it has and what they add up to. Every line has a row number, the product, quantity and unit, and under **Remarks** what the customer wrote on the order. Product codes and order numbers are not printed. The plan number, godown, number of lines, print number, who created the plan and when it was printed sit in the bottom margin of every page with the page number. A long plan fills the first page before it starts a second, a line never splits across pages, and every later page repeats the column headings and names the plan, date and print at the top.

From the second print of the day:

- new and edited lines are **underlined** and starred;
- an edited quantity says what it **was**, in small type under the new quantity (Remarks stay the customer's words only, blank when the order has none);
- lines unticked since the last print are listed once in a **Removed since last print** box.

Every print is kept. **Last print** (on today's plan and against each previous plan) opens the sheet exactly as it was handed out. When nothing has changed since the last print, the plan's button reads **Open Print N** and records no new print.

**A past day's plan is read-only.** It is the record of that day: nothing on it can be ticked, added or changed, it cannot be printed again, and it shows no current stock. Its earlier prints can still be opened.

## Previous plans

The list shows every earlier plan, newest first. Pick a date and press **Search** to find one.
