**Under Process** is the day's production plan. Every morning the Production Head writes down the mixing, loose-can and packing work for the day, prints it on A5, and hands the sheet to the floor. When more work comes in during the day, they add it to the same plan and print again. The floor works only from the printed sheet.

## Making the day's plan

1. Open **Sales → Under Process** (beside Orders and the Dispatch desk) and press **Create daily plan**. The date starts at today. There is one plan per day: if the day already has one, it opens. Once today has a plan the button reads **Plan another day** and starts at tomorrow. A plan cannot be made for a day that has passed.
2. Press **Add line** on the section the work belongs to. Pick the **work category**, the **product**, and type the **quantity**. The size and current stock are filled in from the product.
3. Press **Add**, or **Add and add another** to keep going. Every line is saved the moment you add it.

| Category | You pick | Size | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| SFG manufacturing | the SFG base | none | Litre or Kg |
| Loose can preparation | product and can size | from the product | Cans |
| Packing | the boxed SKU | from the SKU | Boxes or Cans |

**Available now** is the stock at the godown the plan was made at. It is read from the stock screens and is never typed. The plan does not move any stock.

## Adding more during the day

Open the plan and use **Add line** again. Nothing already on the plan changes. If the product is already on the plan, the dialog says so, and the new quantity goes on its own line so the floor sees it as new.

Each line is marked against the last sheet that was printed:

- **Given** (green): on the last sheet, unchanged.
- **New** or **Edited** (amber): not printed yet. An edited quantity shows the old figure struck through.
- **Removed** (red): deleted after the floor had it. The next print tells them.

A banner says how many lines changed since the last print.

## Printing

Press **Print plan**. The A5 sheet opens; use **Print or save as PDF**, with **Margins** left on **Default** in the print dialog ("None" removes the sheet's margins and its page numbers).

The sheet carries the Mahek logo and the date and weekday at the top. Each section heading says how many lines it has and what they add up to. Every line has a row number, the product, quantity and unit, and under **Remarks** what the customer wrote on the order. Product codes and order numbers are not printed. The plan number, godown, number of lines, print number, who created the plan and when it was printed sit in the bottom margin of every page with the page number. A long plan fills the first page before it starts a second, a line never splits across pages, and every later page repeats the column headings and names the plan, date and print at the top.

From the second print of the day:

- new and edited lines are **underlined** and starred;
- an edited quantity says what it **was**, in small type under the new quantity (Remarks stay the customer's words only, blank when the order has none);
- deleted lines are listed once in a **Removed since last print** box.

Every print is kept. **Last print** (on today's plan and against each previous plan) opens the sheet exactly as it was handed out. When nothing has changed since the last print, the plan's button reads **Open Print N** and records no new print.

**A past day's plan is read-only.** It is the record of that day: its lines no longer follow the orders, nothing on it can be added, edited or deleted, it cannot be printed again, and it shows no current stock. Its earlier prints can still be opened.

## Previous plans

The list shows every earlier plan, newest first. Pick a date and press **Search** to find one.


## Orders under process come in by themselves

Every order line whose Production status is **Under Process** on Orders → Production comes into today's plan without anybody typing it, one line per product, marked **From orders**. What the customers wrote in **Remark / customer instructions** on those orders comes with it, each remark once, and is printed under **Remarks**.

- A loose product goes under **Loose can preparation**, in cans.
- A boxed product goes under **Packing**, in boxes — or in cans where the orders do not add up to whole boxes.
- When an order line is marked Ready, held or cancelled, its line leaves the plan: it shows as **Removed**, and the next print tells the floor.
- These lines cannot be edited or deleted on the plan. Change the order, and the plan follows.

SFG manufacturing is still typed by the Production Head: how much liquid the day needs is a judgement, not a sum.

## Planning before Accounts approve

The floor starts at ten and Accounts approve at twelve, so the plan also shows what is **expected**: orders Accounts approved that wait for your MRP check, and TOs still waiting for Accounts. Expected work is shown and counted so the day can be planned. It is never filled or packed until Accounts approve and the MRP check clears it.

- **By product**: per product, the confirmed cans, what waits for the MRP check, what waits for Accounts, what is in stock, and whether it is enough if everything is approved. **Show orders** lists the orders and TOs behind each figure.
- **Expected orders**: every order and TO not confirmed yet, where it is, and a stock check for planning. A TO **on hold** is greyed and not counted. A TO with no godown says **Pick a godown to check**.
- **Bases (SFG)**: the base each product needs, for confirmed and for expected orders, against the approved base in the tank.

**Make the base ahead** (on Bases, or under a short stock check) adds an SFG line to the plan marked **For expected orders**, with the litres still needed filled in. Only the base can be started ahead; filling and packing stay locked.

When Accounts approve a TO it moves to the MRP check column by itself. When you clear it at the MRP check it becomes a firm plan line, marked New, and the next print underlines it. A declined or cancelled TO drops off. A base already made stays in the tank for the next order.

The printed sheet adds a dashed box, **Expected · not approved yet · do not fill or pack**, listing each expected product and what it waits for (MRP check, Accounts). A base made ahead prints with **For expected orders** under Remarks.
