Requirements and their Quotations tab are two screen modules (`requisitions`, `quotations`) in `lib/erp/screens/purchase-flow.ts`, under one screen in the registry. The rules — the stage, landed cost, ranking, units and paste matching — are pure in `lib/erp/engines/purchase-flow.ts`. Department rules are `lib/erp/departments.ts`; per-material duties come through `dutyKit(ctx)`.

## Permissions

- **Module:** <ModuleRef k="erp.requisitions" /> — opens both tabs.

- **Duties:** `request` (offered items, raise and edit), `raisePo` (Create PO).

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="A new stage">
Add it to `requirementStage` and its test. If somebody has to act on it before a PO exists, add it to `REQUIREMENT_ACTION_STAGES`, which the badge counts.
</Step>
<Step title="Never store the stage">
The stored `status` is moved only by approval, receipt and cancel. A typed status can say Received with nothing at the gate.
</Step>
<Step title="Check departments and duties on the server">
The form filters with the same functions, but `raiseRequirement` and `saveRequirement` must refuse on their own.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Known gaps, with file references
