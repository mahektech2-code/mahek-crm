Requirements are one screen module, `requisitions`, in `lib/erp/screens/purchase-flow.ts`; the Link PO / Create PO step is `lib/erp/screens/requirement-po.ts`. The rules — the stage, the PO route, units and paste matching — are pure in `lib/erp/engines/purchase-flow.ts`. Department rules are `lib/erp/departments.ts`.

## From the factory phone

`lib/factory/purchase.ts` is the floor's side: `requirementKit`, `raiseFromFloor` and `cancelFromFloor`, behind the `reqKit`, `reqRaise` and `reqCancel` ops on `/api/factory/rpc`. The phone raises through `raiseRequirementWith`, the function the ERP form uses, with a map of counts by raw material. Inside the same transaction, each count is written onto the item's blind count by `writeCounted`, the same judgement `submitCount` uses. Red flags are passed to `notifyStockFlag` once the requirement has saved. `factory_requirement_sends` holds the key the phone mints, so a resend gets the first answer back instead of a second requirement. `FactoryCtx.reqDepts` decides who sees the screen: crew seat `owner`, or a manager over the department.

## No quotations on a requirement

The Requirements screen's Quotations tab was removed in October 2026: supplier rates are kept on the Quotation manager. `erp_quotations` and the requisition's `quotation_id` and `selection_note` are kept as the record they are; nothing reads or writes them. An old `?view=quotations` link opens the requirements list.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="A new stage">
Add it to `requirementStage` and its test. If somebody has to act on it before a PO exists, add it to `REQUIREMENT_ACTION_STAGES`, which the badge counts.
</Step>
<Step title="Never store the stage">
The stored `status` is moved only by approval, receipt and cancel. A typed status can say Received with nothing at the gate.
</Step>
<Step title="Check departments on the server">
The form filters with the same functions, but `raiseRequirement` and `saveRequirement` must refuse on their own.
</Step>
</Steps>
