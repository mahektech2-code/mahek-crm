The purchase flow is **Requirement → Blind count → Vendor → Approval → Purchase order → Receipt**, and no purchase is completed without a PO. The blind count — a physical count of the shelf, entered before the vendor is chosen — never stops the purchase over what it finds: a difference from the book flags the requirement and notifies stock control, and the purchase carries on. It has its own page: [Stock accuracy](/docs/erp/stock-accuracy/how-it-works). This page covers the requirement and the vendor: who may ask for what, and how a vendor is chosen. [Purchase orders](/docs/erp/purchase-orders/how-it-works) and [Goods receipt](/docs/erp/goods-receipt-and-testing/how-it-works) cover the rest.

## The requirement

<Rule title="One requirement, every item, one save">
A department's monthly list is a hundred items, so a requirement carries as many as it needs (up to five hundred) under one number, `REQ-n`. Each item is its own record — its vendor and PO line are decided item by item — but they are written together: a bad line refuses the whole save and names the line, so a requirement is never half raised.
</Rule>

<Rule title="It says when, where and how badly">
A date, a **required-by** date that cannot be before it, a department, the godown to deliver to, and a priority — Urgent, Medium or For Stock. A requirement past its required-by date while still in flight is flagged "Past its required date", and Urgent ones are counted on the dashboard.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Finished goods are never bought">
Only the item master's categories may be asked for. An item may appear once per requirement: a second line for the same item is refused and points at the first.
</Rule>

### Pasting a list

<Rule title="A pasted row is matched, never guessed">
The quantity is the last number on the row; everything before it names the item. It matches an item exactly (ignoring capitals), or the one item that contains what was typed. A row that names nothing — or that could be two items — is shown back as unmatched, and an item already on the requirement is reported as a duplicate instead of becoming a second line.
</Rule>

## Departments

<Rule title="A requirement is raised for one of four teams, and the team decides the categories">
Mixing & Blending asks for chemicals; Refilling for cans and drums; Packing for boxes and packing stationery; the Production Head for all of them. Somebody whose ERP designation puts them in a department raises for their own department only, sees only their department's requirements and the ones they raised, and cannot cancel another department's. Somebody in no department picks one of the four, and that team's categories apply.
</Rule>

<Callout kind="why">
The form used to offer a free list of departments — Production, Godown / Store, Quality, Dispatch, Office, Maintenance — each of which could ask for anything at all. That is the opposite of what departments are for. Requirements raised under the old labels keep them, and editing such a requirement without changing its department is not refused.
</Callout>

## Every item is bought direct

<Rule title="A requirement goes to one vendor, and the item's default vendor is offered first">
There is no purchase method to decide and no quotation to collect. When an item has a **default vendor** on its raw material record, that vendor is set as the requirement is raised and the requirement is Ready for PO once its blind count is in. Otherwise it waits at **Select vendor**. The vendor can be changed with **Change vendor** until the requirement is on a PO.
</Rule>

<Callout kind="why">
Requirements used to carry a purchase rule — direct, quotation, or the buyer decides — and a quotation item could not reach a PO until enough quotations were in and one was selected. Supplier rates are now kept on the [Quotation manager](/docs/erp/quotation-manager/how-it-works), across every item and vendor, so the rate is looked up there and typed on the PO. Requirements raised under the old rule keep their stored columns, but nothing reads them.
</Callout>

## Who may do what to an item

<Rule title="The Admin Console decides, and nothing on an item overrides it">
Anyone holding Requirements may request any item their department asks for; anyone holding Purchase orders (and purchase money) may raise its PO. Both are granted in Admin Console → Access, directly or through a designation. There is no per-item list of who may request or buy it.
</Rule>

## The stage is derived, never typed

A requirement's stage is worked out from its own record each time it is read: its blind count, its vendor and the PO it is on, with what that PO has ordered and received. The stored status moves only with the flow — Pending, then **Order Placed** when its PO is approved, then **Received** — and there is no button to type one. A status somebody types can say Received with nothing at the gate.

Requirements raised before purchase orders existed show their old status with "(before POs)" and are flagged "Before POs"; nothing back-fills a PO for them.

## Present quantity

Each requirement shows the **present quantity** the godown's re-order level counts as available for the item. Where no level is set at that godown it is blank — said in words on the record rather than shown as zero.
