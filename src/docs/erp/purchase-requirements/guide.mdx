A purchase requirement is a department saying "we need this bought". It is the first step of every purchase: nothing reaches a purchase order, the gate or stock without one. One requirement can carry every item a department needs this month, and each item then goes to a vendor of its own. Supplier rates are kept on the [Quotation manager](/docs/erp/quotation-manager), not here.

## Raising a requirement

<Steps>
<Step title="New requirement">
**Date** (today), **Required by** (when the department needs the goods in hand), **Department**, **Deliver to** (your working godown), **Priority** (Urgent, Medium or For Stock) and **Remarks**.
</Step>
<Step title="Add every item">
Type in "Add an item — type to search", or paste a list: one item a row with its quantity last — "Solvent A, 200", or copied straight from Excel. A row that names nothing the ERP knows is shown to you rather than guessed. Each item shows what is **in stock**, its **unit** and its **vendor** — the item's default vendor where it has one, otherwise "Chosen next".
</Step>
<Step title="Raise requirement">
All items are saved together under one number — "REQ-412 raised · 3 items · 2 with a vendor offered · 1 to choose". If one line is wrong, nothing is saved and that line is marked.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Which department, which items

You can only ask for what your team uses:

| Department | May ask for |
|---|---|
| Mixing & Blending | Chemicals |
| Refilling | Cans and drums |
| Packing | Boxes and packing stationery |
| Production Head | All of the above |

If you work in one department it is chosen for you and only its items are offered. If you are not in a production department (the office, the store), pick the team the requirement is for and its items are offered. Finished goods are never on the list: they are made, not bought.


## Raising one from the factory phone

Whoever is in charge of Mixing, Refilling or Packing, a supervisor over one of those departments, and the Production Head can raise a requirement from the Factory app: **More → Purchase requirement**. It takes two steps:

1. **Pick the items.** For each item, the phone first asks how much is in the godown right now, then how much to buy. It never shows the stock the office has on record.
2. **Say when it is needed and how urgent it is**, add a note if you like, and send it.

What they counted becomes that item's stock check. They see only whether it matches the record. If it does not, stock control is told and buying carries on. The phone then follows each item through to receipt, and an item can be cancelled from it until it is on a PO.

## What happens to each item next

Every item is bought direct from one vendor. If the item has a **default vendor** on its [raw material](/docs/erp/masters) record, that vendor is offered as you raise it; otherwise somebody picks one.

| Stage | What it means | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| **Blind count** | The stock check is on and the shelf has not been counted yet | Whoever counts presses **Count the stock** — see [Stock accuracy](/docs/erp/stock-accuracy) |
| **Select vendor** | No vendor yet | Anyone on the screen presses **Select vendor** |
| **Ready for PO** | The vendor is decided | **Create PO** opens a purchase order for this vendor. **Change vendor** picks another |
| **PO awaiting approval / PO approved / PO sent** | On a purchase order | The PO's approver, then whoever sends it |
| **Partly received / Received / Closed short** | Goods have come in against the PO | The gate |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled with a reason | — |

A requirement already on a PO cannot be edited or cancelled — the PO has to be cancelled or sent back first, and the record says so.

To compare what vendors charge before choosing one, look the item up on the [Quotation manager](/docs/erp/quotation-manager).

## Cancelling

**Cancel requirement** asks for a reason. You cannot cancel another department's requirement, one that is on a PO, or one that has been received.

## Common questions

<Faq q="Why does a new requirement go straight to 'Ready for PO'?">
The item has a default vendor, so the vendor was offered as you raised it. Press **Change vendor** to buy from somebody else.
</Faq>

<Faq q="I can't see the requirements another department raised.">
In a production department you see your own department's requirements, and any you raised yourself. The Production Head, the store and administrators see every department's.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Where did the Quotations tab go?">
Supplier rates are kept on the [Quotation manager](/docs/erp/quotation-manager) now. A requirement no longer collects or selects quotations: you choose the vendor, and the rate is typed on the purchase order.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Create PO is greyed out.">
It says why on hover: Purchase orders is not on your account, or purchase money is not.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The 'Present qty' column is blank.">
It shows what the godown's re-order level counts as available. Without a level for that item at that godown there is nothing to show — set one on [Minimum levels](/docs/erp/stock-levels).
</Faq>
