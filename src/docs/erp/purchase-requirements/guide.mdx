A purchase requirement is a department saying "we need this bought". It is the first step of every purchase: nothing reaches a purchase order, the gate or stock without one. One requirement can carry every item a department needs this month. Each item is counted blind first, then gets its PO by its purchase rule: a **Quotation** item links the PO number the Quotation manager made, a **Direct purchase** item creates its PO here at the item's price. No PO is approved.

## Raising a requirement

<Steps>
<Step title="New requirement">
**Date** (today), **Required by** (when the department needs the goods in hand), **Department**, **Deliver to** (your working godown), **Priority** (Urgent, Medium or For Stock) and **Remarks**.
</Step>
<Step title="Add every item">
Type in "Add an item — type to search", or paste a list: one item a row with its quantity last — "Solvent A, 200", or copied straight from Excel. A row that names nothing the ERP knows is shown to you rather than guessed. Each item shows what is **in stock**, its **unit** and where it **goes to** (Direct · the vendor, Quotations, or Buyer decides).
</Step>
<Step title="Raise requirement">
All items are saved together under one number — "REQ-412 raised · 3 items · 2 direct · 1 by quotation". If one line is wrong, nothing is saved and that line is marked.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Which department, which items

You can only ask for what your team uses:

| Department | May ask for |
|---|---|
| Mixing & Blending | Chemicals |
| Refilling | Cans and drums |
| Packing | Boxes and packing stationery |
| Production Head | All of the above |

If you work in one department it is chosen for you and only its items are offered. If you are not in a production department (the office, the store), pick the team the requirement is for and its items are offered. Finished goods are never on the list: they are made, not bought.


## Raising one from the factory phone

Whoever is in charge of Mixing, Refilling or Packing, a supervisor over one of those departments, and the Production Head can raise a requirement from the Factory app: **More → Purchase requirement**. It takes two steps:

1. **Pick the items.** For each item, the phone first asks how much is in the godown right now, then how much to buy. It never shows the stock the office has on record.
2. **Say when it is needed and how urgent it is**, add a note if you like, and send it.

What they counted becomes that item's stock check. They see only whether it matches the record. If it does not, stock control is told and buying carries on. The phone then follows each item through to receipt, and an item can be cancelled from it until it is on a PO.

## What happens to each item next

First the shelf is **counted blind** — the count is entered without seeing the book. The requirement's drawer then shows the **book comparison** (the count against the reconciled system quantity) and the verdict: **Stock verification passed**, or a **Red flag** where it differs beyond the allowed difference. A red flag tells stock control; the purchase carries on.

Then the PO comes by the item's **purchase rule**, copied from the item when you raised it:

| Stage | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Blind count** | The count has not been entered | Count the stock |
| **Waiting for PO number** | Quotation: bought on the Quotation manager | Type the PO number in the drawer and **Link PO** |
| **Create PO** | Direct purchase: no quotation | **Create PO** in the drawer |
| **Create or link PO** | Buyer decision | Either of the two |
| **Waiting for goods / Partly received** | On a PO | Receive the goods against its PO number |
| **Received / Closed short** | Done | — |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled with a reason | — |

## Getting the PO

Both happen in the requirement's own drawer. Nothing is approved: the PO is ready to receive against straight away.

<Steps>
<Step title="Quotation — Link PO">
On the Quotation manager the admin marks the rate **Ready to buy** and the desk presses **Purchase it** with the number of drums. The PO number is made for them — **PO-1243**. In the requirement's drawer, type that number in **PO number**: the vendor, material, rate, drums and who bought it are fetched and checked against the requirement. Press **Link PO to requirement**. A wrong number can be **unlinked** until goods arrive.
</Step>
<Step title="Direct purchase — Create PO">
Press **Create PO**. It opens over the drawer, in three pages: **Vendor** (the item's default vendor first, and the quantity), **Delivery** (where, by when, payment terms, freight, remarks) and **Review**. The rate is the item's price in Masters → Raw materials — nobody types an amount. An item with no price is ordered at **₹0**, and the Review page says so.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Cancelling

**Cancel requirement** asks for a reason. You cannot cancel another department's requirement, one that is on a PO, or one that has been received.

## Common questions

<Faq q="Why is there a PO number box on one item and a Create PO button on another?">
The item's purchase rule decides. A Quotation item is bought on the Quotation manager, which makes its PO number; a Direct purchase item is created here at its price. A Buyer-decision item offers both.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The PO number is refused.">
It must be a number the Quotation manager made for a purchase of this same item — the message says what it found instead: no such number, a cancelled row, or a different material.
</Faq>

<Faq q="I can't see the requirements another department raised.">
In a production department you see your own department's requirements, and any you raised yourself. The Production Head, the store and administrators see every department's.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why is the rate ₹0 on my PO?">
The item has no price in Masters → Raw materials. Add one there to order it at a rate next time. The lot is held out of stock until the supplier's bill rate is entered.
</Faq>

<Faq q="I changed the item's purchase rule, but the old requirement still asks for a PO number.">
A requirement keeps the rule it was raised with, so a change to the item never re-routes work already in progress. Cancel it and raise a new one if it must change.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The 'Present qty' column is blank.">
It shows what the godown's re-order level counts as available. Without a level for that item at that godown there is nothing to show — set one on [Minimum levels](/docs/erp/stock-levels).
</Faq>
