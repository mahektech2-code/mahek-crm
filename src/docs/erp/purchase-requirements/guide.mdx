A purchase requirement is a department saying "we need this bought". It is the first step of every purchase: nothing reaches a purchase order, the gate or stock without one. One requirement can carry every item a department needs this month, and each item then finds its own way — straight to a vendor, through quotations, or to the buyer for a decision.

## Raising a requirement

<Steps>
<Step title="New requirement">
**Date** (today), **Required by** (when the department needs the goods in hand), **Department**, **Deliver to** (your working godown), **Priority** (Urgent, Medium or For Stock) and **Remarks**.
</Step>
<Step title="Add every item">
Type in "Add an item — type to search", or paste a list: one item a row with its quantity last — "Solvent A, 200", or copied straight from Excel. A row that names nothing the ERP knows is shown to you rather than guessed. Each item shows what is **in stock**, its **unit** and where it **goes to** (Direct · the vendor, Quotations, or Buyer decides).
</Step>
<Step title="Raise requirement">
All items are saved together under one number — "REQ-412 raised · 3 items · 2 direct · 1 for quotations". If one line is wrong, nothing is saved and that line is marked.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Which department, which items

You can only ask for what your team uses:

| Department | May ask for |
|---|---|
| Mixing & Blending | Chemicals |
| Refilling | Cans and drums |
| Packing | Boxes and packing stationery |
| Production Head | All of the above |

If you work in one department it is chosen for you and only its items are offered. If you are not in a production department (the office, the store), pick the team the requirement is for and its items are offered. Finished goods are never on the list: they are made, not bought.

<Callout kind="note">
Some items have named requesters. If an item is missing from your list, or the line says "Requesting … is set to …", only the people or teams named for that item may ask for it.
</Callout>

## What happens to each item next

Each item follows its **purchase rule**, copied from the item when you raised it.

| Stage | What it means | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| **Buyer decision** | The item's rule is "Buyer decision" | The buyer presses **Decide method**: Direct purchase or Quotation, with a note |
| **Select vendor** | Direct purchase, no vendor yet | Anyone on the screen presses **Select vendor** |
| **Collect quotations** | Quotation, fewer quotations in than needed | Somebody with purchase money presses **Add quotation** |
| **Compare quotations** | Enough quotations are in | **Compare & select** — see below |
| **Ready for PO** | The vendor is decided | **Create PO** opens a purchase order for this vendor |
| **PO awaiting approval / PO approved / PO sent** | On a purchase order | The PO's approver, then whoever sends it |
| **Partly received / Received / Closed short** | Goods have come in against the PO | The gate |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled with a reason | — |

A requirement already on a PO cannot be edited or cancelled — the PO has to be cancelled or sent back first, and the record says so.

## The Quotations tab

For an item bought by quotation, collect prices from several vendors and choose one.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a quotation">
From the requirement's **Add quotation**, or on the Quotations tab: the vendor, the date and their reference, the **rate**, **GST %**, **freight**, delivery days, payment terms, **valid until**, and the vendor's document. One quotation per vendor per requirement — a second from the same vendor is refused; edit the first.
</Step>
<Step title="Compare">
Quotations are ranked by **landed cost**: rate × quantity, plus GST, plus freight. The cheapest live one is marked "Lowest landed cost". An expired one is ranked last and cannot be chosen.
</Step>
<Step title="Select one">
**Compare & select** is available once the minimum number of quotations is in. Choosing anything other than the lowest asks **Why this one** — the save is refused without it. The requirement is then **Ready for PO**, at that quotation's rate.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Cancelling

**Cancel requirement** asks for a reason. You cannot cancel another department's requirement, one that is on a PO, or one that has been received.

## Common questions

<Faq q="Why does a new requirement go straight to 'Ready for PO'?">
The item's rule is Direct purchase and it has a default vendor, so the vendor was decided as you raised it.
</Faq>

<Faq q="I can't see the requirements another department raised.">
In a production department you see your own department's requirements, and any you raised yourself. The Production Head, the store and administrators see every department's.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Compare & select is greyed out.">
Either fewer quotations are in than needed — the button says how many — or purchase money is not on your account.
</Faq>

<Faq q="I changed the item's purchase rule, but the old requirement still asks for quotations.">
A requirement keeps the rule it was raised with, so a change to the item never re-routes work already in progress. Cancel it and raise a new one if it must change.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The 'Present qty' column is blank.">
It shows what the godown's re-order level counts as available. Without a level for that item at that godown there is nothing to show — set one on [Minimum levels](/docs/erp/stock-levels).
</Faq>
