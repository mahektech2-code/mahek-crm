A purchase requirement is a department saying "we need this bought". It is the first step of every purchase: nothing reaches a purchase order, the gate or stock without one. One requirement can carry every item a department needs this month. Each item gets its PO straight away by its purchase rule: a **Quotation** item is bought on the Quotation manager, which writes its PO, a **Direct purchase** item creates its PO here at the item's price. No PO is approved.

### One PO for several requirements

Tick the requirements that go to one supplier and press **Create one PO** in the bar at the foot of the list. It opens Create PO on the first of them with the others already on it — one PO, a line each, every line at its own item's price. It is offered only while every ticked row has the same supplier, and only to somebody with Edit on Requirements and Purchase orders on their account.

## The requirement's record

Click a row to open it. Down the left is everything about it: quantity, what is in stock now (with the re-order minimum where the item has one), required by, team, deliver to, priority, purchase method, who raised it and when, and the remarks. On the right:

- the four steps — Requirement, Purchase method, PO number, Receipt;
- **Next step**: what to do now, in a sentence, with its one button;
- the **purchase order** once there is one — supplier, rate (only with purchase money on your account), where it was shared, and how much has arrived;
- **History**: raised, PO created or linked, shared, goods received, cancelled with the reason — read from what actually happened, never typed — and, last, what it is still waiting for.

The foot has **Edit**, **Cancel requirement**, **Open** its PO and the next step. One your access does not allow is shown with a lock; pressing it says why.

## Raising a requirement

<Steps>
<Step title="New requirement">
**Date** (today), **Required by** (when the department needs the goods in hand), **Department**, **Deliver to** (your working godown), **Priority** (Urgent, Medium or For Stock) and **Remarks**.
</Step>
<Step title="Add every item">
Type in "Add an item — type to search", or paste a list: one item a row with its quantity last — "Solvent A, 200", or copied straight from Excel. A row that names nothing the ERP knows is shown to you rather than guessed. Each item shows what is **in stock**, its **unit** and where it **goes to** (Direct · the vendor, Quotations, or Buyer decides).
</Step>
<Step title="Raise requirement">
All items are saved together under one number — "REQ-412 raised · 3 items · 2 direct · 1 by quotation". If one line is wrong, nothing is saved and that line is marked.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Correcting a requirement

**Edit** is on every requirement, at every stage: dates, department, godown, quantity, priority, remarks, and the item itself. Not yet on a PO, a new item brings its own purchase rule. Already on a PO, the PO's item changes too, and so does anything already received on it (its lot is renumbered). The requirement number, stage and PO are the system's.

### Which department, which items

You can only ask for what your team uses:

| Department | May ask for |
|---|---|
| Mixing & Blending | Chemicals |
| Refilling | Cans and drums |
| Packing | Boxes and packing stationery |
| Production Head | All of the above |

If you work in one department it is chosen for you and only its items are offered. If you are not in a production department (the office, the store), pick the team the requirement is for and its items are offered. Finished goods are never on the list: they are made, not bought.


## Raising one from the factory phone

Whoever is in charge of Mixing, Refilling or Packing, a supervisor over one of those departments, and the Production Head can raise a requirement from the Factory app: **More → Purchase requirement**. It takes two steps:

1. **Pick the items.** For each item, say how much to buy. Nothing is counted first.
2. **Say when it is needed and how urgent it is**, add a note if you like, and send it.

An older version of the app still asks how much is in the godown first; what it sends is recorded as a stock check of that item, and it holds nothing up. The phone then follows each item through to receipt, and an item can be cancelled from it until it is on a PO.

## What happens to each item next

Nothing is counted first: the PO comes straight away by the item's **purchase rule**, copied from the item when you raised it:

| Stage | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting for PO number** | Quotation: bought on the Quotation manager | Buy it there, picking this requirement in **Purchase it** — or type the PO number made there in the record and **Link PO** |
| **Create PO** | Direct purchase: no quotation | **Create PO** in the record |
| **Create or link PO** | Buyer decision | Either of the two |
| **Waiting for goods / Partly received** | On a PO | Receive the goods against its PO number |
| **Received / Closed short** | Done | — |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled with a reason | — |

## Getting the PO

Nothing is approved: the PO is ready to receive against straight away.

<Steps>
<Step title="Quotation — bought on the Quotation manager">
A manager marks the rate **Ready to buy** and the desk presses **Purchase it** with the number of drums. Its dialog asks **For requirement** — pick this one, and the PO is written onto it there and then, already **Sent**, under the number the dialog made (**PO-1243**); there is nothing to link. If the purchase was made without picking it, type that number in the requirement's record under **PO number**: the vendor, material, rate, drums and who bought it are fetched and checked against the requirement. Press **Link PO to requirement**. A wrong number can be **unlinked** until goods arrive.
</Step>
<Step title="Direct purchase — Create PO">
Press **Create PO**. It opens over the requirement's record, in three pages: **Vendor** (the item's default vendor first, the quantity, and **Also on this PO** — the other requirements ready for a PO, with no vendor yet or with this one, to put on the same PO with their own quantities), **Delivery** (where, by when, payment terms, freight, remarks) and **Review**. The rate is the item's price in Masters → Raw materials — nobody types an amount. An item with no price is ordered at **₹0**, and the Review page says so.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Cancelling

**Cancel requirement** asks for a reason. You cannot cancel another department's requirement, one that is on a PO, or one that has been received.

## Common questions

<Faq q="Why is there a PO number box on one item and a Create PO button on another?">
The item's purchase rule decides. A Quotation item is bought on the Quotation manager, which makes its PO; a Direct purchase item is created here at its price. A Buyer-decision item offers both.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The PO number is refused.">
It must be a number the Quotation manager made for a purchase of this same item — the message says what it found instead: no such number, a cancelled row, or a different material.
</Faq>

<Faq q="I can't see the requirements another department raised.">
In a production department you see your own department's requirements, and any you raised yourself. The Production Head, the store and administrators see every department's.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why is the rate ₹0 on my PO?">
The item has no price in Masters → Raw materials. Add one there to order it at a rate next time. The lot is held out of stock until the supplier's bill rate is entered.
</Faq>

<Faq q="I changed the item's purchase rule, but the old requirement still asks for a PO number.">
A requirement keeps the rule it was raised with, so a change to the item never re-routes work already in progress. Cancel it and raise a new one if it must change.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The 'In stock' column says no level set.">
It shows what the godown's re-order level counts as available. Without a level for that item at that godown there is nothing to show — set one on [Minimum levels](/docs/erp/stock-levels).
</Faq>
