The receipt side of the purchase flow is `lib/erp/screens/purchase.ts`: the `inward` (Goods receipt), `register` and `barcode` (Drum labels) tabs of the Purchases screen, the `testing` screen, and the raw-material stock tabs `rmStock` / `rmLog` that the Stock screen draws. The arithmetic is pure in `lib/erp/engines/purchase.ts`; the PO side it reports back to is `screens/purchase-flow.ts`; landed cost is read in `lib/erp/stock.ts`.

## Testing

<Code file="src/lib/erp/screens/purchase.ts" symbol="missingEvidence" />

`verify` checks `verifyTest`, refuses on missing evidence, then in one transaction marks the test Verified and — if no register row names this test — calls `createPurchase` with `source: "test"`, the tested density and the PO terms of its inward line (`poTermsOfInward`). A refused lot number rolls the whole verification back.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Every receipt path ends in refreshPurchaseOrder">
Inside its own transaction, so the PO and the stock land together.
</Step>
<Step title="A register row posts through syncPurchaseEntry only">
One entry per purchase, rewritten on every edit. Never insert into `erp_rm_entries` for a purchase anywhere else.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep money behind its power in the save, not just the column">
`savePurchase` falls back to the PO's rate for a caller without `viewPurchaseMoney`; a new money field needs the same.
</Step>
</Steps>
