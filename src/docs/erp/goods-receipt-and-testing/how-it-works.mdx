This is where a purchase stops being a promise and becomes stock. The rules here exist so that every litre in raw-material stock can answer three questions: **which PO bought it**, **did it pass its tests**, and **what did it really cost to get through the gate**.

## Nothing is received without a PO

<Rule title="A receipt names an open PO, and every line one of its items">
Goods inward starts from the item that arrived: the receipt is searched by item, and offers only the open lines of purchase orders that are Approved, Sent or Partly received. Every line names its PO and one of its lines, and one receipt may carry several POs — but only of one vendor, because a GRN is one vendor's delivery; the save refuses a second vendor. "Receive goods" on a PO, and "Add more" on a PR, still name the one PO in the header. There is no typed supplier and no typed item. A hand-entered register row names an open PO's line in the same way.
</Rule>

<Callout kind="why">
In Mahek Plus anything could be received from anybody. Tying every receipt to a PO means every lot in stock answers a requirement a department raised, bought the way its item's purchase rule says. No PO is approved any more — see [Purchase orders](/docs/erp/purchase-orders/how-it-works).
</Callout>

<Rule title="A receipt is never refused for its quantity; it corrects the PO">
Whatever arrived is received. The line's difference from what its PO line still expects (its quantity plus earlier corrections, less what was received, counting the other lines of the same save) becomes a **correction line** under the PO line: the extra, unless the gate chose to leave the PO alone, or the shortfall, only where the gate said nothing more is coming. A correction carries the PO line's own rate and GST and moves the PO's revision on by one. The PO line itself is never edited. A PO received in full in the last 30 days still takes a late truck, as an extra. A correction beyond <Setting k="erp.purchase.receiptTolerancePercent" />% of the original quantity is flagged, never refused. Before November 2026 anything past the PO plus 5% was refused.
</Rule>

<Rule title="A kilogram chemical is weighed, not typed">
For a chemical bought in kg the quantity is the **net weight off the scale**: weight with drums, less drums × the empty drum weight. A scale reading where the drums weigh as much as the load is refused as a misread scale, not recorded as a delivery of nothing.
</Rule>

## Every line goes where it is going, as it is saved

<Rule title="The item's testing list decides, not a person">
A line whose item has a testing list goes to the tester's queue; any other line becomes a register row at once. Mahek Plus asked somebody to press "Send to Testing" or "Send to Purchase" on every line — the item already answered which.
</Rule>

The buttons survive only as the way out for a line that went nowhere — almost always because the register refused its **lot number**. A lot number is the supplier's party code, the item code and the PR number joined together; a number already taken is refused with "That PR number is already taken. Change it, or generate a new one.", and the line waits rather than failing the whole delivery.

## Testing

<Rule title="Evidence for exactly the tests the item requires">
The test asks for what the item's testing list names: a pH photo, a smell (Good, Moderate, Bad), a colour photo, oil-paint, fast-paint, NC-paint and primer photos, a density reading between 0.5 and 1.5 with its photo, a thermocol-pass photo — and a photo of any other test the Testing list names. Recording a test is the department's job: anyone holding Lot testing.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Only the verifier decides, and only on complete evidence">
**Verify** needs the **Verify quality tests** power and is refused while any required evidence is missing. A verified test is closed: its evidence cannot be changed until it is marked Not Verified.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Verifying creates the register row, once">
The first verification writes the lot's register row, carrying the tested density, at the PO line's rate and GST. Verifying again never writes a second. Marking a verified test **Not Verified** leaves the register row in place and says so — removing it is a separate act.
</Rule>

With AI photo reading on, the test photos can be read into readings for the tester to confirm; a reading is a suggestion until somebody saves the review. See [Alerts and AI](/docs/erp/alerts-and-ai).

<Rule title="Lot testing is a queue: a lot is waiting before anybody has opened a test">
A line routed to testing is **To test** from the moment the GRN is saved — listed by its inward line, because no test exists yet, and **Record test** opens the test form already on it. Recorded and undecided is **To verify**; decided is **Verified** or **Failed**. A stored "Not Verified" is also the default before anybody decides, so a test counts as failed only once somebody decided it. To test and To verify are read whole, oldest first, whatever the period: a lot waiting is work, and a period picker on a queue hides the oldest lot — the one that matters. The sidebar badge counts both.
</Rule>

<Rule title="How long a lot has waited is measured from the gate, and 'too long' is a setting">
**Waiting** is the time since the lot was received. It turns red past <Setting k="erp.purchase.testWaitingHours" /> hours — the lot is out of stock all that time — and the threshold is configuration, never a number in the code. 0 never flags. It stops nothing.
</Rule>

## The register and stock

<Rule title="A lot is registered at the PO line's rate and GST">
Goods received against a PO reach stock without anybody typing a rate. The supplier's bill may still move the register's rate; a rate that no longer matches the PO line is flagged **Rate differs from PO**. Rows from before POs carry none and are flagged **Before POs** — nothing back-fills one.
</Rule>

<Rule title="Only a rated purchase posts to stock">
A register row with a rate above zero posts exactly one raw-material stock entry: its **available litres** at its godown under its lot. The entry follows the row through every edit — change the quantity and the stock moves with it. A row with no rate is not in stock and says why.
</Rule>

### The register's arithmetic

| Figure | How it is worked out |
|---|---|
| In litre | kg ÷ density for a kg purchase; otherwise the quantity |
| Available litres | In litre, less any feed-adjusted litres — what is posted to stock |
| Litre rate | rate × density for a kg purchase; otherwise the rate |
| Sub total | quantity × rate |
| GST | sub total × GST % |
| Final amount | sub total + GST, less any feed-adjusted amount |
| Litres per drum | available litres ÷ drums |

### One progress line for the bill

<Rule title="A purchase moves Arrived (or Tested) → Bill received → Matched → Verified">
The bill and the status are one line, so they cannot disagree. Recording the bill **is** the Invoice Received step; a status past Pending means the bill is in hand; a matched purchase cannot be marked "Bill Not Received". Everybody with purchase money may set Invoice Received or Purchase Matched; only somebody with **Set a purchase to Purchase Verified** sets Verified; only somebody with **Set a verified purchase back to Pending** undoes it.
</Rule>

<Callout kind="why">
Mahek Plus carried the test's verdict, a purchase status and a separate "bill received" toggle, and they could disagree — "Purchase Matched" with no bill. Making them one progress line removes the state that cannot be true.
</Callout>

<Rule title="Bills are grouped by party, because one bill comes from one party">
Bills opens on **Bill to come** and groups the lots by supplier; **Rate missing**, **Rate differs**, **Matched** and **Every lot** are the other sub-tabs. **Matched** is a lot whose bill is in hand and whose rate is entered and agrees with the PO line. A party's heading carries **Record bill for n lots**: the same act as ticking the lots and pressing Record the bill — one bill number on each of that party's lots in the open sub-tab that has none — over every page of the group. Bill to come and Matched read the purchase stage, which only somebody with purchase money sees, so for anybody else those two sub-tabs and the group button are not drawn rather than drawn empty.
</Rule>

## Landing cost

<Rule title="The stock rate is the landed rate">
What a lot cost is its material (the bill's value before GST — GST on a purchase is input credit, not cost) **plus its share** of the PR's transport and other inward cost. That landed rate, turned into a litre rate by density, is the rate raw-material stock is valued at — so an SFG batch's cost includes getting its raw material through the gate.
</Rule>

| How it came | Transport cost |
|---|---|
| Supplier Transport, Third-Party Transport | The amount from their bill |
| Mahek Own Vehicle | Kilometres × <Setting k="erp.purchase.ownVehicleRatePerKmPaise" /> paise, copied onto the PR so a later rate change never reprices the journey |
| No Transport Charges | Zero |

<Rule title="Shared by value, to the paisa">
A PR's transport and other cost is shared across its lots **in proportion to their material value**, so freight does not land on cheap bulky items. The shares always add up to exactly what was spent. A lot with no rate yet takes no share until it has one.
</Rule>

With our own vehicle and no approved rate per kilometre, the receipt is refused rather than costed at nothing. A PR whose transport nobody recorded says so on the register rather than reading as free delivery.

## Drum labels

Drum labels (Purchase › Tools) is the register's chemical lots **at every godown you are assigned to** (an administrator is assigned to all of them), with the short label name from the "Short label name (Item=Label)" reference list. It used to show the working godown only, and an empty list because the header named another godown was the commonest dead end on the page; the godown is a column and a Filters section instead. Each row's **Print n** draws the lot's labels from the register — 80 × 80 mm, one per drum — so the printer is never sent typed text; ticking lots and pressing **Print n labels** prints them all in one go. The label page opens for anybody holding Drum labels, Bills or Stock on hand.

<Rule title="Every print is recorded, reprints included">
Each time a lot's drum labels go to paper — sent to the label printer, saved as a print file, or the print dialog shown — the lot gets a row in its print log: how many labels, who, when, and whether it had been printed before. **To print** is a lot with no print; **Printed** says the last print, who printed it and how many times. Lots bought before the log began have no row and read as To print, so both sub-tabs keep the period picker.
</Rule>

## Where the code and the intent disagree today

- The refusal for a missing own-vehicle rate says the rate is set "in ERP settings"; it is a MahekOne setting in the Admin Console, not on the ERP's Settings screen.
- A lot entered by hand on the register (**New purchase** or **Add another**) is registered — and, with a rate, put into stock — without a test, even for a chemical whose item requires one. Only lots that come through goods receipt are sent to the tester.
