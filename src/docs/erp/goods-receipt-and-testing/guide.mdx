When a lorry arrives at the gate, three things happen in the ERP: the goods are **received against their purchase order** (a GRN, numbered as a PR), a chemical is **tested** before anybody may use it, and every lot ends up in the **purchase register** with a rate — which is what puts it into stock. The Purchases screen has three tabs for this — Goods receipt (GRN), Register and Drum labels — and Lot testing is its own screen.

Each line of a receipt is grouped under its PR number, with its POs, date, supplier, godown and transport in the group's heading. The tabs open on **this month** — the period picker in the toolbar shows more — and a line not yet sent anywhere always shows.

<Steps>
<Step title="Search the item that arrived">
Type the item's name or code. Items with a PO still waiting are listed first, with how many POs, how much is pending and from which vendors. An item with no PO is still listed, greyed out as **Cannot receive** — its requirement needs its PO first: linked from the Quotation manager, or created on the requirement.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the PO it came against">
Only the POs still waiting for that item are offered, oldest due date first, each with what was ordered, received and is still pending. Where only one PO is waiting it is picked for you and this step is skipped. The vendor comes from the PO, and the **Godown** starts as its deliver-to godown — change it if the goods came somewhere else.
</Step>
<Step title="Another item from the same truck">
Press **Add another item from this truck** and search again. Only the same vendor's open POs are offered — one GRN is one vendor's delivery — and they may be different POs of that vendor. For a chemical, enter the **Drums** on each line.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter what actually came">
For a chemical bought in kilograms, enter the **Weight with drum** off the scale and the **Empty drum weight** (per drum); the **Weight without drum** is worked out for you, whatever anybody typed elsewhere. For anything else, the **Quantity received** starts at what is pending — change it to what actually arrived. For a chemical, **Drums belong to** starts as the supplier's usual answer; change it where this delivery's drums are returnable, or are ours to keep — see [Empty drums and scrap](/docs/erp/empty-drums-scrap).
</Step>
<Step title="Transport and landing cost">
How the goods came: **Supplier Transport**, **Mahek Own Vehicle**, **Third-Party Transport** or **No Transport Charges**. For supplier or third-party transport enter the cost from their bill; for our own vehicle enter the **kilometres** and the **tempo number** — the cost is worked out from the approved rate per kilometre. Add any **other direct inward cost** (loading, unloading, octroi) and what it was.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
"PR 1230 · 2 lines received against PO-90, PO-92 · 1 to testing, 1 in the register". Every line is saved under one PR number, each against its own PO.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warn" title="More than the PO allows is refused">
A line may take a little more than was ordered — drums never weigh exactly — but beyond that the receipt is refused: "More than the PO allows: … Raise a requirement for the extra."
</Callout>

### After saving

| On a receipt line | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add more** | Adds another line to the same PR and PO |
| **Transport & costs** | Records or corrects the PR's transport and other cost — once per PR |
| **Send to Testing / Send to Purchase** | Only on a line that did not go anywhere — usually because the register refused its lot number. Change the PR number and send it again |

## Testing a chemical

Every chemical lot is tested before it reaches the register. The tester records evidence for the tests the item requires; only the **verifier** decides.

<Steps>
<Step title="Record evidence">
Open the test and press **Record evidence**. The form asks only for the tests this item requires.
</Step>
<Step title="Take the photos and readings">
| Test | Evidence |
|---|---|
| PH | pH photo (and the pH value, read from the photo when AI is on — confirm it) |
| Smell | Good, Moderate or Bad |
| Color | Colour photo |
| Oil Paint, Fast Paints, Nc Paints, Primer | A photo of each |
| Density | The density value (0.5 to 1.5) and its photo |
| Tharmakol Pass | Thermocol-pass photo |
| Any other test on the Testing list | A photo of it |
</Step>
<Step title="The verifier decides">
**Verify** is refused while any required evidence is missing ("Evidence missing: …"). Verifying creates the lot's register row, once.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Add another lot** copies a test to another lot of the same delivery. If AI photo reading is on, **Read test photos** fills the readings for you to review; nothing is saved until you save the review.

## The purchase register

Every lot bought, with what it cost. A row arrives on its own — from a receipt line that needed no test, or from a verified test — at the PO line's rate and GST.

### A purchase moves along one line

**Arrived** (or **Tested**) → **Bill received** → **Matched** → **Verified**.

| Action | Who | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Enter rate** | Purchase money | Only on a row with no rate. "Posted · … of lot … now in stock" |
| **Record the bill** | Purchase money | Records the supplier's bill number; the stage becomes Bill received |
| **Bill Not Received** | Purchase money | Undoes it — refused once the purchase is matched |
| **Change status** | Purchase money | Invoice Received or Purchase Matched; the verifier sets only Purchase Verified |
| **Back to pending** | The reopen power (an admin) | Sets a verified purchase back to Pending |
| **Read supplier bill** | Purchase money, AI bills on | Reads a photo or PDF of the bill and matches it to this PR's rows for you to review |
| **New purchase**, **Edit**, **Add another** | The screen | Enter a lot by hand against a PO, correct a row, or add another lot to the PR |
| **Print drum label** | The screen | Opens the lot's drum label to print — see below |
| **Mark bill received** | Purchase money | On the rows you have ticked: records every selected purchase's bill as received at once |

<Callout kind="money">
A lot is in stock only once it has a rate above zero. A row from a PO already has the PO's rate, so it is in stock the moment it is saved. "Not posted: rate missing" in the Inventory column says why one is not.
</Callout>

Flags on a row: **Purchase rate empty**, **Rate differs from PO** (the bill moved the rate away from the PO's), **Before POs** (bought before purchase orders), **Filled from bill** (AI read the bill).

## Drum labels

A list of chemical purchases at **your working godown**, with each lot's short label name, litres, drums, litres per drum, density and PR bill number — the figures printed on a drum label. **Print drum label** on a row (also on the register) opens that lot's label, 80 × 80 mm and one per drum, to send to the label printer, save as a printer file or print from the browser. The list can still be downloaded.

## Common questions

<Faq q="The PO I need is not in the receipt form.">
It is already fully received, or it was closed or cancelled — or no PO has been made for the item yet: bought on the Quotation manager, or created on the requirement. Only a PO with something pending can be received against.
</Faq>

<Faq q="'! Please Change 4 Digit PR Num Manually or Generate New PR Num!'">
The lot number (party code, item code and PR number) is already used by another register row. The line is kept without a register row; change the PR number and press Send to Purchase.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Own vehicle says there is no approved rate per kilometre.">
Nobody has set the rate yet. Until an administrator sets <Setting k="erp.purchase.ownVehicleRatePerKmPaise" /> in the Admin Console's settings, an own-vehicle receipt cannot be saved — choose the transport that applies or ask for the rate.
</Faq>

<Faq q="A test was verified by mistake.">
The verifier can mark it **Mark not verified**. The register row it created stays — correct or remove that with the office.
</Faq>
