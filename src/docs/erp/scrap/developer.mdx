## The drum

<Code file="src/lib/erp/engines/scrap.ts" symbol="pickDrumSeq" />

`recordEmptyDrum` takes the purchase row `for update`, re-reads the seqs already recorded and picks inside the transaction, so two people weighing one lot get two numbers. `erp_empty_drums_drum_key (purchase_id, seq)` is the backstop. `erp_empty_drums_route_check` makes a status impossible without the owner it needs.

## FIFO

<Code file="src/lib/erp/engines/scrap.ts" symbol="fifoPick" />

The sale locks every available Mahek drum (`for update`), picks, and updates `where status = 'available'`, refusing if fewer rows moved than were picked. Other scrap takes an advisory lock on `scrap:<category>:<unit>` and re-reads the balance.
