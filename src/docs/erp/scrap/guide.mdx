**Scrap & empty drums** follows a drum from the day its material is used up to the day it leaves — sold as scrap if it is Mahek's, returned to the supplier if it is theirs — and follows the money a scrap buyer owes until the accountant has matched every rupee.

## Weighing an empty drum

Open **Record empty drum** (or go to `/erp/empty-drums` on the phone or tablet at the scale).

1. Scan the drum's lot label, or type the lot number. To name one drum of the lot, add `/` and its number — `ATTOL1228/3`.
2. The GRN's details appear: material, supplier, lot, PR, how many drums the lot came in, the supplier's declared tare and who owns the drums. The drum boxes show which are already recorded.
3. Weigh the drum and type its empty weight in kg. The difference from the declared tare is shown as you type.
4. **Save drum**. It goes into Mahek's stock or the vendor's, by the lot's owner.

Record a drum **the day it is emptied**, while its label can still be read. Once it is recorded the label no longer matters — nobody reads it again to sell or return it.

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| *Drum 2 of this lot is already recorded as empty* | That drum was saved before. Nothing is added again. |
| *All 6 drums of this lot are already recorded* | Every drum of the lot is in. Check the label. |
| *No GRN lot … was found* | The label does not match a lot. Do not record it another way — tell the Production Head. |
| *… is above the … kg an empty drum is expected to weigh* | Weigh again. If it really is that, the Production Head can save it with a reason. |
| **Owner to decide** | The GRN did not say who owns the drums. The drum is saved; the Production Head decides for the whole lot. |

## The tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Empty drums** | Every drum ever recorded, with its GRN lot, weight, variance and where it is now. The Production Head decides ownership, corrects a weight or cancels a record here — always with a reason, keeping the original. |
| **Mahek stock** | The ledger of Mahek-owned drums: emptied in, sold out, cancelled out, and the balance after each. |
| **Vendor drums** | Each supplier's drums waiting to go back, how long the oldest has waited, and **Return drums**. |
| **Returns** | Every return, with the drums it took and their lots. |
| **Scrap sales** | Every sale, what it was worth, what has been received and what is outstanding. **New scrap sale** is here. |
| **Other scrap** | Plastic, cardboard and other scrap, recorded in batches as it is collected, and what has been sold of it. |
| **Buyers** | Each scrap buyer: what they bought, paid and still owe, by age. |
| **Receipts** | Cash and bank receipts from scrap buyers, and which sales each is matched to. |
| **Tare variance** | Actual empty weight against the supplier's declared tare, by supplier, material and lot. |

## Selling scrap

**New scrap sale** asks for the buyer (pick one or type a new name), the category, the quantity, the unit and the rate. The amount is quantity × rate.

- **Empty metal drums** are sold by number. The ERP takes the **oldest** Mahek-owned empty drums and records which they were — nobody picks out drums or reads labels. A vendor's drum is never offered.
- **Plastic, cardboard and other scrap** can be sold only up to what has been recorded as collected on **Other scrap**.
- **Invoice**: if an external invoice was issued, its number is required. If not, leave the reference empty or enter a genuine challan number — the sale's **SS** number identifies it. Never make an invoice number up.

## Getting paid

A sale is never marked paid by the person who sold it. When the buyer's money reaches petty cash or the bank, the accountant records it on **Receipts** and matches it to the buyer's sales — by default oldest first. One receipt can pay several sales and one sale can be paid by several receipts. Outstanding is the sale amount less what has been matched.

## Returning vendor drums

On **Vendor drums**, **Return drums** asks only how many went back, the date and the challan. The oldest of that supplier's waiting drums are marked returned.
