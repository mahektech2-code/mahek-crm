Nothing is bought without a purchase order. A PO comes from a requirement by the item's purchase rule — **linked** from the Quotation manager for a quotation item, or **created** on the requirement at the item's price for a direct purchase — and **no PO is approved**: it is ready to send and receive against as soon as it exists. This page is for the store and whoever creates or sends POs.

## Where a PO comes from

<Steps>
<Step title="Linked from the Quotation manager">
For a **Quotation** item. The desk presses **Purchase it** on the Quotation manager with the number of drums, and the PO number is made for them. The dialog also asks which requirement it is for (or **None waiting — raise one with this purchase**), the quantity, the godown and the delivery date, and the **PO appears here at once**, already **Sent** — nobody has to link it. Buying a second material from the same party, the **PO number** box offers **Add to PO-…** for that party's open PO (the one made today first), so several materials bought together are one PO with a line each. A purchase made from an older open page only makes the number; then somebody types it on the requirement's record and presses **Link PO**: the PO is kept here under the same number, with the sheet's vendor, rate and drums, at GST <Setting k="erp.purchase.poGstPercent" />%, already **Sent** — the desk sent it when they bought it. Several requirements linked to one number are one PO.
</Step>
<Step title="Created on the requirement">
For a **Direct purchase** item. **Create PO** on the requirement opens three pages — Vendor, Delivery, Review — over its record. The rate is the item's price in Masters → Raw materials; nobody types an amount, and an item with no price is ordered at ₹0. **Also on this PO** on the Vendor page lists the other requirements ready for a PO with no vendor yet or with the vendor picked — the vendor's own already ticked — so one PO carries every item for that vendor, each at its own price. The PO is **Approved**: send it to the vendor.
</Step>
<Step title="New PO (this screen)">
Still here for a PO of several requirements to one vendor, but it offers only requirements that are ready for a PO and **already carry a vendor** — a direct-purchase item with a default vendor in Masters gets it when the requirement is raised, and a requirement whose PO was cancelled keeps its own. Here the rate and GST are typed on each line. It is created **Approved** — no approval.
</Step>
</Steps>

Every requirement on a PO must be for that PO's vendor, may appear only once, and may not already be on another live PO.

<Callout kind="rule" title="No approval on any route">
A PO is ready to send and receive against the moment it is created or linked. POs raised before this change that still say **Pending approval** keep their **Approve** and **Send back** buttons until they are decided.
</Callout>

**Edit** corrects an **Approved** PO — one created here or on a requirement — while nothing has been received and before it is shared (purchase money needed). A Quotation manager PO is Sent from the start, so it is not edited here.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose how">
**WhatsApp**, **Email** or **Copy link**. Each says what will happen — sent through Wati, or opened on your own WhatsApp with the message written; sent from MahekOne with the PDF attached, or opened in your mail app.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who">
The vendor is ticked, with the number and email from Masters → Suppliers. The godown the goods go to is offered. Type anybody else under **+ Someone else** — mobile numbers for WhatsApp, emails for email, separated by commas. Somebody with no number or email on record cannot be ticked, and says so.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the message and send">
The message is written out from the PO — the items, the total, where and by when. Change it if you like. The link to the PO's PDF is added under it.
</Step>
<Step title="See what happened">
The window then lists each person: **Sent**, or **Open WhatsApp** / **Open email** to send it from your own app, or why it failed. **Copy link** and **Copy message** are there too.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="info" title="The link opens the PDF without signing in">
Anybody with the link can open the PO as a PDF, so share it only with the people it is for. It stops working if the PO is cancelled or closed short.
</Callout>

The first share moves an Approved PO to **Sent**, and the list's **Last shared** column says how, to whom, when and by whom. Sharing needs the purchase-money power, because the PDF carries the rates. **Print / PDF** opens the PO to print or save from the browser.

## Receiving, closing and cancelling

| Action | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Receive goods** | Approved, Sent or Partly received | Opens the goods receipt with this PO and its pending items — see [Goods receipt](/docs/erp/goods-receipt-and-testing) |
| **Close short** | Partly received | Nothing more is expected; its requirements count as received. Needs a reason |
| **Cancel PO** | Approved or Sent, with nothing received | Its requirements go back to waiting for a PO. Needs a reason. A Quotation manager PO can also be **unlinked** from its requirement's record |

Once anything has been received against a PO it cannot be cancelled — close it short instead.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | Created here, not yet sent |
| **Sent** | Sent to the vendor; nothing received yet |
| **Partly received** | Some items, or part of one, have arrived |
| **Received** | Every line received in full |
| **Closed** | Closed short with a reason |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled before anything arrived |
| **Pending approval** / **Rejected** | Only POs raised before approval was dropped |

## Common questions

<Faq q="The vendor I want is not in the New PO list.">
Only vendors with a requirement ready for a PO, and already given that vendor, are offered. Use **Create PO** on the requirement instead — it picks the vendor as it makes the PO. A quotation item is never put on a PO here — buy it on the Quotation manager, or link the PO number from there on the requirement.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I change the rate on a direct-purchase PO?">
Create PO always takes the item's price in Masters → Raw materials, so the right fix is usually to change the price there. **Edit** on this screen does let the rate be typed while the PO is still Approved — not yet shared, nothing received — and it must then be above zero. The supplier's bill rate is entered on the register as before.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Goods arrived but Receive goods is greyed out.">
Goods receipt is a separate screen ("Purchases"). If it is not on your account, ask the store to receive it.
</Faq>
