Nothing is bought without a purchase order. A PO comes from a requirement by the item's purchase rule — **linked** from the Quotation manager for a quotation item, or **created** on the requirement at the item's price for a direct purchase — and **no PO is approved**: it is ready to send and receive against as soon as it exists. This page is for the store and whoever creates or sends POs.

## Where a PO comes from

<Steps>
<Step title="Linked from the Quotation manager">
For a **Quotation** item. The desk presses **Purchase it** on the Quotation manager with the number of drums, and the PO number is made for them. On the requirement's drawer somebody types that number and presses **Link PO**: the PO is kept here under the same number, with the sheet's vendor, rate and drums, at GST 18% (Settings), already **Sent** — the desk sent it when they bought it. Several requirements linked to one number are one PO.
</Step>
<Step title="Created on the requirement">
For a **Direct purchase** item. **Create PO** on the requirement opens three pages — Vendor, Delivery, Review — over its drawer. The rate is the item's price in Masters → Raw materials; nobody types an amount, and an item with no price is ordered at ₹0. The PO is **Approved**: send it to the vendor.
</Step>
<Step title="New purchase order (this screen)">
Still here for a PO of several requirements to one vendor. It is created **Approved** — no approval.
</Step>
</Steps>

Every requirement on a PO must be for that PO's vendor, may appear only once, and may not already be on another live PO.

<Callout kind="rule" title="No approval on any route">
A PO is ready to send and receive against the moment it is created or linked. POs raised before this change that still say **Pending approval** keep their **Approve** and **Send back** buttons until they are decided.
</Callout>

**Edit** corrects a PO created here while nothing has been received against it (purchase money needed).

## Sending it to the vendor

<Steps>
<Step title="Send to vendor">
Choose how: **WhatsApp**, **Email**, **Printed copy** or **Phone**. WhatsApp is offered first when the vendor has a mobile number.
</Step>
<Step title="Send it">
The PO is marked Sent, and a dialog shows the PO written out — every item, quantity, rate, GST, the totals, where to deliver, by when, payment terms, and "Please quote PO-91 on your invoice and delivery challan." **Open WhatsApp** or **Open email** starts the message with the text in it; **Copy** copies it.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Print PO** opens the PO as the vendor receives it, ready to print or save as a PDF from the browser. Printing needs the purchase-money power because the rates are on the paper. **Send again** records another send.

## Receiving, closing and cancelling

| Action | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Receive goods** | Approved, Sent or Partly received | Opens the goods receipt with this PO and its pending items — see [Goods receipt](/docs/erp/goods-receipt-and-testing) |
| **Close short** | Partly received | Nothing more is expected; its requirements count as received. Needs a reason |
| **Cancel PO** | Approved or Sent, with nothing received | Its requirements go back to waiting for a PO. Needs a reason. A Quotation manager PO can also be **unlinked** from its requirement's drawer |

Once anything has been received against a PO it cannot be cancelled — close it short instead.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | Created here, not yet sent |
| **Sent** | Sent to the vendor; nothing received yet |
| **Partly received** | Some items, or part of one, have arrived |
| **Received** | Every line received in full |
| **Closed** | Closed short with a reason |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled before anything arrived |
| **Pending approval** / **Rejected** | Only POs raised before approval was dropped |

## Common questions

<Faq q="The vendor I want is not in the New purchase order list.">
Only vendors with a requirement ready for a PO are offered. A quotation item is never put on a PO here — link the PO number from the Quotation manager on the requirement instead.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I change the rate on a direct-purchase PO?">
No — it is the item's price in Masters → Raw materials. Change the price there; the supplier's bill rate is entered on the register as before.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Goods arrived but Receive goods is greyed out.">
Goods receipt is a separate screen ("Purchases"). If it is not on your account, ask the store to receive it.
</Faq>
