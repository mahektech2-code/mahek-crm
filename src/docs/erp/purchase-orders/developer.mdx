Purchase orders are the `purchaseOrders` screen module in `lib/erp/screens/purchase-flow.ts`, with the arithmetic and status rules in `lib/erp/engines/purchase-flow.ts`. The two routes that make a PO from a requirement — Create PO and Link PO — and the Quotation manager's Purchase it live in `lib/erp/screens/requirement-po.ts`. No route approves a PO. The receiving side lives in `screens/purchase.ts` and calls back into `refreshPurchaseOrder`. The printable PO is its own route, `app/erp/po/[id]/page.tsx`.

## Permissions

- **Module:** <ModuleRef k="erp.purchaseOrders" />. Receive goods also needs <ModuleRef k="erp.register" /> (the Purchases screen, whose Goods receipt tab is `inward`).
- **Powers:** `viewPurchaseMoney` (New PO, Edit, print, share — Create PO on a requirement does not need it), `approvePurchaseOrder` (approve or send back a leftover Pending approval PO).
- Create PO on a requirement also checks the `purchaseOrders` screen; Link PO checks only that the requirement is visible to the person's department.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Never store what was received">
Read it with `receivedByLine`. A stored figure needs a recompute path and a way to be wrong.
</Step>
<Step title="Every receipt path calls refreshPurchaseOrder in its transaction">
A new way of receiving goods that forgets leaves the PO at Sent with the goods in stock.
</Step>
<Step title="Every route that writes a PO checks the requirement again inside its transaction">
`savePurchaseOrder`, `createRequirementPo` and `orderFromQuote` lock the requirement and refuse one already on a live PO, so two POs made at once cannot both take it.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Known gaps, with file references

| Gap | Where |
|---|---|
| A share opened on the sender's own app counts as Sent before it leaves | `lib/erp/po-share.ts` `sharePo` |
| `close` and `cancel` check no power | `purchaseOrders.actions.close`, `.cancel` |
| The company on the PDF and the message is the literal "Mahek Marketing India" | `lib/erp/po-share.ts` `COMPANY` |
| Send back writes `approved_by_id` / `approved_at` for a rejection | `purchaseOrders.actions.sendBack` |
| The screen's subtitle and the `approvePurchaseOrder` power's description still speak of approving a PO | `lib/erp/registry.ts`, `lib/erp/powers.ts` |
| New PO's line form never fills a rate (`rateOf` is declared and left empty), so every New PO rate is typed | `purchaseOrderForm` |
