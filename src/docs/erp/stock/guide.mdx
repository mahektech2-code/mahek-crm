The Stock group answers "what do we have, where is it, and where did it go?". **Stock** shows every lot at every godown, stage by stage, with every entry behind it one switch away. **Boxes & labels** shows every box by its own id and prints its labels. **Item transfers** moves stock between godowns — and writes stock off into Item Lost Record. Minimum levels have their own page: [Minimum levels and reordering](/docs/erp/stock-levels).

### The four stages

| Tab | Counted in | One row is | Comes in from | Goes out by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Raw material** | litres, or pieces | a purchase lot at a godown | a rated purchase; a transfer in | SFG batches; cans filled and boxes packed; transfers out |
| **Semi-finished** | litres | an SFG lot at a godown (with its QC status) | SFG batches; transfers in | FG filling; transfers out |
| **Finished goods** | cans | an FG lot at a godown | FG filling (net of cans lost); transfers in | packing lines; allocation to orders; transfers out |
| **Packed** | boxes | a packing batch at a godown | a complete packing batch; transfers in | allocation to orders; transfers out |

The available view lists only lots with stock above zero. Every row opens to the actions that apply: **Trace this lot**, **Print box labels** on a packing batch, and on a finished-goods lot **Label loose units** and **Print loose labels**.

### Labelling loose cans

Cans that will leave **loose** — not in a box — need their own unit id, so they can be scanned at dispatch.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the FG lot and press Label loose units">
The prompt starts at the cans that do not have an id yet. A lot with no loose SKU to sell as cannot be labelled — box it instead.
</Step>
<Step title="Create unit ids">
"20 unit ids created · print them from 'Print loose labels'". Cans that will be boxed are not labelled here; they get their box's id when the packing batch completes.
</Step>
</Steps>

### The entry logs

Each stage's **Show every entry** list shows the date, the kind of entry (Purchase or Transfer for raw material; the batch, fill or packing batch for the others), the item, lot, quantity, godown and the document that wrote it. An entry whose document has since been deleted is flagged **Source deleted**, and the dashboard counts them as orphaned inventory entries.

### Printing labels

Labels are drawn from the record, never typed. A product label carries the box's id in its QR and the lot it came from beside it. Print:

- **one box** — from its row;
- **a selection** — tick boxes and press Print labels;
- **a whole packing batch** — Print box labels on the batch or on Stock → Packed;
- **every labelled loose can of an FG lot** — Print loose labels on Stock → Finished goods;
- **dispatch stickers for an order** — from the [dispatch desk](/docs/erp/dispatch).

When the sheet is sent to the printer, each box records when and how many times its label was printed.

## Item transfers

Moving stock from one godown to another, or writing it off.

<Steps>
<Step title="New transfer">
**Transfer date**, then **Item type**: Purchase (raw material), Semi Finished, Finish Goods or FG Packing.
</Step>
<Step title="Where from and what">
**From godown** (your working location to start), **Item**, **Lot** — only lots with stock at that godown — and **Lot available**.
</Step>
<Step title="How much and where to">
**Transfer quantity** and **Transfer to godown** — any other active godown. Add a **Remark**.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
"Transfer Solvent A 200?" — then "Transferred 200 of Solvent A to Godown B".
</Step>
</Steps>

Boxes and labelled cans travel with the stock: the lowest-numbered boxes still on the shelf of that lot move to the new godown.

### Writing stock off: Item Lost Record

If you hold the write-off power, **Item Lost Record** appears in the "Transfer to godown" list. A transfer into it **writes the stock off**: "200 of Solvent A written off". Any boxes moved with it are marked Written off. The transfer is flagged **Lost stock** on the list, and the dashboard counts what was written off this month.

<Callout kind="warn">
Stock never moves **out** of Item Lost Record, and it never appears in the "From godown" list. A write-off is final; if goods are found, they come back in as a fresh receipt or are corrected by the office.
</Callout>

| Refusal | Means |
|---|---|
| You Selected The Same Location, Change To Location | From and To are the same godown |
| Select Correct Quantity! | Zero, or more than the lot holds |
| Writing stock off to Item Lost Record needs the lost-stock power | You do not hold it |
| That godown is not active | The destination is switched off |

## Common questions

<Faq q="Stock shows a negative quantity on a can or box lot.">
More cans were filled, or boxes packed, at that godown than its lots held. The extra is left visible on the newest lot rather than hidden — check the receipts and the fills.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why did FG stock drop before the lorry left?">
Finished goods and packed stock leave the lot when they are **allocated** to an order, not when they are dispatched, so they cannot be promised twice.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I delete a transfer?">
No. Make a transfer back the other way; both stay on the list.
</Faq>

<Faq q="A box is not listed on 'In stock & at dispatch'.">
It has been dispatched, rejected, written off or cancelled. Switch to **Every box**.
</Faq>
