`lib/erp/stock.ts` is the one statement of "current stock, lot wise" per stage; every list, form, validation and dashboard tile calls it. The Stock screen's eight tabs are screen modules spread over `screens/purchase.ts` (`rmStock`, `rmLog`) and `screens/production.ts` (`sfgStock`, `fgStock`, `packStock` and their logs). Boxes are `screens/units.ts` over the register in `lib/erp/units.ts`; transfers are `screens/movement.ts`; label sheets are the route `app/erp/print-labels`.

## Permissions

- **Modules:** <ModuleRef k="erp.stock" /> (all eight tabs), <ModuleRef k="erp.units" /> (both tabs), <ModuleRef k="erp.transfers" />.
- **Powers:** `viewCost` (rate and value columns), `lostStock` (Item Lost Record offered and accepted), `dispatchOverride` (reject, mark returned).
- **Label printing** (`requireLabelScreen` in `actions/erp-trace.ts`) accepts any of units, dispatch, packBatches, stock or orders.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="A new consuming document">
Add it to the stage's `outflow` CTE in `stock.ts` — never write a negative entry — and take `lockLot` in its save.
</Step>
<Step title="A new producing document">
Write exactly one inflow entry per source (`source_type`, `source_id`) and re-sync it on every edit, like `syncPurchaseEntry` and `syncSfgEntry`. Add the source type to the orphan count in `dashboard.ts` and to the log.
</Step>
<Step title="Never change a box's history">
Append an event with `unitEvent`. A status change outside `manualMove`'s four moves belongs to its own screen (dispatch, transfer, packing).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Known gaps, with file references

| Gap | Where |
|---|---|
| An FG transfer copies SKU and packing from the product's latest FG entry, not from the lot transferred | `screens/movement.ts` `postTransferIn` |
| The units lists are capped at 5,000 rows with no notice on screen | `screens/units.ts` `unitRows` (`limit 5000`) |
| SFG and fill deletions leave their output entry in stock while giving the input back | `screens/production.ts` delete actions; `stock.ts` |
