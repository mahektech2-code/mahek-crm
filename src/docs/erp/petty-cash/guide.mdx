## Doing the work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give funds">On **Funds given**, press **Give funds** and enter the date, godown, employee, mode and amount. Choose a credit note if one applies.</Step>
  <Step title="Record an expense">On **Expenses**, press **New expense**. The message after saving says what is left with that person, and warns if they have spent more than they were given.</Step>
  <Step title="Verify">A manager opens an expense and presses **Verify**, or ticks several and verifies them together. **Back to Pending** reopens one.</Step>
</Steps>

<Faq q="Why can I not change the amount?">
A verified expense is closed. A manager can move it back to Pending first.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Verify is greyed out.">
A manager verifies expenses.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The available figure is negative.">
More has been spent than was given to that person at that godown in that mode. The row is flagged, and the overnight alerts list it.
</Faq>
