Petty Cash is one ERP module for every rupee that moves outside the order book: what we pay, what comes in, money moved between our own accounts, and lump sums handed to employees. It has **one row of tabs** — **Home**, **Expenses**, **Petty cash**, **Advances**, **Money received**, **Transfers**, **Statements**, and **To approve** when something is waiting — with everything rarely needed under **More**. Every real movement of money is entered **once**, gets one number (`TXN-26-00423`), and changes the balances once.

## Recording money going out

<Steps>
  <Step title="An office expense">**Expenses → Add expense**. An expense is entered when it is paid — there is no "not paid yet". One page: the amount, who was paid, the category, what it was for, the bill number, the date, and **Paid from** — the account the money left. For a bank account it also asks how (NEFT, UPI, Cheque…) and, if you have it, the UTR or cheque number. Add a photo of the bill.</Step>
  <Step title="Production petty cash">**Petty cash → Add petty cash spend**. The same form, for the Production Head's small spends, with a photo of the bill. Paid from can also be **Cash from a customer** or **Cash withdrawn from a bank** — then choose whose cash or which withdrawal, and only cash with something left is offered. The accountant checks it after you save.</Step>
  <Step title="If it says it is a duplicate">The same bill number from the same payee, or for the same amount, is stopped with the earlier transaction named under the bill number. If this really is a different bill, tick **This is a different bill — save it anyway**.</Step>
</Steps>

## Giving an employee a lump sum (advances)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give the advance">**Advances → Give advance**: to whom, how much, the date, what for, the day their bills are due, and the account it is paid from. It is not an expense yet — the money has moved into their hands.</Step>
  <Step title="They submit their bills">Open the advance and press **Submit bills**. One line per bill: date, category, who was paid, bill number, amount and a photo. A spend with no bill needs a sentence saying why.</Step>
  <Step title="The accountant reviews">Each bill appears on **To approve**. **Approve**, or **Send back** with what needs fixing.</Step>
  <Step title="Settle">When nothing is waiting, **Settle**. Anything unspent comes back into an account; anything spent beyond the advance is paid to the employee. Both are one transaction.</Step>
</Steps>

## Money coming in

**Money received → Add money received**: the amount, who paid — a customer from the list, or a name — which account it came into, the date and what it was for; for a bank account, how it came and the UTR. For a customer, it is put against their unpaid bills, oldest first; with none unpaid, the whole amount is kept on their account. **More ▾** narrows the list to money received in bank, in cash, or not yet in bank. A cash receipt shows, when opened, **how this cash was used**: deposited, spent as petty cash, or still in hand. Scrap buyers' payments are recorded on [Scrap sales → Receipts](/docs/erp/empty-drums-scrap) and appear here too.

## Moving money between our own accounts

**Transfers → Move money**: from which account, to which, the amount and the date — the form shows both balances before and after. A withdrawal is Bank → Cash in Hand; a deposit is Cash in Hand → Bank. A transfer changes both balances and is never income or expense. A deposit of a customer's cash can name the receipt it came from.

## Statements, and the bank's own statement

**Statements** is our own record of each account — Kotak Bank, ICICI Bank, Cash in Hand, Petty Cash, one switch each inside it: the opening balance, what was entered going in and out, and the closing balance it leaves. **More › Accounts** lists the accounts themselves, which the accountant sets up with their opening balances. On a bank account each row says **✓ Bank** once the bank's own statement shows that money, or **Not in bank yet**.

The bank's own statement is not kept here. It is uploaded as a PDF on **Accounts › Bank statement management**, read by AI, and each line matched there — to a customer receipt, a vendor payout, or a Petty Cash expense or transfer. A line marked there as an expense, a charge or a transfer is entered on Petty Cash for you, and ticked here.

<Faq q="I need to change an expense.">
The transaction's record on the Petty Cash screens has no **Correct** button yet, though the rule for one is written (whoever entered it until it is approved, then the accountant). Today the way to fix a wrong entry is to **Cancel this transaction** with a reason and enter it again.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I entered something wrong.">
Open it and press **Cancel this transaction**, with a reason. It stays on record, marked cancelled, and stops counting everywhere. Nothing is deleted. It is refused while a bank line is matched to it or cash from it has been paid onward, and a scrap receipt is cancelled from Scrap sales instead.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Approve is greyed out.">
Only the accountant for Petty Cash approves, and nobody approves what they entered themselves.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Where did the old petty cash go?">
The old expense sheet — credits (money given in) and debits (expenses paid out) from October 2022 — is on **More › Old records**, read-only. It opens on this financial year; pick a longer period to see more.
</Faq>
