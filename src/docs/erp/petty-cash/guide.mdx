**Petty cash** is where the factory's day-to-day spending is recorded, approved and paid: tea and diesel, a loading crew, a part for a machine, an advance to a vendor. It keeps three things apart that people usually blur together — **what was spent** (the expense), **what was paid against it** (the payments) and **where the money came from** (the funds) — and every balance on the screen is read off a ledger, never typed.

This page covers the everyday tabs: **Overview**, **Expenses**, **Payments**, **Funds given / transfers**, **Customer cash** and **Vendor outstanding**. The daily cash count, the bank statement, the fund ledger, budgets, reports, Tally and settings are on [Petty cash: closing, bank, budgets and Tally](/docs/erp/petty-cash-closing).

<Callout kind="live">
Limits on this page — how much is approved automatically, how much an approver can decide — are read live from your ERP.
</Callout>

## The three funds

| Fund | What it is | Pays by |
|---|---|---|
| **Bank** | The company's bank account | UPI / GPay, bank transfer, cheque |
| **Bank cash (physical)** | Cash drawn from the bank, held in production | Cash |
| **Customer cash** | Cash customers handed over at the factory | Cash |

Each fund has a **custodian** — the person who signs in to confirm money reaching it and to count it at the end of the day.

## Expenses

An **expense** is one bill or one spend. It records what was bought, from whom, for how much, and whether the bill is attached — never how it was paid.

<Steps>
<Step title="Press New expense">
Fill in **What it was** — date, particular, category, transaction type, godown, who spent it, department — and **The bill** — vendor or payee, total amount, bill number and date, payment terms or due date, whether the goods or service were **Received** or are **Not yet received (a commitment)**, and whether the **Bill / receipt** is *Attached*, *To follow* or *Not available*. Attach the photo when it is attached.
</Step>
<Step title="Say what happened to the payment">
**Unpaid — pending payment** is the usual answer. If the money already went — *Paid in full now*, *Part paid now* or *Advance paid now* — fill in what was paid, from which fund, the payee's acknowledgement, and, because the expense is not yet approved, **why it could not wait for approval**.
</Step>
<Step title="Submit for approval, or save as a draft">
A draft can be edited and is not a record of spending yet. Submitting decides who must approve it — see the table below — and sends it to them.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warn" title="Possible duplicates">
If the same payee already has an expense with the same bill number, or the same amount on the same day, saving stops with *Possible duplicate of EXP-…*. If it really is a separate expense, choose **Yes — it is a separate expense** under *If warned of a duplicate* and save again.
</Callout>

### Who approves it

| When you submit | It goes to |
|---|---|
| At or below <Setting k="erp.pettyCash.autoApproveUpToPaise" /> paise, in a category that allows it, with its bill attached | **Approved by policy** on the spot |
| Up to <Setting k="erp.pettyCash.approverLimitPaise" /> paise | An **approver** |
| Above that, in an owner-only category, or over the month's budget (when the budget rule says so) | The **owner** |

The row says which and why under **Approval needed from** and **Rule applied**. Nobody can approve an expense they raised, submitted or spent — not even an administrator.

### Actions on an expense

| Action | When | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Submit for approval** / **Edit** | Draft or returned | Whoever raised it |
| **Approve** / **Return for correction** / **Reject** / **Take for review** | Submitted or under review | An approver (or the owner, for owner-level) — never your own |
| **Record payment** / **Pay the advance** | Submitted, under review or approved, and something left to pay | Anybody holding the screen (before approval it is an *urgent* payment) |
| **Request payment** | Approved | Anybody — nothing moves until accounts confirm it |
| **Mark received** | A commitment whose goods have arrived | Anybody |
| **Allow without a bill** | Bill not attached | An approver, not on their own expense |
| **Add a document** | Any time | Anybody |
| **Request an adjustment** | Submitted or approved | Anybody — another approver decides it |
| **Set against a bill** / **Recovered in cash** | An advance with money still open | Anybody — an approver decides it |
| **Cancel expense** | Before approval, nothing paid | Whoever raised it, or an approver |

An approved expense is never edited — it is corrected by an **adjustment** (*Vendor credit / return*, *Correction — less is payable*, *Correction — more is payable*, *Write-off*, or setting an advance against it), which somebody else approves.

## Payments

A **payment** is money leaving a fund against one expense. The **Payments** tab lists every one: the fund it came from, the mode, the reference, its status, whether the bank statement has matched it, and whether it is in Tally.

<Steps>
<Step title="Record payment">
Pick **Paid from** (the fund) and the **Mode** that fund pays by, the date, and the amount — never more than what is left. A bank payment needs its **UPI / UTR / cheque reference**. A cash payment needs the payee's acknowledgement: their name, or a photo of the signed slip.
</Step>
<Step title="Or Request payment">
If accounts will pay it, raise a **payment request** instead. Nothing leaves any fund until somebody in accounts presses **Confirm it was paid** with the date, reference and proof. The person who asked cannot confirm it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="money" title="A fund cannot go below what it holds">
A payment larger than the fund holds is refused: *Production cash holds ₹2,000; ₹3,000 cannot be paid from it.* Record a payment request, or move money into the fund first. Only an approver can authorise paying beyond the fund, with a reason — and the refused attempt is logged either way.
</Callout>

| Payment status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Requested** | Asked for; no money has moved. |
| **Confirmed** | Paid; the fund has gone down. |
| **Cancelled** | A request withdrawn. |
| **Reversed** | Undone by an approver, with a reason. The fund goes back up by a new entry; the original stays. |

Accounts mark confirmed payments **verified** after checking them — never their own. An approver can **Reverse** a payment, dated today or on its own date (which reopens that day's cash closing); a payment already matched to the bank statement has to be unmatched first.

## Funds given / transfers

Money moving **between funds** — not spending — is a transfer.

| Kind | From → to |
|---|---|
| **Bank cash withdrawal** | Bank → production cash |
| **Cash handed over to production** | Production cash → production cash |
| **Cash returned to accounts** | Production or customer cash → bank or production cash |
| **Customer cash handed over** | Customer cash → production or customer cash |
| **Customer cash deposited into bank** | Customer cash → bank |
| **Transfer between fund accounts** | Any → any |

<Steps>
<Step title="Press New transfer">
Pick the **Kind**, date, **From**, **To** and amount. A withdrawal or a deposit needs its **Bank reference / slip no.** When moving customer cash, pick the **Customer receipts it carries**.
</Step>
<Step title="It is In transit until received">
Nothing moves yet. The receiving fund's custodian (or accounts) is told to confirm it.
</Step>
<Step title="The receiver presses Confirm received">
Both sides post at once. **Whoever sent the money cannot confirm it.** A transfer still in transit can be **Cancelled** by whoever started it or accounts; a confirmed one can only be **Reversed** by an approver.
</Step>
</Steps>

Money given to employees before the fund ledger existed is still listed here as it was, marked *Before the ledger*, and counts in no balance.

## Customer cash

When a customer hands over cash at the factory, record it on **Customer cash → Cash received**: the customer, the date, the amount, the **customer-cash fund** it is held in, the **invoices it is against** (spread over the oldest first), and the customer's acknowledgement or the receipt you gave them — one of the two is required.

The cash is now in the customer-cash fund — it is **not a sale** and not a payment against the bill yet. Accounts are told to reconcile it.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Where it is** | *Held*, *Handed over*, or *Deposited* — read from the transfers that carried it |
| **Accounts** | Whether accounts have verified it against the customer's ledger |
| **Tally** | Whether its voucher has reached Tally |

Select receipts and use **Deposit into bank** or **Hand over** to move them — the same cash is never deposited twice. Accounts **Verify against the ledger** (not their own recording), with the accounts receipt reference. An approver can **Reverse** a receipt that is still held.

## Vendor outstanding

Every approved, received bill with money still owed, one row per bill: the vendor, the original amount, what is paid, what is **Outstanding**, its **Status** (pending, partially paid), **Days overdue**, an **Ageing** bucket (*Not yet due*, *1–7 days overdue*, *8–15*, *16–30*, *More than 30 days overdue*) and the **Next action**. The summary lists the largest balances; the chips filter by ageing. **Record payment** and **Request payment** work here exactly as on Expenses.

A bill's due date is the one typed, else the bill date plus the payment terms; a bill falling due within <Setting k="erp.pettyCash.dueSoonDays" /> days is flagged as due.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I approved an expense but the fund did not go down.">
Approval does not pay. A payment — recorded, or a request confirmed by accounts — is what takes money out of a fund.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can't I approve this expense?">
You raised it, submitted it, or are the person who spent it; or it needs the owner. The button's hover says which.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Record payment says 'Submit it first'.">
A draft or returned expense cannot be paid. Submit it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="It says 'Not received yet — mark it received first'.">
The expense is a commitment. Mark it received when the goods or service arrive. An advance is paid as its own expense.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I recorded a transfer but the balance has not changed.">
It is in transit until the receiving side confirms it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The same payment request was sent twice by mistake.">
Each form carries its own key; the second send is recognised and nothing is paid twice.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I edit an approved expense?">
No — request an adjustment. The original amount and its approval stay on the record.
</Faq>
