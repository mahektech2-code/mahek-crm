Petty Cash is one ERP module for every rupee that moves outside the order book: what we pay, what comes in, money moved between our own accounts, and lump sums handed to employees. It has four parts across the top — **Overview**, **Expenses**, **Receipts** and **Cash & Bank** — and each part keeps its lists as tabs underneath. Every real movement of money is entered **once**, gets one number (`TXN-26-00423`), and changes the balances once.

## Recording money going out

<Steps>
  <Step title="An office expense">**Expenses › Paid**, then **New expense**. An expense is entered when it is paid — there is no "not paid yet". Give the date it was paid, the category, the amount, who was paid, the invoice number, what it was for, and **Paid from**: the account the money left, how (NEFT, UPI, Cheque, Cash…) and the UTR or cheque number. Photograph the bill.</Step>
  <Step title="Production petty cash">**Expenses › Production petty cash**, then **New petty cash expense**. The same form, for the Production Head's small spends. Paid from can also be **Cash a customer paid** or **Cash withdrawn from a bank** — then pick which receipt or withdrawal the cash came out of, and only cash with something left is offered.</Step>
  <Step title="If it says it is a duplicate">The same invoice number from the same payee, or for the same amount, is refused with the earlier transaction named. Open that one. If this really is a different invoice, tick **This is a different invoice with the same number** and save again.</Step>
</Steps>

## Giving an employee a lump sum (advances)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give the advance">**Expenses › Advances**, then **Give an advance**: who, how much, what for, the day their bills are due, and the account it is given from. It is not an expense yet — the money has moved into their hands.</Step>
  <Step title="They submit their bills">Open the advance and press **Submit bills**. One line per bill: date, category, who was paid, bill number, amount and a photo. A spend with no bill needs a sentence saying why.</Step>
  <Step title="The accountant reviews">Each bill appears on **Needs review**. **Approve**, or **Send back** with what needs fixing.</Step>
  <Step title="Settle">When nothing is waiting, **Settle**. Anything unspent comes back into an account; anything spent beyond the advance is paid to the employee. Both are one transaction.</Step>
</Steps>

## Money coming in

**Receipts › All receipts**, then **New receipt**: when, which account it came into, the amount, how it came and its reference, and who paid — a customer from the list, or a name. A cash receipt shows, when opened, **how this cash was used**: deposited, spent as petty cash, or still in hand. Scrap buyers' payments are recorded on **Receipts › Scrap buyers** and appear here too.

## Moving money between our own accounts

**Cash & Bank › Transfers**, then **New transfer**: from, to, amount and how. A withdrawal is Bank → Cash in Hand; a deposit is Cash in Hand → Bank. A transfer changes both balances and is never income or expense. A deposit of a customer's cash can name the receipt it came from.

## The bank statement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import">**Cash & Bank › Bank statement**, **Import statement**: choose the bank and the CSV from net-banking. A line already imported is skipped. A line that names a transaction's UTR is matched as it lands.</Step>
  <Step title="Match">Open a line that is not matched and press **Match**. The suggestions are transactions already on the books — the same amount near the date, or the UTR named on the line. Pick several to split one line across them; they must add up to the line.</Step>
  <Step title="Only when nothing fits">**Create transaction from this line** makes the expense, receipt or transfer the line shows and matches it, in one step. It is refused while something already on the books may be that line.</Step>
</Steps>

<Faq q="I need to change an expense.">
Open it and press **Correct**. Whoever entered it can correct it until it is approved; after that, the accountant. A transaction matched to a bank line cannot change its amount or account until the match is undone.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I entered something wrong.">
Open it and **Cancel** with a reason. It stays on record, marked cancelled, and stops counting everywhere. Nothing is deleted.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Approve is greyed out.">
Only the accountant for Petty Cash approves, and nobody approves what they entered themselves.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Where did the old petty cash go?">
Funds given and expenses entered before this module are on **Cash & Bank › Old records**, read-only.
</Faq>
