## The unit is the `orders` row

A list is a set of `orders` rows. Each row's TO is the intake whose `origin_order_id` names it, or, for a TO taken in the ERP, the intake whose `order_no` the row's `external_ref` (`ERP-<n>`) carries. The order's facts are read per order number from `erp_orders`, `erp_order_holds` (kind `hold`) and `erp_order_milestones` (not withdrawn).

## The handset

`myOrderStates` in `mbos-service.ts` adds `toNo`, `erpOrderNo`, `journeyStatus`, `journeyTone`, `journeyBanner`, `journeyEditable` and `journeySteps` to each row of `myOrders`. The delta also sends an order whose TO, ERP lines, hold or milestone moved since the cursor, because none of those moves the `orders` row. The phone stores them in handset migration columns on `orders` and draws them in `app/orders.tsx`.
