**My orders** lists every order you took on a call, newest first, and says where each one is.

An order taken on a call starts as a **TO number**, such as TO-1042. Accounts check it. When they approve it, it gets its **order number** and goes to the Production Head's MRP check.

## The tabs

- **TO · waiting for Accounts**: not yet approved by Accounts. You can still change these.
- **On hold or sent back**: held by a desk, or sent back to you by Accounts to correct. The reason is on the row.
- **With accounts**: an order that went to Accounts the older way, without a TO.
- **In production and on the way**: approved, being made, billed or on the lorry.
- **Delivered or closed**: delivered, cancelled or declined. A declined order says why.

Click the TO or order number to see every step with its time and who did it.

## Changing a TO

While it is a TO, the row has **Change** and **Cancel TO**:

- **Change** lets you change the cans, remove a product or add one. A product you add is priced from the shop's price list. If the list has no rate, Accounts add it.
- A changed TO waits for Accounts again. One sent back goes back to Accounts when you save it.
- **Cancel TO** asks why, and the order is cancelled.

Once Accounts approve it, you cannot change it. The Production Head can still change it in the ERP until it is billed, so ask the office.

## Same order punched twice

If the same order (same day, same billing party, same delivery party, same items) is already punched, saving the call shows both side by side. **Same order - don't create** saves the call as Order received and creates nothing; whoever punched the first one is told. **Different order - create it** asks why, and Accounts see the reason.

The same steps show on the customer record's **Orders** tab, and on the Salesman App's order list.
