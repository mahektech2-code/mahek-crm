**My orders** lists every order you took on a call, newest first, and says where each one is.

An order taken on a call starts as a **TO number**, such as TO-1042. The back office and the Production Head check it. When both have, it gets its **order number** and goes to Accounts.

## The tabs

- **TO · being checked**: not yet checked by both people. You can still change these.
- **On hold or sent back**: held by a desk, or sent back to you to correct. The reason is on the row.
- **With accounts**: it has its order number and waits for Accounts.
- **In production and on the way**: approved, being made, billed or on the lorry.
- **Delivered or closed**: delivered, cancelled or declined.

Click the TO or order number to see every step with its time and who did it.

## Changing a TO

While it is a TO, the row has **Change** and **Cancel TO**:

- **Change** lets you change the cans, remove a product or add one. A product you add is priced from the shop's price list. If the list has no rate, the back office adds it.
- Any change clears both checks. The back office and the Production Head check it again.
- **Cancel TO** asks why, and the order is cancelled.

Once it has its order number, it cannot be changed. To change it, ask the office to cancel it and take a new one.

The same steps show on the customer record's **Orders** tab, and on the Salesman App's order list.
