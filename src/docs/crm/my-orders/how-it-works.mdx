## One reading of where an order is

My orders, the customer record's order thread and the Salesman App all show the same status word and the same steps. Each is worked out from facts already recorded:

- Accounts' decision on the TO, its sent-back note, its hold, its decline and its cancellation,
- whether it was marked a possible duplicate, or said to be a different order and why,
- the order number,
- the Production Head's MRP check on the order's lines,
- a hold on any desk, with its department,
- the customer-tracking steps: ready, billed, dispatched, delivered.

Nothing is typed into a status. A step with no fact behind it is not done.

## Who may change a TO

Only the person who took it, and only until Accounts approve it. Accounts may change its lines while they review it, for that order only. Either way the order on the call is rewritten to match the TO, so the Call Log and the customer record show what was changed.

Once Accounts approve it, the order is the ERP's. The Production Head can change it there until it is billed, and nothing is approved again.

## The settings it depends on

The TO exists only while the ERP takes the orders: <Setting k="erp.orders.live" />. With it off, a call's order goes straight to Accounts and My orders shows it with its book status.
