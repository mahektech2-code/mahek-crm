A reminder is a **promise somebody made to a customer** — "I'll ring you back on Thursday", "I'll check the payment on the 20th", "I'll send the price list". Reminders is the list of every promise in your book, which are due today, which are already late, and which are coming up.

You do not tick a reminder off. You **make the call**, and the call closes it.

## Keeping a promise

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Needs action">
Overdue first, then due today. These are the promises the Call Log is also putting at the top of your list.
</Step>
<Step title="Press Call">
The Call Log opens with the customer's panel. The reminder shows as the reason for the call.
</Step>
<Step title="Log the call">
Save it as usual. If the customer answered, every reminder for them that is due today or overdue closes, and the row moves to **Closed** with the line *Closed by the call logged on …*.
</Step>
<Step title="If nobody answered">
The reminder stays open — a ring that nobody picked up is not a promise kept. The row says *Rang on … - no answer, so this is still owed*, and the Call Log's no-answer ladder brings the customer back.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What closes a reminder by itself

| Event | Closes | Line on the closed row |
|---|---|---|
| A call that got through | Every reminder for that customer that is due today or overdue | Closed by the call logged on … |
| An order taken from the customer | Their **order confirmation** reminders, whatever the date | Closed by the order taken on … |
| Money accounts **confirmed** | Their **payment promise** reminders, whatever the date | Closed by the payment accounts confirmed on … |
| A person, by hand | That one reminder | Marked done by *name* on … |

<Callout kind="note">
A payment the customer only **says** they made does not close a payment promise. It closes when accounts find the money. The call in which they told you still closes any reminder that was due that day.
</Callout>

A call never closes a reminder it created itself. If you save a *Follow-up* call for tomorrow, tomorrow's reminder stays open.

## Kinds of reminder

| Kind | Usually written by |
|---|---|
| **call back** | A *Follow-up* outcome, a callback asked for on a collections call, or *Set reminder* |
| **payment promise** | *Payment Promised* on a call, or *Payment promised* on a collections call — dated the working day after the promised date |
| **order confirmation** | A next action on a call |
| **information to send** | A next action on a call |
| **stock check** | A next action on a call |
| **other** | A next action on a call |

## Rescheduling, carrying forward and dismissing

From the row menu (⋯):

- **Reschedule** — pick a new date, optionally update what was promised. The row counts how many times it has moved; after <Setting k="reminders.rescheduleWarningCount" /> moves it says so.
- **Carry forward to tomorrow** — the next working day.
- **Hold other calls until *date*** — only offered when another reason would bring the customer back to the Call Log first. It stops the Call Log asking about orders or check-ins until the promised day. Money and reminders are never held.
- **Open customer record**.
- **Dismiss reminder** — needs a reason, and only appears if you may close reminders by hand. A dismissed reminder is never deleted.

<Callout kind="note">
A date that falls on a non-working day is moved to the next working day when <Setting k="reminders.rollForwardOnNonWorkingDays" /> is on. The message after saving says the date it actually landed on.
</Callout>

## For managers

On team scope you see your whole team's reminders, each with the person it is assigned to, and a card **Overdue reminders by telecaller** showing who is sitting on the overdue pile and how old their oldest is. Managers hold the right to **Mark done** and **Dismiss** by default; that can be moved on the Access screen.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Where did the Mark done button go?">
A reminder is closed by doing what was promised — the call, the order, or confirmed money. Closing one by hand is now for the cases a call cannot reach (settled over WhatsApp, shop shut, written twice) and is given to managers. It is recorded as their decision.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I rang them today and the reminder is still open.">
Either nobody answered, or the reminder is not due yet — a call on Tuesday does not keep a promise made for the 20th. The row says which.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Will the Call Log remind me?">
Yes. A reminder due today or overdue puts the customer at the top of your Call Log, and a reminder that falls due during the day is added to the top of a list that is otherwise settled for the day.
</Faq>
<Faq q="What does 'An earlier reason would call this customer sooner' mean?">
The customer would come back to your Call Log before the promised date — say, a stock check due on Tuesday for a promise on Friday. Use **Hold other calls until …** if your promise covers it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The badge says 6 but I only see 4.">
The badge counts due-today and overdue reminders together — the **Needs action** tab. The Due today and Overdue tabs each show half of it.
</Faq>
