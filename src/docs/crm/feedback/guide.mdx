**Feedback** is how anybody in the team tells the people who build MahekOne that something is wrong, could be easier, is missing, or does not make sense — and where the answer comes back. The **Feedback** button is in the header of every app. The answers are read on **Feedback**, under **Support** in the sidebar of the app you are working in.

## Telling us something

Press **Feedback** in the header. The menu offers **Report a problem**, **Suggest an idea** and **Ask a question**, and below them the last three things you sent with where each one has got to. A small red number on the button counts replies you have not read yet.

**Someone reported this already.** While you type, reports about the same thing are offered. Press **Same problem — add mine to it** and your words and screenshots are added to that report, and every reply on it reaches you. **Mine is different** sends yours as a new one.

When it is sent you get a number — **FB-1042** — and the four steps it goes through: Sent, Seen by the team, Being looked at, Done.

## Reading the answer

Open **Feedback** under **Support** (or **See all** in the Feedback menu, or the notification). Everything you sent from any app is listed on the left — **Open**, **New replies**, **Closed**. The conversation is on the right, with its progress beside it. You can add to any of them: a sentence, a screenshot, or the microphone.

**Done closes it.** When the team marks it done you are told and asked **Is it fixed for you?** Answering **Yes** thanks them. **Still happening** reopens it and puts it straight back at the top of the team's list.

## For the team that answers

Managers and whoever holds the Admin Console answer feedback in **Admin Console → Feedback**. The tabs are queues worked in order: **Needs a reply**, **Waiting on them**, **Being looked at**, **Closed**, **Everything**, and an **Overview**. Each report shows the conversation, everybody who reported the same thing, where it happened and who is holding it. You can reply, write a **team note** the reporter never sees, hand it to somebody, change its status, merge it into another report by its number, or copy the whole report for a developer.
