Price lists tell you **what each shop pays**. In the CRM you can open every list, see which one applies to a customer and why, print or export it, and ask for a special price when a customer wants to pay less. You cannot change a list here — the Accounts desk and the Founder Dashboard make, publish and retire them.

<Callout kind="live">
Figures on this page that come from settings — the GST rate, how long a list stays valid, how much discount you may give — are the values your CRM is using right now.
</Callout>

### The five tabs

| Tab | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Lists** | Every price list and its status. |
| **Documents** | Price-list files (PDFs and photos) that have arrived and are being read into lists. Waiting files show first. |
| **Requests** | Special prices that have been asked for, what the shop pays now, and what was decided. You see the ones **you** asked for; managers see everybody's. |
| **Coverage** | Which shops a published list names — and which shops no list names at all. |
| **Variance** | For a chosen month, every billed order line from the order sheet beside what the list says that shop should have paid. |

## What a list's status means

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <Pill>Draft</Pill> | Being prepared. Prices nobody. |
| <Pill tone="success">Published</Pill> | In force from its start date. Shops it names are priced from it. |
| <Pill>Superseded</Pill> | Replaced by a newer version. Kept so old orders can still be explained. |
| <Pill tone="danger">Withdrawn</Pill> | Taken out of force, with a reason. |
| <Pill tone="danger">Expired</Pill> | A published list past its validity — <Setting k="pricing.defaultValidityDays" /> days from its start unless the list says otherwise. Nobody has replaced it yet, so the office may not honour its rates. |
| <Pill tone="warn">expires in Nd</Pill> | Runs out within a week. |

The **Transport** column says who pays freight: **To Pay** (the shop pays the transporter on delivery, prices are ex-freight), **Paid** (we pay transport, built into the price up to the transporter's godown) or **Not stated**.

**Applies to** lists who the list is for — a state, a city, a salesman, one customer, or *Everybody*. A published list that names nobody says **Nobody — prices no shop** in amber.

## Finding the price for one customer

The quickest way is the customer's record:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the customer">
From the Call Log, the customer list or search.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the Prices tab">
It shows the list that applies to this shop today, every rate **including GST** (switch to **Ex GST** if you need it), the price per litre, and the discounts printed on that list.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the badges">
<Pill tone="danger">Expired</Pill> means nobody has replaced the list yet — check before you promise a figure. <Pill tone="warn">Changed since their last order</Pill> means their last order was priced from a different list: say so before they read it off the invoice.
</Step>
<Step title="Press Why this price? if the customer asks">
It walks you through how the list was chosen — no list for them by name, no list for their salesman, matched on their state, freight to pay — in order.
</Step>
<Step title="Copy price list to send it on WhatsApp">
It copies the customer's name, the list, every rate (incl and ex GST) and the discount terms, ready to paste.
</Step>
</Steps>

If no list applies you see: *No price list applies to this shop.* That means no list in force today names their city, state, salesman, or everybody. Tell a manager — the Coverage tab lists every shop in this state.

## Prices on a call

When you log an order on a call, each product you add shows **the rate per can and the name of the list it came from**. The rates are the same ones on the customer's Prices tab.

- If the order form is set to price orders from the list (currently <Setting k="pricing.useListForOrderValue" />), each line shows what it comes to, and the order shows **Ex-GST**, **GST** and **Total**. A line whose product is not on the shop's list is left out, and the form says *N lines are not on this shop's list, so the total is short by them*.
- If it is not, you see *Rates are from …, shown for reference — an order is still worth what accounts bill for it.*
- If no list applies, the form shows quantities and no money.

### Giving a discount

A **%** box appears on each priced line only if you are allowed to give a discount at all.

| Your level in the CRM | You may give on your own |
|---|---|
| Telecaller (associate) | Up to <Setting k="pricing.associateMaxDiscountBp" /> basis points (100 = 1%) |
| Manager | Up to <Setting k="pricing.managerMaxDiscountBp" /> basis points |
| Admin | Any discount |

The discount is taken **before** GST. If you type more than you are allowed, the save is refused with a sentence like *You may give up to 3% on your own. Above that a manager has to authorise it.* — or, when your limit is zero, *You cannot give a discount on your own. Ask a manager to authorise it.* Save the order without the discount and ask, or ask for a special price.

## Asking for a special price

When a customer wants to pay less than their list for a product:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the customer's Prices tab">
Press **Ask for a special price**. It is greyed out if the shop has no rates to ask against.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the product and type the price they want">
Type the price **including GST** — the figure the shopkeeper says out loud. The form shows the ex-GST figure underneath and how far it is from today's rate in percent.
</Step>
<Step title="Say why">
The reason is required. Whoever decides is not on the call and has nothing else to weigh it on.
</Step>
<Step title="Send it">
You see *Asked. Whoever decides prices has been told.* The request appears under **Asked for** on the Prices tab with the status <Pill tone="warn">Waiting</Pill>.
</Step>
</Steps>

### What happens next

<Flow caption="A special price request" chart={`
flowchart TD
  A["Telecaller asks<br/>(price incl GST + reason)"] --> B["Request waiting<br/>deciders get a notification"]
  B --> C{"Accounts or Founder desk decides"}
  C -- "Approved" --> D["The shop gets its own<br/>'agreed prices' list with that rate"]
  C -- "Refused" --> E["Shop stays on its list"]
  B -- "You withdraw it" --> W["Withdrawn"]
  D --> N["You are notified<br/>with any note"]
  E --> N
`} />

- **Approved** — the customer now has a list of their own (named *Customer A — agreed prices*) and that rate is what the order form uses from then on. A second approved special price for the same shop is added to the same list.
- **Refused** — nothing changes. You get a notification with the decider's note so you can ring the customer back.
- **Withdraw** — only you, and only while it is still waiting. Use it from the row menu on the Requests tab.

## Coverage and variance

**Coverage** shows how many shops are on a list, how many are on **no** list, and how many have no freight term recorded (a list of either term can still name them). The *On no list* table shows up to 200 of those shops with their region, freight term and salesman — these are shops being priced from memory.

**Variance** shows, month by month, every priced line billed on the order sheet beside the list price for that shop. The figures at the top count lines **at**, **below** and **above** the list, lines where **no list resolved**, and the total **given away below the list**. Sort is biggest discount first.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Why can't I edit a price here?">
Prices are set by the Accounts desk and the Founder Dashboard only. The CRM and the Sales Dashboard quote prices; the person chasing a number must not also set the price it is measured in.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The customer says they were quoted a different price.">
Open their Prices tab. If it says *Changed since their last order*, their last order came from a different list — tell them. If the list is *Expired*, check with accounts before confirming.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Are the prices including GST?">
The Prices tab shows **Incl GST** by default with the ex-GST figure beneath. Lists are stored ex-GST and printed inclusive at the list's own GST rate; where a list does not say, <Setting k="pricing.gstBp" /> basis points (1800 = 18%). Inclusive figures are rounded to the rupee, as the office prints them.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Two lists seem to cover the same shop. Which wins?">
The narrowest one: a list for the customer by name, then their salesman, beat, area, city, district, state, customer type, and finally everybody. Press **Why this price?** to see it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I asked for a special price and heard nothing.">
Check the Requests tab. A request stays <i>Waiting</i> until somebody at the Accounts desk or the Founder Dashboard decides it; you are notified either way.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The discount box is missing on my order lines.">
Your discount limit is zero, so the box is not drawn. Ask a manager, or ask for a special price.
</Faq>
