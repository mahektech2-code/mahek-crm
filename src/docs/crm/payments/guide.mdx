Payment Follow-up is the collections list. It shows **who owes money that is past its due date**, how late it is, what they last promised, and whether today is a day to **message** them or **call** them. You work it the same way as the Call Log: open the customer, chase, log what they said, move on.

</Callout>

## The three stages

Every customer on the list sits at a stage, worked out from how late their oldest unpaid bill is:

| Stage | Badge | How you chase |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | 1 · Nudge | WhatsApp only. You cannot log a call at stage 1. |
| **2** | 2 · Call | Calls and messages alternate. Get a dated promise. |
| **3** | 3 · Urgent | Call, and be firm. |

Stage 2 starts at <Setting k="escalation.stage2Days" /> days overdue and stage 3 at <Setting k="escalation.stage3Days" /> days. A customer who refuses to commit or cannot be reached is moved up a stage and kept there, even if their bill dates alone would put them lower.

## Message first, then call

A bill a few days late is usually paperwork, not refusal. So:

<Steps>
<Step title="The bill falls due">
For the first <Setting k="escalation.quietCallDays" /> days nobody rings. The customer gets a WhatsApp reminder every <Setting k="escalation.messageIntervalDays" /> days instead — they appear on **Message today** when one is due.
</Step>
<Step title="Calling opens">
The day after that window closes, they appear on **Call today**.
</Step>
<Step title="After each call">
They rest <Setting k="escalation.callIntervalDays" /> days before coming back to Call today. Messages carry on alongside.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Logging a collections call

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the follow-up">
Press **Call & log**, or open the follow-up from the ⋯ menu. The panel shows what they owe, the oldest bill, when they were last chased, their credit terms, every open bill and every promise so far.
</Step>
<Step title="Ring them, then answer: What did they say?">
Pick one of the outcomes below. Quick-pick phrases are offered for each; you can type your own note.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Everything the outcome produces is saved in one go. The window afterwards says **when this customer comes back to your Call Log**, the same as after any other call.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Outcome | You must fill in | What saving does |
|---|---|---|
| **Payment promised** | Amount promised, Promised by (today or later) | Records the promise and creates a reminder for the working day after, so a broken promise is chased |
| **Already paid** | Amount already paid; a payment proof photo is optional | Records the money as **reported** — waiting for accounts. The customer is not chased for it for a few days. Nothing they owe changes yet. |
| **Call back later** | Call back on | Creates a callback reminder for that date |
| **Dispute raised** | — | Raises a billing complaint and marks their oldest bill disputed. The account is held where it is until the complaint is closed. |
| **Refused to commit** | — | Moves the account up a stage and keeps it there |
| **Not reachable** | — | Moves the account up a stage and keeps it there. Closes no reminder. |

<Callout kind="money" title="'Already paid' does not reduce what they owe">
A customer saying they paid is not the money arriving. It sits with accounts as *reported* until they find it in the bank. Until then the bills stay open, the customer stays on the list, and the row shows **Paid ₹…?** — but nobody chases them for <Setting k="payments.reportedQuietDays" /> days.
</Callout>

## Recording a payment from the list

From the ⋯ menu, **Record a payment** opens a form against one open bill: amount received, how it came (mode), the reference, the date received and — for a cheque — the date written on it.

- If you hold the accounts desk's right to confirm money, it is recorded as **confirmed**.
- Otherwise it is recorded as **reported** and waits for accounts, exactly like *Already paid*.

**Record a promise** writes a dated promise without logging a call outcome.

## Why somebody is not on today's list

| Reason shown | What it means |
|---|---|
| Marked do not contact | A standing instruction |
| Held - a bill is disputed | A dispute is open on one of their bills |
| ₹X on hold with accounts - … | Accounts are checking the money. Not chased until they decide. |
| ₹X cheque dated … - not chased until it can be banked | A post-dated cheque. Quiet until its date, then the usual window. |
| ₹X reported paid N days ago - waiting for accounts to confirm it | Reported money buys <Setting k="payments.reportedQuietDays" /> days of quiet |
| Paid today / yesterday - not chased about money until … | They paid something recently; quiet for <Setting k="payments.paidCoolingDays" /> day(s) |
| Payment promised by … - not chased until it passes | A live promise |
| Already contacted today | One contact a day |
| Only N days overdue - messages only until … | Still in the message-only window |
| Called N days ago - due again on … | Resting after the last call |

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="The customer says they paid. Why are they still on the list?">
The money is reported, not confirmed. They stay on the list with a **Paid ₹…?** flag and are not chased for a few days. Once accounts confirm it, the bills close and they leave the list.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can I not log a call for a stage 1 customer?">
Stage 1 is a WhatsApp nudge only. Send the reminder, or wait for the account to reach stage 2.
</Faq>
<Faq q="They promised to pay on the 20th. What happens on the 21st?">
A payment-promise reminder falls due on the working day after the 20th. If the money has been confirmed by then, the reminder closes on its own.
</Faq>
<Faq q="They gave a cheque dated next month.">
Report it with the cheque date. They are not chased until the date on the cheque, and then only after the usual window for it to clear.
</Faq>
<Faq q="What is a slow payer?">
A customer who has paid at least <Setting k="escalation.slowPayerLateCount" /> bills more than <Setting k="escalation.slowPayerGraceDays" /> days after their due date in the last <Setting k="escalation.slowPayerLookbackMonths" /> months. The flag appears beside their name everywhere.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Somebody I expected is missing.">
Open **kept off today's list**. If they are not there either, they owe nothing past its due date, or their bills have not been stated as owed.
</Faq>
