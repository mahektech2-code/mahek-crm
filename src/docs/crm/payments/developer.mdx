Collections is three pure engines wired to three services. **E3** (`engines/escalation.ts`) turns a customer's bills into a stage, cached in `follow_up_states` by `recomputeFollowUpState`. **E7** (`engines/payment-followup.ts`) turns those states into today's calls, messages and held-back list. `engines/allocation.ts` spreads an amount over bills. The services are `payment-service.ts` (worklist, metrics, ledger reads), `payment-followup-service.ts` (logging a collections call) and `receipt-service.ts` (recording and deciding money).

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Rules go in the engines">
A new hold reason is a branch in `blockingReason` with its sentence; a new stage rule is in `escalationStage`. Both are pure — test them in `engines.test.ts`.
</Step>
<Step title="A new outcome is a PAY_OUTCOMES entry first">
Then the `logFollowUpSchema` enum, then its branch inside the transaction. Retire, never delete — history reads labels back.
</Step>
<Step title="Never write paid_amount">
Write receipts and payment lines; call `recomputeBillPaid` and `recomputeOutstanding`. Every money path keys on `confirmed`.
</Step>
<Step title="Join receipts when summing payments">
A money figure from `payments` must join `payment_receipts` and filter `status = 'confirmed'`.
</Step>
</Steps>
