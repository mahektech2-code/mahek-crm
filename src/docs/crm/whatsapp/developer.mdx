The WhatsApp screen is shared by the CRM and the Accounts app: `src/app/crm/whatsapp/page.tsx` renders `WhatsappPage` with `app="crm"`. Messages are rows in `wa_messages`, templates in `wa_templates`, inbound in `wa_replies`. Everything about preparing, copying, confirming and sending is in `whatsapp-service.ts`; conversations are `whatsapp-chat-service.ts`; the routing decision is the pure `deliveryRoute` in `lib/whatsapp-delivery.ts`; Wati is the provider.

## Read versus write

<Code file="src/lib/access.ts" symbol="whatsappLevel" />

`whatsappRefusal("chat")` demands **write**. `whatsappRefusal("send")` refuses only **read** — a person with no WhatsApp module at all may still send from the payment panel, because those screens always could.

## Wati

- Outbound: `sendAutomatic` posts the approved template and stores Wati's id in `provider_ref`.
- Inbound and status: the webhook calls `applyWatiEvent`, which moves a message forward only (`advancedStatus`) — Wati retries, and a repeat changes nothing.
- The key is read through `lib/secrets.ts`; the founder's on/off switch is `whatsappServiceState()`. Neither is a setting.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Keep copied and confirmed apart">
Nothing but `confirmSent` (and an accepted automatic send) may set `last_confirmed_whatsapp_date` or write a collections attempt. There is a test for each half.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a manual reason to deliveryRoute">
It is pure; add the branch and its sentence, and a test.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a merge field to MERGE_FIELDS and mergeValuesFor together">
Keep `unstated` bills out of money fields, on the server and in the page's preview payload.
</Step>
<Step title="Check write level in the action">
Use `whatsappRefusal`. A hidden composer is not a permission.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Weekly limit uses `now() - interval '7 days'` (database clock) | `whatsapp-service.ts` `sendAutomatic` |
