The WhatsApp screen is where customers' messages arrive and where payment reminders and other template messages go out. It opens on the **chats**, the way WhatsApp itself does. Sending a message, running a batch of reminders, editing templates and reading the message log are tools you open from the same screen.

What you can do here depends on what you were given: **read** the chats, or **reply and send** as well.

## Two ways a message goes out

<Cards cols={2}>
<Card title="Automatic" tone="brand">Sent straight through WhatsApp's business service. You see ✓ when WhatsApp accepts it, ✓✓ when it reaches the phone, and **Read** when they open it.</Card>
<Card title="Manual: copy, send, confirm">You copy the message, paste it into WhatsApp yourself, and press **Confirm** once it has gone. WhatsApp gives no ticks for a pasted message, so your confirmation is the record.</Card>
</Cards>

A message goes the manual way when sending is switched off, when no key is set up, when it is going to a **customer group**, when the template is not linked to an approved WhatsApp template yet, or when you edited the words. The screen tells you which.

## Sending a payment reminder or a template

<Steps>
<Step title="Open New message">
Pick the customer and the template. Templates are grouped by purpose: payment reminder, order confirmation, reactivation, routine check-in, other.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the preview">
The message is filled in from the customer's record — their name, what they owe, their open bills, the latest promise. If a field is empty the message is refused rather than sent as "Dear ,". You can edit the words; an edited message always goes the manual way.
</Step>
<Step title="Send it">
**Automatic**: press send; the ticks appear in the chat. **Manual**: press **Copy**, paste it into WhatsApp, send it there, then come back and press **Confirm**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warn" title="Copied is not sent">
Until you press **Confirm**, a copied message is *Copied, not confirmed* — the customer may or may not have heard from us. It does **not** hold the customer back from the Call Log, and it does not count as a reminder sent. A manager sees every unconfirmed copy in the top card.
</Callout>

## Customers reached two ways

A customer can be set up to receive messages on their **own number**, in their **WhatsApp group**, or **both**.

- **Both** makes **two** messages — the personal one first, then the group one. Work them one at a time: each has its own copy and confirm.
- Confirming either one counts as the customer having heard from us. The other is still shown as waiting until you confirm it too.
- If **both** is set but nobody recorded the group's name, only the personal message is made, and the screen says why.

## Working a batch: Send run

<Steps>
<Step title="Set up a send run">
Choose who: *Stage 1 reminder due*, *Slow payers with a balance* or *Overdue more than 60 days*, and the template.
</Step>
<Step title="Work down the list">
For each customer: **Copy the message**, **Send it from WhatsApp**, **Confirm and move on**. Messages that can go automatically go in one press.
</Step>
<Step title="Pause and come back">
A run you leave is offered again the next time you open the screen.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Replies

A customer's reply arrives in their chat. **Replies to action** counts the ones nobody has dealt with. Reply in the chat, or mark the reply handled. Photographs and documents the customer sends open in the chat.

## Message statuses

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| Prepared | Written, not sent |
| Copied, not confirmed | Copied to paste, nobody confirmed it went |
| Confirmed sent | A person pasted it and confirmed |
| Sending | Handed to WhatsApp a moment ago |
| Sent ✓ | WhatsApp accepted it |
| Delivered ✓✓ | It reached their phone |
| Read ✓✓ | They opened it (only if they have read receipts on) |
| Failed | It did not go — the log says why |
| Not sent | Discarded before it went |

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I sent it from my phone but the customer is still on my Call Log.">
You did not press **Confirm**. A copied message is not treated as sent until somebody confirms it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can I read the chats but not reply?">
Your access is read-only. Replying and sending are given separately, on the Access screen.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why did my message go the manual way?">
The screen names the reason: sending is switched off, no key is set up, it is going to a group, the template is not approved, or you edited it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="It says this customer has already had several messages this week.">
There is a weekly limit per customer on messages sent automatically, currently <Setting k="whatsapp.contactsPerWeekLimit" />. Pasting by hand is your judgement and is not limited.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A number is listed as undeliverable.">
WhatsApp says that number cannot receive messages. Automatic rules stop trying it. Put a working number on the customer's record.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Does a WhatsApp reminder stop the Call Log ringing them?">
A **confirmed** message does, for a few days. A reminder falling due still brings them back.
</Faq>
