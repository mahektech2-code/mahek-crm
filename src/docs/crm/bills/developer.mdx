Sales Bills is a server page over three reads in `payment-service.ts` — `billLedgerPage` (one sorted, paged slice), `billLedgerTotals` (sums over the filter) and `openBillAges` (the open rows for the aging strip) — banded by `bucketise`. Bills are written by the sheet projection (`sheet-projection-service.ts`), by accounts, and by the receivables import; `paid_amount`, `status` and the customer's outstanding are caches in `lib/recompute.ts`.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Never let an import write money">
No path from a sheet may write `paid_amount`, `status`, a receipt or outstanding.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep the tie-breaker in every sort">
`order by <key>, bills.id`. The paging test uses seven bills a day so a broken cursor cannot pass on a page boundary.
</Step>
<Step title="Cast a stored date with the zone">
`bill_date::timestamp at time zone 'Asia/Kolkata'`, never `::timestamptz`.
</Step>
<Step title="Exclude unstated from every money total">
If a new figure is "what is owed", filter `payment_position = 'stated'` like `recomputeOutstanding` does.
</Step>
</Steps>
