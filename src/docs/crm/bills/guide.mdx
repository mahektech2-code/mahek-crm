Sales Bills is the ledger: **every bill raised to customers in your book**, one financial year at a time, with what was billed, what has been paid against it, what is left and how late it is. Open a bill number to see what was ordered on it.

If you want *who owes us*, one line per customer, use [Outstanding](/docs/crm/outstanding). Sales Bills answers *what did we bill*.

## Reading a bill

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| **Bill no** | The bill's number — usually the Tally number, like MMI/26-27/1119 |
| **Date** | The bill date |
| **Due** | The bill's own due date, or the bill date plus the customer's credit period |
| **Amount** | What was billed |
| **Paid** | What accounts have **confirmed** against it |
| **Balance** | Amount minus paid |
| **Overdue** | Days past the due date, while there is a balance |
| **Bucket** | Which aging band the balance sits in |
| **Status** | Unpaid, Partly paid or Paid |

<Callout kind="money" title="Paid means confirmed">
A payment the customer says they made, or one that is still waiting for accounts, is not in the **Paid** column. A bill stays at its full balance until accounts confirm the money.
</Callout>

## Financial years

The ledger shows one financial year at a time — April to March — and opens on the current one. Bill numbers carry the year too: MMI/**26-27**/1119 is a bill from April 2026 to March 2027. Pick an earlier year from the year filter; the list of years goes back to the first bill on record.

The figures across the top and the aging strip always describe **the whole year you picked**, whatever page you are on.

## Looking at one customer's bills

From a customer's record, **See all bills** opens this screen filtered to that customer. The title names them; clear it to go back to everybody.

## Recording a payment against a bill

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the row menu and choose Record a payment">
Only offered while the bill has a balance.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in what came">
Amount received (up to the balance), how it came, the reference, the date received — and for a cheque, the date written on it.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
If you hold the accounts desk's right to confirm money, it is confirmed and the balance drops. Otherwise it is **reported** and waits for accounts; the balance stays until they confirm it.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Why is every bill from last year missing?">
The ledger opens on the current financial year. Pick last year from the year filter. To see everything still owed regardless of year, use Outstanding.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A bill number has the order number added on the end.">
When two orders share a Tally number, the second is numbered with its order number added — MMI/26-27/1119/4521 — so every bill number is unique.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The customer paid but the bill still says Unpaid.">
The money has not been confirmed by accounts yet.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can I not export?">
Exporting the ledger is a manager action in the CRM.
</Faq>
<Faq q="What is on a bill?">
Click the bill number. A sales bill **is** the order — the lines are the order's lines.
</Faq>
