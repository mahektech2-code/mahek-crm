The customers list is `CustomersScreen` (`src/components/customers/customers-screen.tsx`), shared by the CRM and the Accounts app, fed by `listCustomersPage` in `lib/queries.ts`. What a set of filters **means** is one function, `customerFilterClause` — the list, its tiles, its total and both bulk actions run it. The record is `src/app/crm/customers/[id]`, a server page assembling a dozen reads into `RecordScreen`.

Every timeline branch sorts `at desc, id desc` and the cursor is `(at, id) <` the last row. A stored **date** (bill date, receipt date) becomes an instant only through `::timestamp at time zone` the business zone — a bare `::timestamptz` reads in the session's zone and pages the same bill twice.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="A new filter goes in customerFiltersOnly">
Never in the screen alone — the bulk actions must move exactly what the list shows.
</Step>
<Step title="A new seat needs a label and a scope decision">
Add it to `am_role`, `SEAT_LABELS` (the handover test fails otherwise), and decide whether it is sight (`scopedToUsers`) or credit (`ASSIGNED_TO_SQL`). Never both by accident.
</Step>
<Step title="Moving money between people is customer.reassign">
Any write to `sales_am_id`, `owner_id` or `back_office_am_id` goes through `applyAccountManagerChange`, writes history, notifies and stamps `am_decided_at`. `updateCustomer` refuses those fields.
</Step>
<Step title="Cap and count every record panel">
A new panel on the record reads a capped page and a `count(*)`, never "everything".
</Step>
</Steps>
