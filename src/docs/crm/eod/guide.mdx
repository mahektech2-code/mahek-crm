The **EOD Report** is your day in figures — calls, orders, payments, reminders, WhatsApp — worked out from what you logged, plus a ready-made message to paste into the team's WhatsApp group. At the end of the day you **submit** it. You can also look back at yesterday, the last seven days, this month, last month or any range you choose. A manager additionally sees the whole team side by side.

<Callout kind="note">
Nothing on this screen is typed in. Every figure is derived from the calls, orders, payments and reminders you recorded — so a thin report means thin logging, not a thin day.
</Callout>

## Submitting at the end of the day

<Steps>
<Step title="Clear the reminders due today">
Each one must be called, carried forward to another day, or closed. Until then the button reads *Close or carry forward the reminders due today first*.
</Step>
<Step title="Read the figures">
If something looks low, check that every call was logged — the report can only count what is recorded.
</Step>
<Step title="Copy the WhatsApp message">
Press **Copy** and paste it into the team group. The message is built for WhatsApp: short lines, no tables.
</Step>
<Step title="Press Submit EOD">
The report is saved with the time. If you submit again later the same day it replaces the earlier one (*Resubmit EOD*).
</Step>
</Steps>

If you forget, a report is generated for you overnight — a day with no activity still produces a report, so nobody has to wonder whether you worked.

## What each figure means

| Figure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Customers called from the queue** | Different customers you called from your Call Log. |
| **Calls attempted** | Outbound calls you logged. Inbound calls and orders logged without a call are **not** counted here. |
| **Connected** | Outbound calls with any outcome other than no answer. |
| **Inbound received** | Calls the customer made to you. |
| **Missed** | Outbound calls logged as no answer. |
| **Orders** | Orders you took (not cancelled), how many accounts approved, and the approved value. |
| **Orders received without a call** | Orders you logged with no call behind them — real work, kept separate. |
| **Payment follow-ups** | Follow-up attempts you made on money owed. |
| **Promises obtained** | Payment promises you got, and their total. |
| **Payments confirmed** | Payment amounts you recorded, by the payment date. |
| **Reminders closed / created / carried forward** | Your reminders that were completed, the ones you created, and the ones moved to a later day. |
| **Complaints logged** | Complaints you raised. |
| **WhatsApp sent** | Messages confirmed as sent. A message copied and never confirmed does not count. |
| **Monthly target** | Approved order value this month against the customer targets on accounts you own. |

## The team roll-up

A manager sees a table of the team for the same period — calls, connected, missed, orders, order value and target % per person — and on today's view a **Team roll-up** message to copy. Somebody who has not logged anything still appears.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I made 40 calls but it says 32 attempted.">
Only outbound calls you logged count. Inbound calls are counted separately, and orders received without a call are on their own line.
</Faq>
<Faq q="My orders show a value of zero.">
Value is counted only once accounts approve an order. The orders you took are still counted.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can I not submit?">
A reminder due today is still open. Call the customer, carry it forward, or close it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I submit for yesterday?">
No — only today is submitted. Other periods are a historical view. Yesterday's report was generated overnight if you did not submit it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Does the EOD compare me with last week?">
No. The comparison with the period before is on the [Dashboard](/docs/crm/dashboard), which reads the same figures.
</Faq>
