Call History is every call logged in your book, newest first, with what happened on it, what it produced, and **what the screen told the telecaller would happen next**. Use it to look back over a week, to see what a colleague said to a customer before you ring them, and to hand a customer over to somebody else.

## Reading the Next call column

Every call saved since this column existed carries the answer the telecaller saw in the window after saving: *Comes back to your Call Log on …*. The column shows that answer, **as it was on that day**.

| What you see | It means |
|---|---|
| A date in black, **Promised** | A callback the customer asked for. Somebody owes them this call. |
| A date in black, **Scheduled** | The date the rules gave on that day. It moves if the customer orders, pays or asks for a callback. |
| A date in **grey** | The date has passed. It was what the screen said then, not a commitment now. |
| **Needs a decision** | Nobody could reach them — no date was given, and somebody has to decide what happens next. |
| **Nothing scheduled** | Marked do not contact, or nothing would bring them back within four months. |
| **-** | The call was logged before this was recorded. Nothing is filled in after the fact. |

<Callout kind="note">
To see when a customer **actually** comes back today, open their record or the Customers list. Call History shows what was said at the time — the difference between the two is the record of what changed.
</Callout>

## Looking something up

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick a date range">
Today, Last 7 days, This month or All time. Last 7 days is where the screen opens.
</Step>
<Step title="Narrow it">
Type a customer name or a word from a note. Managers can pick one telecaller from **Everyone**.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the details">
Where a call recorded why the customer rang, who rang and what was agreed, hover the details summary to read every answer.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the customer">
The customer's name opens their record.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warn" title="All time is not all time">
The screen loads the newest 400 calls in your book and filters those. When it is holding a full 400 it says so — *132 of the newest 400 calls* — and *All time* reaches back only as far as those 400 go. For older history, open the customer's record, whose timeline pages back to the beginning.
</Callout>

## Handing a customer over

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick the customer in the Handover summary card">
The list is the customers in the calls you are looking at.
</Step>
<Step title="Press Copy">
You get a short message: the customer's name, their last three interactions with date, channel, outcome and note, the last thing promised, and every open reminder with its due date.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste it to your colleague">
In WhatsApp, an email or the customer's notes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the other columns mean

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Timestamp** | When the call was logged |
| **Telecaller** | Who logged it |
| **Channel** | Call |
| **Status** | Left blank on calls logged today — the outcome says whether they answered |
| **Outcome** | How the call ended: Order Taken, No Order, No Answer, Payment Promised… |
| **Notes** | What the telecaller wrote. Hover for the whole note. |
| **Produced** | What the call created: Order, Reminder, Complaint |

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Why are my WhatsApp messages not here?">
Despite the subtitle, this screen lists calls only. Sent WhatsApp messages are on the WhatsApp screen's Message log, and on the customer's record.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The Next call date is in the past and greyed out.">
That is the date the telecaller was given when they saved the call. It has passed; the customer's real next call is on their record.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can I not export?">
Exporting needs the right to export customers, which a manager decides on the Access screen.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I am a manager and I only see my own calls.">
Switch the header to **Team**. On My book you see only your own.
</Faq>
