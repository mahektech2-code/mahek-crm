The Calling desk is where a telecaller works **online leads by phone** — enquiries that arrived from the website, WhatsApp, a portal or a marketplace, and that nobody is going to visit. Each lead gets **at most three qualification calls**. Inside those three you collect what a Prospect needs; the moment it is all in, the lead is **Ready for Prospect** and you convert it. If the calls run out first, the lead is closed or left for you to finish on the record.

<Callout kind="note">
The desk is granted to you personally. If you can open the CRM but not the Calling desk, nobody has given it to you yet — ask whoever manages access. Every other Lead Management screen works without it.
</Callout>

### What is on today's work queue

The queue holds three kinds of lead, in this order:

1. **Ready for Prospect** — everything a Prospect needs is in. Convert it.
2. **Returned** — the Sales Manager sent a request back. Read their note.
3. **Due** — a call or message due today or earlier, soonest first. A brand-new lead nobody has scheduled counts as **Call 1 due today**.

A lead with the Sales Manager, or with a next step scheduled for a later day, is **not** on the queue.

## Working a lead, step by step

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the lead from the list">
The desk record opens. The header shows the reference (for example `TC-1042`), when it was created and its source. Read **What the enquiry said** — the customer's own words, if the lead came from the website.
</Step>
<Step title="Press Start call N / 3">
The call window shows which questions are **worth asking on this call** and which are better left to later. Anything already on file is listed as answered and is **never asked again**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose what happened">
Pick one of the five outcomes (table below). If it was **No answer**, say which kind — no response, busy, switched off, out of network, call rejected or disconnected.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in what they told you">
Only if you spoke to them. Leave anything they did not know blank — a blank is "Not yet captured", never an error.
</Step>
<Step title="Say what happens next">
Choose **Call** or **Message first**, write a line, and pick a follow-up date. The date is suggested two working days out and cannot be in the past.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
The button tells you what will happen: <i>Save call</i>, <i>Save call — Ready for Prospect</i>, or <i>Save call and mark Lost</i>. Read it before you press it.
</Step>
</Steps>

### The five call outcomes

| Outcome | When to use it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Spoke — information collected** | They answered and you captured at least one new answer. | Uses a call. If you did not fill in anything new it is refused — use *asked to call back*. |
| **Spoke — asked to call back** | Reached, but a bad time or only partly answered. | Uses a call. |
| **No answer** | Rang, nobody spoke. | Uses a call. You must say which kind of no answer. Answers cannot be saved with it. |
| **Wrong or invalid number** | Not the business, or the number does not work. | **Closes the lead as Lost** at once. |
| **Not interested** | They said no. | **Closes the lead as Lost** at once. |

<Callout kind="warn" title="A no-answer still uses a call">
All five outcomes count against the three. Three unanswered calls, with the competitor never captured, close the lead as *No response after 3 attempts*. If you only need to send something, use **Message** instead — a message uses no call.
</Callout>

### The eleven answers

| Answer | Needed for a Prospect? | Best asked on |
|---|---|---|
| **Competitor** (which brand they use now) | **Yes — compulsory**. Missing after Call 3 closes the lead. | Call 1 |
| **Product** (which of ours they need) | Yes | Call 1 |
| **Monthly requirement** (litres a month) | Yes | Call 1 |
| Customer type (dealer, manufacturer, distributor, retailer, private limited user) | No — asked when you convert, if still blank | Call 1 |
| Application (what they will use it on) | No | Call 1 |
| Decision maker | No — "ask if known" | Call 2 |
| GST | No | Call 2 |
| Buyer | No | Call 3 |
| Credit days | No | Call 3 |
| Address | No | Call 3 |
| Email | No | Call 3 |

On **Call 3** every answer a Prospect still needs is asked, whatever call it was suggested for.

## When a lead is Ready for Prospect

As soon as the competitor, the product and the monthly requirement are all on the record, the lead turns **Ready for Prospect** — even if that happens on Call 1. No further call is offered.

<Steps>
<Step title="Press Convert to Prospect">
On the green banner, or in the **Move this lead forward** card.
</Step>
<Step title="Say why it is worth pursuing">
Pick one of the listed reasons — for example *They need it regularly (confirmed)* or *Wants to switch from another brand*. Add what the customer said if it helps.
</Step>
<Step title="Answer what the record is missing">
If nobody has said how the lead will be sold, choose **Direct customer** or **Third Party Customer** — it is never chosen for you. If the kind of business or the contact person is still blank, you are asked for them here.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
The lead becomes a **Prospect** and the Sales Manager is told. You see: *Prospect created. Waiting for Sales Manager verification.* Qualification opens once they verify it, and then it is yours to complete.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note">
If your manager has switched off direct conversion, the same button instead **requests** the Prospect: the lead stays a Suspect, shows *Awaiting verification*, and becomes a Prospect only when the Sales Manager confirms it on their verification call. You will see *Prospect requested* in place of *Prospect created*.
</Callout>

## What each status means

| Status on the lead | Means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Call 1 / 2 / 3 pending** | That call is owed. | Make it. |
| **Ready for Prospect** | Everything a Prospect needs is in. | Convert it. |
| **Three calls used** | Three calls made; the competitor is in but the product or monthly requirement is not. The lead is **not** lost. | Close it, or ask the Sales Manager to fill in the rest (see the FAQ). |
| **Awaiting verification** | You requested a Prospect; it is with the Sales Manager. | Nothing. Do not ring the customer again. |
| **Manager follow-up** | The Sales Manager is holding the request while they follow up. | Nothing until they decide. |
| **Returned by Manager** | The Sales Manager sent it back with a note. | Read the note. Resubmit, or mark it Lost. |
| **Prospect** and beyond | It has left the desk's calling. | The record is read-only except where Qualification is yours. |
| **Lost** | Closed, with the reason. | Reverse it if the customer comes back. |

## Messages, next actions, closing and reopening

- **Message** — log a company profile, product image, brochure, video, price list or WhatsApp follow-up you sent. It uses **no call**. If the next step was waiting on that message, it becomes the next call, one day out.
- **Next action** — change what happens next and when, as a call or a message. It can only be changed while the lead is a Suspect.
- **Mark Lost** — pick one of six reasons: *No response after 3 attempts*, *Required information not obtained*, *Customer not interested*, *Requirement not genuine*, *Wrong lead — should not have been raised*, or *Other*. You cannot close a lead while the Sales Manager has it.
- **Reverse Lead** — reopens a Lost lead with a reason. It goes back to the rung it was lost from, and if that was Suspect the **three calls start again**. Earlier calls stay on the record. A lead closed because the Sales Manager's verification failed can only be reversed by them.

## Leads with no owner

A lead nobody owns is on **nobody's** desk. If you can hand leads out, a banner tells you how many there are, and **Show them** lists them. Open one and press **Assign** to give it to somebody who holds the Calling desk. People who cannot open the desk are shown greyed out in the list, with the reason.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I pressed Start call but it says the three calls are over.">
The lead has moved on from Suspect, or the Sales Manager has it, or three calls have already been made this round. There is no Call 4.
</Faq>
<Faq q="It says 'Everything a Prospect needs is already in, so no further call is needed.'">
The lead is Ready for Prospect. Convert it instead of calling again.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I typed an answer that was already on file and it was not saved.">
Answers already on the record are never overwritten by a call — the message says which ones were left unchanged. This protects the figure the Sales Manager checks on their verification call.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The lead says Three calls used. Is it lost?">
No. The competitor is in, but the product or the monthly requirement is not. It stays a Suspect. The desk's own **Edit** button is not built yet, so the remaining answers cannot be typed in here — close the lead, or ask the Sales Manager to complete it from the main lead record.
</Faq>
<Faq q="My request was Returned. Can I ring the customer to fix it?">
No — a returned lead cannot be rung again from the desk. Read the manager's note, then **Resubmit for verification** or **Mark Lost**.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can I not convert even though it says Ready?">
The conversion also checks the Prospect gate: a reason, a kind of business, a contact person, a next action, and a Sales Manager who can verify it. The refusal names what is missing. If it says no Sales Manager covers the lead's region, ask an administrator.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A lead I expected is not on today's queue.">
It is scheduled for a later day, it is with the Sales Manager, or it belongs to another telecaller. Try the search box, or press **Total leads**.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Did the call close my reminder?">
Yes, if somebody answered. A reminder due on the lead is closed by an answered call, the same as on the Call Log; a no-answer or a dead number closes nothing.
</Faq>
