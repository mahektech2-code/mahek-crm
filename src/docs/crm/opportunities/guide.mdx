An opportunity is **something a customer said on a call that they might buy** — "we may need PU sealer next month", "send me a rate for 20-litre thinner". Opportunities is the list of those, with who is expected to follow each one up, so that what a call turned up is worked rather than forgotten on the customer's timeline.

An opportunity is not a lead. It can be on a customer who has bought from us for years.

## Where opportunities come from

Opportunities are raised from the call panel, when somebody answers **Yes** to:

> **Did this call turn up a sales opportunity?**

| Field | Required | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Product | Yes | What they might buy |
| Estimated quantity | No | About 20 cans a month |
| Estimated value | No | In rupees |
| Expected order date | No | Must be today or later |

The question is asked on **every call the customer made to us**. On a call we made, it appears only when the call assistant heard a "maybe" in your notes and filled it in for you to check. Answering **No** is recorded too, so a manager can tell "nobody asked" from "asked, and there was nothing".

<Callout kind="note">
An opportunity lands on the list of the person **whose book the account is in**, not necessarily whoever took the call. If that is somebody else, they get a notification: *A call turned up an opportunity on your account*.
</Callout>

## Working one

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Being worked">
Everything open or being worked, newest first. Check **Past due** first — the expected order day has gone.
</Step>
<Step title="Start working it">
Moves it from *Open* to *Being worked*, so the team can see somebody has picked it up.
</Step>
<Step title="Close it as Won or Lost">
**Won** asks *What came of it? (optional)*. **Lost** asks *Why was it lost?* — and will not save without an answer, because the next call to that customer needs it.
</Step>
<Step title="Reopen if you closed it by mistake">
A won or lost opportunity shows **Reopen**. The note written when it was last worked is kept.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Statuses

| Status | Badge | Means |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Open | Raised on a call, nobody has picked it up |
| In progress | Being worked | Somebody is following it up |
| Won | Won | It turned into business — **your** judgement, nothing is worked out from their orders |
| Lost | Lost | It did not, and the reason is on the card |

<Callout kind="warn" title="Won is what you say, not what the system checks">
Marking an opportunity won does not look for an order. Mark it won when it became business, and say so in the note.
</Callout>

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Is an opportunity the same as a lead?">
No. A lead is an account that has never ordered and is worked in Lead Management. An opportunity is one thing a customer — new or old — might buy, raised from a single call.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why did the opportunity box not appear on my call?">
It is asked on calls the customer made to us. On a call we made, it only appears if the call assistant picked up a possible sale in your notes.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I raised one and it says 'for' somebody else.">
It went to the person whose book the account is in. You can still see it and work it, because you raised it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I change the product or the value?">
Not from this screen. Write what changed in the note when you close it.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Does Lost stop the customer being called?">
No. It only closes the opportunity. The Call Log decides calls from the customer's own buying pattern.
</Faq>
