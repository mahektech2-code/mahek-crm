Monthly Targets is one **shared screen component** (`MonthlyTargetsScreen`) mounted by the CRM at `/crm/targets` and by Accounts at `/accounts/customer-targets`. Reads and writes are in `worklist-services.ts`; the default target and the shortfall split are a pure engine (`engines/targets.ts`); defaults are seeded by the nightly job on the 1st. Top customers is a stored, idempotently generated monthly report read through the second tab. Everything below is cut from the source this deployment was built from.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="One population, one clause">
Totals, page, count and bulk writes all go through `targetListClause`. A new filter goes into `customerFiltersOnly` so the Customers list gets it too.
</Step>
<Step title="Only decisions carry">
If you add a field to a target, decide whether it travels with a hand-set carry in `seedMonthlyTargets` — bills travel with rupees for that reason.
</Step>
<Step title="Gate on the capability">
Both doors read `target.set`. Never reintroduce an `isManager` check.
</Step>
<Step title="Freeze reports, scope at read">
Never re-rank a stored Top customers report; change what a viewer sees only in the read.
</Step>
</Steps>
