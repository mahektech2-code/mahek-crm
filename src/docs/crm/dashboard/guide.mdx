The Dashboard is the first screen you see. For a telecaller it greets you by name and shows the shape of your day: how far through the Call Log you are, what is waiting for you, your target for the month and whether you can submit your end-of-day report. For a manager it becomes the **Team overview**: red flags, the team's figures and a table comparing every telecaller.

Every number on it opens the records behind it.

## The five steps of your day

| Step | Ticked when | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Work the queue** | Every customer on today's Call Log has been worked | Call Log |
| **Close reminders** | No reminder due today or earlier is still open | Reminders |
| **Chase payments** | Nobody is left on the collections worklist | Payment Follow-up |
| **Clear complaints** | No complaint is open, in progress or awaiting the customer | Complaints |
| **Submit EOD** | Never ticked here — it lights up once the queue is worked and reminders are closed | EOD Report |

## What each figure means

| Figure | What it counts | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Calling progress** | Customers on today's Call Log you have worked, out of how many were on it | Always today, whatever period you picked |
| **Calls connected** | Calls **you made** that were answered, out of calls you made. Calls that rang your phone are counted separately underneath. | Connect rate is connected ÷ placed. The team's rate is shown beside yours. |
| **Orders taken** | Orders you logged in the period, whether or not accounts have approved them | Underneath: how many are still awaiting approval, and the **approved** value so far |
| **Missed calls** | Calls you made that nobody answered | Fewer is better — a fall shows green |

<Callout kind="note">
An order you took counts as **taken** the moment you save it. Its value only counts once the accounts desk approves it — that is why the line under *Orders taken* can say "2 awaiting approval".
</Callout>

## Picking a period

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick Today, Yesterday, This week or This month">
The buttons sit above your figures. The choice is in the page address, so you can bookmark it.
</Step>
<Step title="Read what it is compared with">
Beside the buttons: the dates covered and what they are measured against — *vs 11 Aug* for a day, *vs the previous 7 days* for a span.
</Step>
<Step title="Remember what does not change">
The progress bar, the five steps and **Needs you today** are always **today's** work. A month of waiting work is not a thing — only the activity figures follow the period.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Period | Covers | Compared with |
|---|---|---|
| Today | Today | The previous working day |
| Yesterday | The previous **working** day (Saturday, on a Monday) | The working day before that |
| This week | Monday to today | The same number of days immediately before |
| This month | The 1st to today | The same number of days immediately before |

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Why does my Calling progress not change when I pick This month?">
The Call Log is today's list. Its progress is always today's, so it has no month to show. The activity figures beside it do follow the period.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Yesterday shows Saturday's figures on a Monday.">
Yesterday means the previous working day. Sunday is not a working day, so on a Monday it is Saturday. The dates are printed beside the buttons.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Orders taken says 5 but the value is lower than I expected.">
The value underneath counts only orders accounts have approved. The line above it says how many are still waiting.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why do inbound calls not count towards my connect rate?">
You cannot fail to connect a call that rang your own phone. Counting them would push your connect rate up on a day you made fewer calls. They are shown separately.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The Missed calls card says 'retry after 4 pm'.">
That line is fixed text on the card, not a rule. When to try again is decided by the Call Log's no-answer ladder.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Where did the Inactive Watch go?">
Customers who have gone quiet are worked from the Call Log now. The *Customers gone quiet* row opens the Call Log filtered to exactly those customers.
</Faq>
