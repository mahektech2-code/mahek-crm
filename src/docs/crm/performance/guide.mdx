**My Performance** is your own month: the target your manager or accounts set for you, what you have actually done against it, a score out of 100, and — first on the page — **what is short**, in rupees, litres and customers. It always shows **you**, and nobody else. If you carry customers in the CRM, you have a number here, even if you never go into the field.

<Callout kind="live">
Numbers on this page are your CRM's current settings.
</Callout>

## The six things you are measured on

| Component | What counts |
|---|---|
| **Revenue** | The value of approved orders from customers credited to you, **excluding GST**. |
| **Volume** | The **litres** in those orders, worked out from each product's pack. A price rise moves revenue; it cannot move litres. |
| **Product mix** | Each product's share of your sales value, against the minimum, target and stretch share you were asked for. |
| **New customers** | Customers whose **first** counting order landed this month. Raising a lead does not count. |
| **Collection** | How much of the money that was **already overdue on the 1st** you collected this month — confirmed by accounts. |
| **Activity** | Of the tasks given to you that were **due this month**, how many you completed. |

<Callout kind="note">
A component nobody set a target for is **left out**, and its weight is shared among the others. The score is always out of 100 *against what was actually asked of you*. The screen names any component that was left out.
</Callout>

## Reading your month, step by step

<Steps>
<Step title="Read What is short first">
It says what you are behind on in your own units — rupees, litres, customers, a product's share — worst first. Everything else on the page is the working-out.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the per-day figure">
"₹X a day over the N working days left" is what it would take to reach the revenue target. Sundays and company holidays are not counted.
</Step>
<Step title="Look at the mix">
A product **below minimum** costs you the most. Selling more of what already sells well does not make up for it.
</Step>
<Step title="Check where your customers are short">
Your per-customer targets are on [Monthly Targets](/docs/crm/targets).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Whose sales are yours

Every rupee belongs to **one** person:

1. The account's **salesperson**, if it has one.
2. Otherwise its **back-office person**.
3. A lead counts for its **owner** (that is how a lead's first order becomes your new customer).

Nobody else. Two people can both see an account; only one is credited with what it bought.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="It says no target has been set for me.">
You see what you have done, but no score — a percentage against a target nobody set would be invented. Targets are set by your manager or by accounts.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I took an order this morning and Revenue has not moved.">
Orders count once accounts approve them.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why is my collection figure lower than what I collected?">
It only counts money against bills that were already overdue on the 1st, and only once accounts confirm it. A bill that fell due mid-month and was paid is ordinary business, not collection.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can my manager see this screen for me?">
This screen only ever shows the person signed in. Managers compare people on the Sales Dashboard.
</Faq>
<Faq q="It is the 1st and the forecast is missing.">
With no working day finished yet there is no projection — one day multiplied by thirty is not a forecast.
</Faq>
