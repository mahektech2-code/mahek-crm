Tasks are rows in `tasks`, owned by one person (`assignee_id`) and filed under one app (`app`). The CRM screen and the Tasks app read the same functions in `lib/tasks/list-service.ts` (a page at a time) and `lib/tasks/service.ts` (one task, and who may see it); every write is in `lib/actions/tasks.ts`; the rules are pure in `lib/tasks/rules.ts`.

## Lists are read a page at a time

No screen fetches everything a person can see. A list is a scope (whose tasks), the URL's reading, and the date; `taskListPage` counts every tab, counts each group on the page across every page, and returns one page. My day, the Board and the Calendar draw a capped preview with the real count beside it and link to the full list. A team is asked through `teamInSql` — one array parameter, or `users` itself for somebody whose permission reaches everybody — never a list of every id.

## Access

- **Module:** <ModuleRef k="crm.tasks" />.
- The Tasks app is its own grant: <ModuleRef k="tasks.my-work" />, <ModuleRef k="tasks.team" /> and <ModuleRef k="tasks.setup" />.
- The Tasks permissions are `src/lib/tasks/permissions.ts` (pure) and `taskPowersOf` in `src/lib/tasks/powers-service.ts`; a Tasks administrator or a platform administrator holds all of them. They are granted in `setAccess` (`taskPowers`) and drawn under Tasks in the Access dialog.
- Every reach goes through `teamOf(viewer, reach)` — `see`, `give` or `manage`, defaulting to the narrowest. Switching an automatic task is checked in `canManageAutomations` (`src/lib/tasks/setup-access.ts`): whoever holds Run automatic tasks.
