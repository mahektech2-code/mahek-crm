**My tasks** is everything you have to do in the CRM: tasks your manager gave you and tasks you added for yourself. The same tasks are in the **Tasks** app, beside the ones you have in other apps.

## Giving a task

**New task** asks what needs doing, the app it belongs to, and whether closing it needs a **screenshot** and a **remark** — each *Required* or *Optional*. Then who gets it and when:

- **Me**, or **People, teams or roles**. Add rules to **Include** and **Exclude**: a person, everyone under a manager, a designation, a department, or a level in the app. Only people who hold the app you picked are listed.
- A person you name on their own wins over a group — exclude a whole team and include one person from it, and that person still gets it.
- The list under the rules shows exactly who it comes to, and why.
- **Repeats**: *Once* with a due day, or *Daily*, *Weekly* or *Monthly*. A repeating task is added afresh each day it runs, to whoever matches the rules that day.

## Closing a task

Pressing **Mark done** opens two steps:

1. **Is it done?** Tick "Yes — I have done this". If checklist steps are not ticked, it says so.
2. **Proof.** Write what was done, and add a screenshot — each is marked required or optional, as whoever gave you the task chose: choose a file or paste with Ctrl+V. The person who gave you the task reads your remark.

The task then shows who closed it, when, the remark and the screenshots.

**A task from a routine has to be done on its day.** Once its due time passes — or the day ends, if it has no time — it is marked **Missed** and Mark done goes away. You can't do yesterday's task today; you do today's. Missed tasks stay in the **Missed** tab, and your manager sees them on Team workload.

## Handing a task to somebody else

Open the task and press **Transfer**. Pick the person and say why.

- **A colleague** gets a request. They review the task, then accept or reject it, with a reason. It stays yours until they accept, and you can withdraw the request.
- **Your manager**, or anybody above you, can move a task between people in their team straight away. Both of you are told.

Requests waiting for you appear at the top of this screen and in **Tasks → Transfers**.

## Questions

**I can't see a task my manager says they gave me.** It may belong to another app — the banner here counts tasks in other apps. Accounts, the Sales Dashboard, the ERP and the Founder app each have their own **My tasks** in the sidebar, and the Tasks app shows them all.

**Why can't I change "To close it"?** Only whoever gave the task, or a manager above whoever holds it, decides whether it needs a screenshot or a remark.
