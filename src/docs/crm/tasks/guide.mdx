**My tasks** is everything you have to do in the CRM: tasks your manager gave you and tasks you added for yourself. The same tasks are in the **Tasks** app, beside the ones you have in other apps.

## Closing a task

Pressing **Mark done** opens two steps:

1. **Is it done?** Tick "Yes — I have done this". If checklist steps are not ticked, it says so.
2. **Proof.** Write what was done — always. Add a screenshot if the task needs one: choose a file or paste with Ctrl+V. The person who gave you the task reads your remark.

The task then shows who closed it, when, the remark and the screenshots.

## Handing a task to somebody else

Open the task and press **Transfer**. Pick the person and say why.

- **A colleague** gets a request. They review the task, then accept or reject it, with a reason. It stays yours until they accept, and you can withdraw the request.
- **Your manager**, or anybody above you, can move a task between people in their team straight away. Both of you are told.

Requests waiting for you appear at the top of this screen and in **Tasks → Transfers**.

## Questions

**I can't see a task my manager says they gave me.** It may belong to another app — the banner here counts tasks in other apps. Open the Tasks app to see them all.

**Why can't I change "To close it"?** Only whoever gave the task, or a manager above whoever holds it, decides whether it needs a screenshot.
