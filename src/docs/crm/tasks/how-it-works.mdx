A task belongs to **one person** and names **the app its work happens in**. TeleCRM's My tasks shows a person's CRM tasks and personal ones; the Tasks app shows all of them. It is one row either way, so a task closed in one place is closed in the other.

## Closing is a statement with evidence

Closing always needs a **confirmation**. Whoever gave the task decides, when they create it, whether a **screenshot** and a **remark** are each required or optional — and can change either later. The rule is <Code file="src/lib/tasks/rules.ts" symbol="closeProblems" collapsed />, and the server asks it again whatever the screen did.

A screenshot counts only if the person closing the task uploaded it and it is not attached to anything else, so a file from another task is not evidence for this one.

## Who may do what

| Who | Sees it | Edits, closes, transfers | Decides "screenshot / remark required" |
|---|---|---|---|
| The person it is assigned to | yes | yes | only on a task they added themselves |
| The person who gave it | yes | edits; deletes it while it is open | yes |
| Anybody above the assignee on the org chart | yes | yes, and moves it without asking | yes |
| The person a transfer is offered to | yes, to review it | accepts or rejects | no |

The org chart is `users.reports_to_id`, then the HRMS reporting line. A platform administrator is above everybody.

## Transfers

<Code file="src/lib/tasks/rules.ts" symbol="transferRoute" collapsed />

A manager above both people **moves** a task. Anybody else **asks**: the task stays with them until the other person accepts. Rejecting needs a reason. One request can be open per task at a time, and closing a task withdraws any request still open on it.

## Who gets it

A task — once, or repeating — is given to **you**, or to a set of **include** and **exclude** rules. A rule is a person, a team (everyone under a manager on the org chart), a designation or department from HRMS, a level in the task's app, an ERP designation, or everybody.

- Only people who **hold the task's app** are offered, and only the designations, departments, teams and levels found among them, with how many people hold each.
- **A person named on their own wins.** Excluded by name: never gets it. Included by name: always gets it, even if their team or designation is excluded. Everybody else gets it when a group includes them and no group excludes them.
- A manager's rules reach only themselves and the people under them on the org chart.
- The preview shows who it comes to and why, and the server works it out again when the task is saved. The rule is <Code file="src/lib/tasks/audience.ts" symbol="resolveAudience" collapsed />.

## Routines

A **routine** is a task that comes back: daily or weekly on the weekdays it names, or monthly on a day of the month with an optional day it must be done by. On each day it falls due it adds one ordinary task per person, closed like any other. A routine given by rules works out who it is for **afresh each day** — somebody who joins a team or takes a designation later starts getting it on the next day it runs. The rule for which days is <Code file="src/lib/tasks/routines.ts" symbol="occurrenceOn" collapsed />.

- A routine for the 31st lands on a short month's last day rather than skipping the month.
- Each task names its routine and its day, and the database holds that pair to one row — so the hourly job, the nightly one and somebody opening the app can all ask for today, and only the first copy lands.
- A routine is changed, paused or deleted by **whoever set it** or **anybody above its person**. The person it belongs to sees it but cannot switch off one their manager set.
- Changing a routine changes the days to come. Pausing adds nothing until it is resumed, and the paused days are not owed. Deleting keeps every task it already added.

## Where the code and the intent disagree today

- Reminders and the CRM's own automatic follow-ups are not tasks yet. They stay on the Reminders screen and the Call Log.
- A routine task nobody closes stays open and shows as late; nothing closes or skips it automatically.
