These are the manager's screens of Lead Management. **Oversight** shows the gates somebody walked past, everything done to the funnel and under whose authority, and the thresholds the funnel runs on. The **Sales Manager desk** is the working desk of a sales manager — every lead where they are the named sales manager, sorted into queues. **Lost** is the central record of every lead closed lost. **Funnel & conversion** shows the shape of the book, where it came from and what it is dying of.

Each is a separate module. You will only see the ones you have been given.

## Oversight

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|

## The Sales Manager desk

The desk shows **only leads where you are the named sales manager** (an administrator sees all of them), grouped by the salesman or telecaller who owns each lead.

| Queue | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Verify prospect** | Prospects, and calling-desk requests, waiting for your verification call |
| **Qualification review** | Qualifications a telecaller completed and asked you to review |
| **Sample** | Requests to approve, approved samples to send, samples on the road, trials nobody has reviewed |
| **Order confirmation** | Expected orders on file that have not become an actual order yet |
| **Overdue** | Leads whose next action date has passed |
| **Nurture task** | Leads with a follow-up task due today or late |

The tiles and the list are counted from the same rows, so a tile that says 14 always opens a list of 14. The other tabs — **Today**, **List**, **Mine**, **Overdue**, **Pipeline**, **Distributors** — are different cuts of the same book.

<Steps>
<Step title="Start from a queue">
Press a tile to see its leads. Search by name, town, contact, owner or product.
</Step>
<Step title="Open a lead and act">
Verify a Prospect, review a qualification, approve or chase a sample, or confirm an order — each opens its own form.
</Step>
<Step title="Leads you cannot see">
A lead with an owner but no sales manager named is in **nobody's** Sales Manager desk until somebody names one.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Lost

Every lead closed lost, whatever rung it was lost from.

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| **Lost date** | When it was closed |
| **Stage at loss** | The rung it was on when it was lost. *Not recorded* for leads lost before the stage history existed |
| **Lost reason** | The coded reason chosen; *No reason recorded* for old leads |
| **Lost by** | Who closed it |

Filter by owner, type, stage at loss, reason and dates. The tiles count **Total lost**, **Lost this week** and **Lost this month**.

### Reversing a lost lead

Press **Reverse Lead**, choose why it is being reopened (and add a note where asked), and confirm. The dialog tells you where it will go: usually **the rung it was lost from**. A lead lost on a failed verification goes back to Prospect to be verified again; one whose ladder has changed, or with no record of where it was lost, goes to the foot of its ladder; one lost at the rung that makes it a customer comes back one rung below. From there every ordinary gate applies again.

### Deleting and restoring

From All Leads, a manager can **delete** leads (move them to the trash) with a sentence saying why. A lead that has been billed cannot be deleted — deactivate it instead. Deleted leads leave every screen; an administrator can **restore** them.

## Funnel & conversion

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Funnel** | How many leads stand on each rung of each ladder, and how long they have been there; parked leads counted separately. Then **Cohort conversion**: leads raised in a period, followed forward. |
| **Sources** | Where leads came from, and how well each source converts. |
| **Reasons** | Why leads became Prospects, why customers wanted samples, why leads were lost, and why gates were overridden — by salesman and by town. |

<Callout kind="note" title="Reading the cohort">
Cohort conversion follows leads raised in a window for <Setting k="owner.conversionWindowDays" /> days. **Converted** reached the book; **Lost, or past its window** did not; **Not yet** are still inside their window and are counted but not divided. *Nothing has closed its window* means it is too early to give a rate — not that the rate is zero.
</Callout>

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="A lead I manage is not on my Sales Manager desk.">
The desk shows leads where you are the named sales manager. If the lead has an owner but no sales manager, nobody's desk shows it until one is named.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why does the override log show conditions as missing when the work was done?">
It shows what the record said at the moment of the move. If the work was recorded later, the override reason usually says so.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The Reasons tab shows fewer reasons than leads lost.">
Leads lost before reasons were required carry no code. They are counted as *uncoded* so the totals add up.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I change a threshold from Oversight?">
No — it only shows them. Settings are changed in the Admin Console, which checks the whole set before saving; the change then appears here with the name of whoever made it.
</Faq>
