You can take an order at the counter with no signal. You pick the products and the number of **cans**, the app shows litres and boxes, checks the customer's credit, and saves it. Accounts then approve or decline it, and you are told either way.

## Taking an order

<Steps>
<Step title="Start it">
Inside a visit, pick **Order** and press **Take order**. Or press **+ → Punch order**, or **New order** on a customer's record.
</Step>
<Step title="Check who gets the bill">
For a third-party shop, the bill goes to the account that serves it — the screen says *The office bills this shop to …*. You can change it if the customer asks.
</Step>
<Step title="Add products">
Tap a usual product, or search by name, pack size or code. Set the number of cans on each line.
</Step>
<Step title="Read the credit line">
See the table below for what it says and what happens.
</Step>
<Step title="Submit">
The order is saved on the phone. *Order saved · sending to the office now* or *will send when you have signal*.
</Step>
</Steps>

### The credit check

| You see | What happens |
|---|---|
| *Within the credit limit.* | Submit. Accounts approve it as usual. |
| *A little over the credit limit, but within the allowed extra.* | Submit. No extra approval needed. |
| *This is over the credit limit. Your manager must approve it before it is sent.* | The button reads **Send for approval**. Tell the customer the order is subject to approval. |
| *This is far over the credit limit. A senior must approve it before it is sent.* | The same, but a senior decides. |
| *This customer is credit-blocked.* | You cannot take the order. Accounts must remove the block first. |
| *No credit limit on file for this customer.* | The order goes; the limit could not be checked. |
| *This order has no price yet.* | The order goes; the office prices it. |

<Callout kind="note">
The phone checks credit with what it last heard. The office checks again with today's figures when the order arrives — and may refuse it if the customer's balance has changed.
</Callout>

## After you submit

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Not sent** | Still on your phone. It goes when there is signal. |
| **Pending approval** | Accounts have it and are checking. |
| **Approved** | Accounts accepted it. |
| **Declined** | Accounts refused it. You get a notification with their reason — you will need to ring the customer. |
| **Dispatched / In transit / Delivered** | Where the goods are. |

See your orders under **More → Orders**.

### Changing an order

Until accounts decide it, open the order and **edit** it — change quantities or take a line off. Once accounts have decided, press **Send change request to accounts** and **say why it needs to change**. Accounts decide.

### When the office does not accept an order

It appears under **More → Not accepted** with the reason. The common ones:

| Reason starts with | What to do |
|---|---|
| *… is credit-blocked* | Ring accounts before promising anything. |
| *… is now ₹… a can on the … list, not the ₹… quoted* | The price changed. Confirm the new rate with the shop and take the order again. |
| *… owed ₹… on your handset but owes ₹… today* | The balance changed since you last synced. Sync, check with them, try again. |
| *… would take them past their ₹… limit* | Collect against old bills, or ask accounts to raise the limit. |
| *This order has already been recorded* | Nothing to do — it is in. |

## Products and prices

**More → Product catalogue** lists everything we sell, on your phone, with pack sizes. Prices shown are the customer's own price list where the office uses price lists. Every SKU is a pack and a packing — "5 Liter (6 Can/Box)" and "5 Liter (Loose)" are two different things to order.

## A lead that orders

You can take an order from a lead. The lead becomes a customer when it places its **second** order.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Why cans and not litres?">
Cans are what the customer counts and says. The app works out litres and boxes from the pack.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Where do I mark that the goods arrived?">
Not on the phone yet. The office records delivery.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The order total says the office has not turned on order value yet.">
The office has not chosen where prices come from. The order is still taken; accounts value it.
</Faq>
