Before your first day you need three things: the app on your phone, a sign-in, and your phone set up so it can record your route. This page walks you through all three, and explains every message the sign-in screen can show you.

## 1. Get the app

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the download page on your phone">
Open **one.mahekindia.com/download** in your phone's browser and tap the **Mahek MBOS** download. Your manager can also send you the link.
</Step>
<Step title="Allow the install">
Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Say yes for this once. The app is not on the Play Store — it comes straight from MahekOne.
</Step>
<Step title="Open Mahek MBOS">
It appears on your phone as **Mahek MBOS**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note">
When a new version is ready, the app tells you itself and offers to update. You do not need to come back to the download page.
</Callout>

The first time, sign in **where there is signal**. The phone fetches your customers, products and prices — on a big book this can take a minute. Do not close the app while it says it is loading.

### Signing in with no signal

After you have signed in once with signal, you can sign in again **without signal** — with your password, on the same phone, as the same person. This works for <Setting k="mbos.sync.offlineLoginValidityDays" /> days after the last time the phone reached MahekOne. After that you need signal once.

A one-time code needs signal; it cannot be used offline.

## What a sign-in message means

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *That number and password do not match an account that can use Mahek MBOS.* | The number or the password is wrong. | Check both, or use Forgot password. |
| *… has a MahekOne account but has not been given Mahek MBOS.* | Your account exists but the app has not been granted. | Ask your manager to grant it. |
| *…'s MahekOne account has been closed.* | Your account is switched off. | Ask your manager. Nothing on the phone is lost. |
| *You are already signed in on another phone.* | Your account is tied to a different phone. | Ask your manager to release the old phone, then sign in here. |
| *This handset can't be used to sign in right now. Contact your admin.* | This phone belongs to somebody else's account. | Ask your admin. |
| *This phone still has N entries from … that have not reached the office.* | The last person on this phone has unsent work. | They must sign in here once, with signal, to send it. |
| *Signed in, but your book could not be loaded.* | The office could not prepare your data just now. | Try again in a moment. |
| *Signed in, but your data could not be saved on this phone.* | Your phone's storage refused. | Free some space and try again. |
| *No internet. You need signal to sign in on this phone the first time.* | First sign-in, no signal. | Go where there is signal. |
| *This phone has not reached MahekOne for N days.* | Offline sign-in has expired. | Sign in once with signal. |
| *Mahek MBOS sign-in is not switched on yet.* | The office has not finished setting MBOS up. | Tell your manager. |

## 3. Set up your phone

Straight after your first sign-in the app opens **Set up your phone**. It shows one step at a time with one button. Do every step — they are what let the app record your route when the phone is in your pocket.

### Why each permission is needed

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Location — all the time** | Recording your route while you are punched in, and checking you are at a shop when you check in. Never used after you punch out. |
| **Camera** | The selfie at punch-in and punch-out, shop photos, cheque and bill photos. |
| **Microphone** | Speaking a note instead of typing it. |
| **Notifications** | The office telling you an order was approved or declined, a task, a reminder to punch out. |

## If the app stops you at punch-in

When you press **Punch in**, the app checks your phone first. If something is wrong it opens **Before you start** instead of starting the day. Each row says what is wrong and has one button to fix it. There is no skip button — fix the row, come back, and press Punch in again.

If a row says Android will not ask again, the app cannot fix it for you. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Mahek MBOS → Permissions yourself, or ring the office.

### Keep tracking on

**More → Send to office** shows whether your route is being recorded. If it says the phone is stopping MBOS, tap **Keep tracking on**: it walks you through the battery and autostart switches for your phone's make. After changing them, **restart the app** (not the phone).

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I got a new phone. What do I do?">
Make sure everything on the old phone is sent (More → Send to office shows nothing waiting). Ask your manager to release the old phone. Then install and sign in on the new one.
</Faq>

<Faq q="My colleague wants to use my phone tomorrow.">
They can sign in only after everything you did on it has reached the office. When they sign in, your data is cleared from the phone — the office already has it.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Do I have to do the setup steps?">
Set up your phone itself never blocks you. But Punch in will not start the day while location is off, a permission is missing or the battery saver says it will stop the app.
</Faq>
