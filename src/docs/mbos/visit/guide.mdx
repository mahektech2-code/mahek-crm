A visit has four moments: you **set off**, you **arrive**, you **check in** when you walk into the shop, and you **check out** when you leave. In between you write what happened and take an order, a payment or a complaint if there is one. This page walks you through each.

## 1. Set off

Press **Start visit** — on today's route, a customer's record, the customer list, or the **+** button. If you are punched in, the trip is recorded under the vehicle you chose this morning; you are not asked anything. The screen shows **On your way** with how far the shop is, and a **Navigate** button that opens your maps app.

You can only be on your way to one shop at a time. If you have arrived at a shop and not checked in, the app asks you to check in there (or save that visit) first.

## 2. Arrive

When you reach the shop, press **I am at the shop**. The app takes a careful GPS reading and checks you are close to the shop's saved location.

| What happens | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *At the shop · 38 m from its saved location.* | You are within <Setting k="mbos.location.visitMismatchM" /> m. | Carry on. |
| *You are 420 m from this shop's saved location. You must be within N m to check in.* | You are too far — or the saved location is wrong. | Walk to the shop and press again. Nothing has been saved. |
| *No GPS signal yet. You need it to check in.* | The phone has no reading. | Step outside or near a window and press again. |
| *GPS signal is weak.* | The reading is not good enough to tell. | Step outside and press again. |
| *Are you at Customer A?* | The shop has no saved location yet. | Press **Yes, I am at the shop** only if you are. Where you stand becomes the shop's location. |

### When the shop's saved location is wrong

If you are standing in the right shop and the app still says you are too far, the saved location is wrong. After being refused you can go on: press **I am at the shop** anyway and **say why** in a sentence. The visit is saved as **not checked**, and your manager reads your reason.

The app then asks whether the shop's pin is wrong. Say **yes** and your manager is asked to move it to where you checked in. You cannot move it yourself.

## 3. Check in

When you walk in, press **Check in at the shop**. The time in the shop counts from here. This works even with no signal.

## 4. Fill in the visit

The day you will contact them next. Change it if they asked for another day.
</Step>

### Speaking instead of typing

Tap the microphone on the note and speak — in any language. With signal, the app shows you the English it heard; correct it and it goes into the note. With **no signal**, the recording is kept and sent with the visit, and the office writes it out. On a visit the recording always goes to the office with the note.

### Visiting a Suspect lead

A Suspect is a shop you have not yet decided about. The card shows *Visit 2 / 3*.

| Visit | What the app asks |
|---|---|
| Below <Setting k="mbos.leads.visitsBeforeDecision" /> | Nothing extra. |
| From <Setting k="mbos.leads.visitsBeforeDecision" /> | *On the next visit you must decide. Think about it now.* |
| At <Setting k="mbos.leads.maxSuspectVisits" /> and after | **Choose which way this lead goes before you close the visit.** Prospect, Not a prospect, or keep it a Suspect — and if you keep it, say why. Your manager is told. |

The visit itself is **always saved** — the question is about the lead, not the visit.

## 5. Check out and save

Press **Check out of the shop and save the visit**. If the checklist is complete, it saves. If something is missing — too short a time in the shop (less than <Setting k="mbos.visits.minimumDwellSeconds" /> seconds), no note, no result — you can add it, or choose **Save as not checked** and say why. Your manager sees it.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I am in the shop but the app says I am 300 m away.">
The shop's saved location is wrong. Press I am at the shop again, give the reason, and say yes when asked if the pin is wrong.
</Faq>

<Faq q="I pressed Start visit by mistake.">
Press **Cancel trip** on the On your way screen. Nothing is lost.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Where do I claim the auto fare to this shop?">
In **Expenses**, once, for the day. Fares are not asked at each shop.
</Faq>
