A visit is assembled across three local records — a **travel leg** (`data/travel.ts`), an **arrival** kept on disk (`data/arrival.ts`), and the **visit** itself (`data/visits.ts`) — and sent as one `entityType: 'visit'` item to `handleVisit` in `src/lib/actions/mbos.ts`. The gate is pure (`engines/geo.ts` `checkInVerdict`); the checklist is pure (`lib/visit.ts`); the server recomputes both and never refuses on distance.

## The gate

<Code file="mbos-app/src/engines/geo.ts" symbol="checkInVerdict" collapsed />

The order of the first two branches is load-bearing: the fix is judged **before** the pin is looked for, so an unpinned shop is never pinned from a fix too wide to trust.

`arriveHere` in `visit.tsx` calls it with `getFix({ precise: true })` (`Accuracy.High`, the only high-accuracy fix in the app), `mbos.location.gpsAccuracyThresholdM` and `mbos.location.visitMismatchM`. An override reason opens a `too_far` refusal and nothing else. Nothing is written on refusal — the leg stays open, no meter photo exists, no dwell clock. The **one fix** taken there closes the leg and checks the visit in, so the arrival and the check-in can never disagree about where the shop is.

## On the server

<Code file="src/lib/actions/mbos.ts" symbol="handleVisit" collapsed />

Highlights:

- **Never refuses on distance.** Computes `verified`, `unverified_reason` and `distance_from_shop_m` itself. A missing override reason is said in words — it is also the only trace of a payload that did not come from the app.
- **Pins an unpinned shop** from a good fix, guarded by `gps_lat is null` in the statement, not only in the branch.
- **`pin_correction = 'requested'`** where the phone asked and the shop has a pin. No coordinates are stored with it: what is accepted is the check-in fix already on the row.
- **Suspect cap**: a missing decision at the cap does not refuse; it becomes a sentence for the manager. `still_suspect` without a reason is the same.
- **Binds media**: shop and owner photos and the voice note were uploaded naming `parentId: 'pending'`; `bindMbosMedia` re-parents them after the row is safe, or the nightly orphan sweep deletes them.
- **No JS `Date` is bound** in raw SQL — `checkInAt.toISOString()`. A bound Date once made every visit a retry, and the order and payment behind it waited for ever.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Refuse only at the arrival">
Any new refusal belongs before anything is written. Never refuse at the save or on the server.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep one number for the radius">
`mbos.location.visitMismatchM` is both the refusal distance and the flag distance. Do not split it.
</Step>
<Step title="Bind media after the parent">
Any new photo on a visit must be named in `MBOS_PARENTS` and bound by `bindMbosMedia`, or the orphan sweep deletes it.
</Step>
</Steps>
