Everything you do in MBOS is **saved on the phone first**, then sent to the office when there is signal. No signal is normal — your work waits safely on the phone and goes up by itself. This page explains how to see what is waiting, what *Not accepted* means, and what to do when the phone seems stuck.

## How your work reaches the office

<Steps>
<Step title="You save">
A visit, an order, a payment, a lead — it is saved on the phone at once, even in a basement.
</Step>
<Step title="The phone sends it">
Within a few seconds if there is signal; otherwise every minute until there is. Photographs go **after** the record they belong to.
</Step>
<Step title="The office answers">
Most things are accepted. A few are refused, with a reason — they go to **Not accepted**.
</Step>
<Step title="Your book comes back updated">
The same round trip brings down what changed at the office: new shops, approved orders, tasks, messages.
</Step>
</Steps>

## After a visit: the receipt

When you finish a visit, **Visit saved** lists every part with a dot: **green** is done, **amber** is saved but not finished yet — for example *Your manager will see it when this phone sends*, or *Voice note kept. The office will type it out when you are online.* Amber is not a problem; it means *waiting for signal*.

<Callout kind="warn">
**Not accepted is not the same as waiting.** A record waiting for signal will go by itself. A record that was *Not accepted* will not, until you fix it — the phone does try refused records again every few hours in case the office's rule changed.
</Callout>

Something you depended on being refused (say, a visit) stops the records behind it (the order and payment taken on it). Fix the first, and the rest follow.

Nothing on this screen deletes anything unless you press **Remove** and confirm.

## When the phone seems stuck

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Things waiting for a long time with signal | Press **Send now** on *Waiting to send* and read the message. |
| *Sign in again to send this* | Your sign-in has lapsed. Sign in — nothing waiting is lost. |
| New shops or settings never arrive | Update the app if *Update to …* shows. If it still does not arrive, sign out and sign in again. |
| *Your book did not refresh* | Something on the phone could not take the office's data. Tell your manager the exact words; your own work is still being sent. |
| No trail on the Live map, or the office says your phone is quiet | Open **More → Phone setup** and fix every red line — location set to *Allow all the time*, battery saver off for MBOS. Then restart the app (not the phone). |
| Photos stuck | If the office has set photos to Wi-Fi only, they wait for Wi-Fi. Records still go on mobile data. |

<Callout kind="note">
**Signing out does not lose waiting work** if it has already been sent. Before signing out, open *Waiting to send* and make sure it is empty.
</Callout>

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Does the app work with no signal?">
Yes. Everything is saved on the phone and sent later. Your book, prices and tasks are on the phone too.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Will I lose work if the phone dies?">
No. It is saved the moment you press save. It is sent when the phone is back on with signal.
</Faq>
<Faq q="The office says they cannot see my order, but it is not in Not accepted.">
Check *Waiting to send*. If it is waiting behind a visit, the visit has not gone yet. Press **Send now**.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why does my phone tell the office my battery level?">
So your manager can tell a flat battery from a switched-off phone when your trail stops. It is only sent while you are checked in.
</Faq>
