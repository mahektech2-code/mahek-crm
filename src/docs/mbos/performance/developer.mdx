The handset holds a SQLite `performance` table filled from the `performance` pull channel: at most two rows (this month and last), each a copy of a `sales_performance` cache row with its formulation/category bands folded in as JSON. Any other period is fetched live from `GET /api/mbos/performance?from=…&to=…`. Nothing on the phone writes a score. **Reports** is a separate, local-only count over owned tables.

## The pull channel

<Code file="src/lib/services/mbos-service.ts" symbol="performanceFor" collapsed />

The bands are read through **both** keys — `category_id` and `formulation_id` — with a `left join` on each. An inner join on categories once sent an empty mix for every formulation target, which reads on the phone as nothing having been asked rather than as a hole in the payload. `performance.test.ts` sends a formulation target through `buildBootstrap` to pin it.

The channel is gated on `computed_at > since`, so an hourly rebuild reaches the phone on the next delta.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Change the scoring at the office only">
Edit `src/lib/engines/performance.ts`. The phone renders what it is sent and the live route uses the same functions.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a figure to the phone">
Add it to `performanceFor`, the handset's `performance` table, `listPerformance`'s SELECT, and the live route's reading. A column the handset cannot hold is dropped by `upsert`, silently.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep the warning one rule">
If the phone's warning should match the office's, make `priceNotVolume` read `performance.volumeDivergencePoints` from the synced config rather than a constant.
</Step>
</Steps>
