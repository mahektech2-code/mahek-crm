**Performance** shows your month against the target the office set for you: rupees, litres, the mix of products, new customers, collection and tasks, and one score out of 100. **Reports** is simpler — a plain count of what you did this month, straight from your phone.

### What each part means

| Part | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Revenue** | The value of your **approved** orders, excluding GST. An order waiting for accounts does not count yet. |
| **Litres** | How much you sold, worked out from each product's pack size. Prices cannot move this. |
| **Mix** | Whether you sold the products your target asked for, in the shares it asked. |
| **New customers** | Shops whose **first** order landed this month. Adding a lead does not count. |
| **Collection** | Money accounts have **confirmed** in the bank, as a share of what was overdue on your book when the month began. A reported payment does not count until confirmed. |
| **Activity** | Tasks done, out of the tasks set for you. |

If a part has no target, the screen says *You have no target for … So it is left out, and the other parts count more. The score is still out of 100.*

<Callout kind="note">
The score is **worked out at the office**, not on your phone. It refreshes every hour during the day. A few orders this morning may not be in it yet — the time under the score tells you how fresh it is.
</Callout>

If no target has been published for you this month, there is nothing to score and the screen says so.

## Reports

**More → Reports** counts what you did this month and last, from your own phone: visits, orders taken (and how many were refused), litres, order value, and money collected. It needs no signal and is not a score — it is what happened.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I took a big order this morning but my revenue has not moved.">
Revenue counts approved orders only. Your order is waiting for accounts. It is listed under your shops as taken but not yet approved.
</Faq>
<Faq q="My rupees are over 100% but the screen says the month is not as good as it looks.">
Prices went up, so the same quantity earns more rupees. Your litres are below target, which means you sold less. The warning is there so the month is read honestly.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Reports and Performance show different order values.">
Reports counts every order you took on this phone. Performance counts only orders accounts have approved, from the office's records.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I see a colleague's performance?">
No. The phone only ever shows yours.
</Faq>
