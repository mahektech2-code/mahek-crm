Tasks live in SQLite `tasks` on the handset and in `mbos_tasks` at the office. They come **down** on every pull (the `tasks` channel) and go **up** as `entityType: "task"` outbox items — `create` for one the salesman added, `update` for done, answers and moves. Notifications come down on the `notifications` channel into a local table; push is sent by the office through Expo and routed on tap by `PushTaps`.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a task column">
Add it to `mbos_tasks`, the bootstrap/delta SELECT, the handset's `tasks` table and `upsertTasks` — all four, or the pull NULLs it.
</Step>
<Step title="Send a new kind of notification">
Write it through `notifyUser`/`notifyUsers` with an `mbosHref` that is a real handset route, or null. Never a web path.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a sound or channel">
Channels are native on Android; a new channel id needs a new APK, and release builds strip sounds named only at runtime.
</Step>
</Steps>
