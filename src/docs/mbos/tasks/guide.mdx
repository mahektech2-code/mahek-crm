**Tasks** are jobs with a date: ring a shop, visit about a sample, collect a form, answer a few questions for the office. Some you add yourself; most come **from the office** or are raised by the app (a bounced cheque, a sample to chase). The **bell** is where the office tells you what it decided — an order approved, a leave answered, a target set.

Tasks are grouped **Overdue**, **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **This week** and **Later**. The group is worked out from the due date every time you look, so yesterday's *Today* is this morning's *Overdue*.

### Finishing a task

<Steps>
<Step title="Press Mark as done">
A box opens: **What you did · needed**. Write one line — *Gave them the new rate list. They will order next week.* Your manager reads this.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a photo if it helps">
**Add a photo (optional)**. A photo never stops the task being saved.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
The task leaves your list. It goes to the office on your next sync.
</Step>
</Steps>

For a task with questions, press **Answer and finish**. Each question must be answered (the screen says *One answer is still needed* until it is). **Anything else (optional)** is for what the questions did not ask. Some questions update the shop's record directly — the task says so.

### Moving a task

**Move it** offers **Next day**, **A week later**, or a day you pick — never earlier than the day it is already due. You must say why. *Three moves is a story your manager needs.*

<Callout kind="warn">
If a task stays overdue for <Setting k="mbos.tasks.escalationHours" /> hours past its date, your manager is told and you get a *Task overdue* message.
</Callout>

### When the app is closed

The office can reach your phone even when MBOS is not open — a **push notification** on your lock screen. **Tap it and the app opens straight on the thing it is about**, after you unlock. If the app is open, a banner drops from the top of the screen instead.

At night (quiet hours) messages still arrive, but **silently** — no sound.

If you never get push messages, open **More → Phone setup** and check notifications are allowed for MBOS.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I marked a task done but it came back.">
The office refused it — usually because it needed a note saying what you did. Check **Not accepted**, then finish it again with a line of what you did.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A notification opened the list instead of a screen.">
The message is about something this phone has no screen for. The message itself is in the list.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I delete a task from the office?">
No. Finish it with a note, or move it with a reason. If it is wrong, say so in the note.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I got no push but the message is in the bell.">
The bell is the record — it fills on every sync. Push is a courtesy on top and can fail (notifications off, battery saver, phone off). Check the bell when you start the day.
</Faq>
