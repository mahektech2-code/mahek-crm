Your working day starts when you **punch in** and ends when you **punch out**. Both need a selfie. While you are punched in, the app records your route; the moment you punch out, it stops. You can punch out for lunch and punch in again — each stretch is kept separately, and your hours are the total.

## Punching in

<Steps>
<Step title="Press Punch in">
If the app opens **Before you start** instead, your phone is not ready to record your route. Fix each row it lists, come back and press Punch in again. See [Installing and phone setup](/docs/mbos/setup).
</Step>
<Step title="Take a selfie">
The front camera opens inside the app. Take the photo. **There is no way to start the day without it** — if you cancel, nothing is saved and you can try again.
</Step>
<Step title="Say what you are travelling on">
Pick your vehicle for today — bike, car, bus or train, walking, a customer's vehicle. If it has a meter, the app asks you to **photograph the meter**: today's kilometres are counted from this reading.
</Step>
<Step title="Your day has started">
The timer starts and the app begins recording your route. If you were far from your base, the day still starts — you may be asked afterwards why.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note">
No GPS, or a weak one, does not stop you punching in. The day starts and the app records that the location was missing. Only the selfie and a phone that cannot record your route stop a punch-in.
</Callout>

## Breaks

Going for lunch? Press **Punch out** (with a selfie). When you are back, press **Punch in again** (with another selfie). Your route stops while you are on a break and starts again when you return. Your hours for the day are the stretches added together — the break is not counted.

## Punching out

<Steps>
<Step title="Press Punch out">
On Home. From the evening the app reminds you, and the button moves to the top.
</Step>
<Step title="Take a selfie">
The same as the morning. The day does not close without it.
</Step>
<Step title="Photograph the meter, if you have one">
On a metered vehicle, photograph the closing reading. The two readings are your day's mileage — you do not type kilometres anywhere else.
</Step>
<Step title="Add expenses if prompted">
On a day without a meter — bus, train, auto — the app asks you to add today's fares in **Expenses** now, while you remember them. You can say **Later**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## If you forgot to punch out

The next morning Home tells you. The office closes a forgotten day at the **last thing you did** — your last visit or entry — not at a made-up time. If that is wrong, open **More → Attendance**, find the day and press **Ask your manager to fix this day**. Write what happened. Your manager decides.

If you rode a metered vehicle that day, Home also asks you to photograph the meter now if it has not moved, so that day's kilometres are counted.

## Your attendance history

**More → Attendance** lists your recent days.

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **Present** | You worked a full day. |
| **Half Day** | You worked more than half a day but less than a full one. |
| **Absent** | Not punched in on a working day, or worked too little. |
| **On Leave** | An approved leave day. |
| **Weekly Off** | A day you were not expected to work. |
| *Away from base* | You punched in outside the distance from your base. |
| *No punch-out* | You did not punch out; the office closed the day for you. |
| *Sent to your manager* | You asked for this day to be fixed and are waiting for an answer. |

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Does the app track me after I punch out?">
No. Recording starts at punch-in and stops at punch-out — on a break too.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why does the app need location all the time?">
So it can keep recording your route while your phone is in your pocket with the screen off. It still stops the moment you punch out.
</Faq>

<Faq q="My camera will not open.">
The app needs camera permission for the selfie. Open phone Settings → Apps → Mahek MBOS → Permissions → Camera and allow it.
</Faq>

<Faq q="How long are the selfies kept?">
Only long enough for your manager to check them — about <Setting k="mbos.attendance.selfieRetentionHours" /> hours after they reach the office. After that the photo is deleted; the record that a photo was taken stays.
</Faq>
