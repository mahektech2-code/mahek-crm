The **Journey** tab is where you plan your days. The office proposes a city for a day; you say yes or send it back; then **you pick the shops**, because you know which doors are worth a Tuesday morning. On the day, the app puts your shops in a walking order and takes you to each one.

## Planning a day

<Steps>
<Step title="Answer a proposed day">
When the office proposes a day you see **A day is waiting for your answer**. Press **Agree**, or **Send it back** and say why it will not work. You may name a city you would rather go to — that part is optional.
</Step>
<Step title="Or plan a day yourself">
Press **Plan it**, pick a date and the city you will work in. You cannot plan a day that has already passed, or a date that is already on your plan.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the shops">
On an agreed day press **Pick shops**. The list shows **only shops in that day's city**, nearest first. Tick them in the order you mean to walk — the number beside each is the plan. Press save.
</Step>
<Step title="Change your mind">
Open the day and pick again. Unticking a shop takes it off. Saving with nothing ticked clears the day back to agreed.
</Step>
</Steps>

### How "nearest" is measured when you pick

| Planning | Distances are measured from |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Where you are standing now. |
| **Any other day** | The middle of our shops in that city — not your home. |

Shops with no map pin are listed after the others, never dropped.

## Working the day

<Steps>
<Step title="Open today's route">
From Home, press **Today’s route**. Your stops are in walking order, starting from where you are.
</Step>
<Step title="Start the next visit">
Press **Start visit** on the next shop. The app opens the visit and shows you how far the shop is. See [Visiting a shop](/docs/mbos/visit).
</Step>
<Step title="Get directions">
Press **Navigate**. Your phone's maps app opens with the shop as the destination. If the shop has no pin, the maps app searches its name and town instead — that lands you near the name, not on the doorway.
</Step>
<Step title="A shop that is not on the plan">
Press **Add an off-plan stop**, say why, then pick the shop. Your reason goes with that visit.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Near me

**More → Near me** lists shops around you that are **worth a stop right now**, best first — not simply the closest.

| Reason shown | Why it counts |
|---|---|
| You have a task for this shop | The office asked you to do something here. |
| Overdue to reorder | They usually order by now and are well past it. |
| Payment pending | They owe money. |
| Due to reorder | Their usual order day has come. |
| An open lead | A new shop you are still working on. |
| Never visited | Nobody has been. |

A shop with none of these reasons is not listed. Distance counts against a shop, so a far one needs a better reason than a near one. The first shop on the list is the **Next Best Visit**. You can choose how far to look.

## The map

The Customers tab and the shop picker can show your shops on a map. It tells you in words when it cannot show something:

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| *The map is not set up yet. The office must set it up first.* | No map key is set in the office. The list still works. |
| *None of these N shops has a location yet.* | No pins to draw. Checking in at a shop saves its pin. |
| *No signal. No map is saved for this place.* | You have no signal and no offline map here. Save one on Wi-Fi — see [Offline maps](/docs/mbos/offline-maps). |
| *N shops have no location yet, so they are not on the map.* | Some shops are missing from the map only because they have no pin. |

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Why can I not see a shop in another town when I pick?">
A day is one city. A shop elsewhere can be added on the day as an off-plan stop, with a reason.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The office did not accept the shops I picked.">
The day says so, with the office's reason. Usually a shop has left your book since you picked it. Pick again.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why is a shop at the bottom of my route?">
It has no map pin, so the app cannot place it. It stays on the route. When you check in there, the shop gets its pin.
</Faq>
