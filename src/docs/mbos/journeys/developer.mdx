Planning is two outbox entities — `plan_day` (the answer, or a self-planned day) and `plan_stops` (the whole picked list) — handled by `handlePlanDay` and `handlePlanStops` in `src/lib/actions/mbos.ts`. Everything that happens on the day is pure arithmetic on the phone: `engines/route.ts` for the order, `engines/nearby.ts` for Near me, and a deep link for navigation.

## Picking shops

<Code file="mbos-app/src/data/journey.ts" symbol="pickCandidates" collapsed />

Three corrections live in that query and must stay together:

1. **The city filter is in SQL**, because the read is capped at `PICK_PAGE`. Filtering in JavaScript after a `LIMIT` paged the wrong sixty.
2. **The origin is the city's centroid**, one `AVG(gpsLat), AVG(gpsLng)` over the city's pinned shops — not over the search result, or typing a letter moved every distance on the screen.
3. **The `ORDER BY` is distance**, as a flat-earth square (SQLite has no trigonometry); the exact haversine then orders the rows that come back. The cap and the visible order must sort by the same thing.

## The route

<Code file="mbos-app/src/engines/route.ts" symbol="optimiseRoute" collapsed />

`journey.tsx` reads `mbos.route.averageSpeedKmph`, `maxTwoOptPasses`, `maxStopsForTwoOpt` and `minutesPerStop` and passes them in. The engine takes no clock and no store, so the same stops always give the same order. Unpinned stops are appended and flagged.

## Near me

<Code file="mbos-app/src/engines/nearby.ts" symbol="nearby" collapsed />

<Code file="mbos-app/src/engines/nearby.ts" symbol="nextBestVisit" />

`whatIsNearby` takes the freshest fix, reads every pinned shop with its cycle, outstanding, open task and lead stage, and calls the engine with `mbos.location.nearbyPerKilometreCost`. The worth of each reason is a constant in the engine (`WORTH`); only the distance cost is configuration. The lead-stage comparison is case-insensitive because the handset stores the wire's own words ("Contacted", "Negotiation") — a lower-case list once matched none of them.

## The map

`ShopMap` returns a sentence instead of a map in four cases — loading, no key, no pinned shops, and offline with no saved coverage — and draws with a caption for partial coverage or unpinned shops. Note MapLibre React Native's bounds are `[west, south, east, north]`, GeoJSON's order, not the web's `ne`/`sw` pair; getting it wrong opens on the wrong hemisphere rather than failing. Offline coverage is [Offline maps](/docs/mbos/offline-maps/developer).

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Keep the cap and the order in one query">
Any new filter on the pick list goes into SQL before the `LIMIT`, and the `ORDER BY` must match what the screen claims.
</Step>
<Step title="Never invent an origin">
Where neither a fix nor a pinned shop exists, return null and keep the query's order.
</Step>
<Step title="Never drop an unpinned stop">
The route appends; only Near me may omit a shop with no coordinate, because it cannot honestly claim one is near.
</Step>
</Steps>
