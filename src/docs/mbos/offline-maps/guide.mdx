The map on your phone needs the internet to draw streets — unless you have **saved the map** for that area. Save the towns you work in while you are on Wi-Fi, and the map draws in a market with no signal at all.

## Saving maps

Open **More → Maps kept on the phone**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect to Wi-Fi">
Maps can be several hundred megabytes. While <Setting k="mbos.maps.downloadOnWifiOnly" /> is on, the phone will not download on mobile data and says *Connect to Wi-Fi*.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the places you work this week">
Read the size on each row. Save the ones you need.
</Step>
<Step title="Leave the screen open or come back later">
The download carries on. Open the screen again to see how far it has got.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note">
A shop with no pin does not make a place. Checking in at a shop saves its pin, and its area appears here.
</Callout>

## What the map shows out in the field

Wherever a map appears (Customers, Nearby, the journey), it tells you which of four things is true:

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **The map, as normal** | The area is saved, or you have signal. It makes no difference which. |
| **The map, with "Not in the saved map"** | Part of the area is saved. Some shops are on a blank patch — the message says how many. |
| **"Save maps for offline"** instead of a map | No signal and nothing saved here. Tap it to go to the Maps screen. |
| **No map at all, with a sentence** | The office has not set up maps yet, or none of these shops has a pin. |

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="The size was much bigger (or smaller) than the screen said.">
The size is an estimate. A busy market has more detail than farmland. It is right about whether it is tens or hundreds of megabytes.
</Faq>
<Faq q="It says the area is too big.">
The office limits how big one saved map can be (<Setting k="mbos.maps.maxPackMegabytes" /> MB). *Ask the office to make this area smaller.*
</Faq>
<Faq q="My saved map is old.">
After <Setting k="mbos.maps.refreshAfterDays" /> days the row suggests updating. It only downloads what changed. Old maps still work.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Will the app delete my maps to save space?">
No. Nothing is ever removed unless you press Remove.
</Faq>
<Faq q="It says maps are not set up yet.">
The office has to add a maps key. Then maps work after your next sync. Nobody in the field can fix this.
</Faq>
