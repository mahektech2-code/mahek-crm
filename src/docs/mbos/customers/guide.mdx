The **Customers** tab is your book: every shop you work in your area, and your leads. It works with no signal — everything on it is already on your phone. Tap a shop to open its record: its orders, what it owes, its samples and complaints, and everything anybody has done with it.

### Sorting the list

| Order | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Nearest** | Closest to where you are standing. |
| **Nearest a town** | Closest to the middle of a town you pick — for planning a day elsewhere. |
| **A–Z** | By shop name. |
| **Most owed** | Biggest outstanding first. |
| **Longest since visit** | Never visited first, then the longest ago. |

<Callout kind="note">
**Nearest** sorts your whole book by distance. **More → Near me** is different: it lists only shops with a reason to go, best first. See [Journeys](/docs/mbos/journeys).
</Callout>

### What the words on a card mean

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **Active** (green) | Buying on their usual rhythm. |
| **At risk** (amber) | A while past their usual order. |
| **Dormant** (red) | Well past it — they may have stopped. |
| **Lost** (red) | Long past it. |
| **Closed** (red) | The office has closed this account. |
| Nothing beside the name | Nothing to measure yet — they have never ordered. |
| **Reorder due** / **Reorder late** | Their own buying cycle says an order is due, or well overdue. |
| **Third party** | A shop we deliver to but bill somebody else for. |
| **Lead · Suspect** (and other stages) | A lead, and which stage it is at. |

## When the list is empty

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **No area set for you yet** | The office has not given you an area, so your list stays empty. Nothing is lost. Ask your manager. |
| **No customers yet** | You have an area but no shops in it. |
| **No shop matches** | Your search or filter hides everything. Press **Show all shops**. |

The line under the search box tells you your area — *Your area: …*. If it is wrong, you can ask for a different area from the **Journeys** history screen; your manager decides.

### The statement

The **Account** tab lists bills and payments with a running balance, newest first. Only money the office has **confirmed** moves the balance. A payment that is reported, held, rejected or reversed is shown with its word but does not count — so when the shopkeeper says "we paid that", you can see what happened to it.

For the complete account, open **More → Customer accounts**. With signal it fetches the whole account from the office and remembers it, so it opens later with no signal too — and says how old it is.

A **lead** or a **third-party** shop has no statement: we do not bill them. The tab says so.

### "Can buy"

On the Overview tab, **Can buy** is somebody's estimate of what the shop could spend in a month. It shows who made the estimate and when. Blank means nobody has judged it — not that there is nothing to win.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Why did a shop disappear from my list?">
The office moved it out of your book, or changed your area. It leaves your phone on the next sync.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The outstanding figure and the statement do not agree.">
Use the outstanding figure — it is the office's. The statement on your phone covers only part of the history.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I add a shop?">
Yes. If no shop matches your search, press **Add the shop**. A shop we deliver to but bill someone else for needs **who gets the bill** chosen first. A brand-new shop is usually added as a lead — see [Leads](/docs/mbos/leads).
</Faq>
