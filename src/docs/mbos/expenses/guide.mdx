This page covers the money and time that are yours: **travel** (how you got around), **expenses** (what you spent), **leave**, **holidays**, your **salary**, and the **policy** that says what you are allowed. Your manager decides your expenses and leave; the office pays.

## Travel: asked twice a day, nowhere else

You are asked about travel only **twice**:

1. **At punch-in** — how you are travelling today (your bike, a car, bus or train, on foot). If it is a vehicle with a meter, you photograph the meter and type the reading.
2. **At punch-out** — the meter again, if there is one.

Between the two, every visit records its journey **on its own**. You never type a fare at a shop.

<Steps>
<Step title="Punch in and pick how you travel">
On a bike or car: read the meter, type the number, take the photo. The camera will not save one without the other.
</Step>
<Step title="Work your day">
Press **Start visit** when you set off for a shop and **I have arrived** when you get there. The phone keeps the time and distance.
</Step>
<Step title="Punch out and read the meter">
The two readings are your kilometres for the day. They are paid per kilometre — nothing to type.
</Step>
<Step title="On a bus, auto or train day, claim your fares in Expenses">
After punch-out the phone reminds you. **Later** is always allowed, but fares are easy to forget.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warn" title="A common mistake">
Typing the meter with a digit missing. If the closing reading is below the opening one, or the distance is impossibly long, the phone refuses it **while you are still at the meter**, so you can read it again.
</Callout>

If you forgot to read the meter at punch-out, the next morning the phone asks for **last night's closing reading**. It is saved with a note that it was entered late.

### The Travel screen

**More → Travel** shows today's legs and what each is worth. It is a **record**, not a form — the amounts are worked out by the policy on your phone, so you can see them, never type them.

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick what it was and type the amount">
Write who you paid and the bill number if there is one — it stops the same bill being claimed twice.
</Step>
<Step title="Photograph the bill">
A claim with no bill shows *No bill. May not be accepted.*
</Step>
<Step title="If it is over the limit, say why">
The claim still goes. Your manager reads your reason.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
It goes to your manager straight away. There is no day to close.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can claim up to <Setting k="mbos.expenses.backdatedDaysAllowed" /> days back. Older than that, ask your manager to enter it.

**More → Policy** lists exactly what you are allowed — per kilometre, per meal, per night — and the version and date it started. If you are paid less than you expected, read this first.

## Leave

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Leave and press Apply for leave">
Pick the type, then one day, half a day (Morning or Afternoon), or a range.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the day count">
Holidays and your weekly off are not counted. If the dates overlap a request you already made, the phone says so.
</Step>
<Step title="Send">
Your manager decides. The answer arrives in your bell and on the Leave screen.
</Step>
</Steps>

The top of the screen counts **leave you have taken this year**, by type. It does **not** show a balance — whether a request is paid is the office's decision. You can withdraw a request that has not been decided.

## Holidays

**More → Holidays** is the company calendar with **your** days marked. Holidays for another state or city are listed below in grey, with where they are for, so you know why the team there is off.

## Salary

**More → Salary** shows your salary **as HR holds it today**, once, and each month's days worked and reimbursements **beside** it — never added to it. There is no incentive figure. If a figure is wrong, tell HR; it comes from their records.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="My fare is not on the Travel screen.">
Fares are not entered on Travel. Claim them in **Expenses** as *Local transport* (an auto in town) or *Bus or train* (between towns).
</Faq>
<Faq q="My expense says Not accepted.">
Usually a large claim with no bill photo, or a claim older than <Setting k="mbos.expenses.backdatedDaysAllowed" /> days. The message says which.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Why can I not see how much leave I have left?">
The office decides whether each request is paid. The screen shows what you have taken, which you can check.
</Faq>
<Faq q="I punched in on the wrong vehicle.">
Tell your manager. The day's kilometres are priced from the vehicle you picked.
</Faq>
