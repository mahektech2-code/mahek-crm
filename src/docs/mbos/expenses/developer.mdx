Travel, expenses and leave are all **owned** records: written locally, queued, and handled at the office by `handleTravelLeg`, `handleExpenseDay`, `handleExpense` and `handleLeave`. Holidays, the travel modes, the expense policy, leave balances and salary come **down** only. The policy engine is shared, so the handset prices a leg with the same rules the office pays from.

## Expenses

<Code file="mbos-app/src/data/requests.ts" symbol="claimExpense" collapsed />

The claim names its **day** and is queued behind the `expense_day` item, so it never arrives naming a day the office does not have. `category` is the four-value legacy enum the server accepts; `kind` is the precise one the policy prices on — sending a kind as a category would reject every auto fare.

## Changing it safely

<Steps>
<Step title="Pricing changes go in the policy, not a screen">
The handset's figures come from the synced policy and the shared engine. A number typed into `travel.tsx` would disagree with what is paid.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep the day first">
Anything that names an `expenseDayId` must be queued with a dependency on the day item.
</Step>
<Step title="A meter rule is checked twice">
Change `checkOdometer` on the handset **and** the server check in `handleTravelLeg`; the server is what protects against an old build.
</Step>
</Steps>
