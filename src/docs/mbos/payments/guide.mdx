When a shop hands you money, you record it on the phone **before you leave the counter**. The phone makes a receipt straight away — even with no signal — and you can send it to the shop on WhatsApp. The office checks the money later. Until then, the shop's balance does not change.

<Callout kind="money">
Recording a payment does **not** reduce what the shop owes. It tells the office the money is coming. Accounts reduce the balance only when they find the money in the bank.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick the shop">
If you are on a visit, the shop is already chosen. Otherwise pick from the list of shops that owe money, or search.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the bill">
Tick the bill they are paying. If they pay more than the bills you ticked, the screen says how much is extra: *It is kept as advance on the account.* If they are not paying a particular bill, tick **Not against a particular bill** — the money goes against their oldest bills first, and anything over sits on account.
</Step>
<Step title="Say how they paid and how much">
Pick the mode and type the amount. For a cheque, read the number and the date **off the paper** and take a photo before you give it back.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
The phone shows *Take ₹X?* with the shop, mode and cheque details. **You cannot change it later on this phone**, so check it now.
</Step>
<Step title="Send the receipt">
The phone offers **Open WhatsApp** with the receipt already written. You press send. If the shop has no number saved, you can only copy it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note">
The receipt number starts with **TMP-**. That is a temporary number. The office gives the real receipt number when the phone syncs.
</Callout>

### Banking cash or handing in a cheque

<Steps>
<Step title="Tap I banked it (or Bank all on one slip)">
The phone asks *Yes, I banked it?* For a cheque it asks *Yes, handed in?*
</Step>
<Step title="Photograph the slip">
Take a photo of the bank slip. Without one the row says *no slip photo. The office has only your word.*
</Step>
</Steps>

### A cheque that bounced

Tap the cheque and choose **It bounced**. You must say what the bank said. The office is told, and a high-priority task appears on your Tasks: *Call the shop. The cheque bounced.* The shop believes it has paid — somebody has to ring them.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="I recorded the payment. Why does the shop still owe the same amount?">
Because the office has not found the money in the bank yet. A payment from the field is *reported*. Accounts confirm it, and only then does the balance go down.
</Faq>
<Faq q="My payment says Not accepted. What happened?">
Usually the bill you named was already paid by the time the phone synced. The message says which bill. Record the payment again against the bills that are open now. Nothing was counted, so you are not taking the money twice.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I edit a payment after I confirmed it?">
No. The confirm screen says so. If you made a mistake, tell accounts — they can reject or reverse it at their desk.
</Faq>
<Faq q="How long can I keep cash before banking it?">
<Setting k="mbos.payments.cashDepositSlaHours" /> hours from when you collected each one. Each collection has its own clock.
</Faq>
<Faq q="My manager got a message about my collection. Why?">
Collections of <Setting k="mbos.payments.managerNotifyThresholdPaise" /> paise or more are reported to your manager. It is not a complaint — large amounts are simply told.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Where do I see what the shop owes and has paid?">
Open the shop, then the **Account** tab. It shows their bills and payments for the last <Setting k="mbos.sync.statementMonths" /> months.
</Faq>
