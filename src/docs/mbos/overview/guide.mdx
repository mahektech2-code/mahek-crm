MBOS is the app on your phone. It is called **Mahek MBOS** in your phone's settings. You use it to start your working day, go to shops, take orders, collect money and add new shops — and it works **even when there is no signal**. Everything you do is saved on the phone first and sent to the office when the phone can reach it.

This page is the map: who the app is for, what a working day looks like, and where everything is. Each part of the app has its own page — follow the links.

## Who uses it

<Cards cols={2}>
<Card title="Field salesman" tone="brand">You work a city or an area the office gives you. You visit shops, take orders, collect payments and find new shops. Your phone holds **your** customers and nobody else's.</Card>
<Card title="Manager on a phone">A sales manager can also sign in to MBOS. A manager sees their team's book, and is never left with an empty phone for want of an area.</Card>
</Cards>

The office does not use MBOS. They see your work on the Sales Dashboard — see [Sales Dashboard overview](/docs/sales/overview).

### The four tabs and the + button

| Tab | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your day, today's route, your leads, your target, and anything the office needs from you. |
| **Journey** | The days the office has proposed to you, the days you have agreed, and the shops you picked for each. See [Journeys](/docs/mbos/journeys). |
| **Customers** | Your book — every shop you work — and your leads. See [Customers](/docs/mbos/customers). |
| **More** | Everything else, in four groups: Work, Me, Resources, Settings. |
| **+** | Start visit, Punch order, Collect payment, Add lead, Add expense, Create task, Request sample. |

### What is under More

| Group | Items |
|---|---|
| **Work** | Near me, Tasks, Leads, Pending actions, Orders, Payments, Customer accounts, Samples, Reports |
| **Me** | Attendance, Leave, Holidays, Salary, Today's travel, Expenses, What you are allowed, Performance |
| **Resources** | Product catalogue, Documents, Offline maps |
| **Settings** | Profile and preferences, Send to office, Set up your phone, Not accepted, Sign out |

A small number beside an item means something is waiting there — for example *2 overdue* beside Tasks, or *5 to send* beside Send to office.

## Your day, step by step

<Steps>
<Step title="Punch in">
On Home, press **Punch in**. The phone checks that it can record your route, then asks for a selfie. Your day starts when the photo is taken. See [Starting and ending the day](/docs/mbos/attendance).
</Step>
<Step title="Open today's route">
Press **Today’s route**. It lists the shops you picked for today, in an order that saves walking. See [Journeys](/docs/mbos/journeys).
</Step>
<Step title="Visit a shop">
Press **Start visit** on the next shop. When you reach the shop, press **I am at the shop**, then check in. Fill in what happened, take an order or a payment if there is one, and check out. See [Visiting a shop](/docs/mbos/visit).
</Step>
<Step title="Take orders and money as they come">
From the visit, or from the **+** button. See [Orders](/docs/mbos/orders) and [Collecting payments](/docs/mbos/payments).
</Step>
<Step title="Punch out">
When your day ends, press **Punch out** and take a selfie. If you are not on a metered vehicle, the app reminds you to add today's fares in Expenses.
</Step>
</Steps>

## No signal? Keep working

You do not need signal to work. Every order, payment, visit and note is saved on the phone straight away, then sent when the phone finds signal — on its own, every minute or so.

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| *Order saved · sending to the office now* | It is saved on the phone and going now. |
| *Order saved · will send when you have signal* | It is saved on the phone. Nothing is lost. It will go by itself. |
| A number beside **Send to office** | That many things are still waiting to go. Open it to see each one. |
| A number beside **Not accepted** | The office could not accept something, and says why. Open it and fix it. |

<Callout kind="warn">
Do not uninstall the app or clear its storage while anything is waiting to send. What has not reached the office is only on your phone.
</Callout>

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="Can I sign in on a friend's phone?">
No. Your account is tied to one phone. If your phone breaks or you get a new one, ask your manager to release the old one, then sign in on the new one.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why is my customer list empty?">
If it says **No area set for you yet**, the office has not given you an area. Ask your manager. If it says **No customers yet**, you have an area but no shops in it yet.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can somebody else use my phone after me?">
Only once everything you did on it has reached the office. Until then the phone refuses anybody else and says how many entries are still waiting.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The office says they cannot see my order.">
Open **More → Send to office**. If the order is still there, it has not been sent yet — find signal and press **Send now**. If it is under **Not accepted**, the office refused it and the reason is written there.
</Faq>
