A **lead** is a shop that has not bought from us yet. You add it on the phone the first time you meet them, and then you move it up, step by step, until it orders. The phone tells you at every step **what is still missing** before it can move — and it works with no signal.

## The steps a lead climbs

Which steps depends on the kind of sale:

| Kind of sale | Steps |
|---|---|
| **Direct customer** (we bill the shop) | Suspect → Prospect → Qualification → Sample trial → Sample received → Sample review → Negotiation → First order → Delivery → Payment → Second order → Customer |
| **Third-party customer** (a distributor bills the shop) | The same, without *Sample received* |
| **Distributor** (appointing one — no longer offered for new leads, but older leads still climb it) | Suspect → Prospect → Qualification → Management review → Commercial discussion → Distributor approval → Agreement → Initial stock order → Active distributor |

A lead added before these steps existed has no kind of sale and climbs the old six: New → Contacted → Qualified → Negotiation → Won (or Lost).

<Steps>
<Step title="Read Still to do">
Every step has its own list. Fill what it asks — a tick is not enough where there is a box for the actual answer.
</Step>
<Step title="Press Move up">
When nothing is missing, the button turns on. If you press it while something is missing, the phone says <i>Not yet</i> and names what.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the next action">
Every move asks what happens next, on which day, by whom. A lead may never sit with nothing promised.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Suspect or Prospect: deciding after a few visits

A new lead is a **Suspect**. You have about two visits to decide if it is worth working.

- From visit <Setting k="mbos.leads.visitsBeforeDecision" />, the card warns you to decide.
- At visit <Setting k="mbos.leads.maxSuspectVisits" />, you **must answer before you can close the visit**: **Make it a Prospect** or **No, not a Prospect**. Keeping it a Suspect asks you why.

Your visit is never refused. If you go past the limit, your manager is told.

## GST number

For a lead on the old six steps, the office needs the shop's **GST number** before it can become *Qualified*. Add it in **Prospect details**. The phone checks it as you type — *One character of that GST number is wrong. Check it against their certificate.*

## What happens when a lead is qualified

When a lead on the old six steps becomes *Qualified*, your **lead manager** (the person you report to) is told, and a **validation call** appears on their list for the next working day. They ring the shop to check your visit: did you come, did you explain Mahek, is the need real. Their answers sit beside yours — they do not replace them.

What the office found is shown on the lead page under **The office called them**, beside what you recorded. If the call is made from a phone, turning a Prospect down needs a reason.

**Trial started**, then **Tried**.

An **approved** sample moves the lead to **Negotiation**, which is what lets you take its first order.

## On hold and lost

- **On hold** — a real prospect that is not moving right now (a shutdown, a budget quarter). Pick why, the day it comes back, and the next action. It comes back to your **Lead actions** list by itself on that day.
- **Lost** — nobody will call this shop again. Pick why. It stays on the list under *Lost*.

## Lead actions

**Lead actions** is your morning list for leads, with four views: **Pending today**, **Late**, **On hold** (and coming back), and **Nothing pending** — leads with no next action at all. Fix the last one first.

## Questions people ask

<Faq q="The Move up button is grey.">
Something on the *Still to do* list is missing. The phone checks the same rule the office does, so pressing it with something missing would only be refused later.
</Faq>
<Faq q="A lead the office added is not on my phone.">
It arrives on your next sync. If you are editing that lead and your change has not gone yet, the office's version waits until yours has gone.
</Faq>
<Faq q="My lead says Not accepted.">
The office refused the change. Open **Not accepted** to see why — often a missing GST number or reason.
</Faq>
<Faq q="Can I take an order from a lead?">
Only once it reaches Negotiation. No price or order is promised before then.
</Faq>
